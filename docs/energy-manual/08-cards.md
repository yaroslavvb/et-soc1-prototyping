# 8. Card-to-card variation

Every catalogue and rerun table in sections 2 to 5 was measured on three cards with the same binaries, in the version-3 check of 26 September: aifoundry2 and aifoundry3 (firmware 1.3.1; aifoundry3 pinned at 600 MHz by its 0 W TDP) and aifoundry1's card 1 (firmware 1.2.0). The hot line of section 6 is aifoundry2 and aifoundry3 only (23 September). aifoundry1's card 0 overheats and is left out (docs/findings/14-card-behaviour.md).

| Comparison | aifoundry3 / aifoundry2 | aifoundry1 card 1 / aifoundry2 |
|---|---|---|
| Instruction and byte energies, 392 catalogue entries, 3 passes each, each card at its own die temperature | median **0.972**, 10th–90th percentile 0.916–1.004, range 0.81–1.23 | median **0.962**, 10th–90th percentile 0.902–1.141, range 0.76–1.25 |
| The same, per instruction and per byte (medians) | 0.971 and 0.973 | 0.954 and 1.147 |
| The catalogue pass by pass (the version-3 check, 99% interval) | 0.976 [0.961, 0.990] | 0.967 [0.950, 0.985] |
| Relay, rings and levels (and the hot line on aifoundry3) | median 0.953 over 24 entries, range 0.73–1.10 | median 1.130 over 19 entries, range 0.94–1.50 |
| Tensor-unit switching, fp32 random data (section 3.2), each card at its own launch temperature | 0.907 (0.964–0.973 at the die temperature of each launch, note C2) | 1.023 (1.023–1.024 at the die temperature of each launch, note C2) |
| Idle against aifoundry2's law (the version-3 idle cycles) | +1.01 W at 54–84 °C | +10.07 W at 56–81 °C |
| Die temperature under load, catalogue median (aifoundry2: 74 °C) | 58 °C | 61 °C |
| Minion and SRAM rail voltage (aifoundry2: 518 and 704 mV) | 522 and 698 mV | 499 and 751 mV |

**What it says.** aifoundry3 reads 3% lower than aifoundry2 in the median (0.972) and aifoundry1 card 1 reads 4% lower than aifoundry2 in the median (0.962), each card at its own die temperature; the 23 September catalogue, which this one replaces (its data: `docs/reports/data/2026-09-23-energy-manual/catalogue-23sep.json`), gave 0.950 for aifoundry3. aifoundry1 card 1 reads 0.954 per instruction but 1.147 per byte. Against each card's own common scale, once the 392 comparisons are allowed for, no entry on aifoundry3 and two entries on aifoundry1 card 1 (`wire/hop2/zeros` 1.22× and `l1fill/stride128/random` 1.28×) differ beyond the noise of three passes (99%, Bonferroni). The rerun entries of sections 4.2, 5 and 6 do not share the catalogue's scale.

**What sets the scale.** The energy per operation rises with die temperature on aifoundry2, +0.48% per °C [+0.07, +0.90] and aifoundry3, +0.30% per °C [+0.18, +0.42], and is not resolved on aifoundry1 card 1, +0.35% per °C [−0.11, +0.81] (a 30-entry panel run hot and cool on each card, 6–14 °C apart; 99% intervals): enough to account for aifoundry3's gap, its die 16 °C cooler here, so that aifoundry3 itself differs from aifoundry2 is not established. With aifoundry3 cool and aifoundry2 warm, as here, the panel gives 0.985 [0.977, 0.993]. Switching power itself follows the die: the random-data fp32 matmul drew +1.2 W [+0.3, +2.1] more on aifoundry2 launched at 83 than at 77 °C and +2.0 W [+1.5, +2.5] more on aifoundry3 launched at 65 than at 58 °C. By V² alone the rail voltages would make aifoundry3's instructions cost 2% more and its SRAM accesses 2% less; and aifoundry1 card 1's instructions cost 7% less and its SRAM accesses 14% more: the voltage does not explain aifoundry3's scale, and is in line with aifoundry1 card 1's cheaper instructions and dearer bytes, which its cooler die would not explain.

**How the bars split.** For the catalogue's 392 shared entries the pass-to-pass scatter on one card is 1–2% (median standard error) and about half of the bar an entry carries is the difference between the cards, the rest the day. The smallest signal, the hot line (about 1.2 W over idle), carries one of the widest bars, ±17%, mostly because its first session read 1.4 W against 1.0–1.2 W in every pass since, and because on so small a signal the leakage correction, a few tenths of a watt, moves each pass by 10–25%; the DRAM relay's bar (±13%) is mostly aifoundry1 card 1 against the other two.
