"a2", "a3" and "a1c1" are aifoundry2, aifoundry3 and aifoundry1's card 1, each as its own mean ± its pass-to-pass standard error. **What the table says.** A contended atomic costs 17× the same atomic spread over 32 lines, and its cost is not on the requesters: the shire that hosts the line keeps its share of the atomic, but its own other loads stop ([One hot line stops a shire](https://spacesheep.dev/@yaroslavvb/et-soc1-hot-line), docs/findings/17-hot-line.md). Waiting itself is cheap — a stalled minion draws about 1.2 mW, less than a spinning one (1.9 mW, section 2) — so a barrier's energy is small next to the leakage the card burns while it lasts.

The contended row's bar is wide mostly because of the first session: the whole chip stalled drew 1.4 W over idle in it, against 1.15 W in the mean of the passes since, and the per-operation figure divides that small number by a rate the bank fixes at one per 10 cycles.

**The packed atomics and scatter-add** (E48, three passes on each of three cards, 26 September; the last six rows of the table). A packed atomic applies its eight lanes one after another: `famoaddl.pi` adds into a shire's table at 12.7 G updates/s, the rate of scalar `amoaddl.w` on the same tables (12.8 G/s), for 1.45× its energy per update; at the home L3, `famoaddg.pi` runs 2.80 G/s against 2.79 G/s, for 1.55×. Every update counts: the verify launches checked every counter of the shared tables, and none was lost. On a table private to each hart, the vector unit's own gather, `fadd.ps` and scatter runs at 139 G/s at 0.036 nJ in the L1; 18.2 G/s at 0.85 nJ in the L2; 18.2 G/s at 0.80 nJ in the own scratchpad; 422 M/s at 22.2 nJ in DRAM. Past 10 G updates a second, the rate [Influence functions on the ET-SoC-1](https://spacesheep.dev/@yaroslavvb/et-soc1-influence-functions) asked of a scatter-add: gather-add-scatter in the L1, the L2 and the own scratchpad, and `famoaddl.pi` and `amoaddl.w` on a shire's table; nothing that updates the home L3 or DRAM.
