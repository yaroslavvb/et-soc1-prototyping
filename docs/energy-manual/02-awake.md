# 2. A core that is awake

The cost of a minion that is running and doing as little as it can: an `addi` loop, no memory, no data. Everything an instruction costs in section 3 is on top of section 1 and includes the awake core.

Every entry is **mean** [lo–hi]: the mean over every pass on every card, and the full range those passes spanned. "a2", "a3" and "a1c1" are aifoundry2, aifoundry3 and aifoundry1's card 1, each as its own mean ± its pass-to-pass standard error.

**The addi loop is not the floor.** It increments seven registers, so its operands change on every instruction. With both harts a `nop` costs 5.1 pJ [4.8–5.7] and a `fence` 4.4 [4.0–4.8] per instruction ([3.1](03a-every-instruction.md)), so the awake core is about 4.4–5.1 pJ per issue slot. The ablation's loop issued at half the one-hart `addi` loop's rate, on hart 0 only; it is the loop behind the figures in [Why is the ET-SoC-1 low power?](https://spacesheep.dev/@yaroslavvb/et-soc1-why-low-power), 1.5 mW per minion and 8 pJ per instruction on aifoundry2. The version-3 check resolves it from idle on aifoundry2 (1.51 W [0.58, 2.44]) but not on aifoundry3 (0.40 W [−0.07, 0.86]) and aifoundry1 card 1 (0.73 W [−0.68, 2.14]) (99% intervals over four runs). But the registered switching values carry a launch-temperature offset (AMENDMENTS.md, note C2): they are referenced to 80.9 °C (aifoundry2 and aifoundry1 card 1) and 55.8 °C (aifoundry3) while the runs launched at whole-degree readings of 80.1, 57.4 and 80.0 °C in that order; the references were taken at launches on a downward step of the reading, so the die at these launches was at the reading or up to 0.96 °C above it, which the reading cannot tell apart, so at the die temperature of each launch aifoundry2's are between 0.62 W lower and 0.15 W higher, aifoundry3's are 0.91–1.43 W higher and aifoundry1 card 1's are between 0.73 W lower and 0.05 W higher, on some cards as large as the loop itself: at that temperature it draws 0.78–1.56 W on aifoundry2, 1.33–1.86 W on aifoundry3 and 0.00–0.78 W on aifoundry1 card 1. Per minion, the matmul's power is flat from 256 to 1,024 active minions on aifoundry2 (1,024 against 256: 1.09); as registered it is 1.24 on aifoundry3 and 1.25 on aifoundry1 card 1; at the die temperature of each launch, where the offset moves the smaller 256-minion runs most, it is 1.05–1.16, 1.02–1.09 and 1.24–1.39 in that order, so which card is flat is not settled.

**Rules.** An awake minion costs about 2 mW; a second hart adds about 1 mW; a minion stalled on a contended atomic draws less than one spinning (1.2 against 1.9 mW). Keeping 1,024 minions awake for a second is 2–3.1 J, against 36 J for the card at 80 °C, so the awake cost is small next to leakage and next to real instructions.

The bars here are ±9–9%, a little wider than the catalogue's median, because the signal is small: 2–3 W over a 26–43 W idle that drifts by a few tenths of a watt with the die temperature. On the two-hart loop aifoundry3 reads 1.05× and aifoundry1 card 1 reads 0.96× aifoundry2.
