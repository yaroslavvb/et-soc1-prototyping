The useful ideaWiring needs three dimensions: tracks travel sideways, and vias connect the levels.

Four illustrative metal tiers alternate their track direction. Vertical vias connect the layers. Layer separation is adjustable. This is a conceptual model, not an ET-SoC-1 wiring layout.

Model notes & public sources

ET-SoC-1 is fabricated in TSMC’s 7 nm process family. “7 nm” is a technology name, not the width of every feature. These models illustrate general interconnect and tri-gate concepts; they do not reconstruct the chip’s proprietary process or physical design.

The channel glow indicates illustrative inversion charge. In a narrow fin, charge can extend into the body (volume inversion); it need not occupy three separate sheets. Symbolic dots do not predict electron count, speed, current or leakage. Multiple fins add gated perimeter, but real drive depends on geometry and device conditions.
