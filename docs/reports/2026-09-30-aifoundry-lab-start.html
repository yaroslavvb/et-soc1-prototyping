New user? Start here

2 October 2026 (PDT) · for new users of the AI Foundry ET-SoC-1 lab · the lab dashboard

Three steps. Steps 1 and 2 are commands you paste into a terminal on your own computer: they create your own account on a lab machine and start Claude there, as you. Step 3 is one line you give that Claude.

Your computer

Only a terminal: you paste the ssh commands of steps 1 and 2. No agent runs here.

The lab machine, as you

Claude runs here, in your own account, in a tmux session called claude. All the work happens here. Root is used once, in step 1, only to create your account.

Before you start: join the lab's Tailscale network (ask Roman, the lab lead, in #community-lab on the AI Foundry Discord). Each ssh below may print a login.tailscale.com link: open it in your browser, approve it, and the command goes on.

Lowercase letters, digits, - and _, starting with a letter. It fills the commands below.
