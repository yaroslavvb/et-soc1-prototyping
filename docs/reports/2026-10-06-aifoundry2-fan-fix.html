Covers Tue 6 Oct 2026 (week 41), 15:54–16:50 PDT: aifoundry2 in the AI Foundry lab at Studio 45. Written Tue 6 Oct 2026, about 16:45 PDT, from the lab's live collector (one record every 5 s), the test logs and photos of the BIOS screens.

Update, 7 October 2026: the fix held for about a day. At 03:55 on 7 October the cooling at the card got worse, and at 16:46 the idle card ran away to 138 °C and fell off the PCIe bus again. It is out of service. What happened on 7 October.

The card is fixed. Idle, it now holds at 64 °C (27.4 W). On 2 October the same idle card climbed past 95 °C within the hour and fell off the PCIe bus at 138 °C. Under the 8-minute load test it reached 76 °C, well inside its limits. Only the heaviest load still drives it to its 90 °C cap within a minute, more slowly than on 21 September.

Reading the numbers

  • "Die" is the mean of the 34 minion-shire sensors, read live. It is what every chart here shows.
  • The "hottest" figure is a peak, not a live reading. The card reports its sensors' minimum and maximum since its statistics were last reset, and nothing has reset them since today's boot. So after the heavy run it reads 93 °C while the card idles in the 60s, and it will stay there until the next reset or reboot. (On 2 October it tracked the live temperature only because the card heated the whole time.)

What was done

  1. 15:54, the BIOS. Del at boot, Advanced Mode; F6 opens Smart Fan 6 (the lab notes said Smart Fan 5).
  1. 15:56, the cause on screen. Every fan header followed the CPU temperature (43 °C) on the Normal curve: 58% of full speed at 40 °C, 25% at 20 °C. The card's slot sensor (PCIEX16) read 57 °C, the hottest on the board. An idle host meant slow fans, while the idle card kept heating.
  1. About 16:00, the fix. CPU_FAN Speed Control set to Full Speed, then TUNE ALL to copy it to every header. Fan Stop stays Disabled and Control Mode stays Auto (at full speed the fan gets full power either way). System 5 read 2,368 RPM, and within five minutes PCIEX16 fell from 57 to 55 °C and PCIEX8 from 51 to 48 °C.
  1. About 16:03, saved. F10, save and exit; the machine booted (16:05). The card came up on the bus at 67 °C.
  1. About 16:06, a full-reset reboot, the one in the lab's card-behaviour notes that power-cycles the slot. The machine was up at 16:07.
  2. Checks. The card enumerated at 16 GT/s x8, the driver (et_soc1 0.20.0) loaded, the kernel logged no PCIe errors since boot, nobody held the card, and the live collector has read it every 5 s since 16:05.

What changes for people using the lab

  • aifoundry2's card is back in service. The lab's dashboard links this page from its Worklog, under 6 October.
  • Its fans now run at full speed all the time, so the machine is louder. If that's a problem, aifoundry3's setting is the fallback: Manual, with every point of the curve at 50% or more.
  • The setting lives in the BIOS. A BIOS update or a CMOS reset puts the fans back on the Normal curve; set it again then. (Smart Fan 6's Save Fan Profile can keep a copy.)
