A schematic of Esperanto's ET-SoC-1 that you can click through: the die with its mesh of shires, a shire with its cache and minions, a minion with its vector unit, whose lanes also run the tensor instructions. Six animated flows show where data goes and what it costs, with the latency, bandwidth and energy measured on the lab's cards. A tour steps through it all in twelve steps.

How to use it

  • Click any part (or Tab to it and press Enter) for what it is, its numbers with their units and card coverage, and a link to the report that measured them. Point at a number to see its source; from the keyboard, Tab to a fact in the details list. Click again (or Enter again) on a shire or a minion to zoom in; the path under the diagram, or Backspace, zooms out.
  • Flows 1 to 6 animate a load that misses to DRAM, the latency ladder, TensorSend across the mesh, the relay against DRAM, gathers and scatters, and the host pushing work over PCIe. Space pauses and resumes; the details panel shows each leg's numbers.
  • Tour steps through twelve slides: Right and Left arrows, PageDown and PageUp (a clicker) or the arrow buttons move, Space pauses a flow, F goes full screen, Q or End tour ends the tour (in full screen, Esc usually leaves full screen instead). The tour enlarges the panel's text and hides its fact lists.
  • For a projector: P (or the Panel button) hides the details panel so that the die takes the whole width, and D switches between light and dark. The same from the address: ?theme=light, ?panel=off. The stage fits one screen from 1280 × 720 up; on a narrow screen the drawing scrolls sideways.
