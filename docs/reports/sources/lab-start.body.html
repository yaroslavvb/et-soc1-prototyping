New user? Start here

30 September 2026 (PDT) · for new users of the AI Foundry ET-SoC-1 lab · part of the ET-SoC-1 measurement reports

The lab is three machines with four ET-SoC-1 cards, shared by several people in #community-lab channel of AIFoundry discord.

Each card is an ET-SoC-1 on PCIe: 1,088 small RISC-V cores. The machines are x86_64 Ubuntu 24.04, reached over the lab's Tailscale network. The lab is starting to log card use on them (who, which program, when; never arguments or files), so that everyone can see when a card is free: et-usage shows it where it is installed.

What you do

  1. Ask Roman for access. Roman is the lab lead. Ask him, on the AI Foundry Discord's #community-lab, for an account on the lab machines and an invite to the lab's Tailscale network.
  2. Join Tailscale. Accept the invite and install Tailscale. Later, approve in your browser the login links your agent shows you.
  3. Give your agent the prompt. Type your username below, copy the prompt, and paste it into your coding agent (Claude Code on your laptop works). The agent picks a free machine and card, sets up a Claude that lives there with Remote Control (a few steps at your keyboard; Remote Control needs a claude.ai plan), runs a first test, and asks you before it touches a card.

The username you were given. Leave it blank and your agent will ask.
