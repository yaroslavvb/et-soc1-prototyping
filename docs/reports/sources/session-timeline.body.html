The session timeline

19–29 September 2026, all times Pacific (PDT) · built from the session’s transcripts, the lab machines’ queue logs and the repository · data and tools in yaroslavvb/et-soc1-prototyping · part of the ET-SoC-1 measurement reports

One Claude Code session ran the ET-SoC-1 work for a week. It started with the owner’s first message at 09:19 on Saturday 19 September and this snapshot ends at . Four things happened at once, and the chart below puts them on one time axis. The owner sent messages. The agents were the main agent and subagents, most of them started by workflow runs. The cards were four ET-SoC-1 cards in three lab machines. The artifacts were deploys of published pages and commits to the repository.

The owner’s messages appear only as summaries of at most 12 words, never their text. One report for the lab lead appears without its name, content or link.

      5. How it was counted

        Data: docs/reports/data/2026-09-27-session-timeline, which holds the seven extracts and timeline.json, which this page draws. Generator: tools/timeline/build_timeline_data.py. Extraction scripts: tools/timeline, whose README.md gives their order and settings. They read the session’s transcripts and the lab machines’ logs, which are not in the repository, so the committed extracts are the page’s inputs; tools/timeline/sanitize_extracts.py removes from them what the page withholds and checks that nothing private is left.
