## Caveats

Three passes, one card, one clock, designed tiles. All three held tiles are twins of `signs`, not `signs`: their sums are n × a seed, and `signs`' moving sums are small exact integers. `z_ss`'s A words are zeros from three of the four sign patterns, because (+0, +0) would close the gate, and its products and aligned operands are zero. fp32's `s_abs` differs from `signs` in its draws and has absorbed products, which also swing the adder's output, by the bench above `signs` in three blocks. So R3 and R4 are not like for like (result 2). fp32's one number on zero products, about 0.35, crosses sessions. The reads see op ends only (result 6). The board's step in fp16 carries one low pass, a dip the registered test kept; the rail's does not. On `signs` words with S = 0 (25 of 256 in fp16, 50 in fp32) C stays small and the RTL's operand order can change (`txfma_c2.v:144-152`), so `signs`' alternation counts are a sign-stream count there. The watts are the board's; the rail figures are about 0.8–0.9 of them.
