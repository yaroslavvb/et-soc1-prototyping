## R0 on this arm

This README's note 12 records the four 'wrong' checks: p3a (`gemmL1x1x25` fp16, E212's tile `fld16_mant`) at runs 040 and 073, words 16,384, within 0, exact 0; p3b (E217's tile `h_th_in`) at runs 014 and 085, within 12,160, exact 0; each process rc 1; the twins read 'approx' (p3r at 035 and 075) and 'exact' (p3z at 041 and 072). It reads them as the host's check at this depth, "but this lane did not test that". Arm A's checks on aifoundry1 card 1 give the same counts (p3a at runs 035 and 076, p3b at 008 and 092), and the same two tiles read 'approx' at `gemmL1x1x1` (K 1,024) in E212's and E217's sessions (within 16,384, exact 0).

The desk reading (arm A's dated note 1, section 2; post hoc, REPORTED; NOT TESTED on a card):
- **The check** (rgm136 `host/main.cpp`, sha256 `f9b2279f838ad304...`, the host source both arms built (E224's kit pins it: arm A's harness and arm B's box source list), lines 823-856, 995-1013 and 1050-1059) compares 64 sampled C tiles word by word with a reference summed in double: 'within' when |C - want| <= 1e-4 x the sum of the absolute products. With one tile file every pool tile is that file's tile (lines 786-792), so each C tile is 800 k-tiles of one 16 x 16 partial product and the 64 tiles are equal (12,160 = 190 x 64). `fld16_mant`'s 1,024 halves are all positive, in [1, 2): the tolerance is 1e-4 of each word's own value.
- **The depth.** An fp32 accumulator that drops the bits below its running sum's last place at each addition (rounding toward zero, or truncating the smaller operand at alignment) loses part of a unit in that last place every time, always in the same direction, so for same-sign terms its loss grows in proportion to K: of the order of K x 2^-25 of the sum, which is **7.6e-4 at K 25,600** (above the 1e-4 tolerance) and **3.05e-5 at K 1,024** (inside it); this lane's emulation gives 5.3e-4 and 2.1e-5 (the median word) for per-product rounding toward zero. Rounding to nearest errs both ways, and its errors largely cancel (2.5e-5 at K 25,600).
- **The emulation:** of 28 candidate fp32 accumulations, the 13 that give p3a's counts at both depths (0 of 256 within at 800 k-tiles; 256 within and 0 exact at 32) all drop low bits, per product or per pair of products; the six fused round-to-nearest models put p3a at 255-256 within at 800 k-tiles and leave 1-6 exact words a tile at 32. p3b's 190 of 256 lies inside the range of the 11 of those 13 that also give its counts at 32 k-tiles (144 to 256 within at 800), but none gives it.
- **The reading:** the same counts on both cards are those of an fp32 accumulation that drops the bits below its running sum's last place per product or per pair of products, checked against the host's fixed tolerance of 1e-4 at a K 25 times that of E212's and E217's runs of these tiles. So R0's FAIL is read as the host's fixed tolerance failing at this depth for an all-positive repeated tile, not as a GEMM computed wrong. The records keep the check's counts only, not C's words, so neither the card's exact datapath nor C's values are established here, and nothing of this was tested on a card. The same counts on two cards, one at 600 MHz and this one under its governor, point at the arithmetic and the check, not at either card.

**What stands:** R0 reads FAIL on both arms by its registered words and labels every other verdict of each arm and the pooled ones; no point is dropped or re-chosen and nothing is re-scored. A later kit could scale the check's tolerance with K (for same-sign data, about K x 2^-25 of the sum), check pattern tiles only at small K, or print the sampled C words so that a desk can tell the datapath (REPORTED: a suggestion, not a change to E224).
