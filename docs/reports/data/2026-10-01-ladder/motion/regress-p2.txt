What differs, and why (all by design):
- "pip false at depth N" (kb, during, tour, newpa and some fuzzer bursts): the suites' invariant that the levels'
  mini-map of the scale above shows when the camera is two or more scales deep. The page no longer draws it: the Up bar
  names the scale outside and goes there, as on the chip diagram.
- phone (3), parts390 and parts360 (their P1, and the harness's null tap that follows), bar's B6 (MISMATCH: two taps at
  the L2 meant to zoom two scales deep): one tap zoomed on the committed page; now a tap selects and a double-tap zooms,
  as on the chip diagram. (bar's B6 never ran on the base: the base run stopped at B5 on a harness error.)
- fuzzp-11, -22, -33 (harness errors in bursts with "scale"): the phone fuzzer taps the scale control #zc, which is gone
  (the breadcrumb and the Up bar replace it); no invariant broken.
- "unselected tab" (fuzzkb): the keyboard focus left on a tab not selected; the same on the base page.
The fault the suites found: on the build before the fix, fuzz3-d's burst 8 broke six invariants (the fuzzer looked in
the middle of a hand-over, which __memState().zooming did not count; tracing it found a tab clicked during a waiting
move of the ladder losing its level). Fixed: zooming counts both cameras and the hand-over, and a move asked of the
levels' camera cancels a hand-over on its way (ml_ladder_test.mjs T23).
