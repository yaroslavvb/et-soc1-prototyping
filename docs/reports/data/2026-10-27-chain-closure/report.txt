  The measured price per flipped bit barely moves — 87 to 129 fJ over m = 1 to 23 — while the
  single-bit price list says it should run from 106 to 382 fJ per bit over the same masks.
  The position premium is a SINGLE-BIT effect: it does not add when bits flip together.

  three candidate laws on these seven rungs:
    flat, 107 fJ per flipped bit, position ignored rms  14.4%   worst  22.7%
    price list x m^(alpha-1), alpha = 0.775        rms  28.5%   worst  62.5%

  >>> the flat law wins, and the free power law's exponent 0.993 is 1.00 within its scatter:
      inside one word, board power counts flipped bits and all but ignores which ones.
