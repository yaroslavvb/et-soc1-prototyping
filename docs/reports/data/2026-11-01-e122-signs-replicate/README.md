## Caveats

Four passes, one session on each of two cards of one design, one clock, designed tiles: every accumulator pattern is a rank-one seed (`c_mx_f` matched to `s_walk`'s bit count, `b_B_z` to `b_B_w`'s sums) beside random ±1 operands, not a real tensor. Each card ran its own build of the workload, as E122.md registered ("the card's own tree"): the kernel ELF and both hosts' hashes differ from card 1's (kernel `d521e432…` against `990807f4…`, power host `20e760c2…` against `e0fbecce…`, read-back host `92d15e46…` against `79bf5f2d…`); the read-back patch's source is identical (`main.cpp`, `bf5435ac…`) and the cycles per op are equal (546.001) (C4). The location is a statement about watts on the board, paired within passes; "goes with the accumulators differing" names what the tiles change, and the bench's model, not a measurement, says which nets. `b_B_z` has 468 fewer alternations an op than `z_s` and 216 fewer than `b_B_w`, at a price nothing here measured for a held tile whose accumulators differ; E108 priced them post hoc on card 1 (R12 0.68–1.05, MOST in every case) and E122 did not re-price them, so the same caveat holds: how much of the rest goes with the sums changing is not established. R7's tiles hold by absorption, whose subtraction swing `s_walk` lacks, so a share above 1 is not a negative walk on its own. The cross-card tolerances (0.75 W, 0.15, 0.30, 0.60) are E122's own choice, frozen before the data; a real difference smaller than the tolerance reads PASS or INCONCLUSIVE, not "equal". R10's HOLDS rests on the primary and four of the seven arms (gates off, auto, 0.2414, 0); with the dips kept every pass is kept here (no span-gate set-aside, where E108's dips-kept arm set two passes aside), but the dips widen its interval to ±1.02. The leakage arms 0.3803 and 0.545 set pass 12211 aside (`int8.zeros` departs −0.29 and −0.45 W from its straight line) and fail the tie, as E108's did. The watts are the board's; the rail figures are about 0.8 of them, and on the rail R12 reads 1.029 ± 0.057 and R7 1.114 ± 0.095.
