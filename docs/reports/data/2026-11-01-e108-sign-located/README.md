## Results

**In plain words.** On one card, in one session, random ±1 costs 4.80 ± 0.32 W more than all +1 in fp32 and 4.98 ± 0.11 W in fp16, within 0.27 W of E106's steps (R2 PASS). **The like for like item 5 names reads alike:** on zero products, with the sums read back as held, fp32 keeps 0.414 ± 0.036 of its step and fp16 0.427 ± 0.038, a difference of +0.012 ± 0.022 (R3 ALIKE; INCONCLUSIVE with the board meter's dips kept or no leakage correction). The cross-session "about 0.35 against 0.45" goes. fp32's absorbed design keeps 0.10 ± 0.04 more than its zero-product one in the same session (R4 ABSORBED HIGHER: 0.517 against 0.414). **In fp32 the rest is located, on designed tiles: most of it goes with the 256 accumulators holding different values, so that a lane reads, adds and writes back a different word every micro-op.** On zero products, with B's signs, a tile held within each op at the walking tile's op-end sums costs 0.970 ± 0.013 of B's rest as measured (2.91 of 3.00 W; R12 MOST, in every arm), and the walking tile costs 0.09 ± 0.04 W more than it (M4 0.11). That 0.09 W is a tile contrast, not the price of the sums changing: the walking tile also alternates 216 more times an op, and what an alternation costs in a held tile whose accumulators differ, nothing here measured. Priced post hoc at the per-alternation figures this lab has measured, R12 reads 0.68–1.05, MOST under every pricing, and the walk's own part −0.17 to +1.64 W, so how much of the rest goes with the sums changing is not established (result 1). Held by absorption, with both sides' signs, a tile whose lane words toggle as many bits as `s_walk`'s costs 1.13 ± 0.09 of the rest (2.53 against 2.24 W; R7 MOST, INCONCLUSIVE with the dips kept); post hoc, 1.12 ± 0.09 with `c_mx_f`'s lane-bus toggles beyond `s_walk`'s taken off. Post hoc, the watts come to roughly 30–50 fJ per differing bit of the lane's C-read and result words (31.5–51.4 across six contrasts). The axes: A's signs cost what B's do per alternation (R5 PRICED ALIKE); B's signs alone carry the whole step (5.00 W against both sides' 4.73 W), and A's add nothing measurable on top of them walking (R6, −0.27 ± 0.19 W; the held leg is INCONCLUSIVE); the accumulators cost more as more of their bits differ (R8 RISES twice: sign only 0.16 ± 0.34 W, sign and exponent 0.79 ± 0.48 W, all three 2.53 ± 0.29 W); the alternation costs 0.485 ± 0.185 of its held price on walking sums in fp32 (R9 FALLS), and fp16's walking premium over fp32's is 1.61 ± 0.48 (R10 INCONCLUSIVE, E104-R6 narrowed from ±2.73). ## What the page may now say

## Caveats

Four passes, one card, one clock, designed tiles: every accumulator pattern here is a rank-one seed (`c_mx_f` matched to `s_walk`'s bit count, `b_B_z` to `b_B_w`'s sums) beside random ±1 operands, not a real tensor. The location is a statement about watts on the board, paired within passes: "goes with the accumulators differing" names what the tiles change, and the bench's model, not a measurement, says which nets. `b_B_z` has 468 fewer alternations an op than `z_s` and 216 fewer than `b_B_w`, at a price nothing here measured for a held tile whose accumulators differ: priced post hoc, R12 reads 0.68–1.05 (MOST in every case) and the walk's own part −0.17 to +1.64 W, so neither the direction of that bias nor how much of the rest goes with the sums changing is established (result 1; `corrections-check.txt` C2). `c_mx_f`'s k = 0 row carries 712 more designed bus bits an op than `s_abs`'s (4,076 against 3,364), about 0.006 W at M4's bus price; M4's bus term over `s_abs` (0.29 W) is mostly the lane's nets carrying the differing C words, which `s_walk` has too (0.27 W), and with only `c_mx_f`'s excess over `s_walk` taken off, R7 reads 1.12 ± 0.09 (C1). R7's tiles hold by absorption, whose subtraction swing `s_walk` lacks, so a share above 1 there is not a negative walk on its own. The dips-kept arm keeps two passes, 10810 and 10813: E78's span gate sets aside 10811 (`fp32.randn` spans 1.111 W) and 10812 (`fp16.randn` spans 1.102 W). So its intervals take t(0.975, 1) = 12.71 against the primary's 3.18 and come out 4–59× wider; it moves R3, R4, R5, R7 and R9 to INCONCLUSIVE, with their means moving by up to 0.11 in the ratios (R3 +0.110, R4 +0.080, R7 −0.081, R9 +0.068) and by −0.244 W in R5. The leak-0 arm keeps three passes (10811 set aside: `fp16.zeros` departs −0.262 W from its straight line) and moves R3, R5 and R9 to INCONCLUSIVE, with their means within 0.05 (C4). The rail, which the dips do not touch, gives R12 1.005 ± 0.122 and R7 1.31 ± 0.69 (one pass's `c_mx_f` rail reads high). The watts are the board's; the rail figures are about 0.8–0.9 of them. The fp16 side of the rest was not split here: the location is fp32's, on B's signs (R12) and both sides' (R7).

## What this changes

- **For the blog (Horace follow-up, §5):** on one card, on designed tiles, a random sign's ~5 W has two measured parts. The held part, its sign bits and the adder alternating between adding and subtracting while the sums hold, depends on the design: 0.41–0.43 of the step on zero products, about the same in fp32 and fp16 in one session; 0.52 absorbed in fp32; 0.19 on fp16's cancelling products (E106). In fp32, most of the rest goes with the running sums the signs scatter: a lane alternates between accumulators that now hold different values and reads, adds and writes back a different word every micro-op, post hoc roughly 30–50 fJ per differing bit. A walking tile at the same op-end sums costs only 0.09 W more, but it also alternates 216 more times an op, so how much of the rest goes with the sums changing is not established. fp16's rest was not split.
- **For the kinds page and the hub:** item 5's "The rest is by subtraction and not located" goes for fp32, with the line above, and stays for fp16; "a random-sign tile itself cannot be held" stays true and no longer matters for fp32's location, because `b_B_z` holds the walking tile's op-end sums; the like for like is done (ALIKE); E104-R6 narrows to 1.61 ± 0.48. Item 6 gains a blind test on 8 new tiles (the held tiles whose accumulators differ, and one side's signs, held and walking): M4 read them to 0.084 W rms, high on all 8. Over all 25 tiles it reads 0.153 W rms, but 17 are reused tiles whose frozen predictions earlier sessions had already scored, among them the alternation on walking sums the item lists as open.
