## What a blog reader can now be told

Each sentence holds on one card at 600 MHz, one 16 × 16 window of a layer, over the format's all-zeros level, unless it says otherwise. **Not to be said:** "real numbers cost what random costs" as a rule, or "in every model"; "attention" beyond GPT-2; "fp16 does the same except in attention" without ViT's unresolved cell; "a live int8 lane costs less and the gate takes the rest"; "an int8 ReLU tile costs less than its live share" without T5; "the same numbers" across formats, or 55% as if comparable with 32%; "one huge activation"; "0.005 W, nothing"; a slope with ±0.005 or ±0.026 as its uncertainty; "predicted before the run", or "blind" with no statement of commit order and model choice; "closed" for item 3 in int8.
