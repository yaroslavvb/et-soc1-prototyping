## Interpretation (labelled: not measured by E94)

Inside a rule, more gating lowers D along a line whose slope holds about D(random) per unit of gate share. That fits results 3, 4 and 8, and it is why a one-line law on a number from kinds.json does about as well as a five-term predictor inside a kind. It is not proportionality to the live share: the line through `fp32.sparse50`'s draws reaches +1.39 ± 0.63 W at g = 1, and E93 read +0.65 W against live share × cost on a half-gated int8 tile, which may be the same excess seen another way. Nothing here shows that gated lanes cost nothing. E94 reads only power, so it cannot separate "the lane is gated" from "fewer live operands toggle"; E93's negative-zero result is what supports the gate. It is a statement about offsets from a measured tile: E90 C1 stands, and absolute prices need a measured neighbour. The dense kinds' small spread fits operand bits priced at about the operand bus's own cost, with the multiplier and result path set by the distribution, which every draw of a rule shares; result 9 is a correlation, and E94 does not separate those stages.

## Caveats

One card, one clock, one session, three passes. Nine draws know an SD to a factor of 0.68–1.92, six to 0.62–2.45, four to 0.57–3.73: that is why the dense claim is pooled. R8 tests the translation on three rules only; the kinds' means on aifoundry3 are translated, not measured there; the card scale (E82: 1.031, sd 1.2%) is a median over kinds, and the nine draws' own regression reads 1.10 ± 0.16, so 1.03 may run slightly low. The rule is synthetic: nothing here says what a real tensor's "kind" is, and the speed columns were not re-drawn. The gate law's ratio form is E94's, worked out on E90's draws, and R6 and R7 are its first test on new draws; the idea was E93's first (C7), and its slope is too steep in `fp32.sparse50` (result 8). "Beating Q130(n)" is one rule, `fp16.nn_like`, and centred Q130(n) is slightly better. Results 7, 8 and 9 are post hoc. The registered intervals over draws leave out the leakage arms, which here move offsets by up to about 0.15 W (`fp32.sparse50`, −0.48 to −0.74 W). int8's bus counts are provisional (kinds README), so its bus readings are reported only. Noise is pooled over a rule, which is conservative for an anchor (three runs a pass) and optimistic for a single tile, and a tile's pass-to-pass values carry a within-pass structure: the committed `fp32.sparse50` read 6.742, 7.140, 7.183 W, and it ran ahead of the first zeros anchor in two of those passes, where such tiles read −0.305 and +0.276 W against their other passes (C10); the two-way fit does not remove that. It cancels here (−0.670 ± 0.380 W with the pass's mean zeros), and with the tile's own SD its offset's interval is ±0.514 W, which still excludes 0.
