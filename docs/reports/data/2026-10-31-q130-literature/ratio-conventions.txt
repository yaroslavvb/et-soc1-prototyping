  So the x13.6-against-x1.6 contrast is a statement about the QUIET-DATA
  FLOOR, and only over idle.  The floor as a SHARE of each machine's own
  busiest switching is the convention-robust way to say it:
    A100 fp32    zeros  147.6 W of a  240.5 W switching budget = 61.3%
    A100 fp16    zeros  149.1 W of a  199.7 W switching budget = 74.6%
    A100 fp16-T  zeros  135.2 W of a  248.8 W switching budget = 54.4%
    A100 int8    zeros  130.0 W of a  217.7 W switching budget = 59.7%
    ET-SoC-1 fp32  zeros   2.00 W of a  27.11 W budget =  7.4%  (21 Sep)
    ET-SoC-1 fp32  zeros   3.23 W of a  27.89 W budget = 11.6%  (E67 session)
    ET-SoC-1 fp32  per MAC, E38's three cards: 3.0%, 4.1%, 6.8%
  => the quietest data still costs the A100 54-75% of its busiest
     switching; on the ET-SoC-1 it costs 3-12%.  THAT is the finding,
     and the ratio is just its reciprocal (x8.6-x32.9 against x1.3-x1.8).
  Note the A100 floor is a measured FLOOR -- the quietest data run -- not a
  demonstrated data-independent component, so write 'as little as', not
  'no matter what the numbers are'.
