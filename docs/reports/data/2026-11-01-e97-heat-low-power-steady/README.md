## Joins and traps

- **A sum's baseline.** Against zeros, E89's straight baseline makes cycle 1's 2 s step larger than cycle 0's, 1.07–1.24 times on the float kinds, because a 30 s half's chain ends 0.9–1.7 s early and its baseline ends falling (E89 C6; [`tools/claims-v3/e97/desk/c6_e89.txt`](../../../../tools/claims-v3/e97/desk/c6_e89.txt)). The nulls of § Results 2 say the same line reads high. On the float sums it reads 4–9% above the other baselines (medians 1.04 over the quadratic, 1.08 over cycle 0, 1.09 over the [−8, −2) s line), on the int8 sums about 3–7% above cycle 0 and the [−8, −2) s line, and level with the quadratic. The direct cells depend on the baseline less: quadratic over line 0.925–1.000, mean 0.97 ([`corrections-check.txt`](corrections-check.txt) [C3]; [`results.md`](results.md) § The 2 s step). Direct and sum cells are also confounded with S (≤ 4.2 W against ≥ 5.7 W).
- **r mixes frames.** Against idle, a launch's whole cost is in the swing, and the minion rail carries more of it (0.85–0.91 of the board's swing against 0.86–0.89 against zeros, 0.81 on fp32 random). Compare r within a frame; the over-idle cells are comparable across frames.
- **"Settled" here is a plateau, not a steady state.** The rise is read over a hold's last 300 s, on a plateau that moved by up to 0.8 °C. A stage slower than about 10 minutes that adds under about 3% of the rise is not resolved.
- **The table's ± is the idle mismatch only**, not a bound and not an interval over replicates. Quote about 0.9 °C per board W, with the failed test beside it; 0.87–0.92 is not an interval (with ±0.4 °C it spans 0.86–0.95).
- **The 34-sensor mean is not a calibrated die temperature:** one conversion for every sensor, and the firmware's reading runs about 1 °C below it (E89).
- **The stop is the firmware's reading between launches:** it read 78 °C at 216.5 s and the runner saw it at 221.3 s. The hold's reduced phase began 1.5 s later, because the fast stages drop 3 °C within a launch gap; the die was never held above the stop.
- **Command 74's raw codes are this card's thermal build's** (E89's trap), and every launch runs on all 32 shires, so the per-sensor pattern says nothing about a kind run on part of the die.
- **The derived int8 cells use E89's int8 zeros**, from another session. This run's own idle-to-zeros lead-ins read the same: 0.334 ± 0.006 °C (n = 7) against 0.340 ± 0.006.
- **The heat table's ± is lap-level on the cell**, and the pass-level ± of its rise stands beside it: the pass-level ± is 0.19 to 6.9 times the lap-level, median 2.3 (int8 sorted 0.002 and 0.006; int8 wsparse50 0.077 and 0.294). Give the page no lap ±.
- **Command 74 group 0 as a read** rests on behaviour: no latch moved backwards, no fault bit, the configuration unchanged at both ends of every pass. The handler's source is not in the firmware copy we hold.
