## The answer

**On aifoundry2, a third more clock (600 to 800 MHz) buys ×1.13 in the 4-block walk the L3 serves (`c4s`), what E119 found in the 8-block one, in fp32, fp16 and int8 alike; and only ×1.05 to ×1.07 in the two walks past the L3: ×1.067 with each block's C beside it (`c8`, 40 MB) and ×1.052 with C shared (`c16s`, 50 MB), less than even the cold walk's ×1.065.** With E119's seven, all ten of E107's arms are now measured on this card. - **`c4s` is the L3-served walk again** (R1: LIKE THE L3 WALK in all three formats, X within 0.003 of E119's `c8s`; OUTSIDE THE CLOCK, share of the way from M1 to M2 −0.36, −0.38 and +0.17). In this session `c4s`'s T at 800 MHz is 0.998 of `c8s`'s (fp32 zeros), and its fit, C 401 ± 3 cycles and M 0.762 ± 0.004 µs (95%, launches within the session), is close to `c8s`'s split but not the same: C is 2.9% higher and M 2.1% lower than today's `c8s` (390 ± 4 cycles, 0.778 ± 0.005 µs), and 2.5% and 1.7% from E119's (392 ± 2, 0.776 ± 0.003), each by more than the intervals allow. The two differences offset, so X agrees to 0.003 ([C3](#corrections-after-verification-mon-2-nov-2026)). Half the blocks, nearly the same pace: what the L3 serves sets it, not how many blocks.
- **The walks past the L3 are DRAM walks** (R2(a): PASS in all six cells, X within 0.002-0.020 of the cold walk's 1.065, 1.064 and 1.072). **Which model, the frozen guess failed** (R2(b)): `c16s` reads h = 0.27, 0.36 and 0.16 on the way from W to S (BETWEEN, BETWEEN and THE COLD WALK'S WAIT), not THE SHARE; `c8`'s int8 reads 0.26 (BETWEEN); `c8`'s fp32 and fp16 are UNRESOLVED as registered (the models 0.010 apart; h 0.82 and 1.00). Post hoc, by the fits: `c16s` keeps most of the cold walk's wait (M 1.880 ± 0.007 µs against 1.957 ± 0.010) and loses about 100 of its clock-following cycles (C 279 ± 5 against 378 ± 7). So sharing C took away mostly minion cycles, the rest DRAM wait: of the 0.24 µs it saves at 600 MHz in fp32, about two thirds is cycles (0.17 µs) and one third wait (0.08 µs); in fp16 57% and in int8 83% is cycles. The cold walk's split is from E119's session, so this compares two sessions ([C2](#corrections-after-verification-mon-2-nov-2026)). The walk left is more wait-bound than the cold one (wait is 84% of T at 800 MHz, against 80.5%), so the clock buys ×1.052 there against ×1.065.
- **The governor turns that into almost no sustained gap past the L3**: random fp32 holds 790 MHz sustained on `c8` and `c16s` at 54 W (R6 PASS: gaps ×0.998 and ×1.004). In the L3 walk random fp32 settles at 731.9 MHz, held at the trip (63.5 W, die 64.9 °C), so zeros lead by ×1.040; R6 FAILED there, because it was frozen against E119's `c8s` (663.7 MHz) and the whole card ran faster today (below). Like for like in this session, `c4s` ran 14.6 MHz under `c8s` (731.9 against 746.5 MHz) at 0.5 W less, and their gaps are ×1.040 and ×1.023.
- **In a 6 s burst the kind costs nothing on the new arms**: zeros and random both hold 796-800 MHz (gaps ×0.997-1.003, every format), and random's first-second power at 800 MHz came 3.9-11.8 W under its estimate (R8 PASS).
