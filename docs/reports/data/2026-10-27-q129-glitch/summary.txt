AGAINST THE RECORD
  tape-out flow (tfma.glitch): tree 1244 -> 1871 toggles/MAC, x1.504, x1.4 in energy
  open flow, this run:         tree 628.2 -> 1499.9 toggles/MAC, x2.387, x2.329 in energy
  so the open tree glitches 1.59x as hard, and its energy factor is 1.66x the record's.
  BUT the two flows' TIMED tree counts are 1.25x apart, against 1.98x at zero delay: counting glitches brings them together.
  Q124's "glitches add about 40% to the tree's energy" is the x1.4 above. In the tree it is x2.33; at the WHOLE UNIT it is x1.33, so a page that applied 1.4 to a unit-level
  figure was about right and one that applied it to the tree understated by 1.7x.

WHAT THIS DOES NOT SETTLE
  - one stimulus (randn) and one netlist pair; the Hamming-ladder rungs were not run,
    so whether the fJ-per-flipped-bit slope carries the same factor is NOT measured.
  - the design misses its 1250 ps SDC by about 2x, so these delays belong to a netlist
    that would not run at 800 MHz; and the SDF is ASAP7 TT at 0.700 V while the energy
    is priced at the 0.517 V rail, where every delay would be longer.
  - inertial delay with full pulse rejection is the conservative model; a transport
    model would report more glitches.
