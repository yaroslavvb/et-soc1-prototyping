## What counts as a finding

Report what is **wrong**, with proof:
- a number or statement that disagrees with the ground truth (or with the lab's own newer data);
- a source that does not say what the fact claims (wrong file, line, page, field, or a misquote);
- wrong arithmetic in a derived number, wrong units, wrong rounding of a displayed number;
- a kind label that is not honest (e.g. "measured" for something computed, "spec" for something no document states);
- an inconsistency: the same quantity given differently in two places (panel vs fact vs prose vs drawing);
- the drawing contradicting the documents (a count, a label, a connection, a placement);
- physics or engineering that is wrong in a generic or textbook fact.

Not findings: style, wording, layout taste, missing extra detail, an estimate or inference the page already marks as
such (unless the mark itself is wrong or the estimate is far off), and honest caveats. Group repeated instances of one
root cause into one finding. Every finding needs: what the page says (exact quote and where: fact id, scale and part,
flow stage, prose section), what is true, the evidence (path + line or PDF page + exact quote), where in the sources the
wrong text or value is generated (`file:line`), and an exact fix (old → new). Say how confident you are. Precision
matters more than volume: a wrong "finding" costs the owner more than a missed nit.
