# E150: E149's frozen toggle model on a second, cheaper GPU type (registration)

Registered Mon 2 Nov 2026 (week 45), evening PST, on the Intel, **before the first GPU call** (the declared smoke
included). Q138, in the owner's words that evening: *"all cards are available, feel free to settle any unanswered
questions regarding horace's power model using mordast, runs on aifoundry and modal for sanity checks on GPUs (don't
exceed $20 on modal)"*, with Q137's goal, *"to be able to predict the power requirement and heat generation of various
kinds of numeric computations as accurately as possible"*. It answers, on a second GPU type, the kinds page's
[section 11](https://spacesheep.dev/@yaroslavvb/horace-kinds-of-numbers#11-what-is-still-open) **item 6, "a power model
across kinds"**, and narrows **item 2, "a GPU measured the same way"** (E149 measured one GPU model, the A100).

## The question

E149 ([`tools/gpu/e149/E149.md`](../e149/E149.md), data `docs/reports/data/2026-11-02-e149-gpu-model-check/`) fitted
toggle models of the ET-SoC-1 model's form on an A100 under its cap and froze them (`model-e149.json`, pushed in
`e929b00f`). **Does that frozen form carry to a second GPU type, refitting only one scale per board?** The second type
is an **L4** (Ada, the generation after the A100's) or an **A10G** (Ampere, a different die of the A100's generation),
picked by the smoke's rule below. On it: do E149's core ladders behave as on the A100 (the structure rules), and do the
frozen models, times one scale per board fitted on those ladders, predict E149's held-out kinds blind? And for heat:
the second GPU's die rise per W, and the held-out kinds' 30 s rises predicted from the frozen power path.

## Platform

Modal workspace `yaroslavvb`, `gpu="L4"` or `gpu="A10G"`, 2 CPU cores, 8 GiB; E149's image (`debian_slim`, Python 3.11,
`torch==2.5.1`, `nvidia-ml-py==12.560.30`, `numpy==2.1.3`). App and Volume `e150-second-gpu`. Code:
[`e150_app.py`](e150_app.py), E149's harness with only the changes its docstring lists (the NVML sampler, the kind
builders, the features and the windows are E149's, unchanged). **Reads only**: NVML getters and `nvidia-smi -q`;
nothing is set on the GPU. The GEMM is E149's: `A @ B.t()`, A (M, K) and B (N, K) K-contiguous, bf16 and fp16 by
`torch.mm(out=)`, int8 by `torch._int_mm(out=)` into int32, M = N = K = the format's size.

## The smoke (S), declared, and the rule that picks the GPU and the sizes

**S**: one container on an L4 and one on an A10G, in parallel: static reads; the random kind (bf16 and fp16 randn,
int8 −128…127) and zeros of each format at 512³, 1024³, 1536³ and 2048³; a 45 s warm hold (bf16 random at 1024³); one
pass over the 24 windows (5 s cells, each followed by a 3 s reference of bf16 randn at 512³). Plumbing and sizing only:
its numbers are not scored and fit or predict nothing.

**The rule (E149's amendment A1, for that GPU's cap):** for each GPU type and format, the largest of the four sizes at
which the random kind's window is valid (the board's maximum SM clock, as NVML reports it, in every 100 ms sample, and
no power-cap, slowdown or thermal reason bit, 0x4 | 0x8 | 0x20 | 0x40 | 0x80, in any) and draws at most **90% of the
board's enforced power limit** (A1's 360 W of 400 W). A type **qualifies** if bf16 has such a size and its random
window draws at least **25 W** more than its zeros window there. **E150 runs on the L4 if it qualifies with all three
formats** (the newer generation answers "across generations"); else on the A10G if it qualifies (a format with no size
is dropped and said so); else on the L4 if it qualifies; else E150 is no-go. [`analyze_e150.py`](analyze_e150.py)
`choose` applies it mechanically and writes `choice-s.json`. If the chosen type's boards never hold their NVML
maximum clock under load with no reason bit set, a dated amendment written before the measured run says which clock
counts.

## The measured run (M): two boards, two phases each

Two containers (boards A and B) on the chosen type, in parallel. Each: static reads; **every kind is built and its
features computed before any window** (E149's `side_feats`, `cross_feats`, `acc_feats` on the GPU; the same seeds as
E149, but on another GPU type torch's CUDA generator spreads a seed over a different grid, so the bytes are E150's own:
both boards of one type build the same bytes, checked by sha256); a 30 s idle; a 120 s reference hold (bf16 randn) and
a 60 s bf16 zeros hold (heat); **phase L**, two passes over the core ladders; **phase H**, two passes over E149's
held-out kinds; then **holds** for each phase-H kind and bf16 randn (bf16 zeros 10 s, then the kind 30 s); a 20 s idle.
Unit A runs each phase's first pass forward and its second reversed, unit B the opposite (one fixed shuffle, E150's
seed). Windows, zeros anchors (each format's zeros at the start, middle and end of every pass), validity, d and y are
E149's ("How a window is read"): **y = watts over the same GEMM on zeros, at the board's maximum clock**, each window at
its own fast-pole temperature (E149.md note N1).

**Phase L (72 kinds, every one an E149 stage-1 kind, its spec and seed unchanged):** bf16: randn; zeros scattered 25,
50, 75, 90% and in blocks 50, 75%; zeros; the mantissa ladder base1, k = 1, 3, 5, 7; the exponent ladder k = 1, 3, 5;
the sign ladder p = 0.10, 0.25, 0.50; single positions mantissa 0 and 6, exponent 0 and 4; mantissa k = 3 on A only
and on B only; signs (fixed +, A fixed −, random); order (sorted along K, the same values shuffled, sorted along M/N,
sorted and rolled); square waves b = 1, 4, 16, 64. fp16: randn, zeros, zeros 50 and 75% scattered and 50% in blocks,
base1, mantissa k = 1, 3, 5, 7, 10, exponent k = 1, 3, sign p = 0.5 on both and on A only, mantissa bits 0 and 9,
mantissa k = 5 on A only. int8: random, zeros, zeros 25, 50, 75, 90% scattered and 50% in blocks, base1, k = 1, 3, 5, 7,
the low, high and sign groups, sign on A only, random on A only and on B only.

**Phase H (20 kinds): E149's stage 2 without bf16 randn** (a phase-L kind): bf16 Horace He's nine (zeros, rand,
sparse75, ternary, onebit, pi, twos, checker), post-GELU × LLM-like weights, the power law, low rank, uniform sorted
along K and `sparse_neg`; fp16 zeros, rand, post-GELU × weights; int8 zeros, quantized post-GELU × quantized weights,
ternary, twos. **17 are scored blind** (the zeros are the anchors).

## The frozen models and the one scale per board

The models are **E149's seven frozen forms, unchanged** (`model-e149.json`: G1 the registered primary, the ET-SoC-1
form; G0, S1, G1s, G2, G3, G4 its registered comparisons), applied by E149's `features` and `predict_one` to E150's own
operand features. Nothing in them is refitted. For a model m, a kind k of format f and a board b:

- **the rate conversion** r_f(b) = the board's measured multiply-add rate for format f (the mean TFLOPS of phase L's
  valid cells of f) ÷ E149's (the mean over its stage-1 kinds of f in `reduce-stage1.json`: about 157 TFLOPS bf16 at
  2048³, 109 fp16 and 148 int8 at 1024³). It is measured, not fitted: E149's coefficients are watts at the A100's
  rate, so one energy per flip at another rate gives watts in proportion.
- **the per-board scale** c_m(b) = Σ y q / Σ q², with q = r_f(b) × m's A100 prediction, over phase L's kinds on that
  board (every format, zeros excluded): **one number per board**, the second GPU's energy per operand-bit flip at one
  multiply-add relative to the A100's, in m's form.
- **the blind prediction** for a phase-H kind on board b: c_m(b) × r_f(b) × m's A100 prediction; its error is the mean
  over the two boards of (measured y − prediction).
- **the band**: E149's frozen 90% band for G1 (± 1.645 × G1's nested rms of the format, A100 W) × the format's mean
  c × r over the two boards.

Reported beside them (no verdict, except F5's comparison): a per-format scale (three numbers a board), a plain scale
without the rate conversion, the unscaled A100 watts, and **the native refit**: G1's form with every coefficient
refitted on phase L's y (pooled over the boards, per format, E149's `fit_format`), predicting phase H the same way.

**The freeze:** `analyze_e150.py freeze` reads phase L's reduction only (passes 1–2, `reduce … L`) and writes
`predictions-e150.json`: the rate ratios, every scale, every blind prediction and band, the heat model, the zeros
offsets and the structure rules. **It is committed and pushed before phase H's windows are reduced** (`reduce … H`,
which prints no phase-H y). The predictions are a mechanical function of E149's frozen file, E150's operand bits and
phase L's y; nothing is tuned.

## Heat

E149's two-pole model of the die driven by board power (`fit_heat`), **refitted on the second GPU** on phase L's session
(from 25 s before the reference hold to the last phase-L cell; both boards pooled, shared τ₁, τ₂, r₁, r₂, per board an
intercept and an initial-transient term), the leakage slope λ from the reference hold (from 10 s in), each format's
zeros offset from phase L's passes, and σ_x from each board's own fit predicting the other's session: all written into
`predictions-e150.json` at the freeze. A cooler's poles are the board's, not the model's form, so they are refitted
like the scale. A hold's rise is the mean over its last second minus the pre-hold's last second, measured and predicted
alike (E149's `heat_holds`), predicted three ways:

- **the registered path (E149's construction)**: the pre-hold's measured zeros power + the format's zeros offset + the
  kind's scaled G1 prediction + λ × the simulated rise. **It counts the fast pole's leakage twice** (E149.md note N2):
  y already holds each window's own fast-pole leakage (about λr₁ of it on the A100), and the path adds λ × the whole
  simulated rise on top;
- **the corrected arm, registered here**: the same path with G1's y × (1 − λr₁);
- **from the measured power**: the fitted model driven by the hold's measured watts.

## Rules, which can fail

**V0** (E149's): at least 90% of phase L's kinds keep all their windows valid on each board; else the run is reported
partial and the invalid windows are named.

**The structure on the second GPU** (E149's `stage1_rules`, unchanged, on phase L): R1a (bf16, int8), R1b, R2 (bf16
mantissa, bf16 exponent, fp16 mantissa, int8 bits 1–7), R3a, R3b, R4a, R4b, and B1 (the two boards agree). On the
A100 all held except R4b (lag 1 is not the pairing). **C1: the structure carries if at least 9 of the 11 structure
verdicts (R1a ×2, R1b, R2 ×4, R3a, R3b, R4a, R4b) repeat E149's.**

**Blind, the frozen form with one scale per board** (17 kinds; S₂ = phase L's y of bf16 randn, the second GPU's span
from zeros to random; error = measured − predicted):
- **F1**: G1's rms ≤ 0.20 × S₂ (E149's G1 read 0.16 × S on the A100's own new boards).
- **F2**: G1's |bias| ≤ 0.10 × S₂.
- **F3**: ≥ 13 of 17 inside G1's scaled band.
- **F4**: G1's rms ≤ 0.7 × G0's (density alone, scaled the same way).
- **F5**: G1 frozen with one scale per board predicts within 1.25 × the rms of G1's form refitted natively on the
  second GPU's phase L (freezing the A100's prices costs little against refitting them all).
- **F6**: the two boards' G1 scales agree, ratio within 0.9–1.1.

**Heat:** **H1**: the registered path's rms ≤ 0.8 °C. **Hc**: the corrected arm's rms ≤ 0.8 °C. **H2**: from the
measured power, rms ≤ 0.6 °C. **H3**: ≥ 75% of the blind kinds' rises inside ± 1.645 × √(σ_x² + (the 30 s rise per W
× the kind's power band / 1.645)²) (the registered path). **H4**: the two boards' die rise per W (per-board fits) within
a ratio of 0.8–1.25 (E149's C1: such a fit can be bent by a slot's drift; said so where it is).

Reported beside them, not scored: every model's and arm's rms, bias and worst error, by format and board; the
scales as energy ratios; the in-sample rms of the one-scale fit on phase L; E150's features against E149's for the
same kinds; every rule's numbers.

[`selftest_e150.py`](selftest_e150.py) writes synthetic logs in the harness's format for plan M (power from E149's
frozen G1 × a known scale per board × the simulated rate, E149's simulated two-pole heat path with leakage, noise, one
capped window) and runs reduce L → freeze → reduce H → score: it recovers the rate ratio, the scales (within 6%) and
the heat path, drops the capped window, finds the 17 blind kinds outside the scale's fit, holds F1–F6 and H1–H4 on data
the form makes, and fails F1, F3 and F6 on data it does not ([`selftest.txt`](selftest.txt)).

## What this does not do

- It sets nothing on the GPU: no power limit, no clocks. It touches no lab card, posts nothing in `#community-lab`, and
  reads nothing from the archive.
- One second GPU type, two boards; one size per format, chosen by the cap; square K-contiguous operands; cuBLAS through
  torch 2.5.1. The models are predictors in the ET-SoC-1 model's form, not either GPU's circuit.
