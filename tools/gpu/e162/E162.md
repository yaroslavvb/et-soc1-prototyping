**The complaint it answers.** The kinds page's

row-against-K kinds (rows sorted, K shuffled; K sorted, rows shuffled; per-row phase against shared phase) or a

under its cap would answer that".

## The question

E149 ([`tools/gpu/e149/E149.md`](../e149/E149.md)) found that an A100's power under its cap follows operand bit changes,
but that the ET-SoC-1 model's pairing (neighbours along K) is not the A100's (R4b fails), and that only the forms that
add the row direction (operands one row apart at one k) predicted its held-out kinds better; the best of them (G4)
was unstable out of kind, because for kinds random along rows its row and K-lag counts are nearly the same numbers.
**E162 asks which pairing a GPU pays for, row against K**, with kinds that set the row-direction flips and the K-lag
flips independently, on two GPU types: **does a constrained (ridge) row+K form, fitted on stage-1 ladders and frozen,
beat E149's frozen G1 blind, on held-out kinds measured on boards the fit never saw, by a resolved margin, while staying
stable out of kind?** The same question is then asked on a later generation, an **L40S** (Ada, AD102), if it has
headroom under its cap.

## The kinds

"Row" means the operand's first index: M for A, N for B (both are stored rows × K). Two-value kinds use **v0 = 1.0 and
v1 = 1.9921875 in bf16** (0x3F80, 0x3FFF: the seven mantissa bits apart), 1.0 and 1.9990234375 in fp16 (0x3C00, 0x3FFF:
ten mantissa bits), 1 and 127 in int8 (bits 1-6). Every E162 kind is built on the GPU from its own seeded generators
(seed labels `e162/<fmt>/<name>/<side>`); E149's kinds keep E149's names, specs and seed labels, so on an A100 they are
E149's very bytes (checked by sha256 against E149's committed feature records). "Both" means A and B are drawn the same
way, independently.

**New stage-1 kinds** (E162's fit set; `_new_stage1` in `e162_app.py`):
- **bf16, square waves along K with one shared phase** (every row the same wave, so no row-direction flips), blocks
  b = 1, 2, 4, 8, 16, 32, 64: `kS1` … `kS64`. Beside E149's `sq<b>`, the same waves with **a random phase per row**
  (row-direction flips in half the pairs at any row lag), they separate the row term at fixed K-lag flips.
- **bf16, square waves along the rows with one shared phase** (every row constant along K, so no K-direction flips),
  b = 1 … 64: `rS1` … `rS64`; and one with **a random phase per column** (`rC16`, b = 16).
- **bf16, rows against K at the same value statistics**: `rowc` (each row one randn value, constant along K) against
  `colc` (each column one randn value, constant down the rows); `rowcA_colcB` and `colcA_rowcB` (one of each);
  `rollM`, randn **sorted along the rows and each column rolled by its own random offset** (smooth along the rows,
  random along K), E149's `drollK` transposed.
- **bf16, one-sided**: `kS1A`, `kS1B`, `rS1A`, `rS1B` (the wave on one operand, the other the constant 1.0).
- **fp16 and int8** (A100 only): `kS1`, `kS16`, `rS1`, `rS16`, `rowc`, `colc`.

**A100 stage 1** (52 kinds): the 36 new kinds, each format's zeros, and **13 bridge kinds from E149's stage 1**: bf16
randn, `sq1`, `sq16`, `sq64`, `dsortK`, `dshufK`, `dsortM`, `drollK`, `mk3`; fp16 randn, `mk5`; int8 random, `k3`.

**L40S stage 1** (52 kinds, bf16): the 24 new bf16 kinds and 28 of E149's bf16 stage-1 kinds, the core the G1 form
needs: randn, zeros, `zs50`, `zs75`, `zb50`, `zb75`, `base1`, `mk1`, `mk3`, `mk7`, `ek1`, `ek3`, `ek5`, `sp25`, `sp50`,
`mb0`, `mb6`, `mk3A`, `mk3B`, `sfix`, `srand`, `dsortK`, `dshufK`, `dsortM`, `drollK`, `sq1`, `sq16`, `sq64`.

**The held-out kinds** (`_held`; measured only in stage 2, on new boards, after the freeze; **22 on the A100, the 14
bf16 ones on the L40S**). bf16: `t16x16`, `t4x32`, `t32x4` (one randn value per tile of 16 × 16, 4 rows × 32 K, 32 rows ×
4 K); `kB8_4` (a square wave along K, b = 4, one phase per block of 8 rows); `checkv` (the two values alternating with
(i − k)'s parity); `stripeK0`, `stripeR0` (randn with every odd column, or every odd row, zeroed); `walkK`, `walkR`
(random walks along K, or along the rows, steps of 0.05, a random start each); `walk2` (u_i + v_k, walks along both);
`sinpos` (the transformer positional table: rows are positions, K the dimensions); `rdup8`, `cdup8` (randn with each
row, or each run of 8 columns, repeated 8 times); `rollM_A` (A the rolled column sort, B randn). fp16 and int8: `t16x16`,
`walkK`, `rdup8`, `cdup8` (int8 walks as 100 sin(w)). None of the 22 has been measured anywhere.

## How a window is read

E149's ("How a window is read"), unchanged: a pass is a reference window (bf16 randn at the bf16 size, 3 s), then for
each kind a **cell window (5 s)** followed by a reference window; each format's zeros also run at the start, middle and
end of every pass; one fixed shuffle (E162's seed). NVML every 100 ms in its own process. A window's power is the mean
of the readings after its settle (cell 1.5 s, reference 1.0 s). **d** = cell − the mean of its two references; **y** =
d − the mean d of the format's zeros in that pass: watts over the same GEMM on zeros, each window at its own fast-pole
temperature (E149.md note N1). A window counts only if the SM clock read the held clock in every sample and no
power-cap, slowdown or thermal reason bit (0x4, 0x8, 0x20, 0x40, 0x80) was set in any (V0). A kind's y is the mean
over its valid replicates; its SE their sd / √n. **Stage 1: two boards × two passes** (board 1 pass 1 forward, pass 2
reversed; board 2 the opposite). **Stage 2: two new boards × one pass** (board 1 forward, board 2 reversed). After
stage 1's last window the sampler is stopped and the held-out kinds (and, on the A100, E149's 109 stage-1 and stage-2
kinds, from E149's seeds) are built only to compute their features.

**Features** are E149's (`side_feats`, `cross_feats`: per side and bit position the share of pairs (k, k + s) in one
row whose bit differs, K-lags s = 1, 2, 4, 8, 16, 32; between consecutive rows; the ones; `nzprod`, `gtr<s>`,
`psgn<s>`, `negprod`) **plus the row-direction flips at row-lags r = 1, 2, 4, 8, 16, 32** (`rlag<r>`: the share of row
pairs (i, i + r) at one k whose bit differs; E149's `row` = `rlag1`) and **the row-direction gate transitions**
(`gtrR<r>`: the share of multiply-adds whose gate state changes when A steps r rows with B fixed, plus the same for B).

## The L40S smoke (declared) and its rule

**S**: one L40S container: static reads; bf16 randn and zeros at 1024³, 1536³, 2048³, 3072³ and 4096³; **the plumbing:
every E162 kind of every format built at 512 with its features and one GEMM** (no window, no power read for them); a
10 s idle and a 45 s warm hold (bf16 randn at 1024³); one pass over the ten sizing windows (5 s cells, 3 s references
of bf16 randn at 1024³). Plumbing and sizing only: not scored, fits and predicts nothing.

**The rule** (`analyze_e162.py choose`, mechanical; E149's A1 and E150's rule for this GPU): **the held clock H** is
the NVML maximum SM clock if the bf16 zeros window at 1024³ holds it in every sample, else that window's lowest SM clock
provided it carries no reason bit. A size **qualifies** if its random and zeros windows both hold H in every sample with
no reason bit, the random window draws **at most 90% of the enforced power limit**, and **at least 25 W more than
zeros**. The L40S runs bf16 at the largest qualifying size. **If no size qualifies, the L40S is cap-bound (or
clock-bound) in this harness: that result is recorded (each size's clock, power and reason bits) and the L40S arm
stops** (no stage 1, no stage 2). A100 validity is E149's (1410 MHz in every sample).

## The models

**G1, the comparison** (the critic's "against G1"). On the A100 it is **E149's frozen G1** (`model-e149.json`, pushed
in `e929b00f`), unchanged, applied by E149's `predict_one` to E162's features. On the L40S it is **G1's form fitted on
the L40S's own stage-1 rows** by E149's `fit_format` (OLS, no intercept), like for like with RK.

**RK, the constrained row+K form** (`analyze_e162.py` `rk_features`, `fit_rk`): per format, at a K-lag s and a row-lag r,
the features `nzprod`, `gtr<s>`, `gtrR<r>`, the flips of each field at K-lag s (A + B; E149's fields), bf16's exponent
and mantissa position trends and fp16's mantissa trend at lag s (E149's `_fpos`), the A − B asymmetry at lag s, `psgn<s>`,
**the flips of each field at row-lag r (A + B) and the A − B asymmetry at row-lag r**. Fitted by **ridge** with no
intercept (zeros are 0 by construction) on columns scaled to unit RMS over the training rows, penalty α = λ × the
format's row count, **λ ∈ {10⁻⁴, 3·10⁻⁴, 10⁻³, 3·10⁻³, 0.01, 0.03, 0.1, 0.3, 1}** chosen per format by exact
closed-form leave-one-kind-out (LOKO) error; **(s, r) ∈ {1, 2, 4, 8, 16, 32}² chosen once per GPU, pooled over the
formats**, by the lowest total LOKO squared error (each format at its own best λ). **The nested error** is fully nested:
for every left-out kind, (s, r) and λ are chosen again on the remaining kinds only, then that kind is predicted.

**The A100's fit rows** (pooled): E149's stage-1 kinds with E149's measured y (`reduce-stage1.json`) and E162's
features of E149's bytes (a kind whose bytes differ from E149's by sha256 is left out and named), plus E162's 36 new
kinds with their y divided by **the session scale per format** c_f = Σ y₁₆₂ y₁₄₉ / Σ y₁₄₉² over that format's bridge
kinds (bf16 9, fp16 2, int8 2), so every row is on E149's stage-1 footing. The bridge kinds' E162 y set the scale only;
each format's zeros are excluded. **The L40S's fit rows**: its stage-1 kinds' y (one session, no scale).

**Registered comparisons** (fitted and frozen the same way, no verdict except where a rule names them): **G1p**, G1's
form by OLS on the A100's pooled rows (to tell the form from the data); **G4p**, E149's G4 form (its own lag scan) by
OLS on the same rows; **RK by OLS** at the chosen (s, r) (what the ridge adds); E149's other frozen forms (G0, S1, G1s,
G3, G4) as they are. On the L40S, beside RK and G1: G4 by OLS on its rows, and **the A100's frozen G1 and RK carried
over** by E150's method (the measured bf16 rate ratio, L40S over E149's A100, and one scale fitted on the L40S's stage-1
rows). **RK's band** for a held-out kind: ± 1.645 × RK's fully nested rms of its format (coverage reported).

**The freeze**: `analyze_e162.py freezeA` (and `freezeL`) reads stage 1 only and writes the model file and the
held-out predictions; **both are committed and pushed before stage 2's first call** on that GPU. The predictions are a
mechanical function of stage 1's y and the operand bits; nothing is tuned after.

## Rules, which can fail

**V0** (E149's): at least 90% of a stage-1 session's kinds keep all their windows valid on each board; else the run is
reported partial and the invalid windows are named.

**Stage 1, which pairing** (each GPU's own stage-1 session, raw y; "resolved" = a difference > 2 SE with ≥ 3 replicates
on each side, E149's test):
- **W1, the row direction costs**: a phase per row costs more than one shared phase, square waves along K at b = 1, 16
  and 64 (E149's `sq<b>` against `kS<b>`), all three resolved.
- **W2, neighbours along K are a pairing when no row flips**: `kS1` costs more than `kS64`, resolved (R4b's test with the
  row flips removed).
- **W3, neighbouring rows are a pairing when no K flips**: `rS1` costs more than `rS64`, resolved.
- **W4**, rows against K at the same values (`rowc` against `colc`, each format) and **W5** (`drollK`, rows random and K
  smooth, against `rollM`, K random and rows smooth): labelled ROW>K, K>ROW or UNRESOLVED.
- **W6** (reported): the one-sided waves, A against B.
- **R4b again** (E149's, on the bridge or core kinds): `sq1` costs more than `sq64`, resolved.

**STABLE** (stage 1): **(i)** per format, RK's fully nested rms ≤ **3 ×** its in-sample rms (E149's G4 ran 11-40 times;
a well-specified ridge fit with about 25 rows and 13 terms can reach 2 times, as the selftest shows), and **(ii)**
pooled over the formats, RK's fully nested rms ≤ the G1 form's OLS LOKO rms on the same rows (G1p's on the A100).
Both must hold.

**BEAT** (stage 2, blind; held-out kinds on new boards; error = measured y − predicted, all formats pooled, unscaled):
**rms(RK) ≤ 0.8 × rms(G1)**, and **the 95% paired bootstrap interval of rms(G1) − rms(RK)** (10,000 resamples of the
kinds, seed 162) **lies above zero**. Both must hold. On the A100 G1 is E149's frozen G1 (**BEAT**, the registered
primary) and, as a second registered rule, G1p (**BEAT_p**: does the form beat G1's form on the same data); on the L40S
G1 is its native fit (**BEAT_L**).

**The critic's rule** is BEAT and STABLE together, on each GPU.

**Heat, HT1** (stage 2, no extra GPU time): each blind cell's die reading over its two references (the cell's mean die
temperature after its settle minus its references' mean), predicted by the frozen two-pole heat model driven by the
session's measured power with the cell's samples replaced by **p_ref + the pass's zeros d + the model's y**, for RK
and for G1; and by the measured power alone (the heat model's own floor). **HT1: RK's rms ≤ G1's rms** (°C over the
blind kinds, each the mean of its replicates). The heat model is E149's frozen pooled one on the A100 (`model-e149.json`
`heat.pooled`: τ₁ 0.2 s, τ₂ 31.8 s, 0.116 °C/W) and E149's `fit_heat` refitted on the L40S's stage-1 session, frozen
with its freeze. y already carries the cell's own leakage, so no leakage term is added (E149's double count, note N2,
does not arise).

**Reported beside them, not scored**: every model's rms, bias and worst error by format and board; a board-scaled
secondary arm (E149's A2: each stage-2 board's predictions × its measured y(bf16 randn) ÷ the fit's span); **the
seen-kinds arm** on the A100: RK, G1p and G4p on E149's 17 held-out kinds, scored against E149's committed stage-2 y
(their bytes rebuilt here; these kinds and their watts are public, so the arm is not blind to the designer: no
verdict); E149's frozen G1 on E162's 36 new stage-1 kinds (out of G1's fit, on this session's boards); the bridge
kinds' E162 y against E149's; the scan table; the coefficients; RK's band coverage.

## What I expect (registered, honest)

From E149's stage 1 (`drollK`, smooth along K but rows out of step, cost 143 W against 103 W sorted; the per-row-phase
waves rise with b although their lag-1 flips fall): **W1 holds; W2 fails** (as R4b failed, even without the row
flips); **W5 and W4 read ROW>K** in bf16; **W3 is a guess** (a row pairing might be 8 or 16 rows apart, MMA tiles, where
period 2 has no flips). **STABLE holds and BEAT holds on the A100**, with RK's blind rms near E149's best post hoc forms
(10-15 W against G1's 20-30 W), at perhaps 60% for BEAT, since one dominating kind can widen the bootstrap interval
across zero. **The L40S**: no expectation beyond "the structure resembles the A100's"; it may also be cap-bound at
every size that gives a usable signal, in which case the arm stops.

## What this does not do

- It sets nothing on the GPU: no power limit, no clocks (item 2's lower cap needs a host that allows `nvidia-smi -pl`, and his word). It
  touches no lab card, posts nothing in `#community-lab`, and reads nothing from the archive.
- RK is a predictor, not either GPU's circuit: which operands meet on which wire is set by cuBLAS's kernel and the
  tensor cores' dataflow, which nothing here reads. "Which pairing a GPU pays for" means which counting predicts its
  power, out of kind and on new boards.
- Two GPU types; one GEMM library (cuBLAS through torch 2.5.1); square K-contiguous operands; one size per format.
