## Card budget and time

**About 40 card-minutes**: 165 bursts at E165's measured 14.26 s a burst (its s1: 158 bursts in 37.55 min) is **39.2 min**, plus the smoke and the
two stop tests (E165: 0.97 min). **The session's cap is 42 card-minutes** (`run_e119.py` starts a run only if its planned time fits; it cuts pass
3's tail); **the slot is 48 minutes** from the queue's start, its hard stop inside it. **The registered subset for a short window:** passes 1-2 (110
bursts, about 26 min): the subset protocol under `--cap 28` in a 34-minute slot; every rule is scored on what is counted. On aifoundry2 E173 is the
card day's only run of ours (the critic's day-1 plan). The day's one `#community-lab` post gets aifoundry2's clause before the first launch and loses
it at the release (section 15; the card lanes of the card day write it). If the lab approval lapses, the detached queue keeps running and the data
is collected when the box answers again. An idle login of another user does not block (the owner's ruling of 1 Nov 2026); any other user's
activity, device process or lock ends the session at once.
