## What it does to predicting power and heat

M9 (Q140(m)) predicts, from the bytes a matmul multiplies, its **power** D (board W over the format's own zeros at 600 MHz) and its
**heat** (the die's rise over the kinds harness's 4.5 s run, M7's heat law carried). On this card both go through **M8's kept card
term**: D on card 1 = **0.9616** × D on aifoundry3 for every format (`m9.json` › `card1_scale_by_format`: fp32 and fp16
0.9615998850616304, int8 0.9615854710541776), and card 1's die rise per board W 0.1151 °C/W. The terms under test are M9's:
int8's radix-4 Booth partial-product count (`bpp_r4`, +2.205 fJ a flip) beside M8's near-whole-byte discount at half size (`bsat`,
-29.3 fJ a bit), the floats' exponent stage (`xstage`, +0.648 fJ a count) beside M8's C-register term (`c_end`, +6.186 fJ a flip),
and M9's recalibrated bands (k 1.996 floats, 1.864 int8).

**Before** (what is claimed for a new kind on this card, nothing of it blind for M9): M9 nested leave one kind out on card 1's
training rows, **0.211 W rms fp32 (171 rows), 0.178 W fp16 (145), 0.185 W int8 (84)** (`nested-m9.json` › `families.*.band.pools`,
`*|c1`); out of kind over every committed row, int8 0.177 W against M6's, M7's and M8's forms on the same folds 0.251 / 0.256 /
0.261 W, floats 0.193 against 0.194-0.198 W. Pool 6's blind record on this card (E157, as registered): M8 **0.386 / 0.098 / 0.327
