## M2-5. What is read, and how (registered here, before any datum; [`arm_m2_read_e186.py`](arm_m2_read_e186.py) is the arms' reader)

- **Reported, never a rule.** The arms are read after E186's data are collected and scored. No E186 rule, threshold, verdict or registered
  forecast depends on them (E186.md section 8). Their readings use words of their own, never the scorer's verdict words.
- **The measured side is the session's reduced trains and kinds as the scorer's file carries them** (`scored-e186.json`, in E182's
  `scored.json` form, the file note M1's reader reads): `trains` > each train's interval (lo null when the scorer reads it NOT SCORED), and,
  when kinds passes ran, `reported` > `kinds` > each kind's D_w.
- **The model's side** is [`m2-october-forecast-model-e186.json`](m2-october-forecast-model-e186.json), refused (exit 2) unless its sha256 is
  the one the reader pins (`ab00ac05...`); each kind's key is read from E186's list, refused (exit 2) unless its sha256 is pinned too
  (`9af274eb...`) and its words are the json's.
- **The words,** for each arm, are note M1's: the arm's centre lies **inside, above or below** the measured 95% interval; its sign is **the
  estimate's or the other**; the estimate lies **inside, above or below the arm's band**. For a kind, the measured D lies **inside, above or
  below the arm's band**. An item the arm does not cover reads **not covered**; one the session did not run, **not run**; one the scorer
  reads NOT SCORED, **not read**. Card 1's figures are never read.
- **The errors, beside the words:** the arm's figure less the measured one, in W, for every train and kind read; and for each arm, over its
  trains read, the count, the count with its centre inside the interval, the mean error and the rms error; over its kinds read, the same with
  the measured D inside its band.
- **As E186 is planned (FULL),** v4 (M12's set) reads 9 of the 10 session trains (H16 not covered) and v5, v6 and v7 read all 10, with no
  kind (FULL reads no level); on the nine int8 and fp32 session trains the four arms carry the same figures. **In the fallback (PASSES)** no
  train is read, and the fifteen kinds are: v4 (M12's set) 13 (not K19 or K20), v5, v6 and v7 15.
- **To run it by hand,** after the collection and the scorer, from the lab's top:
  `/usr/bin/python3 -B tools/claims-v3/e186/arm_m2_read_e186.py --scored <data dir>/scored-e186.json --out <data dir>/arm-m2-e186`
  (it writes `arm-m2-e186.json` and `arm-m2-e186.txt`). **On the day, step 11 v3 runs it** (the kit's dated note 3, `KIT.md.dated-note-3.md`):
  after step 11 v2 unchanged, which ends with note M1's reader, a gate holds this note's list at its count and own sha256 with every entry at
  its bytes, then runs this line on a copy of the scorer's file and puts only the two outputs into the data directory. A refusal reads "M2
  NOT READ", never stops the step and never changes the session's scoring; the data README records it.
- **Not changed:** note M1's reader and gate, key 2 and E187's reserve order. A further version enters only by a further dated note before
  E186's first datum, with its own reader or a new version of this one; added after the first datum, none is scored.
