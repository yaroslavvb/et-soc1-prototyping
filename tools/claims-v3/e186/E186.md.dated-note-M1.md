## M1-4. What is read, and how (registered here, before any datum; [`arm_m1_read_e186.py`](arm_m1_read_e186.py) is the arm's reader)

- **Reported, never a rule.** The arm is read after E186's data are collected and scored. No E186 rule, threshold, verdict or registered
  forecast depends on it (E186.md section 8). Its readings use words of their own, never the scorer's verdict words.
- **The measured side is the scorer's file** (`scored-e186.json`, in E182's `scored.json` form, which E186's kit ports): `trains` > each
  train's interval, and, when kinds passes ran, `reported` > `kinds` > each kind's D_w.
- **The model's side** is [`m1-october-forecast-model-e186.json`](m1-october-forecast-model-e186.json), refused (exit 2) unless its sha256 is the
  one the reader pins (`bf9a1b02...`).
- **The words:** for a train, v4's centre lies **inside, above or below** the measured 95% interval; its sign is **the estimate's or the
  other**; the estimate lies **inside, above or below v4's band**. For a kind, the measured D lies **inside, above or below v4's band**. A
  train or kind v4 does not cover reads **not covered**; one the session did not run, **not run**; one the scorer reads NOT SCORED, **not
  read**. Card 1's figures are never read.
- **As E186 is planned, the arm reads nothing:** none of the ten session trains is covered, and FULL reads no kind's level. In the fallback,
  the kinds passes would read K09 alone. **Key 2** orders E187's reserve only (E186.md section 3): among the trains key 1 leaves tied at

  **S15, R3, R4, X8, R1, R8, R6, R2, R7, R9, EA, EB**.
