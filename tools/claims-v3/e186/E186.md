## 5. The rules (each with its statistic, threshold and verdicts; section 6 gives the power)

### 5.1 The train's statistic

E182's, unchanged (`e182_stats.py`, E182.md section 5.1): a launch's reading is the mean of its 10 Hz `board_w` samples from its start plus 170
ms to its end after E82 A2's dip rule; a quad is sigma (L2 + L3 - L1 - L4) / 2 so every quad reads D(F) - D(S); a process's value the mean
of its complete quads; a unit two processes of opposite orientation; **the train's estimate the mean over units with its t(0.975, n - 1)
interval** (half-width about 0.0051 W at 32 units, registered); fewer than 8 units, NOT SCORED. A process during which a stop fired, or
with a launch whose record says not ok, gives no value.

### 5.2 The train rules (primary), one per session train

For each of the ten trains (E186-H16, E186-S23, E186-Q63SIGN, E186-Q63SWAP, E186-B15B7, E186-B23B15, E186-Q3SIGN, E186-Q3SWAP, E186-A15A7,
E186-A23A15), with `e186_stats.py`'s words:

| word | statistic | threshold | verdicts |
|---|---|---|---|
| **SIGN** | the train's interval | against 0 | POSITIVE / NEGATIVE / NOT RESOLVED |
| **SIZE against M12's band** (the primary) | (estimate - M12's line) +- sqrt(hw^2 + (1.96 se_M12)^2) | holds 0 | PASS / FAIL HIGH (M12 above: it prices more than the card reads) / FAIL LOW |
| **SIZE against M10** | the same with se 0 (E182's size_of) | holds 0 | PASS / FAIL HIGH / FAIL LOW |
| **WHICH** | the two SIZE words | mechanical | M12 (only M12 PASS) / M10 / BOTH / NEITHER |

## 11. What the rules cannot say

- A train reads a difference between two kinds, never a level: FULL reads no level, and the int8 levels' anchor is used only in the fallback.
- Every train holds its words still through a launch: nothing here prices bits that move, and held-bit prices are averaged over where the
  bits sit (the position trains EB, EA, R7 and R9 are in the reserve).
- The splits take a held bit to cost the same in the quartet's two products and in both kinds of each train; the slopes take each ladder step
  to add its 8 bits at one price, with P10's difference as M10 gives it.
- H16's three arms are all fp32-derived; a NONE word says fp16's placement is none of them, not what it is.
- One card, one session: nothing here separates a card's level from a gain, and no other card is run.
- A first-order carry-over from one launch to the next attenuates an A B B A contrast by (1 - c); the trains report it (section 5.4), and no
  rule corrects for it.
