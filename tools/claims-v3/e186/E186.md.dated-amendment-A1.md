## A1-1. E186-M when s is not read (the verifier's fault 2; its ruling accepted)

Section 4.2 says that if the session's float scale s cannot be read, *the four ladder trains' SIZE words, E186-GB and E186-GA read NOT
SCORED; their SIGN words stand. Nothing else depends on s.* The last sentence is not so: **E186-M's z for the four ladder trains depends on
s too**, because both models' lines for B15-B7, B23-B15, A15-A7 and A23-A15 carry P10's offset times s (M10's line is that offset; M12's is
it plus M12's increment). So, registered here before any datum:

- **When s is not read, the four ladder trains leave E186-M** (and every reported joint word: over all ten, and by family). E186-M then
  reads over the session trains other than H16 whose lines need no s and whose SIZE words are scored: **S23, Q63-sign, Q63-swap, Q3-sign
  and Q3-swap, five at most**, with section 5.3's statistic unchanged (Q = sum z^2, each model CONSISTENT when Q is at most the 95% point
  of chi-square at the number of trains read; the words M12 / M10 / BOTH / NEITHER). Their WHICH words read NOT SCORED with their SIZE
  words; their SIGN words stand.
- This is the reading `score_e186.py` has applied since the kit (KIT.md section 4, "s not read"), the one under which no scored word uses
  an unread s. The E186 verifier asked for it to be ruled; its ruling (accept the kit's reading and record it beside the registration) is
  carried out here. When s is read, nothing in A1-1 applies.

**Its power** (registered here; [`power_e186_a1.py`](power_e186_a1.py), [`power-e186-a1.json`](power-e186-a1.json),

## A1-2. The trains' pairing gate (the verifier's fault 1, with the kit's dated note 1)

In six of the ten trains (H16, S23 and the four int8 trains) both kinds give the same C word for word, so E186-C cannot tell which tile
set a launch ran (KIT.md, its desk test's D8 and D9). The kit's sentence that the trains' reduction requires the host's own record on every
process is not so (E182's `reduce_train` requires 13 launches, ok and the orientation only); only the smoke's train gate read that record,
on H16's two smoke processes. So, registered here before any datum:

- **E186-PAIR** (a gate, read at collection over every train block the session ran): every launch record of the block, the calibration
  launch included, (1) is one of the plan's processes and launches, once; (2) carries the registered schedule's kind for that launch (the
  smoke gate's schedule: launch n < 1 the orientation's first letter, launch n >= 1 its letter (n - 1) mod 4); (3) carries the train's
  type, where the record names one (the host's record always does); (4) names the scheduled kind's registered tile; and (5) has a C
  record in the block's C log, paired by its start and end times,
  whose fnv is the FNV-1a 64 of that kind's registered tile bytes (each tile checked against its registered sha256 first).
- **A train with any launch record failing (1)-(5) reads NOT SCORED**, exactly as a train with a launch not PASS reads under E186-C: its
  SIGN, SIZE and WHICH words NOT SCORED; it enters no joint word and no E186-TR-N pool; the rules read from it follow section 5.3 for a
  train NOT SCORED (SPLIT3 or SPLIT63 NOT SCORED with its swap or sign train; E186-GB or E186-GA over the other ladder train's 8 bits
  alone; E186-S23R NOT SCORED with S23). **E186-C is unchanged** and still reads the check of C alone.
- E186-PAIR's words: **PASS** (every train block read paired) or **FAIL**, naming the trains not paired. It is a gate on the records,
  never a measurement.
- **What it cannot show:** that the card's operands were the recorded tiles. For the six equal-C trains that rests on the train host's
  construction, as the kit says (E182's patch sets the device pointers, the C log's tiles and both record fields from one variable).
- **Its power:** on a session whose records are as the plan says it reads PASS (E182's six committed train blocks: 5,712 of 5,712 launch
  records paired, post hoc), so every power figure in `power-e186.json` stands. On the kit's synthetic sessions each of five faults (a
  launch's kind off the schedule with its records consistent, a launch's tile path, a C record's fnv, a missing C record, a block with no
  C log) makes its train NOT SCORED and leaves every other train's words as before (KIT.md.dated-note-1.md, its scorer's self-test).
- The kit's dated note 1 carries it out (`pairing_e186.py`, `score_e186_v2.py`, `collect_e186_v2.sh`, step 11 v2).
