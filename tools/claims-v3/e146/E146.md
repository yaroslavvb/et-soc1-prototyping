# E146: a large GEMM's time per op with C's stores as a term, and what the stores cost in power, under aifoundry2's own governor (Q138)

Mon 2 Nov 2026 (week 45), about 19:40 PST. Registered before the claim and the first launch. It answers the rest of the kinds page's
[section 11, item 1, "Where a large GEMM falls"](https://spacesheep.dev/@yaroslavvb/horace-kinds-of-numbers#11-what-is-still-open), the part
[E136](../../../docs/reports/data/2026-11-02-e136-large-gemm/README.md) left: its frozen law predicted a real large GEMM's time per op 7-13% slow, and
*post hoc* pricing C's store cadence (one store every 256 ops against E119's every 32) moved fp32 to -1.5%, while int8 (-7%) and the GEMM the L3 holds (-11%)
stayed unexplained. Q138 is the owner's ask of 2 November 2026, about 18:40 PST: *"feel free to settle any unanswered questions regarding horace's power
model"*, with Q137's goal, *"to be able to predict the power requirement and heat generation of various kinds of numeric computations as accurately as
possible"*. So E146 freezes, from E119 and E136 only, a store term for time and one for energy, and an alternative that has no store term at all, then
measures a ladder of store cadences that tells them apart, and one GEMM shape nobody has run, predicted first.

have E136's registered sha256. A minion stores each C tile once, after KB × 32 ops: **KB sets the store cadence**.

## The stops, and the test that proves they read live samples

**E136's registered stops are kept, under the keys the watcher reads**: one die reading (the minion shires' mean or hottest sensor) at or above **80 °C**, or
the board above **93 W on three samples in a row**, ends the session (`protocol.json` `safety`: `abs_stop_c`, `board_cap_w`, `cap_consecutive`). **A finding
made while building this: E136's `protocol.json` named its stops `die_c`, `board_w` and `board_samples`, keys `run_hpl.py`'s watcher does not read**
(`Watcher.run`: `sf.get("abs_stop_c", 85)`, `sf.get("board_cap_w", 97)`, `sf.get("cap_consecutive", 3)`), so E136 ran on the watcher's defaults, 85 °C and
97 W on three samples, not on its registered 80 °C and 93 W. Neither was reached (its highest reading 76.09 W, die 66 °C), so no stop was needed; the records
owe E136 a dated note. E126's protocol used the right keys. `run_e146.py` refuses a protocol whose `safety` lacks any of the three keys, under `--dry` too.

**The stop-path tests** (the brief: *"Registered stops must read live samples (E139's 65 W stop watched a file written only at pass end - never again; test
the stop path in the smoke)"*). After the smoke and before the session, the queue runs two sessions through the same runner, sampler and watcher, each with
one stop lowered so that it must fire: **stoptest-board** (`protocol-stoptest-board.json`: `board_cap_w` 30 W on three samples; one 6 s burst of `D8` fp32
random, E136's 52.5 W) and **stoptest-die** (`protocol-stoptest-die.json`: `abs_stop_c` 30 °C; the die reads above 40 °C at rest). The queue goes on to the
session only if the board test ended with exit 4 for "board", its `safety_trip` mark came while the work launch ran and within 1.5 s of the third live sampler
line over 30 W, and the launch ended within 3 s of the trip; and the die test ended with exit 4 for "die" within 1.5 s of the first live sampler line, before any
launch. Either failing ends the queue (QUEUE-HALT) with no session. `selftest_e146.py` shows the check passing on a live trip and failing on a trip read at a
pass's end (E139's failure), on a stop that never fired and on a late die trip; the toy-card dry run fired both (`logs/dry-queue.txt`).

**The power limit**, E119's admission rule unchanged: a cell runs only if its estimate (p800 = B(800) + s(800) c(600), the law's c) is at or below 88 W;
after any run whose board reading reached 93 W, no cell with an estimate at or above that run's goes on. The highest planned estimate is `L1` fp32 random's
79.6 W (`D1` fp32 random 78.5 W): the rule over-read every one of E136's 20 bursts (by 4-15 W in E119's walks), so the board is expected near 66 W, where the
burst cap holds it. Nothing here drives the card toward a PMIC over-power or over-temperature reset: this card's own alarm level is unmeasured (item 14, E117
not run), E126 read 85.62 W at its highest on it with no event, and E136 76.09 W.

## Frozen predictions

`predictions-e146.json`, printed in `predictions-e146.txt`, from committed inputs only (`make_predictions_e146.py`). **Every term this run tests is fitted on
E119 and E136 alone.** With n_L3 and n_F the lines an op that miss the shire's L2 and come from the L3 or fresh, n_miss their sum, N the ops between C stores,
and E119's aifoundry2 fits T(f) = C/f + M of c1 (no line missing), c8s (128 from the L3) and c16 (128 fresh), each storing every 32 ops:

- **M0, E136's frozen law (the baseline, "before"):** T0 = T_c1 + n_L3/128 (T_c8s − T_c1) + n_F/128 (T_c16 − T_c1).
- **MS, the store term (registered primary):** T_S = T0 + (n_miss/128) σ(f) (1/N − 1/32). Every E119 arm stores every 32 ops, so the law prices 1/32 of a
  store into every op; a store's cost shows only where lines miss (c1's 528 FMA cycles leave no room to hide 1/32 of it). σ per format by least squares at
  600 and at 800 MHz over E136's two GEMMs, then σ(f) = Cs/f + Ms through the two: fp32 3.17 and 4.83 µs a store, fp16 3.16 and 4.93, int8 7.80 and 8.04.
  It reproduces E136's GEMMs to +4.2% (`g2k`) and −1.3% (`g4k`) in fp32.
- **MAB, the operand split (registered alternative, no store term):** a missing line costs by operand and place, solved exactly from c8s, `g2k`, c16 and `g4k`
  at each clock. At 800 MHz in fp32: an A line from the L3 7.9 ns of op time, a B line from the L3 1.3 ns (B streams into the multiplier through TenB; the
  kernel waits on A), a fresh A line 16.0 ns, a fresh B line 11.4 ns. It reproduces E136's two GEMMs exactly, by construction.
- **Power, P0 (E136's law, "before")** and **PS, the store's energy (registered primary, "after")**: E136's energy per op e0 = (P0 − B) T0, plus ε (1/N − 1/32),
  ε per format and kind fitted at 800 MHz on E136's two GEMMs from e = (p800 − B(800)) T800 (fp32 zeros 371, random 618; fp16 251, 289; int8 267, 295 µJ for
  one store step, every minion storing one 1 KB tile), then P_S = B(f) + max(0, e_S)/T_S(f) (the floor: never under the base power B(f)).
- **The burst clock:** E119's dither at the burst cap 66.15 ± 2.75 W on PS's levels. **The die:** E136's laws as frozen, a burst's die_max = die_start + 0.1445
  (P_burst − 21.5); a sustained window = min(65.5, 0.379 die_start + 0.621 (44.0 + 0.67 (P_sus − 21.5))), P_sus on footing G2 (E136's better footing: the
  sustained cap 60.83 W) and on G1. die_start is each run's own.

## Rules, which can fail

The full text and bars are in `predictions-e146.json` `rules`; `selftest_e146.py` shows each passing on the frozen numbers and failing far off them, R1's
verdicts at MS's and at MAB's numbers, its fallback, the preflight refusing E136's keys, and the stop check (`selftest.txt`: all pass).

- **R0, the ties:** every check of C exact or approx (a FAIL labels every other verdict); the smoke's cycles per op within 50-250% of MS's; `L2` and `D8`
  (E136's `g2k` and `g4k`) T800 within 2% of E136's measured, per format (a second session).
- **R1, the store term (primary):** ρ = T800(`L8`) ÷ T800(`L4`); h = (ρ − ρ_MAB) ÷ (ρ_MS − ρ_MAB). **THE STORE TERM** at h ≥ 0.75, **NO STORE TERM** at
  h ≤ 0.25, BETWEEN otherwise, NEITHER below −0.5 or above 1.5. Per format; fp32 primary. If `L8`'s T800 is past the midpoint to MS's 24 MB bound (its A no
  longer held by the L3), R1 is UNRESOLVED and the pair `L2/L4` is read the same way.
- **R2, the ladder:** every ladder cell's T800 within 5% of MS's; the rms relative error of M0, MS and MAB over the 24 cells; the lowest predicts best; X within
  0.03 of MS's.
- **R3, burst power:** each ladder cell's first-second board power at 800 MHz within 4 W of PS(800); the rms of PS and P0; at or below the estimate + 1 W.
- **R4, the store's energy:** on `L4/L8`, ε_meas = (e(`L8`) − e(`L4`)) ÷ (1/256 − 1/128), e = (p800 − B(800)) T800, within a factor of two of the fitted ε,
  per format and kind (a cell where PS sits on its floor is not scored); `D4/D8` printed.
- **R5, the fresh shape `F6`:** T800 within 5% of MS's (M0, MAB printed); X within 0.03; burst p800 within 4 W of PS(800); burst die_max within 3 °C; the
  two sustained runs: clock within 30 MHz of G2's, board W within 4 W, the window die within 3 °C.
- **R6, the die:** every burst's die_max within 3 °C of die_start + 0.1445 (P_burst,S − 21.5); rms (and P0's).
- **R7, the kind:** every cell's T at or above 790 MHz within 1% of its zeros'.
- **R8, the passes:** each cell's T800 and p800 per pass (A-D = pass 1, E = pass 2) and R1's ρ per pass; a verdict that holds in one pass and not the other is
  reported so. One session: no interval between sessions.

The reduction is `reduce_e146.py` (E126's collect() and E119's clock_x, as E136's). **Every README number of measured against predicted is measured −
predicted, per cell and as an rms per quantity and model** (`results.json` `errors`).

## What this changes for the prediction (Q137, Q138)

The time term of the power model has one open variable for GEMMs whose operands miss the caches: what a store of C costs, and whether the lines' price
depends on which operand they feed. E146 measures, on the same card and binary as E136, **whether time per op follows the store cadence at fixed lines
(the store term) or the operand of each missing line (the split)**, and what a store costs in energy, then scores both against a shape neither saw. The README
reports the out-of-sample error of time per op before (M0, E136's law) and after (MS and MAB), of burst power before (P0) and after (PS), and of the die, per
quantity and per model.

## What this does not do

No new binary, no vendor binary, no sys_emu run (the shapes are arguments of E136's kernel, whose check of C over the whole K runs in every launch). One card,
one session; two passes of the ladder, so the per-pass spread is shown but no interval is between sessions. No room thermometer. Zeros and random only: the
kinds' power at a store cadence is E136's (sparse and real were run there on `g4k` and `g2k`).
