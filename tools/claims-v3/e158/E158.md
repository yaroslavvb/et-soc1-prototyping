# E158: a large GEMM's time per op as the L3's retention by reuse distance, under aifoundry2's own governor (Q139)

Tue 3 Nov 2026 (week 45), about 05:00 PST. Registered before the claim and the first launch. It answers the kinds page's
[section 11, item 1, "Where a large GEMM falls"](https://spacesheep.dev/@yaroslavvb/horace-kinds-of-numbers#11-what-is-still-open), the rest that
[E146](../../../docs/reports/data/2026-11-02-e146-gemm-stores/README.md) left, and **rank 7 of Q138's final critic**: *"A large GEMM's time per op: the L3's
retention by reuse distance ... Register a ladder of B-panel reuse distances from 20 to 56 MB, with and without C's traffic, with MAB's frozen prices
predicting the L3-served share. Rule: the fitted retention curve predicts F6's time per op within 3%."* Q139 continues the owner's ask of 2 November 2026,
about 18:40 PST: *"all cards are available, feel free to settle any unanswered questions regarding horace's power model"*, with Q137's goal, *"to be able to
predict the power requirement and heat generation of various kinds of numeric computations as accurately as possible"*.

**Why.** E146 refuted a store term for C (R1) and found that its operand-split model MAB, with prices solved exactly from four earlier runs, predicts the
GEMMs the L3 holds to 1.4%, but that a strict-LRU 32 MB L3 calls lines fresh that the card evidently served from the L3: through MAB's prices, the far B
lines of `D2` were 57-59% L3-served and those of the fresh shape `F6` 33-38%, while `D4`'s were not. So `F6` ran 9-10% faster than MAB (and than every
frozen model: R5 T FAIL ×3). E146 could not say whether the L3 keeps lines past 32 MB of reuse distance, or whether C's stores take less of the L3 than
the model gives them. E158 measures both.

## What runs

**aifoundry2 card 0 under its own governor** (65 W / 65 °C, read by `ettelem config` at the queue's start and end and after every run). **Nothing is set on
the card**: no TDP, threshold, clock or voltage; no `--reset-ms`, no statistics reset; no firmware-revisions query; no residency read; no governor move.

**The binary is E136's, unchanged** (rgm136's `SP_GEMM_LARGE`, built on aifoundry2 at 15:37 PST on 2 Nov 2026; E146 ran the same three programs): every shape is
an argument, `gemmL<MB>x<NB>x<KB>`, and E146's preflight refuses the session unless the three device programs have E136's registered sha256. **Every cell is
zeros** (the file's zero tile through the pool, E146's zeros cells): the kind does not move a large GEMM's time per op (E146 R7, worst 0.66%; E136 and E126
R7), and zeros hold every cell near 38 W, far under this round's 82 W limit for aifoundry2.

**The ladder.** A minion walks the C blocks mb outer, nb inner; a column tile's B panel is used twice in a row (the minion's two row tiles, a short
distance) and then again only after the other NB - 1 panels, A's panel and the row's C blocks have gone by. Every rung has **MB = 1**, so A's panel is
reused across the row's C blocks at 8-25 MB, the L2 model's hits are E146's, and the **64 B lines an op whose fate is open (the far B lines) all sit within
about 1 MB of one LRU stack distance**. The desk ([`desk/reuse_e158.py`](desk/reuse_e158.py), `reuse_e158.txt`, `.json`) computes, for every line the
shire's L2 misses, its exact LRU stack distance in the L3, the operand tiles (d_AB) and the C tiles (d_C) apart, with E136's L2 model and E146's L3 step
order imported unchanged; it reproduces E146's 32 MB and 24 MB splits line for line on E146's nine shapes. (E146's §7 distances were nominal: exactly,
`D2`'s far B lines sit at 34-38 MB, `F6`'s at 42-48 and 54 MB, `D4`'s at 54-62 MB.) **Three families put different shares of C's traffic in the same
distance**, which is what separates "the L3 keeps lines past 32 MB" from "C takes less of it":

| family | arm | far B distance, C counted (operand + C) | C's share | a store every |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| **K2** ("C-heavy") | `gemmL1x<NB>x2`, NB 4-13 | 19, 23, ..., 55 MB (12-30 + 7-25) | 37-45% | 64 ops |
| **K4** | `gemmL1x<NB>x4`, NB 2-8 (NB 2 is E146's `L4`) | 19, 25, ..., 55 MB (16-40 + 3-15) | 16-27% | 128 |
| **K6** ("C-light") | `gemmL1x<NB>x6`, NB 1-6 | (NB 1: none; A at 19 MB) 27, 35, 43, 51, 59 MB (24-48 + 3-11) | 11-19% | 192 |
| E146's, again | `F6` = `gemmL3x4x6` (the scored shape), `D2` = `gemmL4x8x2`, `D4` = `gemmL4x8x4` | `F6` 50.5 (43.5 + 7.1); `D2` 38.2; `D4` 61.5 | 14%, 39%, 24% | 192, 64, 128 |

There is no arm with no C traffic at all: `run_gemm_large` stores every C block to its own place (it does not read E107's `SP_GEMM_C_SHARED`), and a new
binary is out of scope; so "without C's traffic" is the C-light family, at 11-19% of the distance against the C-heavy family's 37-45%. The per-rung
numbers are in `predictions-e158.txt`.

**The design** is E146's (E136's: E80's with E105's, E119's and E126's parameters): a burst is one 6 s process from a die reading at or below 55 °C, its
tail 4 s. The time per op comes from the launches whose kernel clock (`cycles_max ÷ wall`) reads 790 MHz or more, and at 590-610 MHz for the clock's gain X
(E119's `clock_x`, unchanged); **cycles per op from the measured clock** are `cycles_max ÷ ops` at those launches, and C of the fit T = C/f + M. **The order**
(`protocol.txt`): SMOKE (the 30 s rest reading, then four 2 s launches of zeros with their check of C: `K4n2` fp32, `K2n13` fp16, `K6n6` int8, `F6` fp32); the
**stop-path tests** (below); A: pass 1, the 23 rungs in fp32; B: pass 1 of `F6` in fp32, fp16 and int8, `D2` and `D4` in fp32; C: pass 2, A and B again,
shuffled (run_hpl.py's one generator, `order_seed` 158); E: the K4 rungs in fp16 and int8; F: pass 3, A and B again, shuffled. **98 bursts, about 20
card-minutes**, plus the smoke and the tests (about 2.5 min); **the session's cap is 30 card-minutes; at most 40 card-minutes in all**; the claim is
"for 1 h". **The registered subset**, if the cap or the slot cuts: blocks A, B and C (two passes of the ladder and of `F6`). Q139's gates: no new card
session after 10:30 PST, every run ends by 11:30 PST.

## The stops, and the test that proves they read live samples

**Q139's rule for aifoundry2** (its PMIC over-power configuration is unread, and Q138's bursts peaked at 87.95-87.98 W): no cell whose predicted peak
board power exceeds 82 W runs as designed, and every registered board stop is at most 85 W on live samples, tested in the smoke. **The predicted peak** is
E119's admission estimate, p800 = B(800) + s(800) c600 (E136's law for each shape's lines; it over-read every one of E136's 20 bursts, by 4-15 W in
E119's walks): **the highest of the 78 cells frozen (26 shapes × 3 formats; 42 of them run) is 39.8 W** (`predictions-e158.json` `cells`), so no cell is duty-cycled or dropped. **The session's
stops**: one die reading (the minion shires' mean or hottest sensor) at or above **80 °C**, or the board above **60 W on three samples in a row**, ends the
session (`protocol.json` `safety`, under the keys run_hpl.py's watcher reads: `abs_stop_c`, `board_cap_w`, `cap_consecutive`). The admission limit is
**82 W** at the estimate, and after any run whose board reading reached 60 W no cell with an estimate at or above that run's goes on. `run_e158.py` refuses a
protocol whose stops lack a key, whose `board_cap_w` is above 85 W or whose `limit_p800_w` is above 82 W, under `--dry` too (`selftest.txt`).

**The stop-path tests** (E146's, unchanged in the queue). After the smoke and before the session the queue runs two sessions through the same runner,
sampler and watcher, each with one stop lowered so that it must fire: **stoptest-board** (`protocol-stoptest-board.json`: `board_cap_w` 30 W on three
samples; one 6 s burst of `K4n8` fp32 zeros, which E146's zeros bursts put at 34-38 W at 800 MHz over a 22 W rest) and **stoptest-die**
(`protocol-stoptest-die.json`: `abs_stop_c` 30 °C; the die reads above 40 °C at rest). The queue goes on only if the board test ended with exit 4 for
"board", its `safety_trip` mark came while the work launch ran and within 1.5 s of the third live sampler line over 30 W, and the launch ended within 3 s of
the trip; and the die test ended with exit 4 for "die" within 1.5 s of the first live sampler line, before any launch. Either failing ends the queue
(QUEUE-HALT) with no session. `selftest_e158.py` runs the check on E158's queue: it passes on a live trip and fails on a trip read at a pass's end, on a
stop that never fired and on a late die trip; the toy-card dry run fired both (`logs/dry-queue.txt`).

## Frozen predictions

`predictions-e158.json`, printed in `predictions-e158.txt`, from committed inputs only (`make_predictions_e158.py`, through `model_e158.py`). **Time per op is
MAB's form with MAB's frozen prices** (E146's `predictions-e146.json` `fitted.<t>.ab`, solved exactly from E119's c8s and c16 and E136's g2k and g4k; E119's
c1 for the base): T(f) = T_c1(f) + A_L3 pA + B_L3 pB + A_F qA + B_F qB at 600 and 800 MHz, with the lines a retention curve leaves. A retention curve r(x)
is the probability that the L3 still holds a tile at weighted distance x = d_AB + w d_C, w being how much of C's traffic occupies the L3 (1 in E146's model).
Frozen, per rung and format (T600, T800, X, C and M, cycles per op at 800 MHz, the far-B share):

- **STRICT-C**, strict LRU at 32 MB with C occupying the L3: E146's MAB exactly (it reproduces E146's MAB T800 on E146's nine shapes within 0.002 µs).
  The "before". It says every rung at 35 MB or more runs with its far B lines fresh (fp32 T800 1.975 µs in K2, 1.996 in K4 and K6) and every rung at
  31 MB or less with them from the L3 (1.326, 1.346).
- **STRICT-NOC**, the same with C taking none of it (w = 0): the far B lines held below 32 MB of operand distance (K2 at every rung; K4 to NB 6; K6 to NB 3).
- **PRIOR**, a logistic r(x) fitted through MAB's frozen prices on E146's committed KB ≥ 2 ladder cells only (`L2`, `L4`, `L8`, `D2`, `D4`, `D8` in three
  formats; never `F6`, and not the KB = 1 shapes, whose miss is A's L2 hits): x50 31.25 MB, s 1.0 MB, w 0.49, 1.1% rms on its 18 cells. It predicts, for
  example, K2n9 at a share of 0.70, K4n5 at 0.24, K6n3 at 0.23, and **`F6` at a share of 0**, so its `F6` T800 is STRICT-C's: 2.125 µs in fp32 (E146
  measured 1.912: -10.0%), 2.128 in fp16 (-10.4%), 2.117 in int8 (-9.1%).
- **The share axis**: for each rung, T800 with its far B lines all fresh and all from the L3 (every other line as STRICT-C says), so a measured T800 reads as
  a share s = (T_allF - T800) / (T_allF - T_allL3); in fp32 the axis spans 0.65 µs, so 1% of T800 is about 0.03 of share.
- **The zeros' power**: E136's law as frozen for each shape's strict lines (P0 at 600, 700 and 800 MHz; 37.0-38.5 W at 800) and the admission estimate.

**Fitted on the ladder** (the scorer, frozen in `model_e158.py`): **ISO** (registered primary), for each w on a grid 0-1 in steps of 0.05, the rungs'
fp32 shares (every pass pooled, each rung weighted by its passes) against their far lines' mean x, made non-increasing by pool-adjacent-violators; w is
the value with the least rms residual (the middle of a tie: a step-like curve fits exactly over a range of w, the rungs being 4-8 MB apart), its
near-best interval every w within max(0.01, 0.25 × the least) of the least; r(x) is the isotonic values joined linearly between rungs, flat beyond
the ends, held to [0, 1]. **LOGI** (the parametric alternative): a logistic in x with (x50, s, w) by least squares on the shares.

## Rules, which can fail

The full text and bars are in `predictions-e158.json` `rules`; `selftest_e158.py` (`selftest.txt`: all pass) builds rows from known truths through the same
model and checks each rule reads them: a soft curve with C occupying the L3, E146's strict model, strict LRU with C taking none of it, a half-weight C,
retention that differs by family against the C share, `F6` 10% off its curve, a rung MAB cannot carry, a failed check of C.

- **R0, the ties:** every check of C exact or approx (a FAIL labels every other verdict); the smoke's cycles per op within 50-250% of STRICT-C's; the shapes
  E146 ran, run again here (`K4n2` = E146's `L4`, `D2`, `D4`, `F6`), T800 within 2% of E146's measured T800 per format run (a second session).
- **R1, the shape of retention:** each rung's far-B share, through MAB's frozen prices: CONSISTENT if every share lies in [-0.10, 1.10] (MAB can carry the
  rung), else INCONSISTENT naming the rungs; STRICT if every rung is within 0.15 of STRICT-C's share, else SOFT (and the same against STRICT-NOC).
- **R2, C's occupancy w** (ISO's near-best interval): **C OCCUPIES THE L3** if it holds w = 1, not w = 0, and starts at or above 0.5; **C TAKES NONE OF IT** if
  it holds w = 0, not w = 1, and ends at or below 0.5; PARTIAL if it holds neither end; UNRESOLVED otherwise; **NO SINGLE CURVE**, whatever w, if ISO's
  least rms exceeds 0.06 in share (the three families do not fall on one curve at any w). LOGI printed beside it.
- **R3, THE RULE (the critic's):** ISO at its w, with MAB's frozen prices and `F6`'s lines from the desk, predicts `F6`'s T800 measured in this session within
  **3%**, per format; **fp32 primary**. LOGI gets the same verdict, printed; both also printed against E146's `F6`. STRICT-C and PRIOR are the befores.
- **R4, out of sample beyond `F6`:** ISO's T800 for E146's committed 24 ladder cells (and the 18 with KB ≥ 2), and `D2`, `D4` here, against STRICT-C's and PRIOR's;
  BETTER if ISO's rms on the 18 KB ≥ 2 cells is at least 2 points below STRICT-C's (PRIOR was fitted on those 18: in sample there).
- **R5, the formats:** ISO, fitted on fp32, with each format's own MAB prices, predicts the K4 rungs' T800 in fp16 and int8 within 3% each (the count).
- **R6, the passes:** each rung's share per pass (A-B pass 1, C pass 2, F pass 3; E, the formats, pass 1); ISO refitted on each pass alone, its w and its `F6`
  prediction per pass; a verdict that holds in one pass and not another is reported so. One session: no interval between sessions.
- **R7, power (reported):** the zeros' burst p800 against P0 with the strict lines and with ISO's lines; rms and the count within 4 W.
- **R8, cycles per op (printed):** per shape and format, the fit T = C/f + M and `cycles_max ÷ ops` at the launches at or above 790 MHz, per pass.

The reduction is `reduce_e158.py` (E126's collect() and E119's clock_x, as E146's). **Every number of measured against predicted is measured -
predicted** (relative for time), per rung and as an rms per model (`results.json` `errors`).

## What this changes for the prediction (Q137-Q139)

Time per op sets a GEMM's energy per op (E = P T) and, on the governed card, its clock and power. E146 left MAB's line prices, which carry the L3-held
shapes to 1.4%, with one wrong input on shapes that miss: the strict-LRU line classes. E158 measures **the term that replaces them, an L3 retention curve
r(d_AB + w d_C)**, on 23 rungs whose open lines sit at one distance each, in three shares of C's traffic, and scores it on `F6` (never fitted) and on
E146's committed ladder. The README reports **the out-of-sample error of time per op before (STRICT-C, E146's MAB, and PRIOR) and after (ISO)**: on `F6`
(E146 measured -9.1 to -10.4% against STRICT-C), on E146's 18 KB ≥ 2 cells and on `D2` and `D4` here; and the zeros' power before and after the curve
moves lines between the L3 and DRAM (R7).

## What this does not do

No new binary, no vendor binary, no sys_emu run; no arm with no C traffic at all (the large-GEMM kernel stores every C block to its own place). Zeros only:
the kinds' power at these shapes is not measured (E146's and E136's random cells were). One card, one session; three passes of the ladder, so the per-pass
spread is shown but no interval is between sessions. MAB's prices stay frozen: if the L3 also keeps some of `D8`'s lines, the prices MAB solved from `g4k`
carry that, and the README says so. The KB = 1 shapes' A lines (E146's other miss) are not in this ladder.
