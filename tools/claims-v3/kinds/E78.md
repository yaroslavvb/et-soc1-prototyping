# E78: the kinds-of-numbers energy matrix (Q130, aifoundry3 card 0, 600 MHz)

*Pre-registered Sat 31 Oct 2026 (week 44), on the Intel, before the Discord claim and the first launch. The frozen
numbers are in [`predictions-e78.json`](predictions-e78.json), committed with this file. Any change after this commit
goes in the Amendments section at the end, dated, before the data it touches; no band moves. Card: **aifoundry3
card 0 only**, 600 MHz, ~522 mV, TDP 0 — the boot-time value `et-board-clock-guard` writes, which E78 never changes.
The kinds are Q130(s)'s revision 2 (`7d9c0d9e`): the clock for this registration started there.*

**The question.** Horace He's result is that a matmul runs faster on predictable data. On this chip, at a pinned
clock, the same question becomes: how much switching power, and how much energy per operation, does each **kind of
number** cost, in each **format**? E64 measured fp32 on ten kinds but fp16 and int8 on three. E78 runs **all 51
kinds of [`kinds.json`](kinds.json)** — 18 fp32, 17 fp16, 16 int8, the same tiles Q130(b) replayed in the RTL and
Q130(c) predicted — on one card, at one clock, in one protocol, so the blog's kinds table gets a measured power and
energy column for every cell, and the two frozen models get scored against it.

## What runs

| the tie | `anchor_pi`, the host's built-in fp32 `--values pi`, first and last in every pass: E60–E79's drift anchor and the tie to their passes. The same bytes as `fp32.pi`'s tile, so the two also test built-in against file |
| a pass | 65 runs: the tie, three segments of 21–22 runs, the tie. Types and kinds interleave, and no kind keeps a position from one pass to the next, so drift and heat cannot alias onto a kind. **Pass 7810 only:** `fp32.a_randn_b_zeros` runs first after the opening tie (Q130(c)'s R9: "the single most informative cell ... run it first") |
| passes | **7810–7815 planned (six), at least four.** Smoke 7801. Two unrecorded `anchor_pi` burn-in runs open each pass |

## Protocol

- **Launch reading** (E60's, unchanged): 2 s random-fp32 heater launches until the minion-shire reading is ≥ 59 °C,
  then idle until it first shows ≤ 58 °C. A run whose reading is not reached is skipped and recorded, never launched
  off protocol; three in a row stop the pass.
- **The quiet rule (new).** After the reading shows 58 °C, the runner keeps idling until **2.0 s** have passed since
  the end of the last device process (heater or run), as long as the reading stays at 58; if it falls to 57 it heats
  again. Why: E79's amendment A7 found its idle bracket contaminated. E79's committed telemetry says why
  ([`e78_desk_checks.txt`](e78_desk_checks.txt)): board power falls to idle within 0.2 s of a process ending (the 100
  ms bin before a run ends reads 34.81 W, the bin from +100 ms 26.33 W, +2 s 26.05 W; block [1]), but in **109 of E79's 480 runs
  (22.7%)** the bracket `[t0−1.2 s, t0−0.2 s)` began before the previous process had ended (median gap 1.91 s, p10
  0.21 s; block [2]). With the rule the bracket begins ≥ 0.8 s after the previous process; on E79's gaps it would
  have added 495 ms a run on average, extending half the runs (block [2]).
- **Runs** are 4 s. The power window is `[t0+1 s, t0+3 s)` after the first launch record, as E65/E67/E70/E79.
- **Safety** (unchanged): every device process under `timeout 10`; `--stop-file` on every launch; the pass stops at a
  78 °C reading, on a telemetry line older than 3 s, or when another user appears on this card; the card lock is held
  for the pass and released between passes.
- **Card state, read only:** `ettelem config` at the start and end of every pass, inside the lock (TDP must read 0
  and the threshold 65). **No knob is written anywhere in E78**: no governor move, no TDP, threshold or clock set, no
  reset. `run_e78.sh setup` opens no device node at all (E79's departure D2).
- **Departure D1** (E60's, kept by every aifoundry3 run since): a user who is only logged in does not block; another
  user on this card, any other user's device process, or an unreadable `et-holders` does, failing closed.

## Pre-registered analysis (`reduce_e78.py`)

**Per run** (one process of one item at one position of one pass), with `t0` the start of its first launch record:

- `P` = mean board power over `[t0+1, t0+3)`; `p80 = P − k (T̄window − T_launch)`, 21 Sep's leakage correction;
- `I` = the **idle bracket**, mean board power over `[t0−1.2, t0−0.2)`, valid only with ≥ 8 samples, starting ≥ 0.8 s
  after the previous process, at a reading within 1.5 °C of the launch reading; `idle80` corrected the same way;
  **`S = p80 − idle80`**, the run's switching power over idle;
- **the minion rail** reads as a first-order lag of board power: on E79's ten passes `rail = a + c·IIR_τ(board(t−d))`
  fits with τ = 0.95–1.00 s (median 0.950), d = 0.2 s in every pass and c = 0.800–0.829 (median 0.810), rms
  0.30–0.41 W against 3.26–4.05 W with no lag (`e78_desk_checks.txt` [3]). So the rail is **deconvolved**:
  mean(r) + τ·(r(b) − r(a))/(b − a) over each window shifted by d, τ = 0.95 s, corrected with c·k. The rail arm is
  reported beside the board arm, never instead of it; τ = 0.80 and 1.10 s are its sensitivity arms;
- `R` = the work the host's launch records complete inside the power window (prorated), in minion-ops a second;
  `duty = R / (600 MHz × 1,024 / cycles_per_op)` is reported beside it (expected ≈ 0.996: E70 measured 1.1208–1.1210
  Gop/s against the nominal 1.1253);
- `ΔT` = the die reading's mean over the window minus the launch reading: the heat column.

**Gates, per run** (every failure counted and reported): every window sample at 600 MHz; the window's mean minion
voltage within ±3 mV of the pass median; an on-protocol launch at a reading of 58 °C with ≥ 2.0 s quiet; every
launch record `ok` and every record's `cycles_per_op` within 0.5% of **546 / 546 / 318** (fp32 / fp16 / int8); ≥ 15
window samples and no gap over 0.5 s; launch records covering the window (work done in it ≥ 0.97 of the nominal
rate); and the tile the host dumped on the item's first run of the pass equal to
kinds.json's sha256 (`--dump-tiles`).

**Gates, per pass.** A pass is set aside if more than 2 runs fail a gate; if a type's zeros anchor's middle run
departs more than 0.25 W from the straight line through its first and third, or spans more than 0.60 W; if
`anchor_pi` drifts more than 0.60 W first to last; or if a random anchor spans more than 1.0 W. A TDP other than 0
or a threshold other than 65 at either end puts the pass in a separate report, never pooled. Why these values: E79's
`anchor_pi` drifted 0.08–0.53 W first to last in a pass, and its 0.3 W gate set 2 of 10 passes aside; E70's drifted
0.03–0.24 W (`e78_desk_checks.txt` [4], from their committed `runs.csv`). E78 corrects drift by interpolation, so its gate only has to catch drift too
large or too bent to interpolate. **No pass is chosen after the fact:** the final reduction is every pass that
passes the gates.

**Per pass**, each type's three zeros runs are interpolated linearly in time (flat beyond the first and last):

- **`D = p80 − Ẑ_type(t0)`**, switching power over the type's own zero-data level: **the quantity every prediction is
  scored on** (Q130(c)'s basis);
- `D32 = p80 − Ẑ_fp32(t0)`, over fp32's zeros (Q130(b) r2's basis);
- one value per (pass, item) is the mean over its kept runs. Over passes: the mean, its SE from the between-pass
  scatter, and a 95% interval `t(0.975, n−1) × SE`. **The unit of replication is the pass.** A quantity with fewer
  than 3 kept passes is unresolved, and any rule resting on it is NOT SCORED, never passed.

**Energy.** `pJ per op = 10¹² × W / R`, per pass then averaged, from S (the whole switching over idle) and from D
(the data-dependent part). Per MAC: ÷ 4,096 (fp32), 8,192 (fp16), 16,384 (int8). Per flop (fp32, fp16): ÷ 2 × MACs.
int8 is quoted per MAC only.

**Leakage.** The primary slope is `auto`, the median within-run slope of runs that heat ≥ 1 °C, as E70 and E79.
The registered arms are **E71's measured aifoundry3 idle slope, 0.3803 W/°C**, and **0**. Every rule is scored at
the primary and its verdict at each arm reported beside it; a verdict that moves between arms is reported as
slope-sensitive. On synthetic data with a known 0.15 W/°C the within-run estimator returns 0.053 W/°C
(`selftest_e78.txt`): the die reading moves in 1 °C steps while the true rise inside a window is smaller, so the
estimator is attenuated. That is why the arms are scored, not just printed. The size of the term: on E67's runs
the reading rose 3.31 °C over the window for random fp32 and 0.70 °C for zeros (`e78_desk_checks.txt` [5]), so the
correction of D(fp32 randn) is k × 2.6 °C: 0.2 W at a within-run slope of 0.08 W/°C, 1.0 W at E71's 0.38. That is
4% of the step; it cannot move a rank (R1) and barely a ratio (R3), but it can move R4's randn replication.

**Checks reported, not predictions (E78-R5):** the idle bracket's consistency, median |S(kind) − S(type zeros) −
D(kind)| ≤ 0.15 W; the rail's lag re-fitted on E78's own passes, median within [0.80, 1.15] s; duty within
[0.990, 1.000] for every kind; carry-over, the slope of a run's residual on the previous run's switching, |β| ≤ 0.01
W/W or < 3 SE; and the built-in pi tile the host dumps equal to kinds.json's `fp32.pi` tile. A failed check is
reported with the quantity it qualifies and moves no verdict.

**The self-test.** `selftest_e78.py` writes six synthetic passes in the exact layout the runner writes, in the
registered order, with Q130(c)'s predictions as the injected truth, an idle that drifts 0.3 W a pass, a leakage
term, integer die readings, launch gaps, heaters and a rail lagging at τ = 1.00 s. The reducer recovers every D to
0.091 W, every S to 0.122 W and every D32 to 0.091 W, the rail at 0.79–0.81 of the board (c = 0.81), and re-fits the
lag at 1.00 s ([`selftest_e78.txt`](selftest_e78.txt)). Nothing it prints is data.

## Decision rules

**E78's own rules** (thresholds machine-readable in `predictions-e78.json` › `rules`):

| | rule | threshold | why this value, before the data |
|---|---|---|---|
| **R1** rank | Spearman ρ of predicted against measured switching power **within each type**, over Q130(c) A1's out-of-sample kinds (fp32 15, fp16 14, int8 13). Primary model Q130(c) on D; Q130(b) r2 A and B on D32 beside it | **ρ ≥ 0.80** in each type | with 13–15 kinds, ρ = 0.80 rejects no association at p < 0.001 and leaves room only for local swaps. Q130(c) registered 0.90 for itself, scored separately |
| **R2** accuracy | rms of (measured − predicted) within each type, same sets and models | **fp32 ≤ 2.0 W, fp16 ≤ 2.0 W, int8 ≤ 1.0 W** | 8–9% of the random-data step Q130(c) predicts for fp32 and fp16 (24.2 and 22.3 W), 14% of int8's (7.1 W); 1.4× the clean out-of-sample fp32 score Q130(c)'s A1 computed (1.46 W) |
| **R3a** one-sided zeros | D(`a_randn_b_zeros`)/D(randn) and D(`a_zeros_b_randn`)/D(randn) in fp32 and fp16; `a_full_b_zeros`, `a_zeros_b_full` against `full` in int8; per pass, mean | **≤ 0.15** per cell | every lane is gated while one operand bus keeps moving. Q130(c)'s gate arm says 0; its bus arm 0.21–0.45× randn in fp32/fp16 (0.21–0.27× with A1's zero products unsigned). 0.15 sits between them. int8 `a_zeros_b_full` cannot discriminate (both arms ≤ 0.11×) and is reported, not used to choose an arm |
| **R3b** sparse90 | D(`sparse90`)/D(random), per type | **≤ 0.25** | 90% zero elements gate 99.2 / 96.1 / 82.2% of lane-cycles (kinds.json); gate arm 0.02 / 0.05 / 0.11×, bus arm 0.23–0.31×. `wsparse90` (fp16, int8) is reported beside |
| **R3c** magnitude | fp32 D(`randn_small`)/D(randn) and D(`randn_large`)/D(randn) | **both in [0.909, 1.10]** | σ = 2⁻¹⁰, 1 and 2¹⁰ flip 82,244 / 82,214 / 84,896 operand bits an op (max/min 1.033, kinds.json); 10% is three times that spread. *Bits matter, magnitude does not* |
| **R3d** fewer mantissa bits | fp32, paired per pass: D(`fp16grid`) − D(`bf16trunc`) and D(randn) − D(`fp16grid`) | PASS if both 95% lower bounds > 0; FAIL if either upper bound < 0; else INCONCLUSIVE | operand bits 34,980 < 43,136 < 82,214. | **R3e** order alone | paired: D(randn) − D(`randn_sorted`) in fp32 and fp16, D(`full`) − D(`sorted`) in int8 | per cell PASS if the 95% lower bound > 0, FAIL if the upper bound < 0; the rule passes only if all three pass | identical operand multisets (kinds.json verifies them word by word), only the stream order differs. The power saving is reported beside the operand-bit saving (26 / 27 / 23%) |
| **R3f** static bits | int8 D(`neg_ones`) − D(`ones`): every bit set against one bit a byte, neither moving, neither gated, the same accumulator walk | **PASS if the 95% interval lies inside ±0.30 W**; FAIL if it lies wholly outside; else INCONCLUSIVE. fp32 and fp16 the same, reported | E67's six constant routes spanned 0.33–0.42 W in fp32 (its README, the two whole-operand additivity paragraphs); 0.30 W is 16% of int8's whole constant-to-zeros step (E79: 1.834 W) |

out-of-sample kinds against the frozen `switching_w_ci95` (≥ 70% fp32, ≥ 50% fp16/int8), R1-A1 on

**Q130(b) r2**, both variants: per kind, error = predicted − measured D32 and z = error/√(σ²pred + se²); per type
the rms and mean error and the share with |z| ≤ 2; E78's R1 and R2 applied on the same sets. g re-fitted per format

**Failures are reported as failures**, with the measured value beside the band. No band moves after this commit.

## Card budget

**At most 90 card-minutes, including the smoke and the heating gates. Expected: ~66** — smoke ~1.5 min, then six
passes of ~10.7 min (65 runs and 2 burn-in at ~9.2 s a run: E79's measured 8.7 s, `hlp/README.md` A3, plus 0.5 s
for the quiet rule;
sampler start, config reads, 15 s between passes). `queue_e78.sh` starts a full pass only if its projected end (the
longest pass so far; 11 min before the first) is inside **85** card-minutes of the queue's start and before **19:15
PDT**. With fewer than four passes done it runs a tier-1-only pass if that fits inside 88 card-minutes and the
deadline; otherwise it stops. A pass in flight is never cut. Fewer than four kept passes: every rule NOT SCORED.

## How to run it (for the run lane)

On the Intel: confirm this commit is on origin/main. Copy `tools/claims-v3/kinds/` from it into the aifoundry3 tree
that holds the binaries, which E67, E70 and E79 ran from (`~/aifoundry`), e.g.
`rsync -a tools/claims-v3/kinds/ aifoundry3:aifoundry/tools/claims-v3/kinds/`. Then, on aifoundry3, from that tree:

```bash
bash tools/claims-v3/kinds/run_e78.sh setup        # no device access: lib.sh's sha256 against the freeze, 51 tiles
                                                   # against kinds.json, the binary's strings, the plan, a V3_DRY pass
# -> post the #community-lab claim (one line, CONTEXT section 4), THEN:
bash tools/claims-v3/kinds/run_e78.sh smoke        # six 2 s runs; check cycles/op 546 / 546 / 318 and ok
bash tools/claims-v3/kinds/run_e78.sh go 7810 6    # detached; the budget rule decides how many of the six run
bash tools/claims-v3/kinds/run_e78.sh status       # bounded checks only
bash tools/claims-v3/kinds/collect_e78.sh          # after the queue ends: reduce, both arms, score, assemble
```

Then copy `build/e78-out/2026-10-31-e78-kinds-energy-matrix/` to `docs/reports/data/` on the Intel, write its
README (results first; registered against happened; departures dated), and release the claim by editing it.

## Joins and traps

- **E64's names.** int8 `full` is E64's int8 `--values randn` (uniform −128..127) and `pos` its `--values uniform`
  (0..127). Without this the 23 Oct int8 cells do not join.
- **A `file:` run's metadata is wrong and its math is unchecked** (`main.cpp:587-591`, `:614`): ignore `nnz_a`,
  `a_elems` and `fp16_one_sided_pairs`; use kinds.json. E78 verifies no arithmetic.
- **One tile a cell is a draw.** Quote the tile's measurement for the run, and kinds.json's `replicates` for the rule.
- **int8's bus and Hamming counts are provisional** in kinds.json; its gated fractions are not.
- **fp32 `smallint` is not zero-free** (1/16 of its words are zero, 11.8% of lane-cycles gate); `pow2` and
  `bf16trunc` are the zero-free low-entropy fp32 kinds.
- **E78 does not validate the A-side weight in ungated lanes**: its one-sided kinds are fully gated. E70's `nrd_a` /
  `nrd_b` pair is that measurement.
