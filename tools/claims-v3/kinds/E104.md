# E104: does E92's fp16 "frozen" sum hold? The accumulators read back, and fp16's premium on sums that really do or do not move (aifoundry1 card 1, 600 MHz)

*Written Sun 1 Nov 2026 (week 44), Q132 (lane `a1c1-e104-cread`, label `E104`), before the claim, before any launch and before any E104 tile had run on any card. It tests on silicon the fault lane `a1c1-e102-bits` found, [`docs/reports/data/2026-11-01-e102-balanced-bit-prices/README.md`](../../../docs/reports/data/2026-11-01-e102-balanced-bit-prices/README.md) § "A fault in two lab tools" with [`fault_e92_fp16_e102.py`](fault_e92_fp16_e102.py) and [`fault_fl_ns_e102.py`](fault_fl_ns_e102.py), as E102's verifier sharpened it. Frozen numbers: `predictions-e104.json`; tiles and every expected accumulator: `e104/e104.json`; scorer: `score_e104.py`, all in this commit.*

## The question

E92 ran fp16 tiles it called frozen: a k-group-0 product of 2^31, every later product absorbed. It read their watts as the price of the adder alternating between adding and subtracting in a sum that does not move: "fp16 pays 1.55× fp32 per alternation", "about 92% of fp16's random-sign step remains with the sum frozen". **Nobody has ever read C back on these tiles.** Three readings of fp16's accumulate are on the table:

| reading | what it says | where it comes from | `h_sub`'s C08 at n = 2 | `hh_sub`'s C08 at n = 2 |
|---|---|---|---:|---:|
| **frozen** | C = fl(n S): an absorbed subtraction leaves C alone | E92's and E88's emulation, `make_e88.emulate_launch`, round-to-nearest, checked over four ops by `make_e92.self_check` | 4f800000 | 4f800000 |
| **the manual's** | "added with no intermediate rounding", and the final sum rounds toward zero: exact sum, then truncate | ET Programmer's Reference Manual §9.3.3, TensorFMA16A32; [`manual_e104.c`](manual_e104.c) | 4f7fffe9 | 4f800000 |
| **the RTL's** | each aligned term truncates separately; a negated term that lost bits gets no carry-in, so any absorbed product that subtracts takes one unit off the sum, even if its partner adds | `vpu_tensorfma.v:692-693`, `txfma_c2.v:309-310`, `txfma_f4.v:82-85`; `accum.c`, `fp16_model.py`, the fixed bench | 4f7fffe9 | 4f7fffe9 |

fp32 rounds to nearest (frm, which the firmware clears; `vpu_tensorfma.v`, `kernel.c:636`), so its manual and RTL readings are one, and its frozen designs stand unless a tile's near-identical operands make fl(n S) wrong (`fp32.bj_6`, `pm_pi`, the fault's Part 1).

If either moving reading is right, `fp16.h_sub`'s two halves part by 12% by op 200,000, one lane's result bus swings about a dozen bits on every micro-op, and E92's fp16 watts stand while its reading goes. E104 reads C back at n = 2, 16, 1,000, 199,874 and 541,999, and it builds the fp16 designs E92 lacked: one that truly holds under any of the three readings (`z_`), one whose halves stay together while the adder swings (`u_sub`), and a moving-sum pair (`m_h_sub`, `w_h_add`).

## What the watts say under each reading

The bench's model M4, frozen before E95 and tested blind in E103 (0.314 W rms over 23 float tiles built to break it): D = rate × (k_core Δ(multiplier + adder and normaliser nets) + k_bus Δ(lane operand bus nets) + k_rest Δ(other nets) + a Δ(clocked register bits)), fitted on E78's 33 float kinds alone, card 1's scale 0.9722 ± 0.0019 from E82. **The bench's counts run on real sums, so M4 is the RTL's reading.** No coefficient is refitted here; `make_predictions_e104.py` recomputes them and asserts they equal `e103/selection.json`. E92's frozen reading is carried to this card by the same 0.9722, as an estimate.

| contrast | **E92's frozen reading** | **M4 on the RTL's sums** |
|---|---:|---:|
| fp16 `h_sub` − `u_sub`: the two halves' swing on the result path | **0.44 W** (1/15 of the alternations) | **3.27 ± 1.44 W** |
| fp16 `u_sub` − `h_add`: the adder swinging, the halves together | 6.18 W | 3.72 ± 0.51 W |
| fp16 `z_sub` − `z_add`: the alternation on zero products, the sum truly held and the adder's output still | 6.62 W | 3.58 ± 0.51 W |
| fp16 moving premium, `m_h_sub` − `w_h_add` | 1.552 × fp32's: 4.20 W | 2.65 ± 0.88 W |

**A trap I found at the desk, so it is in the design:** a zero product does not reproduce an absorbed one, even on fp32, where both sums hold. M4 puts fp32's `h_sub` − `h_add` at 3.99 W and `z_sub` − `z_add` at 2.56 W, because an absorbed subtraction also swings the adder's output (C − ε, then rounded back): the bench counts 698,688 more nets in the adder and normaliser on `fp32.h_sub` than on `fp32.z_sub`, none in the result stage. So `z_` measures the inverted operand alone. `u_sub` is what separates E92's premium from the result path: the same adder swing as `h_sub` on 14/15 of the micro-ops, the two halves meeting at the end of every op.

## Decision rules

The read-back rules need no pass. The power rules are on the primary reduction, and every verdict is also computed at the arms; a verdict that moves is marked arm-sensitive. **Fewer than 2 kept passes: every power rule NOT SCORED.** Contrast intervals are t(0.975, n − 1) over passes, paired within a pass.
