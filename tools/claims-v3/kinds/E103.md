# E103: a blind test built to break the bench's power model (aifoundry1 card 1, 600 MHz)

*Written Sun 1 Nov 2026 (week 44), Q132 (lane `desk-rtl`, label `E103`), before the claim, before any launch and before any E103 tile had run on any card. Item 6 of the kinds page's section 11: "a power model that predicts every kind". The frozen numbers are [`predictions-e103.json`](predictions-e103.json), the scorer `score_e103.py`, the self-test `selftest_e103.py`, all in this commit.*

## The question

The RTL bench of Q132(b) counts what each tile switches. Fitted on E78's 33 float kinds alone, a few constants turn those counts into watts, and they have done well on tiles nobody built to hurt them. **E103 builds tiles to hurt them.** From a pool of 215 candidates it takes the 30 where the bench's model and the simple predictors disagree most, and it reads them on another card. If the model is only a good curve through the kinds E78 happened to have, it fails here. If it beats the simple predictors here, the counts are carrying what they claim to carry.

## The model under test, frozen

**Floats, M4** (`docs/reports/data/2026-11-01-q132-rtl-bench/predict_m4.py`, committed `b995463e`): D = rate × (k_core × Δ(multiplier, adder and normaliser nets) + k_bus × Δ(lane operand bus nets) + k_rest × Δ(other nets) + a × Δ(clocked register bits)), each over the type's zeros.

| constant | value | ± (jackknife) |
|---|---:|---:|
| k_core | 1.624 fJ | 0.033 |
| k_bus | 7.533 fJ | 0.420 |
| k_rest | 2.222 fJ | 0.099 |
| a | 1.066 fJ | 0.009 |

Fitted on E78's 33 fp32 and fp16 kinds and on nothing else: fit rms 0.136 W, leave-one-out 0.155 W.

**int8, I3:** its own terms, because its multiply and adds are behavioural RTL with no nets inside them. k_nets 3.092 fJ, k_bus 16.737 fJ, a 1.795 fJ, on E78's 15 int8 kinds alone: fit rms 0.181 W, leave-one-out 0.248 W. These are fitting constants, not comparable with the float ones.

**Card 1's scale, from E82 alone, in E103's own frame** (E82 with its dips removed, `posthoc/e82-nodips.json`, through the origin over that family's kinds): **floats 0.9722 ± 0.0019, int8 0.9721 ± 0.0103.** Nothing else moves between the cards. Out of sample on this card, already read: floats E88 0.229 W, E94 0.176 W; int8 E88 0.365 W, E94 0.295 W.

**Each tile's interval** is t(0.975, n − 2) × √(s² + x′Cx + (p·se_scale)² + m²): s the rms of errors already read on this card (0.176 W over 88 floats, 0.255 W over 30 int8), C the coefficients' jackknife covariance, m the tile's standard error over three launch points.

**The form was chosen after reading E78, E82, E86, E88, E90, E92, E94 and the bit ladders** (`docs/reports/data/2026-11-01-q132-rtl-bench/README.md` § 11), so those do not test it. E95 does, by the clock (`b995463e` precedes its data), and E103 does.

**What I already know is weak, and where E103 pushes on it** (README §6): the A side's prediction runs high by 15–20%; E60's order pair and high-toggle ladders with a busy partner run high by about 13%; gated kinds ran 0.17–0.65 W low under the first model, M2; int8 has no nets inside its arithmetic.

## The tiles

[`make_e103_pool.py`](make_e103_pool.py) writes **215 candidates**, each a pure function of its name: sign structures on moving sums, near-cancelling sums, exponent spread, truncated mantissas, mantissa bits in one position on one side, order of a fixed multiset, gate share with operands moving or still, −0.0 and denormal words, fp16's two elements, combinations of these, and int8's widths, sides, gates and orders. Every candidate runs through the bench at three launch points and **passes the functional check**, and through Q130(n)'s predictor and E92's flop-price refit ([`e103/pool-predictions.json`](e103/pool-predictions.json)).

[`select_e103.py`](select_e103.py) fixes the choice with no measurement of any tile, because none exists: inside each family of each format it takes the tile with the largest disagreement, max(|M4 − Q130(n)|, |M4 − flop price|), at most one per design stem. **Exceptions, all stated:** `fp32.s_bhalf_alt` is forced, so that a moving-sum alternation on fresh draws is in the set, where E92's primary could not resolve one (0.41 ± 3.19 W); and one gated tile in each float format is chosen by |M4 − M2| instead, because that tile tests the bus's own price. 13 fp32, 10 fp16, 7 int8. Between them, frozen: over the float tiles M4 and Q130(n) differ by an rms of 3.81 W (2.58 W without `fp32.z_dn_ab`), M4 and the flop price by 1.80 W (0.99 W without it), and M4 and M2 by 0.38 W; over the int8 tiles M4's own form and Q130(n) differ by 0.94 W.

## Decision rules

All on the primary reduction. Every verdict is also computed at the arms (dips kept; E78's pass gates opened; the leakage arms), and a verdict that moves is marked arm-sensitive. **Fewer than 2 kept passes: every rule NOT SCORED.** A tile with fewer than 2 kept passes is left out and named. An interval on a gap is a bootstrap over tiles, 10,000 draws, seed 10300.

| rule | what | threshold | why this value, before the data | if it fails |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| **E103-R1** | **the model's accuracy on the 23 float tiles:** rms(D − M4) | **PASS ≤ 0.60 W; FAIL > 1.00 W**; else INCONCLUSIVE | out of sample on this card it has read 0.18–0.23 W, and these tiles are chosen to hurt it, so three times that is still a working model; 1 W is what E60's worst ladder already reached, and about what the flop price disagrees with it by | the model does not predict tiles built to break it: state where |
| **E103-R2** | **it beats the named null, Q130(n)'s predictor, on those tiles:** rms(Q130(n)) − rms(M4) | PASS if the 95% interval is above 0; FAIL if below; else INCONCLUSIVE | Q130(n) was the best predictor before the bench, and it was never fitted to anything E103 reads. It is the model M4 must beat. The gap without Q130(n)'s worst tile is reported beside, so one tile cannot decide it silently | the counts add nothing a tile's bit statistics do not |
| **E103-R3** | it beats E92's flop-price refit on those tiles | the same | that refit saw E92's designs, and M4 did not: a hard comparator | the bench is no better than one extra stream count |
| **E103-R4** | **int8's own terms on the 7 int8 tiles:** rms(D − I3) | **PASS ≤ 0.40 W; FAIL > 0.80 W**; else INCONCLUSIVE | I3 has read 0.24–0.36 W out of sample | int8 has no working model: state it |
| **E103-R5** | I3 beats Q130(n) on those tiles | as R2 | | |
| **E103-R6** | **the bus price earns its term:** rms(M2) − rms(M4) on the float tiles | as R2 | M4 has two more constants than M2, chosen after reading E94 | keep M2 |
| **E103-R7** | **the frozen intervals:** the share of all 30 tiles whose D lies inside its 95% interval | **PASS ≥ 80%; FAIL < 50%**; else INCONCLUSIVE | the pool behind s is not adversarial, so 95% will not hold here; below 50% the intervals mean nothing on new designs | the intervals may not be quoted for a new kind of tile |
| **E103-R8** | **the A side's named weak spot:** mean (D − T)/(M4 − T) over the float `h_a_` and `o_a_` tiles, T M4's frozen clock term, 95% over tiles | **PERSISTS** if the upper bound is below 0.95; **GONE** if the interval lies in [0.95, 1.05]; else UNRESOLVED | E67 read 0.84 and E70's A ladder 0.80 on this ratio | it names the weak spot; it does not decide R1 |
| **E103-R9** | **gated kinds' named weak spot:** mean D − M4 over float tiles with the gate closing at least 10% of slots, 95% over tiles | **PASS** if inside ±0.30 W; **FAIL** if it excludes ±0.30 W; else INCONCLUSIVE | M2 ran 0.17–0.65 W low here, and the bus term was added for this | the bus term does not carry what it was added for |
| **E103-S1** | the session tie against E82, dips removed | `fp32.randn` 24.00, `fp16.randn` 21.49, `anchor_pi` 8.14 ±0.50 W; `int8.full` 6.84 ±0.25 W | E94's bands | a card that moved: report, and read R1 with the shift named |

**What I expect, frozen so it can be wrong:** R1 PASS near 0.5 W, with the largest misses on the A side and `c_near_rand`; R2 PASS; R3 PASS, narrowly, without the denormal tile; R4 INCONCLUSIVE, with `i_w2_2` and `i_w8_1` the misses, because small int8 values toggle nets the behavioural multiplier does not have; R6 PASS; R7 INCONCLUSIVE or PASS; R8 PERSISTS; R9 PASS.

**The statement the page gets, decided now.** R1 and R2 both PASS: "fitted on 33 kinds on one card, the bench's model predicted 23 float tiles built to break it on another card to an rms of X W, against Y W for the best simple predictor." R1 FAIL: "it does not", with the tiles that broke it. Any other outcome is stated as its verdicts. **Only tiles that ran are scored. No tile is dropped, re-drawn or added after the first pass, and the model does not change.**

## Self-test

`selftest_e103.py` writes synthetic card-1 passes with E78's own generator after installing E103's items and order (card 1's 65/65 reads, 499 mV), injects card 1's meter artifact, then runs `reduce_e103.py` and `score_e103.py` on three worlds: **model** (every tile at M4 or I3: R1, R2, R3, R4, R7, R9 PASS, R8 GONE, S1 PASS), **null** (every tile at Q130(n): R1 and R2 FAIL), **aside** (the A side's switching 20% low and the gated tiles 0.6 W high: R8 PERSISTS, R9 FAIL), and checks that two kept passes score and one does not. [`selftest_e103.txt`](selftest_e103.txt): 20 of 20. Nothing there is a measurement.

## Card budget

**About 25 card-minutes**, three passes of 50 runs, each pass about 480 s by E94's 9.2 s a run. `queue_e103.sh` runs with `E103_BUDGET_S=1620`, `E103_FIRST_S=500`: a pass starts only if its projected end is inside the budget and before the fixed deadline, 07:00 PST; a pass in flight is never cut. The whole session must end by 07:15 PST. It starts only after lane `a1c1-e102-bits` releases card 1, and never with another of our sessions on aifoundry1.

## How to run it (for this lane)

On the Intel, after this commit is on origin/main, copy E103's files only to aifoundry1's `~/nekko/tools/claims-v3/kinds/`. On aifoundry1, from `~/nekko`:

```bash
bash tools/claims-v3/kinds/run_e103.sh setup      # no device access: hashes, tiles, plan, dry run
# -> read #community-lab for an open claim, then post: "using aifoundry1 card 1 for 1 h, until ~HH:MM <zone>"
bash tools/claims-v3/kinds/run_e103.sh smoke      # pass 10301: eight 2 s runs; cycles/op 546 / 546 / 318, 600 MHz, 65/65 reads
bash tools/claims-v3/kinds/run_e103.sh go 10310 3 # detached; the budget rule decides
bash tools/claims-v3/kinds/run_e103.sh status     # bounded checks only
```

Then copy `build/claims-v3/aifoundry1-c1/kinds/p1031*`, `kinds-smoke/p10301` and `build/e103-*.log` to the mini or the Intel and run `PY=python3 nice -n 10 bash tools/claims-v3/kinds/collect_e103.sh 'p103[1-9][0-9]' <copy>`; copy `build/e103-out/2026-11-01-e103-model-blind-test/` to `docs/reports/data/`, write its README (results first; registered against happened; departures dated), and release the claim by editing it.
