# E106: a random sign's ~5 W on one card: `signs` and `ones` beside held tiles with both operands' signs moving, read back (aifoundry1 card 1, 600 MHz)

*Written Sun 1 Nov 2026 (week 44), Q132 (lane `a1c1-e104-cread`, label `E106`), before the claim, before any launch and before any new E106 tile had run on any card. It answers the line [E104's README](../../../docs/reports/data/2026-11-01-e104-c-readback/README.md) left on item 2 of the kinds page: "the split of a random sign's ~5 W is an estimate by subtraction across two cards; fp16 has no held tile with both operands' signs moving". Frozen numbers: `predictions-e106.json`; tiles and every expected accumulator: `e106/e106.json`; scorer: `score_e106.py`, all in this commit.*

## The question

A random ±1 costs about 5 W more than all +1, in both formats (E90, E92). How much of that is there when the running sum holds? E92 measured the step on aifoundry3, E88 and E104 the held part on aifoundry1 card 1, so every share so far crosses two cards through a scale. And fp16's only held tile with random signs, E104's `z_s`, keeps A's signs still. **E106 runs the step and the held tiles on one card, in one session, paired within each pass, and reads every accumulator back.**

## The tiles

[`make_e106.py`](make_e106.py) writes them; E78's and E92's run byte for byte. New, all fp16, all holding C on the RTL's arithmetic, the manual's sentence and fl(n S) alike, so a read-back miss means the tile is not what it is called:

| tile | design | C | eff_sub |
|---|---|---|---|
| `x_c` | A words (1, 1), B words (lo, hi) = (1, −1), k-group-0 (2, 2) × (4, 4): each micro-op's two products cancel exactly | +16 n | fixed: `x_s`'s baseline |
| `x_s` | `x_c` with each A word's sign and each B word's sign drawn at random at k-group ≥ 1 (E104's `x_` design, with random signs) | +16 n | both flip, in opposite phase, on nonzero aligned operands |
| `z_ss` | E92's `fp16.s_abs`'s B words (random ±1 in each half), A words at random among the raw-nonzero zeros (−0, −0), (+0, −0), (−0, +0) at k-group ≥ 1, `c_abs`'s seed | n × 2^31 | both flip independently, on zero products; A's sign bits move |

Beside them: `fp16.c_abs` (`z_ss`'s baseline), `fp16.signs`, `fp16.ones`; `fp32.signs`, `fp32.ones`, `fp32.c_abs`, and `fp32.s_abs`, which already holds with both operands' signs moving (read back in E104). fp32 needs no new tile: it has one product, and its zero words cannot carry A's signs.

`z_ss` is the nearer twin of `signs`: its two `eff_sub` flip independently, as `signs`' do. `x_s`'s flip in opposite phase, so its aligned operands partly undo each other in the carry-save adder; the bench says so (below). The (+0, +0) word cannot be used, because the raw-word gate would close the lane, so `z_ss`'s A signs are not uniform (3 of 4 patterns).

## Decision rules

The read-back rule needs no pass. The power rules are on the primary reduction, and every verdict is also computed at the arms; a verdict that moves is marked arm-sensitive. **Fewer than 2 kept passes: every power rule NOT SCORED.** A share is held / step, per pass, paired; its interval is t(0.975, n − 1) over passes.

| rule | what | verdict | why, and how it can fail |
|---|---|---|---|
| **E106-R1** | **every tile at every length, every word of all 1,024 minions**, against `e106.json` | **PASS** if every read equals it; **FAIL** at any miss; a minion that disagrees reads UNREAD | the three readings coincide on these tiles (asserted at the desk). A miss means a held tile does not hold on silicon, and then its share means nothing |
| **E106-R2** | **the step on one card:** `signs` − `ones` in each format, against E92's aifoundry3 value × 0.9722: fp32 4.88 W, fp16 4.83 W | **PASS** if both means lie within ±1 W; else **FAIL** | E104's shares rest on this carry. M4 says 4.80 and 4.85 W |
| **E106-R3** | **fp32's held share on one card:** (`s_abs` − `c_abs`) / (`signs` − `ones`) | **ABOUT HALF** if the interval lies inside [0.35, 0.65]; **MOST** if it lies above 0.65; **LITTLE** if below 0.35; else INCONCLUSIVE | across two cards it read 0.50. M4 says 0.55 |
| **E106-R4** | **fp16's held share, both operands' signs moving:** (`z_ss` − `c_abs`) / (`signs` − `ones`) | **ABOUT HALF** inside [0.30, 0.70]; **MOST** above 0.70; **LITTLE** below 0.30; else INCONCLUSIVE | E92's withdrawn reading was 0.92, which would read MOST. E104's `z_s`, A's signs still, read 0.45 across two cards. M4 says 0.46 |
| **E106-R5** | **the same with nonzero aligned operands:** (`x_s` − `x_c`) / (`signs` − `ones`) | the same bands | the second design, so the held part does not rest on zero products alone. M4 says 0.22, because `x_s`'s two `eff_sub` flip in opposite phase, so a LITTLE here is what the bench expects and it would make the held part a bracket, not a number |
| **E106-R6** | **the two formats:** fp16's `z_ss` share less fp32's `s_abs` share, per pass | **ALIKE** if the interval lies inside ±0.15; **DIFFER** if it lies outside; else INCONCLUSIVE | E104: "similar, not shown equal". M4 says -0.09 |
| **E106-R7** | **the bench's model on E106's 10 float tiles:** rms(D − M4) | **PASS** ≤ 0.60 W; **FAIL** > 1.00 W; else INCONCLUSIVE | E103's and E104's line |
| **E106-S1** | the session tie against E82, dips removed | `fp32.randn` 24.00, `fp16.randn` 21.49, `anchor_pi` 8.14 ±0.50 W; `int8.full` 6.84 ±0.25 W | E94's, E103's and E104's bands |

Reported, no rule: `signs` − `ones` less each held part, per pass, which is the rest; `z_ss` − `c_abs` beside E104's `z_s` − `c_abs`, what A's signs add; the rail for every contrast; every contrast's M4 miss.

**What I expect, frozen so it can be wrong:** R1 PASS; R2 PASS; R3 ABOUT HALF; R4 ABOUT HALF near 0.46; R5 LITTLE or INCONCLUSIVE near 0.22; R6 ALIKE or INCONCLUSIVE; R7 PASS; S1 PASS.

**The statement decided now.** R3 and R4 both ABOUT HALF: "on one card, about half of a random sign's ~5 W is there with the sum read back as held, in both formats"; item 2's last line goes, with the split measured on designed tiles, and R5 says how far it depends on the design. R4 MOST: fp16 really is unlike fp32, E104's floor was too low, and E92's 92% was right for a reason it did not know. R4 or R3 LITTLE: less of the step is the adder than E104 estimated. R2 FAIL: the carry across cards was wrong, and E104's shares need rewriting with these steps.

## Self-test

`selftest_e106.py` writes synthetic card-1 passes with E78's own generator after installing E106's items and order (card 1's 65/65 reads, 499 mV), injects card 1's meter artifact, and runs `reduce_e106.py` and `score_e106.py` with a synthetic read-back on two worlds: **half** (every held share 0.50) and **most** (fp16's 0.92, fp32's 0.20, fp32's step 2 W off). It also checks that a tile's words off reads R1 FAIL, one minion off reads UNREAD, and one kept pass scores no power rule. 21 of 21 checks pass; 10 tiles.

## Card budget

**About 20 card-minutes** where the request said about 15: the smoke, about 1 min; the read-back, under 1 min; **four passes** of 30 runs at about 8.7 s a run, about 290 s each. Three would be 15, but E104's primary lost a pass to a registered gate on `int8.zeros` and fell to two passes, t = 12.7, and these rules are bands on a share: at n = 2 they would read INCONCLUSIVE on noise. `queue_e106.sh` runs with `E106_BUDGET_S=1250`, `E106_FIRST_S=310`: a pass starts only if its projected end is inside the budget and before 10:30 PST. Two kept passes are enough to score.

## How to run it (for this lane)

On the Intel, after this commit is on origin/main, copy E106's files only to aifoundry1's `~/nekko/tools/claims-v3/kinds/`. On aifoundry1, from `~/nekko`:

```bash
bash tools/claims-v3/kinds/run_e106.sh setup      # no device access: hashes, tiles, plan, dry runs
# -> look: et-who, the card lock; read #community-lab for an open claim, then post the one line
bash tools/claims-v3/kinds/run_e106.sh smoke      # pass 10601: eight 2 s runs; cycles/op 546, 600 MHz, 65/65 reads
bash tools/claims-v3/kinds/run_e106.sh readback   # pass 10602: every read
bash tools/claims-v3/kinds/run_e106.sh go 10610 4 # detached; the budget rule decides
bash tools/claims-v3/kinds/run_e106.sh status     # bounded checks only
```

Then collect off the box, on the mini or the Intel, never on aifoundry1 while a card runs, and write the README results-first in `docs/reports/data/2026-11-01-e106-held-signs/`.
