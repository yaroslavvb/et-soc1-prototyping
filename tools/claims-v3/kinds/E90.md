# E90: sign patterns and tile-to-tile spread (Q130, aifoundry3 card 0, 600 MHz)

*Pre-registered Sat 31 Oct 2026 (week 44), on the Intel, before the Discord claim and the first launch, in Q130's
evening round (optional lane; the Q130 critic's items 6 and 13). The frozen numbers are in
[`predictions-e90.json`](predictions-e90.json), written by [`make_predictions_e90.py`](make_predictions_e90.py) from
the committed sources it names (every number read from its file, never retyped) and committed with this file. Any
change after this commit goes in the Amendments section at the end, dated, before the data it touches; no band moves.
Card: **aifoundry3 card 0 only**, 600 MHz, ~522 mV, TDP 0 and threshold 65 °C as read (never set). E90 writes no
knob: no governor move, no TDP, threshold or clock set, no reset, no `--reset-ms`.*

**The questions.**

1. **Sign patterns** (critic item 6). Of E78's 51 kinds only int8's ±64 is a random-sign kind. E64's fp32 `signs`
   (±1.0 both sides) cost 13.10 W over zeros on aifoundry2, about 4.5 W above a constant, while the sign bit alone,
   flipped at every read, costs 0.563 W on the B side (E65 `bit_31`) and 0.561 W on the A side (E67 `abit_31`). Is a
   random sign priced by the sign bits it flips, or does it cost far more (the result path: products and running sums
   that change sign)? E90 measures random signs at a fixed magnitude (±1, and ±pi with a full mantissa) against the
   same constant, and E78's random-normal tile against **the same magnitudes with every sign bit cleared or set**.
2. **Tile-to-tile spread** (critic item 13; E78 C5). Every E78 interval is one fixed tile's repeatability. How far do
   fresh draws of the same rule move? E90 runs three new draws each of the three rules the critic names — fp32
   `sparse50`, fp16 `nn_like`, fp16 `uniform01` — beside E78's committed tile of each, in every pass.

E78's `randn` tiles (the anchors) flip 1,104 A and 1,778 B sign bits per fp32 op and 1,872 / 4,226 per fp16 op; their
magnitude bits are **identical** to `abs_randn` and `negabs_randn`. With every sign the same, a product's sign never
changes: `abs_randn` and `negabs_randn` give the same products (− × − = +), so they differ only in a static set bit.
The redraw seeds are `int.from_bytes(sha256("E90/<type>/<kind>/draw<r>")[:8], "little")`, recorded per tile in
e90.json with the rule's replicate band (kinds.json `replicates`, 400 draws). All nine draws lie inside their rule's
2.5–97.5% band; E78's committed `fp16.nn_like` and `fp16.uniform01` tiles do not (E78 C5).

## The frozen predictions

**The bits model** (the lane brief: the sign kinds are predicted from the per-bit sign prices of E65/E67/E79). A sign
kind's step over its reference is the price of the sign bits it flips:
`P_bits = dW_B(item) × flips_B(tile)/flips_B(item) + dW_A(item) × flips_A(tile)/flips_A(item)`, where the items flip
their sign bit at every designed read (fp32: E65 `bit_31` dW 0.5629 W at 4,096 B flips/op, E67 `abit_31` 0.5607 W at
2,048 A flips/op; fp16: E79's fp16 B jg row 15, 0.313 W at 8,192 — a bound — and fp16 A row 15, 0.327 W at 4,096) and
the flips are counted by E65/E67/E79's rule (`make_e90.sign_flips_per_op`). E67's post-hoc clean-pass price (0.2970
W) is reported as an arm.

| step (paired per pass) | rule | P_bits | threshold (2 × P_bits) | E67 clean arm | Q130(n)'s predictor, implied step |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| `fp32.signs` − `fp32.ones` | R1 | **0.592 W** | 1.184 W | 0.454 | 12.203 − 8.257 ≈ 3.9 W |
| `fp16.signs` − `fp16.ones` | R1 | **0.317 W** | 0.634 W | | 13.721 − 8.448 ≈ 5.3 W |
| `fp32.pm_pi` − `anchor_pi` | R1 | **0.680 W** (0.570 sign bits + E78's file-vs-built-in pi offset 0.110) | 1.359 W | 0.548 | 15.753 − 8.360 ≈ 7.4 W |
| `fp32.randn` − `fp32.abs_randn` | R2 | **0.547 W** | 1.093 W | 0.404 | ≈ 0.3 W |
| `fp16.randn` − `fp16.abs_randn` | R2 | **0.311 W** | 0.622 W | | ≈ 0.9 W |
| `fp32.negabs_randn` − `fp32.abs_randn` | R3 | **0** | band ±0.30 W | | 0 |
| `fp16.negabs_randn` − `fp16.abs_randn` | R3 | **0** | band ±0.30 W | | 0 |

The two models disagree by a factor of 5–10 on the fixed-magnitude steps: the bits model says a random sign costs
its sign bits (about 0.3–0.7 W), Q130(n)'s predictor (fitted on E78's 51 kinds, run unchanged on each tile:
`predictions-e90.json` › `tiles.*.q130n_predictor`) and E64's aifoundry2 cell say several watts. **The redraws** are
predicted, as the brief says, by E78's measured value of their rule (fp32 sparse50 7.302 W, fp16 nn_like 17.602 W,
fp16 uniform01 19.426 W), with the spread unknown; Q130(n)'s predictor's deltas against the committed tile are frozen
beside them: fp32 sparse50 +0.779 / +0.884 / +0.191 W, fp16 nn_like −1.072 / −1.188 / −1.525 W, fp16 uniform01
−0.111 / −0.035 / −0.034 W. int8.signs has no bits prediction (its sign change flips bits 1–7, and E79 has no int8 B
price list that alternates); Q130(n)'s point is 5.605 W [4.718, 6.493].

## Decision rules

All on D at the primary leakage slope, paired per pass; each verdict at the three arms is reported beside, and a
verdict that moves is marked slope-sensitive. 95% intervals are t(0.975, n−1) × SE over kept passes. **Fewer than 3
kept passes in the primary reduction: every rule is scored on the registered dips-removed arm (E86's posthoc_dips
method: board-reading samples 5–20 W low just after a launch boundary removed, then E78's reduction), labelled as
that arm; fewer than 3 there too: NOT SCORED.**

| | rule | threshold | why this value, before the data |
|---|---|---|---|
| **E90-R1** fixed magnitude | each step `fp32.signs − fp32.ones`, `fp16.signs − fp16.ones`, `fp32.pm_pi − anchor_pi`: PASS if its 95% upper bound ≤ 2 × P_bits, FAIL if its lower bound > 2 × P_bits, else INCONCLUSIVE; R1 passes if all three pass, fails if any fails | 2 × P_bits | the sign bits' price, with a factor 2 for the price lists' own spread (E67's A price is 0.30–0.56 W by pass set; E79's fp16 B sign price is a bound). A random sign changes the sign of each product and each running sum; if that path costs watts, the step is several times P_bits (E64; Q130(n)) |
| **E90-R2** random magnitudes | each step `t.randn − t.abs_randn`, fp32 and fp16, the same three-way test | 2 × P_bits | the same tile with and without random signs: the cleanest sign-only contrast on random data |
| **E90-R3** static sign bit | each step `t.negabs_randn − t.abs_randn`, fp32 and fp16: PASS if its 95% interval lies inside ±0.30 W, FAIL if entirely outside, else INCONCLUSIVE | ±0.30 W | E78-R3f's band for a static set bit; the products are identical, so only the operand bit differs |
| **E90-R4** representative tile | per rule, the mean over its three fresh draws of D(draw) − D(committed tile): PASS if its 95% interval lies inside ±0.50 W, FAIL if entirely outside, else INCONCLUSIVE; R4 passes if all three pass | ±0.50 W | the brief's prediction (E78's value of the rule) at E86-R5's session-tie scale for fp32/fp16 cells |
| **E90-R5** the predictor tracks draws | over the nine draws, measured D(draw) − D(committed) against Q130(n)'s frozen delta: PASS if rms ≤ 0.50 W and no draw with a predicted \|delta\| ≥ 0.30 W is resolved (95%) on the other side of zero; FAIL if rms > 1.00 W or any such draw is; else INCONCLUSIVE | rms 0.50 / 1.00 W | does the tile's own gated share and bit counts say where a draw lands? |
| **E90-S1** session tie (reported) | D of fp32/fp16 randn, ones, anchor_pi, the three committed redraw tiles within ±0.50 W of E78's, int8 full within ±0.25 W | E86-R5's bands | a failure means the session moved, not a model |
| **E90-S2** predictor intervals | count of the 17 E90 tiles inside Q130(n)'s frozen 95% interval | ≥ 14 of 17 | E86-R1's spirit on new kinds |

**Reported, never a verdict:** for every E90 tile and E78 kind, D, S, pJ per op / MAC from S and D, ΔT; each step
in W and per flipped sign bit (fJ); the tile spread per rule: the SD over its four tiles of the pass-mean D against
one tile's between-pass SD, and the tile variance component max(0, Var_tiles − σ²_pass/n); int8.signs against E78's
int8 `ones` (1.561 W), `neg_ones` (1.628 W) and `highbit` (3.233 W); E78-R5's checks; every gate failure.
**Failures are reported as failures.** No band moves after this commit.

**What the rules cannot say.** Three passes is a small sample (t = 4.30); the bits model assumes per-bit prices
measured with the other side constant add up in random data; fp16's B sign price is a bound; a step that beats the
bits model says something beyond the operand sign bits costs power, not which stage (the RTL bench that could split
operand from accumulate toggles returns no correct multiply-add, Q130(b)). The redraws are three draws of three
rules; their spread estimate has 2 degrees of freedom per rule. One card, one clock.

## Self-test

`selftest_e90.py` writes three synthetic passes with E78's own generator (`selftest_e78.make_pass`) after installing
E90's items and order, every E90 tile at its frozen prediction, and runs `reduce_e90.py`: 126 of 126 runs kept, every
tile and reference recovered within 0.106 W, every dumped tile passes the sha256 gate
([`selftest_e90.txt`](selftest_e90.txt)). Nothing it prints is data. The scorer (`score_e90.py`) implements the
rules above as written here; it is written after this commit and before any pass is read, and it changes no rule.

## Card budget

**At most 30 card-minutes, claim to release**, including the smoke, the configuration reads and the heating gates.
Expected about 28: the smoke ~1.5 min, then passes of ~8.3 min (44 runs at E86's measured ~10 s a run plus the
block's ~55 s) with 15 s between them. `queue_e90.sh` starts a pass only if its projected end (the longest measured
pass; 500 s before the first one ends) is inside **26** card-minutes of the queue's start and before **22:25 PDT**;
a pass in flight is never cut. No session starts after 21:45 PDT; every run ends by 22:30.

## How to run it (for this lane)

On the Intel, after this commit is on origin/main: `rsync -a --exclude __pycache__ tools/claims-v3/kinds/
aifoundry3:aifoundry/tools/claims-v3/kinds/`. On aifoundry3, from `~/aifoundry`: `bash tools/claims-v3/kinds/run_e90.sh
setup` (no device access), then the claim, then one read-only `ettelem config` under the lock, then `run_e90.sh smoke`,
`run_e90.sh go 9010 3`, `run_e90.sh status`. Collect `p90*` and `kinds-smoke/p9001` to the Intel and run
`PY=/usr/bin/python3 nice -n 10 bash tools/claims-v3/kinds/collect_e90.sh 'p90[1-9][0-9]' <copy>`; the data go to
`docs/reports/data/2026-10-31-e90-signs-spread/`.
