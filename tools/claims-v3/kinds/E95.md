# E95: more real tensors' energy, and exact zeros letting the gate act (Q132, aifoundry3 card 0, 600 MHz)

*Pre-registered Sun 1 Nov 2026 (week 44), on the Intel, before the Discord claim and the first launch, in Q132's run (item 3
of the kinds page's section 11). **Every tile's predictions and the claims C1–C12 are Q132(r)'s, frozen before any card ran
these tiles**: [`real2/predictions-real2.json`](real2/predictions-real2.json), commit `6ae4c3d5`, sha256 `0957e0c295990ac95cded56430697fb0683c9cf451d90f6943c8ff8d6a0b021a`. E95
adds no prediction. What it adds is frozen in [`predictions-e95.json`](predictions-e95.json), written by
[`make_predictions_e95.py`](make_predictions_e95.py) from the committed sources it names (every number read from its file,
never retyped): the plan, the harness's sha256 and the session tie's reference cells. Any change after this commit goes in the
Amendments section at the end, dated, before the data it touches; no band moves. Card: **aifoundry3 card 0 only**, 600 MHz,
~522 mV, TDP 0 and threshold 65 °C as read (never set). E95 writes no knob: no governor move, no TDP, threshold or clock set, no
reset, no `--reset-ms`.*

**The question.** E86 measured three windows of one model, GPT-2 small: real fp32 and fp16 cost 97–99.6% of random data, real int8
78–89% of uniform random int8, and no 32-bit operand word was zero, so the zero gate never fired. Q132(r) cut thirty new tiles from
six public models. E95 runs them **beside E78's anchors in every pass**, on E78's card, clock, protocol, code and reduction, and asks:

1. Do real fp32 and fp16 numbers with no exact zeros stay within 2% of random in other models (GPT-2's other layers, BERT, ViT-B/16)? (C1, C2)
2. When a ReLU network hands the gate exact zeros, does power follow the live share, in fp32, in fp16, and in int8, where the same zeros
   cover fewer whole words? (C3a, C3b, C4, C5)
3. What does a true fp16 forward pass cost against rounding the fp32 activations? (C11)
4. How far do int8 calibrations move power: on a GELU layer with one huge activation, on E86's own window, and with asymmetric
   activations on a ReLU layer? (C6, C7, C8)
5. Two things nobody asked for: bf16-grid weights (C10), and a real layer whose words are 64% −0.0 (C9), the real-network case of what
   E93 tests on synthetic tiles on another card.

## Decision rules

**The claims are `claims.E95` in `predictions-real2.json`, and `score_e95.py` implements them as frozen, reading their tile lists, thresholds and predicted
numbers from that file.** Their conventions bind: all on D at the lane's primary leakage slope (`auto`, E78's estimator on E95's own runs); r(x) = per pass D(x) / D(ref), ref the same
pass's `fp32.randn`, `fp16.randn` or `int8.full` (its three runs' mean); intervals t(0.975, n − 1) × SE over kept passes; a difference paired within the pass. Between PASS and FAIL a
claim reads INCONCLUSIVE. Fewer than 3 kept passes: NOT SCORED. A claim short of its population is UNDER-RESOLVED, never PASS (a FAIL its resolved members already force stands). Each
verdict at the three arms (0.1994, 0.3803, 0) is reported beside, and one that moves is marked slope-sensitive (NOT SCORED and UNDER-RESOLVED aside).

| claim | says | PASS if | FAIL if | predicted |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| **C1** | no-zero fp32, fp16 within 2% of random in every model: cells GPT-2, BERT, ViT × fp32, fp16 | every cell's mean r interval lies inside [0.98, 1.02] | any cell's lies entirely outside | to E78's random: fp32 0.987–0.988, fp16 0.971–0.976; the predictor's own fit: 0.989–0.995. E86's fp16 attention tile read 0.971, so this can fail |
| **C2** | they sit below random on average | each format's mean r has its 95% upper bound < 1 | a lower bound > 1 | fp32 0.9875, fp16 0.9724 |
| **C3a** | a ReLU tile's power follows its live share (4 fp32, 3 fp16 tiles) | all 7 D's inside the gate law's interval | two or more outside | the table |
| **C3b** | D's least-squares slope on live share is about L | slope / L in [0.85, 1.25], both formats | either outside | from (a): fp32 0.984, fp16 0.963; known on E78's and E90's gated tiles: 1.039, 0.937 |
| **C4** | the same numbers cost more in fp16 than fp32, because a zero must fill the whole 32-bit word | r(fp16) − r(fp32), paired: lower bound > 0 on all three pairs | any upper bound < 0 | vgg16_fc2 0.28 → 0.40; resnet18_l3c1 0.55 → 0.60; t5small_l0_down 0.05 → 0.11 |
| **C5** | int8 ReLU tiles follow bits more than live share | |D − (a)| < |D − (c)| on both tiles | (c) nearer on both | `resnet18_l3c1` (a) 3.616, (c) 5.887 W; `vgg16_fc2` (a) 2.705, (c) 4.821 W |
| **C6** | the calibration is a lever on a GELU layer with an outlier: `gpt2_l11_down_p999` − `gpt2_l11_down_maxabs` | paired lower bound ≥ 2.0 W | upper bound < 2.0 W | +3.994 W |
| **C7** | E86's window ranks committed < per-token < p99.9, with p99.99 beside committed | per-token's, p99.9's lower bounds > 0 and p99.99's interval inside ±0.40 W | either upper bound < 0, or p99.99's interval entirely outside ±0.40 W | p9999 +0.041; per_token +0.171; p999 +0.244 W; INCONCLUSIVE is the likely outcome |
| **C8** | asymmetric activations show the gate nothing: `vgg16_fc2_asym` − `vgg16_fc2` | paired lower bound > 0.5 W | upper bound < 0 | +1.396 W |
| **C9** | −0.0 passes the gate: `fp32.r2_vit_l11_down`, 64% −0.0 words | D's lower bound > 11.909 W | D's upper bound ≤ 11.909 W | 20.423 W blind; 9.342 W if the gate took it |
| **C10** | bf16-grid weights cost clearly less: `fp32.r2_t5small_l0_up` | r's upper bound < 0.97 | lower bound ≥ 0.97 | r 0.9082 |

- **Every gated tile's distance from (a) and from (c)**, which is nearer, D ÷ (c), and a live lane's cost over random's, (D − g·G) / ((1 − g)·L), in all three formats, tier 2's and `gpt2_l11_down_maxabs` included. `fp32.r2_vit_l11_down`'s distance from 20.42 W and 9.34 W, and which it lands on.

**Failures are reported as failures**, with the measured value beside the band. No band moves after this commit.

**What the rules cannot say.** One tile a cell is a draw, and on a conv layer the draw matters: over 20 windows `resnet18_l2c1`'s gate spans 0.00–0.71 (real2 README, departure 6). C3's slopes rest on 3–4 tiles. Each C1 cell is one or two windows of one layer. The rows are real but the batch is ours (16 patches, tokens or images). Q130(n)'s predictor is a post-hoc fit, not a mechanism, and it prices B flips at 0, which E65 contradicts; a miss on T5's tile is a finding. int8's bus counts are provisional (the reconstructed TIMA stream); its gated fraction is not. C5's two predictions rest on few gated int8 misses. The law's L is a full-mantissa random kind. E95 is one card at one clock: speed under the governor is E96's, and another card's reading of −0.0 is E93's. A D says what a tile costs, not which stage spends it, and the RTL bench that could split that returns no correct multiply-add (Q130(b)). Four passes is a small sample (t = 3.18, or 4.30 with one set aside).

## Self-test

`selftest_e95.py` runs in two parts ([`selftest_e95.txt`](selftest_e95.txt)). Every claim's PASS, FAIL, INCONCLUSIVE and UNDER-RESOLVED branch on small tables of per-pass values, through the scorer's own functions and the frozen thresholds; fewer than 3 passes reads NOT SCORED. Then E78's own generator (`selftest_e78.make_pass`) writes four synthetic passes in E95's 53-run order for two worlds and `reduce_e95.py` and `score_e95.py` run on them: with the frozen (a) points as the truth, every tile and anchor comes back within 0.15 W, 212 of 212 runs are kept, every dumped tile passes the sha256 gate, and C12 reads 25 of 25; with gated tiles at the law, −0.0 gated and the other claims' effects reversed, C2, C5, C6, C7, C8, C9, C10 and C11 read FAIL and C9 lands on the gated reading. Nothing it prints is data. `block_e95.sh` also ran end to end at the desk in a dry run on a scratch tree with a stub binary: 55 launches, 30 on real2 tiles and 3 on E86's.

## Card budget

**At most 45 card-minutes, claim to release**, including the smoke, the configuration reads and the heating gates. Expected about 41: the smoke ~1 min, then passes of ~9.4 min (55 launches at E92's measured 10.25 s a launch, its block's overhead included) with 15 s between them. `queue_e95.sh` starts a pass only if its projected end (the longest measured pass; 600 s before the first one ends) is inside **41** card-minutes of the queue's start and before **06:45 PST**; a pass in flight is never cut. Every run ends by 07:15 PST.
