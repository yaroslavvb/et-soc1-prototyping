## 4. The readings stated here before any datum (where E193.md leaves a choice to the kit)

- **The sampler: the runner's own, `--sampler`, through E128's guard.** E193.md 4 allows either the lab session's sampler (`--telemetry`)
  or the runner's (`--sampler`), never both. The kit takes `--sampler` with E128's guard as the program: the runner then starts one
  sampler per runner call after its holder check and the card lock (before it opens the management node), exempts it by its process id
  (the guard execs the reader, so its process id is the reader's), and stops it with SIGTERM in every case. **This answers
  reg-e193's finding 4**: each runner call's telemetry file is started for that call (`<OUT>/telemetry.jsonl`), so the runner's reader,
  which reads its file from the start, sees only that call's own samples. One consequence, stated: the runner's first 20 s of samples
  (its idle reference) are read once for the reference and not through the live stop lines, as the runner as committed does.
  `--telemetry` and `writes_file` are therefore not on the session's path (section 5's audit says so).
- **The first card command** is the session's holder check (`et-who --check`), before the start config read: the cap counts from it,
  **its time stamped as the check starts** (before the two ps rules and `et-who`; verify-e193's finding 4: the kit's first form stamped it
  after `et-who` returned, up to its 30 s bound later).
- **The one ops node** (verify-e193's finding 3): after the holder check, the session counts `/dev/et*_ops` as the runner's
  `ops_nodes()` does and stops, no device opened, unless it reads exactly `/dev/et0_ops`; only then the start config read, with
  `ET_DEVICES=0` (the card the runner's `--device 0` names). The kit's first form made that read before any count, with `ET_DEVICES`
  unset, and left two nodes or none to the smoke's runner.
- **The deadline**: `--deadline` = the first card command + the cap (A 3,600 s, B 3,000 s) - 120 s, and never past the window's last end -
  180 s. After it no run starts; the run in flight (at most about 46 s: idle-down 30, the gap 5, the device process 11), the runner's
  clean-up (its sampler's stop, at most 10 s) and the closing config read (at most 10 s) fit in the 120 s, so the sitting's last card
  command ends before its cap. The smoke and the main run share the deadline. A sitting cut by it is reduced as it stands (E193.md 6).
  **The margin, for the readiness check** (verify-e193's note): sitting A's deadline, 58 minutes after its first card command, leaves
  about 10 minutes beyond the runner's own estimate (46 minutes of wall time, plus about 2 for the smoke) for its idle-down waits, which
  request.md's figures leave out; sitting B's, at 48 minutes, about 18 (28 plus 2). Long idle-downs can cut sitting A's last pass. That
  is the lab's registered cap (E193.md 6), not changed here.
- **A crash of the main run** (verify-e193's finding 5): the runner exiting non-zero with no stop recorded (`check_e193.py stopreason`:
  "no stop recorded"; a traceback, or a kill from outside) is not one of E193.md 9's stops, and E193.md 11 has no row for it. The session
