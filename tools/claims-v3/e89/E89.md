# E89 — heat below about 12 W of switching, measured directly (Q130, aifoundry3 card 0, pre-registration)

*Written Sat 31 Oct 2026 (week 44), 18:20–19:05 PDT, on the Intel, before the card was claimed. Frozen numbers:
[`predictions-e89.json`](predictions-e89.json) (written by [`make_predictions_e89.py`](make_predictions_e89.py)).
Data will go to `docs/reports/data/2026-10-31-e89-heat-low-power/`.*

## The question

E78 measured every kind's switching power at a pinned 600 MHz, but its heat column resolves only the 19 kinds above
about 12 W of switching: the die reading the host gets is the integer mean of 34 sensors, it moves in whole degrees,
and E78's window is 2 s (E78 README, correction C1). Every int8 kind and the low-power float kinds are therefore
extrapolations, *heat = S × 0.118 °C/W* (S = switching power over idle). The thermal camera did not detect int8
either (E85: 64.5 ± 28.3 mK, p 0.054). The critic of Q130 ranks this seventh: "It needs a longer window or a finer
reading." E89 does both: a finer reading and a sustained, repeated drive, and it asks one question: **does a
low-power kind heat the die by the same degrees per watt of switching power as fp32 random data, the resolved
reference?** If yes, E78's extrapolation becomes a measurement; if no, the heat column below 12 W changes.

## The finest reading the card offers, with nothing set

- **The firmware.** The governor's temperature is `pvt_get_minion_avg_temperature()`: the integer mean of the 34
  minion-shire sensors, each converted to whole degrees with integer arithmetic (et-platform `836a4ab`,
  `driver/pvt_controller.c` `pvt_ts_conversion`, `pvt_get_minion_avg_temperature`; Q130(d) README Results 1). The
  host's `DM_CMD_GET_MODULE_CURRENT_TEMPERATURE` returns that mean, the I/O shire's sensor and two peak-holds; the
  SP statistics' `minion_c` is a 5-sample moving average of the same integer, rounded to an integer
  (`thermal_pwr_mgmt.c` `CMA`). No stock read returns a single sensor, and the 12-bit raw codes (0.061 °C) never
  leave the SP.
- **Roman's patch 0003.** aifoundry3 card 0 runs the thermal build flashed on 7 Oct (release 1.4.0, BL1/BL2 0.22.0,
  minions 0.25.1 — the version E79 read this morning). It answers `DM_CMD_GET_ASIC_PVT_SAMPLES` (74): group 0 returns
  the 34 sensors one by one, each in whole degrees (current, high, low). The 7 Oct heat-spreading session read it at
  20 Hz for 34 min with no errors (the owner's record of that session; its client `heatmovie.py`). Group 6 of the same command
  resets the sensors' latches: a write, never sent (the sampler refuses any group but 0 and 4, in code).
- **Why it is finer.** Each sensor truncates at its own offset, so the mean of the 34 truncated readings moves in
  steps of 1/34 °C (0.029 °C), where the firmware's integer of it moves in whole degrees. Averaging sensors dithers
  below a degree even if every sensor is quiet; averaging in time adds only what the sensors' own noise provides.
  E89 measures both (the per-sensor dither in the idle bracket and inside blocks, `instrument` in `e89.json`).
- **The check.** The floor of the 34-sensor mean must equal the firmware's own `minshire_avg` (E89-R0).
- **Fallback, registered.** If command 74 does not answer at the probe, E89 runs with lib.sh's `ettelem` sampler and
  scores every rule on the integer mean; R0 is then FAIL, and R1–R4 are expected INCONCLUSIVE on the weak blocks.

## Design (and why it is not the literal brief)

**The brief** asked for 3–5 minute sustained windows per kind in E78's frame (idle brackets, a die-temperature start
gate), at least two passes, the six int8 kinds and the float kinds below 12 W, within 45 card-minutes. Three facts,
established at the desk before any card time, changed that:

1. **No window that fits reaches a steady state.** The lab's heat model of this card type has stages of 60, 150, 400
   and 2,500 s, and the 400 s stage carries most of the steady resistance (0.86 of 1.47 °C/W;
   [`docs/findings/11-thermal-model.md`](../../../docs/findings/11-thermal-model.md)). A true steady rise needs about
   30 minutes per kind; eight kinds and two passes would need eight hours.
2. **A window from a gate measures the gate, not the kind.** Simulated on that model with E78's powers
   ([`desk/sim_gate.py`](desk/sim_gate.py), [`desk/sim_gate.txt`](desk/sim_gate.txt)): after E78's preheat to 59 °C
   and fall to 58 °C, the die keeps relaxing toward a cooler heat sink, so over 26–60 s windows zeros and fp32
   sparse90 read *negative* rises, and the low-power kinds' rise per watt comes out 0.1–0.9 of the reference's, in a
   world where heat follows power exactly.
3. **A chain of zeros and kind segments is biased by its own history.** The same model
   ([`desk/sim_chain.py`](desk/sim_chain.py), [`desk/sim_chain.txt`](desk/sim_chain.txt)): with 90–120 s segments,
   the slow stages carry the previous segments' heat, and int8 −1 reads 0.65–0.74 of the reference's degrees per watt.

**What E89 does instead: a lock-in on the die's own sensors.** Each kind is switched against its reference as a
square wave of 30 s halves (period 60 s), the frame E63 and E85 used with the camera. The response at the switching
frequency is set by the fast and middle stages (die, spreader); the slow stages see only the block's mean and add a
smooth drift, which the fit removes. On the model it is unbiased whatever the order: every kind reads 0.99–1.01 of
the reference ([`desk/sim_lockin.py`](desk/sim_lockin.py), [`desk/sim_lockin.txt`](desk/sim_lockin.txt)). Each block
runs 2 min 20 s and each kind 4 min 40 s over the two passes. That is the brief's sustained drive, delivered as a
square wave.

- **Blocks, each a kind against its reference** (all tiles kinds.json's, E78's, sha256 checked before every pass):

  | block | kind | against | E78 switching power (the denominator) |
  |---|---|---|---|
  | i8full | int8 full (random int8) | int8 zeros | D 7.056 ± 0.059 W |
  | i8gauss | int8 gauss_q | int8 zeros | D 6.598 ± 0.069 W |
  | i8small | int8 smallint | int8 zeros | D 6.003 ± 0.142 W |
  | i8sp50 | int8 sparse50 | int8 zeros | D 5.463 ± 0.146 W |
  | i8neg1 | int8 neg_ones (−1, the constant) | int8 zeros | D 1.628 ± 0.169 W |
  | i8zero | int8 zeros | **idle** (no launch) | S 2.605 ± 0.028 W |
  | f32ones | fp32 ones (the float constant) | fp32 zeros | D 8.257 ± 0.221 W |
  | f32randn | fp32 randn — **the reference** (resolved in E78) | fp32 zeros | D 24.725 ± 0.082 W |

  D = E78's switching power over the type's own zeros, S = over idle (`e78.json` `items.<type.kind>.D`, `.S`, six
  passes). int8 zeros has no rise over its own zeros by definition, so its block measures it over idle, E78's
  "all zeros, over idle" cell (extrapolated to 0.31 °C at 2 s). The float kinds below 12 W are represented by fp32
  ones (the cheapest float kind that is not mostly gated, and the float side of "a constant costs a third of random");
  the budget has no room for a second.
- **A block:** a 20 s lead-in of the reference, then 2 cycles of [kind 30 s][reference 30 s]. A half is filled with
  back-to-back launches of `sparsity_host --test fma` on 1,024 minions, each ≤ 6.8 s under `timeout 10`, scheduled on
  the block's clock (an idle half launches nothing). Clock: 600 MHz, pinned by the card's boot-time TDP 0, never set.
- **A pass** (`block_e89.sh`): the card lock, a read-only `ettelem config`, the sampler, the start gate (idle until
  the die reading is ≤ 62 °C, at most 60 s), a 15 s idle bracket, the eight blocks — the seven low-power blocks
  shuffled with `Random("E89|aifoundry3|<pass>")`, then f32randn last (its heat would otherwise ride under the
  others) — a 5 s tail, the sampler stopped with SIGTERM, a second read-only `ettelem config`. Passes **8910** and
  **8911**, back to back (`queue_e89.sh`). Order: 8910 i8full, i8sp50, i8small, f32ones, i8zero, i8gauss, i8neg1,
  f32randn; 8911 i8sp50, i8zero, i8small, f32ones, i8neg1, i8gauss, i8full, f32randn.
- **The sampler** (`pvt_sample.py`, 10 Hz): six reads a tick — board power (33), SP statistics (60), the firmware's
  temperature (27), the 34 sensors (74, group 0), on-die voltages (32), clocks (53) — written with ettelem's keys plus
  `pvt`. No statistics reset (no `--reset-ms`, the evening rule).

## What is measured (the reduction, [`reduce_e89.py`](reduce_e89.py))

- **H, the heat per watt at 1/60 Hz.** Per block, one least-squares fit over its two cycles: a shared quadratic trend
  plus one fundamental per lap (cycle), for the board power and for the 34-sensor mean. H = T fundamental / board
  fundamental per lap (°C per W), projected on the reference block's mean phase. Per block: the mean over its 4 laps
  (2 per pass), ± t(0.975, 3) × SE over laps — the lap is the unit, as in E85; the pass-level mean and spread (n = 2,
  E78's convention) are reported beside it. Also: the temperature fundamental itself (°C), the board fundamental and
  its ratio to (2/π) × E78's watts, and "°C per W of E78's switching power".
- **r_K = H_K / H_ref**, the test of E78's extrapolation, with a delta-method SE and t(0.975, min(n) − 1).
- **The same on the firmware's whole-degree mean** (the reading E78 had): what the old instrument would have seen.
- **Per sensor:** each sensor's H per block (34 values), and its correlation with the reference's pattern.
- **E78's 2 s window, on the fine reading (step2):** at each kind half's first launch record t0, the mean over
  [t0 + 1, t0 + 3) s minus a straight-line baseline fitted to [t0 − 5, t0 − 0.2) s and carried to t0 + 2 s; per W of
  E78's D (S for i8zero). This is E78's †-cell, re-measured.
- **The 30 s swing (swing30):** the last 10 s of each kind half minus the mean of the last 10 s of the halves around
  it — the sustained rise over the reference, as far as a 30 s half carries it; per W of E78's D. Reported.
- **Gates (per block; a block failing any is dropped from every rule):** every sample at 600 MHz, minion 515–530 mV,
  every launch rc 0 with a record, every record's cycles per op within 0.5% of 546 (fp32) / 318 (int8), at least
  5 samples per second.

## Predictions (frozen in `predictions-e89.json`) and rules

From the heat model (Foster chain above, E78's leakage slope 0.1994 W/°C as feedback): **H = 0.188 °C/W at 1/60 Hz**,
phase −34°; with E78's watts and the 0.90 compute share of a half, the temperature fundamentals are 0.76 (i8full),
0.71 (i8gauss), 0.65 (i8small), 0.59 (i8sp50), 0.18 (i8neg1), 0.28 (i8zero), 0.89 (f32ones) and 2.67 °C (f32randn).
The prediction that matters: **r = 1 for every block** (one coefficient for every kind, which is what S × 0.118 °C/W
assumes).

| rule | what | PASS | FAIL | predicted |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| E89-R0 | the per-sensor reading works | ≥ 99% of samples carry 34 valid sensors and the floor of their mean equals the firmware's `minshire_avg` in ≥ 95% | otherwise | PASS |
| E89-R1 | each block's heat is detected (8 cells) | 95% lower bound of the temperature fundamental > 0 | 95% upper bound < 0 (else INCONCLUSIVE) | PASS ×8 |
| E89-R2 | heat follows switching power (7 cells) | r's 95% interval inside [0.80, 1.25] | r's 95% interval wholly outside [0.80, 1.25] (else INCONCLUSIVE) | PASS ×7 |
| E89-R3 | the reference's H against the heat model | H_ref's 95% interval overlaps [0.141, 0.264] °C/W (0.75–1.40 × 0.188) | no overlap | PASS |
| E89-R4 | E78's 2 s window, fine reading (7 cells) | the ratio (step2/W)_K / (step2/W)_ref, 95% interval inside [0.70, 1.43] | wholly outside (else INCONCLUSIVE) | PASS for the six blocks ≥ 2.5 W; i8neg1 PASS or INCONCLUSIVE (its step is ~0.12 °C, four steps) |

Reported, not scored: H's absolute value per block and per W of E78's switching power; the whole-degree mean's
amplitudes and detection; per-sensor H and the pattern correlation with the reference (each sensor is whole degrees,
so its amplitude is quantized and a pattern may differ for that reason alone); the 30 s swing; step2 of the
reference against E78's 0.118 °C/W; the dither indices; the gate and idle levels.

**Self-test** ([`selftest_e89.py`](selftest_e89.py), [`selftest_e89.txt`](selftest_e89.txt)): two synthetic passes in
the runner's and the sampler's exact formats, from the heat model on this schedule, 34 sensors truncated with random
offsets and 0.15 °C noise. When heat follows power, the reducer returns r = 0.990–1.009 for all seven blocks, H 0.184–0.188
°C/W against the model's 0.188, and R0–R3 PASS (R4 i8neg1 INCONCLUSIVE). When int8 gets 0.70 of the heat per watt, R2
FAILs for five int8 blocks (i8neg1 INCONCLUSIVE) and passes for fp32 ones.

## Card budget, safety, claim

- **45 card-minutes:** the probe (~10 s), the smoke (~1 min: i8full, 10 s halves, one cycle), passes 8910 and 8911
  (~19.5 min each), two session `ettelem config` reads. The queue starts a pass only if its projected end is inside
  42 minutes of the queue's start and before 21:40 PDT. The claim says `for 1 h`.
- **Nothing is set.** No governor, TDP, threshold, clock, reset, statistics reset, PCIe, driver, firmware or PMIC
  command. The management traffic is reads: `ettelem config` and the sampler's six commands (command 74 group 0 only).
  Every launch and the probe run under `timeout 10`; the card lock is held (`flock -n`); the sampler stops on SIGTERM
  (a request in flight completes); a pass stops if another user holds the card, at a 78 °C die reading, if the
  telemetry stops, or on a STOP file. Other users' idle logins do not block (E78's D1); their logins never enter a file.
- **The order of work:** this file, the predictions, the code and the desk checks are committed and pushed; then the
  `#community-lab` claim; then `run_e89.sh probe` (three sampler ticks: does command 74 answer?), `smoke`, and
  `go 8910 2`, detached on the box. Departures from this text get dated amendments, written before the data they touch.
