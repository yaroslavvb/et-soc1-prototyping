## What it does to predicting power and heat

M9 predicts, from the bytes a matmul multiplies, its **power** D (board W over the type's own zeros at 600 MHz) and its **heat** (the die's
rise over a 4.5 s kinds run). On aifoundry3 M9's power has no card scale (its units are this card on Q139(u)'s footing) and its heat is
M7's law, carried (0.1186 °C per board W on this card). **The terms under test** are M9's: for int8 a radix-4 Booth partial-product count
with B recoded (`bpp_r4`, +2.205 ± 0.271 fJ a flip) beside M8's near-whole-byte discount at half size (`bsat`, −29.3 ± 9.4 fJ a bit); for
the floats the bench's exponent stage (`xstage`, +0.648 ± 0.363 fJ a count) beside M8's C-register term (`c_end`, +6.19 ± 1.90 fJ a
flip); its constants refitted on M8's rows plus pool 6 on the one footing; its recalibrated bands (k 1.996 floats, 1.864 int8); and
M7's heat law, carried. **Before** (the error M9 claims for a new kind, nested leave one kind out, out of kind and not blind,
`nested-m9.json`): on aifoundry3's rows fp32 **0.200 W** (241 rows), fp16 **0.168 W** (124), int8 **0.169 W** (90); over both cards floats
0.193 W and int8 0.177 W, against M6's, M7's and M8's forms on the same folds at 0.198 / 0.197 / 0.194 W (floats) and 0.251 / 0.256 /
