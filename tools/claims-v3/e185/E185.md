## 6. The rules (registered)

Each is read on E185's scored holds (complete by E166's X1, as M11's § 1 scores a hold), on A10's reading, with the frozen gains. n is the
number of scored holds, and τ = σ_c / √n with σ_c = 0.0884 °C (M11's § 4); τ = 0.0361 °C at n = 6. **Every arm rule is SCORED only with at
least 4 scored holds, at least one fp32.randn and one int8.neg_ones among them**; else NOT SCORED, with the reason.

| rule | statistic | verdict |
|---|---|---|
| **E185-RF**, the rail chain against the frozen chain | D = rms(e_Rl) − rms(e_F) over the scored holds | BETTER at D ≤ −τ; WORSE at D ≥ +τ; else NOT RESOLVED |
| **E185-BF**, the board gain against the frozen chain | D = rms(e_B) − rms(e_F) | the same |
| **E185-RB**, the rail chain against the board gain | D = rms(e_Rl) − rms(e_B) | the same |
| **E185-BEST** | the arm with the smallest rms | that arm if every other arm's rms exceeds it by at least τ; else NOT SEPARATED, naming the arms within τ of the smallest |
| **E185-C**, the cool-down shape, per arm | over the big holds with a complete cool-down window (n_c ≥ 3, else NOT SCORED): d_h = the window's miss less the hold's plateau miss (both against the hold's own anchor); the mean d̄ and its 95% t interval at n_c − 1 | THE SHAPE MISSES (cooler or warmer than the chain) if the interval excludes 0 and \|d̄\| ≥ 0.05 °C; THE SHAPE HOLDS if the interval lies inside ±0.10 °C; else NOT RESOLVED |

**A hold whose rail readings are missing** over its anchor or plateau window is NOT SCORED for Rl, and the three-arm rules then read on the
holds all three arms score.

**Reported beside, deciding nothing:** each arm's mean miss per kind (H, L, S); each hold's e, cool-down miss, rail share and P under each arm;
the one-pass reading's misses; HM-E144 on H_E (as registered) and the same ratio on H_E2; FL5(c) on the rests; the rms over the large rises.

**M11's own registered readings** need no change to read E185. HM-LG, HM-AG and S7-W read at k = 2 (E181 and E185), and A1-4's path and the
x1.032 arm with them. HM-E144 and HM-HE read on H_E. HM-CAL-I8 reads L1, S and L2 if the order lets it (here L1 and L2 run before S, so it reads
NOT SCORED). HM-CAL needs Z, so it reads NOT SCORED by design. All of them run through M11's registered decision code, on A10's reading, and
decide by § 6 as registered.

**What each outcome means (registered; one way only):**

| E185-BEST | what M11 carries on aifoundry3 |
|---|---|
| **Rl** | the rail-driven chain becomes M11's aifoundry3 reading **from session 4 on**, with G_Rl = 1.19380 frozen; F and B become reported arms |
| **B** | the board gain G_B = 1.02678 enters M11's aifoundry3 reading from session 4 on, in place of the x1.032 arm; F a reported arm |
| F, NOT SEPARATED or NOT SCORED | nothing changes: the frozen chain stays M11's primary, and B and Rl stay reported arms for the next session |

- An arm that enters, and that a later blind session reads WORSE than the frozen chain (its RF or BF rule), is carried as a reported arm, never

- E185's verdicts change no E181 or E185 reading of M11's registered rules.
