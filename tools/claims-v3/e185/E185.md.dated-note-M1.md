## M1-4. What is read, and how (registered here, before any datum; [`arm_m1_read_e185.py`](arm_m1_read_e185.py) is the arm's reader)

- **Reported, never a rule.** The arms are read after E185's data are collected and scored. No E185 rule, threshold, verdict or registered
  forecast depends on them, and they never enter E185-BEST.
- **The measured side is E185's own,** as step 22 commits it: `scored-m11.json`, written by `score_e185.sh` with M11's scorer v5.
  - **The room** is the session's start gate's `room_c` (R0's room, its last 120 s). **The pattern** is the one the measured pass ran, from
    its room mark's `he_seconds` (3.2 s the cool pattern, 2.6 s the warm); without that mark, A6-3's rule on the room, said so.
  - **Each hold's rise R** is the score's `R_C`, for every hold the score reads scored; a hold it does not score is not read.
  - **Each big hold's cool-down rise** is recovered from the score's two cool-down misses and the frozen G_B: the score writes e_F = R_c -
    P_F,c and e_B = R_c - G_B P_F,c at 4 decimals, so P_F,c = (e_F - e_B) / (G_B - 1) and R_c = e_F + P_F,c, to about +-0.004 C. The same
    recovery is first checked on the hold's plateau against the score's own `R_C`; a miss above 0.01 C leaves that hold's cool-down not read.
    G_B must equal the score's frozen gain and `arms-e185-train.json`'s, or the reader refuses (exit 3).
  - **The measured interval** is R +- 1.96 sigma_c, sigma_c = 0.0884 C (M11's REGISTRATION.md section 4; E185.md section 6), for a rise and
    for a cool-down alike: +-0.173 C.
- **The model's figure at the session's room:** among the plan rooms of the pattern the session ran (cool: 49.4, 50.3, 50.8 and 51.5 C; warm:
  52.5, 53.0 and 53.9 C), linear in the room between the two that bracket it, or from the two nearest at that end when the room lies outside
  them (extrapolated, and said so with the distance); the central and the 1 sd alike. The model's json is refused (exit 2) unless its sha256
  is the one the reader pins.
- **The words:** the model's central lies **inside, above or below** the measured interval; the measured R lies **inside, above or below the
  model's own 95% band** (central +- 1.96 sd), with z = (R - central) / sd. Then, per arm, the rms and mean miss over the rises read and over the
  cool-downs read, set **beside** E185-BEST's rms for F, B and Rl (on the measured watts, as the score gives them) and beside the same three
  arms' planned forecasts at the same room by the same rule (from the plan's watts, as the model's are). Words of their own, never E185's.
- **Not read:** the S and S2 cool-downs (not covered); a hold the score does not read scored; a cool-down the score gives no window for.
- **To run it**, after step 22's score and before the data commit, from the lab's top:
  `/usr/bin/python3 -B tools/claims-v3/e185/arm_m1_read_e185.py --data <E185's data dir> --out <E185's data dir>/arm-m1-e185` (it writes
  `arm-m1-e185.json` and `arm-m1-e185.txt`). A refusal does not block the data commit; the data README records it.
- **What E185 can tell:** v4 equals v3, so E185 reads the model as one forecast under two names and cannot tell the versions apart. At 50.8 C the model's
  rises sit 0.10-0.34 C from F, 0.04-0.17 C from B, 0.05-0.20 C from Rl (M1-3), up to about two of the measured interval's half-widths (0.173 C), so a single hold
  seldom separates them; the rms over the six rises is the reported comparison.
