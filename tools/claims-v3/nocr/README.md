# nocr: where the mesh's stops are, and which way it routes (hub rungs 31 and 32)

Two card experiments for the chip diagram, written 28 September 2026 and not yet run on a card. The theories,
predictions and decision rules are in [`PREREG.md`](PREREG.md) (a draft until development ends); the workload is
[`workloads/nocroute`](../../../workloads/nocroute/README.md).

- **R31 (`exp-mesh-stops`).** Time the stock counter syscalls `SYSCALL_PMC_SC_SAMPLE` (a cache bank's counter in any
  shire) and `SYSCALL_PMC_MS_SAMPLE` (a memory shire's) from each of the 32 compute shires, one caller at a time. Each
  call is a few M-mode ESR loads and stores over the mesh (in the compiled firmware: pmc 0 two loads and two stores,
  cache-bank pmc 3 one of each, pmc 7 none), so its cost is a constant plus a term per hop to the target: fitted over
  the 992 compute-to-compute pairs, then used to place shire 32 among the four grey cells and each memory shire on
  the ring outside the grid (the chip diagram's grey "confirm" cells; memory shire 2 was a tie in the DRAM-latency
  fit).
- **R32 (`exp-route-order`).** The E27 wire kernel (1 KB tensor loads, two in flight, 32 minions per reader) in
  mirrored families of stream sets: under dimension order a row family's replies share one directed link under x
  first and its requests share the reversed link under y first, and a column family the mirror image; positive
  controls (straight flows: shared under both orders) and negative controls (no packets shared under either). The
  order is the family whose data share falls further (a request never costs a link more than its reply); the same
  sets as tensor stores (R32w) give the requests' order, and so whether replies follow the requests' rule.
- **Q63's second test** (per hop or per mm through a memory-shire column) is not run: the geometry offers no such
  hop without new routing assumptions (`PREREG.md`, last section).

## Passes and cards

| Pass | Kind | Where | Needs |
|---|---|---|---|
| 9 | smoke: 2 callers, 1 master caller, 3 reads and 1 write of 10 ms | any allowed card | no HALT marker |
| 1-8 | development | aifoundry1's card 1 only (fw 1.2.0, 600 MHz fixed); `V3_DEVICE=1` | this card's smoke ended ok; no HALT |
| 11-19 | validation | aifoundry3 (fw 1.3.1, pinned 600 MHz); aifoundry2 only after DV2 (refused before 00:30 UTC 30 Sep, and while a DV2 block runs) | the lock; this card's smoke ended ok; no HALT |

Refused always: aifoundry1's card 0, `V3_FORCE` outside a dry run, a pass number outside these ranges; and on a card
(not dry) without `et-who` or without its lock file `/run/lock/etsoc-shire<N>.lock`. One pass runs one device
process at a time (each under `timeout 10`, the host with an 8 s budget): R31's three mesh launches (1 process),
three read and three write repeats of R32 (6 processes, 47 launches of 50 ms each), and last R31's shire-32 launch
(1 process), which is the only call path no earlier work has used.

**The HALT marker** (`build/claims-v3/<card>/nocr/HALT`, one JSON line per reason). A launch that did not finish (the
host aborts a stream after 6 s and marks the launch `"timed_out":true`; or `timeout 10` kills the host, rc 124), any
failure of an R31 process, any failure in the smoke, missing telemetry, or a die at 90 C writes it and ends the
block. Every later nocr block on that card then refuses to start (exit 2), so neither `queue.sh` (which only logs a
failed block and starts the next one 20 s later) nor `run.sh` can launch on a card that may be wedged. A person
looks at the card (`et-who`, the block's `marks.jsonl`, the host's `.err` files) and removes the marker by hand.
An R32 process that fails otherwise (a minion error, a stream error, the budget) is recorded in the block's note and
`marks.jsonl` and the pass goes on: its launches that were not ok are dropped by the data rule, the rest count.

## Commands

**Development on aifoundry1's card 1**, from aifoundry2's checkout (`A1` is aifoundry1's full tailnet name; AGENT.md
§4). `queue.sh` is not used on aifoundry1: with `V3_DEVICE` set it samples the die before every block (lib.sh's
`die_c`: `timeout 20 ettelem`, no card lock, no et-who check). `run.sh` calls the blocks directly, and each block
does its own checks.

```bash
A1=<aifoundry1's full tailnet name>
bash tools/claims-v3/nocr/deploy.sh $A1     # refuses while a queue/run/block runs there, et-who shows a holder, or et-who is missing;
                                            # copies nocr + nocroute to ~/nekko, builds build/nocroute (nice -n 10, -j4, --clean-first),
                                            # checks every digest, lib.sh and queue.sh against this tree, and that build/ettelem/ettelem exists
ssh $A1 'cd ~/nekko && V3_DEVICE=1 V3_DRY=1 bash tools/claims-v3/nocr/block.sh 9 > /dev/null && tail -1 build/claims-v3-dry/aifoundry1-c1/nocr/p9/reduce.txt'
# the smoke, detached (ettelem handles no SIGHUP); about 1 minute:
ssh $A1 'et-who --check && cd ~/nekko && mkdir -p build/claims-v3 && V3_DEVICE=1 setsid nohup bash tools/claims-v3/nocr/run.sh 9 \
  > build/claims-v3/nocr-run-aifoundry1-c1-p9.log 2>&1 < /dev/null &'
ssh $A1 'cd ~/nekko && pgrep -af "[n]ocr/(run|block).sh"; tail -2 build/claims-v3/nocr-run-aifoundry1-c1-p9.log;
  cat build/claims-v3/aifoundry1-c1/nocr/HALT 2>/dev/null; cat build/claims-v3/aifoundry1-c1/nocr/p9/block.json build/claims-v3/aifoundry1-c1/nocr/p9/reduce.txt'
# after reading p9/reduce.txt: three development passes, detached; about 4 minutes

  > build/claims-v3/nocr-run-aifoundry1-c1-dev.log 2>&1 < /dev/null &'
ssh $A1 'cd ~/nekko && pgrep -af "[n]ocr/(run|block).sh"; tail -4 build/claims-v3/nocr-run-aifoundry1-c1-dev.log; cat build/claims-v3/aifoundry1-c1/nocr/HALT 2>/dev/null'
```

**Dry runs**, here, with no device access (the host is replaced by `nocr.py fakehost`, a model of each theory):

```bash
V3_DRY=1 NOCR_DRY_CARD=aifoundry1-c1 NOCR_FAKE_THEORY=xy bash tools/claims-v3/nocr/run.sh 9 1
python3 tools/claims-v3/nocr/reduce.py build/claims-v3-dry/aifoundry1-c1/nocr
nice -n 19 python3 tools/claims-v3/nocr/nocr.py selftest     # every planted model through plan, fakehost and reduce (about 10 s)
```

The planted models (`NOCR_FAKE_THEORY`, and `selftest`'s list): `xy`, `yx`, `retrace` (replies y first, requests x
first, posted stores), `wide` (no link saturates), `c51` (a 51.2 GB/s link), `adapt`, `master-bottom` (shire 32 at
(5,3)), `ms-swap12` (memory shires 1 and 2 exchanged), `hub` (ESR calls through one stop), `xy-c1` and `yx-c1`
(requests cost as much as replies: reads cannot tell the order), `xy-c075`, `xy-shared` (one network for requests
and replies), `source-fw` (the access counts of `pmu.c`'s text, not the compiled code), `posted`, `weak-writes`
(remote stores at 15% of the reads' bandwidth), `narrow-req` (a 60 GB/s request network, stores at half the reads'
bandwidth), `quiet` (no background traffic, free requests) and `statw-heavy` (a
quarter of the ESR calls delayed by up to 600 cycles). Every model but `quiet` has the master's stats worker delaying
15% of ESR calls by 100-200 cycles and requests costing a quarter of a reply. `selftest` checks every registered
verdict against the planted answer: 19 models, 0 mismatches (28 September, after the review's fixes). The stops are
tested with `NOCR_FAKE_FAIL=<process> [NOCR_FAKE_RC=n]`, `NOCR_FAKE_TIMEOUT=<process>`, `NOCR_FAKE_NOTOK=<process>`,
`NOCR_FAKE_DIE=<C|none>` and `NOCR_FAKE_MHZ=<MHz>`.

## Card time

Per full pass: about 30 s of device-open time (R31 about 3 s, R32 about 24 s, shire 32 about 2 s) and about 10 s of
1 s ettelem samples, about 1 minute of wall clock with the 2 s pauses. The smoke: about 10 s. Development (smoke + 3
passes): about 5 minutes of wall clock with run.sh's 20 s gaps, under 2 minutes of card time; validation the same.
The load is light (at most ten shires streaming, or one hart per shire calling), so the die should not warm by more
than a few degrees.

## What the block enforces

`et-who --check` for this card (a missing et-who refuses) and lib.sh's `others_present` before the block and before
every device process; the card lock for the whole block (`block_begin`, fd 9; a missing lock file refuses);
`timeout 10` per device process; a 1 s ettelem sample of this card before every process (die mean and clock), a
HALT at a 90 C mean or with no telemetry, and a process whose clock reads off 600 MHz is skipped (its data could not
count); the HALT marker and the smoke gate above; no sampler, no queue drain, no heater and no `dev_mngt_service`
(the stock build opens aifoundry1's card 0 management node too; lib.sh's `heat_to` can call it, so nocr never heats,
on aifoundry2 either). The kernel refuses shire 33, shire ids above 32, banks above 3, memory shires above 7, any pmc
but 0, 3 and 7, a call list that is not a multiple of 8 entries or an unaligned output (error 4), and any scratchpad
but a compute shire's (never shire 32's, which holds the firmware's buffers).
