## 1. The question, and the answer frozen here

**Does MM1, built and fitted on rgm136 alone, forecast a second matmul kernel from the matrix sizes and the operations it contains, given only a
desk map of the new kernel's tile order?** Two changes, one at a time, on the same card in one session: **(W) the tile order** - rgm211 walks the C
blocks nb outer, mb inner, so A's row panel becomes the far operand and B stays near (in rgm136 it is the reverse) - and **(C) the load
operations** - rgm136's own non-cooperative branch (every minion loads for itself: 1,024 line requests a shire-op instead of 192), with **(X) exact
controls** where both walks are one op stream.

**In short, frozen:** MM1 puts rgm211 15-25% slower than rgm136 where A is the far operand (w06-w10), 10-12% faster where B's large panel was the
far operand (w04, w05), within 5% inside the L3 and at the knee (w01-w03), and exactly equal at the controls. **The bandwidth reading, reworded
(critic-kernel2 finding 1):** at w06, w07, w09 and w10 the two walks fetch about the same fresh lines **a shire-op** (79.7 against 81.8 at
3x5x7), so F1's shire-level line-cost law says about equal time (1.00-1.03); but the chip's distinct DRAM lines a step rise 1.38-1.57 times there
(an A tile is read by 4 shires, a B tile by 8, so a fresh A line a shire-op is a quarter of a DRAM line and a fresh B line an eighth), and the
chip-lines null NC, F1's law on the chip's lines, also puts rgm211 26-30% slower there (17% at 4x3x6). So a measured slowdown alone does not single out MM1's per-op
mechanism (an A-fresh stream in most of rgm211's ops); the ratio's size, pair by pair, does: MM1 and NC part by 5-26% in ln at eight of the ten
pairs (2% at w01, 1.7% at w09; `power-e211.json` › `separation_ln`), and H2 decides between them. **Family C, frozen:** C1 (E101's measured cooperation, carried) puts
gemmLnc at 0.956 of gemmL's wall cycles, 0.915 of its counter's mean, and 1.17 (2x3x2) and 1.15 (3x5x7) times its J over idle; MM1-v1 literally
(C0) says no change at all.

## 2. The kernels and the binaries

**rgm136, E136's kernel, unchanged** (`tools/claims-v3/e136/rgm136/`, never edited here; its ten sources by sha256 in `kernel-e211.json`, equal to
E206's registered list): the whole ELF `aace138caf8071cc7679134211045a89fb09b65cc9a170bfe33409fd36b4fda0` (E136's build record), `.text`
`61315bf6a449c2823fbffc33834b4c0a68aa19e8436a29c1124b15c04114b763`, loadable identity `649d40793e459e51db9a590ca90dd1363da3a00398f98938c3ef6e190514e2b7`
(E206's LOAD_PIN) - all three reproduced by this registration's desk build. Its large GEMM `gemmL<MB>x<NB>x<KB>`: C (MB x NB blocks of 1,024 x
512) = A (MB x KB) x B (KB x NB), blocks of 64 x 32 x 32 tiles; every minion owns two row tiles and one column tile of every C block, loads A with
its neighbourhood (8 minions) into its L1 scratchpad and B with its position across the shire's 4 neighbourhoods through TenB, runs each C tile's
whole K (KB x 32 multiply-adds) before its one TensorStore, and walks the C blocks **mb outer, nb inner**.

**rgm211, THE SECOND KERNEL** (`tools/claims-v3/e211/rgm211/`, new files only): made by `make_rgm211.py` from rgm136 as committed by exact,
asserted edits (`make_rgm211.py --check` re-makes it byte for byte); `rgm211.diff` (`3767fae1...`) is the whole difference, 124 changed lines in
four files:
- **the walk** (`kernel/sparsity.c`): `run_gemm_large_nm`, `run_gemm_large`'s text with its two outer loops swapped (**nb outer, mb inner**); the
  partition, the cooperative loads and their groups, the order inside an op (A load, B load, the finished tile's store, wait, multiply-add), the
  accumulation bits, the TensorStore and the final waits are `run_gemm_large`'s. `entry_point` dispatches `SP_GEMM_LARGE | SP_GEMM_NB_OUTER`
  (`0x400`, `sparsity_args.h`) to it. `run_gemm_large` itself is not edited.
- **THE WALK TAG** (critic-kernel2 fix 2): after its timed section (after `o->cycles` and `o->count`) `run_gemm_large_nm` writes `SP_WALK_TAG_NM`
  (`0x453231314E4D574B`) to `o->aux`, which `entry_point` copies to every participating hart's result line; `run_gemm_large` never writes `aux`,
  so rgm136's walk leaves 0 (`Out` is zero-initialised and the host zeroes the results buffer before every launch). In the desk build's
  disassembly the tag is formed after the last hardware-counter read of the walk.
- **the host** (`host/main.cpp`): two arms beside gemmL - `gemmLn<MB>x<NB>x<KB>` (the nb-outer walk, cooperative loads) and `gemmLnc<MB>x<NB>x<KB>`
  (gemmL with every minion loading for itself: `coop` 0, `run_gemm_large`'s own non-cooperative branch, compiled into rgm136's ELF since E136
  and never run in the large walk). **THE WALK CHECK:** on every large-GEMM launch the host counts the reporting harts whose `aux` is the tag; a
  gemmLn launch is ok only if every one carries it, a gemmL or gemmLnc launch only if every one carries 0; each launch's SPARSITY line prints
  `"walk"` (nm / mn / none), `"walk_tagged"` and `"walk_ok"`, the check line `"walk"` and `"walk_launches_bad"`, and the check line is ok (and the
  process exits 0) only if the C check holds and no launch's walk was wrong. **A gemmLn process given rgm136's ELF** (which ignores `0x400`, runs
  gemmL's walk and passes its C check) therefore fails. **THE EARLY REFUSALS** (critic-kernel2 fix 5): `validateArm()` runs in `main()` before
  `Session` opens the device and creates the runtime (whose creation sends an abort on every queue of every card it enumerates): the type, the
  arm (`parseArm`), the per-shire rule, tenb's gen, the gen, and every tile file's 2 x 1,024 bytes; `testArm` parses the same arm again.

**Build** (both trees, as E136): `cmake -S <tree> -B <build> -DCMAKE_PREFIX_PATH=/opt/et -Wno-dev` and `cmake --build <build> -j4`; this

## 9. What E211 cannot say (stated before the data)

- **Two walks of one kernel family on one card.** rgm211 changes the C-block order and nothing inside a C block; family C changes the load mode and
  nothing else. A kernel with another partition, another block shape, B resident across the row tiles, two C tiles at once, K split across minions
  or a TensorReduce is not tested: "which operations are contained" is tested here for loads only.
- **No epilogue** (bias, activation, requantization) and **no L2-scratchpad place** (E202's MNIST pass has both; it is the candidate for the next
  kernel test). No other card, clock, format beyond fp32 and one int8 pair, data kind, or shire subset.
- **The ratio separates mechanisms only partly**: MM1 (per-op A-fresh latency) and NC (the chip's DRAM traffic) agree in direction at every pair;
  H2 decides between them only where their sizes part (eight pairs by 5-26% in ln); a measured slowdown matching neither is a third mechanism.
- **MM1-S against MM1** reads which pricing of A's fresh lines the card follows on rgm211's points only, through J over idle given the seconds; the
  awake terms and the L2 and L3 prices are not separated by E211.
- **Family C** has two shapes and one twin run each: CT and CE read E101's carryover there; they do not separate the cooperation cost into its
  synchronisation and its L2 traffic, and nothing scores MM1's rho beyond C1's rule.
- **The map is a model**: the L3 as one fully associative LRU over 1 KB tiles under MM1-v1's logistic retention; its tie is to E206's registered
  lines on rgm136's walk; rgm211's map is checked only by construction (the walk a parameter) and by the exact controls until the card speaks.
- **Twins**: E206's four twins are in MM1-v1's fit; E207's three will have run first; the twins' absolute errors are REPORTED, never verdicts.
- **No transistor switch is measured**; MM1's switch columns are estimates beside the forecasts.
- *Added by A1:* **two compiled loops, not one loop walked two ways.** rgm211's ELF runs its walk in fewer instructions an op than rgm136's
  (31 against 36, section 6), so every measured ratio carries any code-path offset beside the walk's effect. The controls read it where the
  walks are one op stream; A1 removes a common offset that both controls agree on; an offset that changes with the shape (a fixed number of
  cycles an op, or one hidden behind the memory waits at the A-far shapes) is removed only to within the controls' spread, and one that parts
  the two controls by more than 0.005 leaves family W "not a model test".
