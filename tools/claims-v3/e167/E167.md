## Added here, before any E167 data

### G1, G1Q's start gate: its REST must read 49 °C or less

- **The wait.** G1Q's REST block starts with E98's cool gate set to **49 °C** (`"start": {"mode": "cool", "max_c": 49, "max_wait_s": 240}`;
  E161's REST had 99 °C and 5 s, a gate that never waits): the 120 s anchor begins once the live whole-degree die reads 49 °C or less, after
  at most 240 s of waiting with no launch.
- **The gate.** After the REST, before G1Q's first work process, the runner reads the anchor exactly as the scorer will (the mean of
  `minshire[0]` over the REST file's last 120 s). **G1Q's hold runs only if it reads 49.0 °C or less.** Otherwise (or if fewer than 600 of
  the 1,200 expected lines are there) no work process starts: the session ends with **exit 6** ("gate"), G1Q reads **NOT RUN (the start
  gate)**, RK3-b reads **"not scored: the start gate"**, END is skipped (no hold ran), and the queue goes on to the 15-minute rest and G2Q.
  G2Q has no gate: in the trip regime its plateau is the trip rule and its clock is the prediction.
- **Why 49.** RA1.2: the primary puts G1Q in the trip regime above a rest die of **49.97 °C** (at E161's rest-board law), where R3 scores
  it against the trip rule's 65.9 °C, which is not heat from the bits. A REST of 49.0 °C keeps T_rest + 0.5 = 49.5 °C under that edge, so
  RK3-b stays scorable over the readout (RA1.2 still decides it at the measured anchors). E161's REST read 47.0 °C.
- **No retry.** G1Q is not tried again in the same session (the budget has no room: 35.1 + 4 of 40). If the gate stops G1Q, RK3-b and rank 3
  are not answered on this session pair; that is reported, never read as a failure.

### E2, a reported arm with the day's zeros-anchor offset from E165

- If E165 runs first on aifoundry2 the same day, its zeros-anchor bursts (E80's harness) are its session's own reads of M9's zeros level.
  The scorer takes them (`--e165-anchors <file>`: per format, the bursts' board W, MHz and die start, the form `predict_m9_a2.session_delta`
  reads; or the three δ values E165's reduction computed with it) and recomputes δ_t with **`predict_m9_a2.session_delta`, mechanically**,
  then `a2_hold(..., "M9a", delta=δ)`.
- It is **reported beside the primary, never the primary and never a verdict**. The primary stays D4's fallback offset, as frozen
  (Q140(e) R2: "If E167's card session also runs zeros anchors on E80's harness, a reported arm recomputes δ with them through
  `predict_m9_a2.session_delta`, mechanically"). E165's anchors are another session than E167's holds, so the arm reads the day's level, not
  the hold's.
- With no E165 anchors the arm reads "not computed (no E165 anchors)".

## Stops (E161's), on live samples, tested in the smoke

- A hold ends at a die reading **≥ 72 °C** (the minion shires' mean or the hottest-sensor field, on one live sample) or a board reading
  **over 80 W on 3 consecutive live samples**. `run_hpl.py`'s watcher reads every live sampler line as it is written, touches the stop file
  and ends the session (exit 4); the queue then runs only END / END2 and stops.
- These are stricter than section 26's general aifoundry2 stops (85 W on three live samples, 76 °C) and govern. `run_e167.py` refuses any
  protocol whose stops are looser.
- The stop path is tested on live samples in the smoke (STOPTEST-DIE at 30 °C and STOPTEST-BOARD at 30 W, the same watcher code); either
  failing ends the queue before any hold.
- Nothing here drives the card toward a PMIC over-power or over-temperature reset: the highest predicted peak is 68.2 W (95%), E144b ran
  fp32 random to 87.98 W on this card with no alarm, and E161's same two holds peaked at 46.77 W (G1Q) and 65.77 W (G2Q), with no sample
  over 80 W and the hottest sensor at 70 °C (`results.json` › `holds` › `extremes`).
