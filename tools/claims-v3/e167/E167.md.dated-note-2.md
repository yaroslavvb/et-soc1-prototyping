## X2, the incomplete-window rule (E166's X1, in E167's terms)

E167.md scores G1Q and G2Q over [b − 300 s, b) of whatever ran. It never said what a hold cut short reads: a safety stop (exit 4), the
queue's cutoff STOP file or SIGTERM, the protocol's card-minute cap. E166's registration has X1 for exactly this case. X2 is X1 for E167.

**A hold is complete** when all of these hold:
- its step ended **"done"** (`end.json`'s reason exactly `done`) and its marks hold **no `safety_trip`**;
- it ran **at least the registered protocol's HOLD work processes**: G1Q **93**, G2Q **91** ([`protocol-g1q.json`](protocol-g1q.json),
  [`protocol-g2q.json`](protocol-g2q.json)). One failed process (rc ≠ 0) is the harness's own tolerance (`run_hpl.py` ends a session at
  the second) and is reported;
- its span b − t0 is **at least the frozen plan span less 30 s**: G1Q 782.97 − 30 = **752.97 s**, G2Q 770.97 − 30 = **740.97 s**
  (`predictions-q140e.json` › `E167.plan_span_s`; E161's own spans exactly);
- the telemetry has **at least 5 lines a second** (half the 10 Hz sampler's) in the anchor window, the REST file's last 120 s as
  `reduce_e161.rest_reading` reads it (**600 lines**), and in the plateau window [b − 300 s, b) (**1,500 lines**).

**An incomplete hold is treated by why it is incomplete:**
- **Ended by a safety line:** the step's reason starts `safety:`, or its marks hold a `safety_trip`, after the hold's first work process
  started. That is run_hpl.py's watcher: the die line at 72 °C on the shires' mean or on the hottest-sensor field, or the board line over
  80 W on 3 consecutive samples. **It counts against.** RK3-b reads FAIL, the hold reads outside for RK3-c, and every carried verdict
  (G1Q's band and clock share, G2Q's trip rule and trip clock) reads FAIL. Over the frozen rest-die table (44-48 °C) the primary's highest
  simulated in-work boards are 46.32 W (G1Q) and 65.34 W (G2Q), and its plateaus at most 63.47 °C (G1Q at a 48 °C rest; the gate admits
  49.0 °C, about 1.2 °C more) or the trip rule's 65.9 °C (G2Q), far under both lines. So a safety line is itself evidence against the
  prediction. A `safety_trip` mark with the reason `stop_file` counts as a safety line too: the watcher sets its trip, touches the stop
  file and then writes the mark, so the session can read the stop file before the trip and end with `stop_file`. The mark is written
  either way. The scorer reads, from the step's telemetry, which field reached the line first, and reports it.
- **Ended by anything else** (the queue's cutoff STOP file, a signal, the protocol's card-minute cap `done:max_card_min`, an error, another
  user on the card, no `end.json`, thin telemetry): **it is excluded.** It reads **NOT SCORED (incomplete window: <why>)**, and its
  readings stay in the output as post hoc.
- **Not run** (no HOLD work process: the start gate G1, or a stop before the hold's first process, which ends the queue): excluded.

**How the rules read with X2** (registered here; `score_e167_x2.py`):

| rule | complete | ended by a safety line | excluded or not run |
|---|---|---|---|
| **RK3-b** (G1Q) | as registered (RA1.2's regime precondition first, unchanged) | **FAIL** (unless RA1.2 already reads "not scored") | **NOT SCORED (incomplete window: <why>)**; a gated G1Q keeps "not scored: the start gate" |
| **RK3-c** | as registered | the hold reads **outside** | see below |
| the carried rules | as registered | **FAIL** | **NOT SCORED (incomplete window)** |

- **RK3-c, in X1's form:** FAIL if any hold rising 10 °C or more is outside ±1.5% or ended by a safety line. With nothing excluded, R4 as
  registered. With a hold excluded or not run, PASS only if both expected large rises (G1Q and G2Q) are complete and inside, so **NOT

## The hottest-sensor field is a latch

**`minshire[2]` is not the current hottest sensor.** At firmware `836a4ab` (BL2 0.22.0), `pvt_get_minion_avg_low_high_temperature` returns
the shires' mean (`current`), the lowest low and the **highest high**. `pvt_get_min_shire_ts_sample` reads each shire's high from the PVT
sensor's `smpl_hilo` register (its `SMPL_HI` field), a latch of the highest sample (`device-bootloaders/src/ServiceProcessorBL2/driver/
pvt_controller.c`). On aifoundry2 it read **70 °C on all 50,594 committed telemetry lines**: E159 11,317, E160 18,303 and E161 20,974,
at idle (shires' mean 44 °C) and under load (66 °C).
- **So E167.md's "the hottest sensor at 70 °C" for E161** (§ Stops, from `results.json` › `holds` › `extremes`) quotes the latch: an upper
  bound. No sample on the card passed 70 °C through E161's two holds, or the latch would have risen. The same holds for E167.md's stops
  wording, "the hottest-sensor field": it is that latch. The stop is unchanged.
- **The box's own 5 s history carries the same field** (card 0's `max`; it read 70 °C wherever `minshire[2]` did, in E161's window). In
  the committed history it reads 70 °C from Mon 2 Nov 23:08 PST (E144b) through E161's end. It fell twice before that, on Sun 1 Nov at
  00:53 and 17:23 PST (the second inside E111, whose camera step sent the statistics reset). So something can clear it. **E167's chain
  sends no statistics reset and nothing else that writes**, and resetting the latch would be a write, outside this round's rules.
- **What follows for E167.** If the field reads 72 °C or more when E167 starts (E165 runs first on this card), every E167 step's die line
  fires at its first sample. The smoke then ends the queue before any hold. That is fail-safe, but E167 cannot run that day. **A box step
  is added before the claim:** [`latch_check_e167.py`](latch_check_e167.py) reads the field from the box's own history file (a file read,
  no device command), or from sampler lines. It reads GO under 72 °C, NO-GO at 72 °C or more (do not claim E167), and UNREADABLE when the
  history is stale (the smoke's first samples are then the check). Its desk test is [`logs/latch-check-desk.txt`](logs/latch-check-desk.txt):
  GO at 70 °C on E161's committed history and telemetry, NO-GO on a made-up 72 °C line, UNREADABLE on a stale or cardless history.
- **Under X2 a stop on the latch during a hold counts against**, like any safety line. The latch moves only when a sample passes its
  value. E161's same two holds never moved it past 70 °C, so a trip at 72 °C means a sensor reached 72 °C, hotter than any sample on this
  card since the latch was last set. The scorer reports which field fired.

## The 82 W admission is static (E161's form)

Section 22's rule (a tile, shape or hold whose predicted peak board exceeds 82 W does not run as designed on aifoundry2) and section 26's
"82 W run-time admission" are met **at the desk, as E161's were**, by M8's 95% peak rule. The peaks are G1Q **53.33 W** and G2Q **68.15 W**
(`predictions-q140e.json` › `E167.peak_rule_M8_unchanged_hi95_W`), margins of 28.67 W and **13.85 W** under 82 W. Neither hold is
duty-cycled or dropped. No run-time admission code exists in E167's kit, and none is added: nothing in the queue changes which tile runs.
The live stops (over 80 W on 3 consecutive samples; 72 °C on one) are stricter than section 26's general aifoundry2 stops (85 W on three,
76 °C). They govern, and the smoke tests them.

## The in-work boards (a nit)

E167.md's "the primary's highest simulated in-work boards are 45.8 and 65.3 W" are the values at E161's anchors (a 47.0 °C rest). Over the
frozen rest-die table (44-48 °C), G1Q's rises to **46.32 W at 48 °C** (`E167.by_rest_die.G1Q.48.0.primary_M9a_at_d.board_in_work_W_max_sim`);
G2Q's stays 65.32-65.34 W at every rest die. Nothing changes: both are far under the 80 W line.

## The collector

`collect_e167.sh` runs rsync and ssh with no timeout. It runs on the Intel, but a Tailscale check would hold the call until a person
answers it, and the round's section 9 says to stop at once. [`collect_e167_bounded.sh`](collect_e167_bounded.sh) is the same collector
with every call under `timeout` (900 s for the runs directory, 120 s for each small file and each box's history). It watches each call's
stderr once a second and stops at once, with exit 7, on a `login.tailscale.com` line. Its desk test, with fake ssh and rsync that never
connect, is [`logs/collect-bounded-desk-test.txt`](logs/collect-bounded-desk-test.txt): a normal pass, a hung rsync stopped at its
timeout, a Tailscale link stopping the run in 1-3 s, and a hung history read skipped. **It replaces `collect_e167.sh` on the card day**
(the kit README's dated note).

## What this does to predicting power and heat

Nothing in any prediction: `predictions-q140e.json` and its 26 inputs are byte-identical, and no threshold moves. X2 keeps E167's blind
test honest when a hold is cut short. A hold that ran hot cannot escape as "incomplete", and a hold cut by the clock cannot fail RK3-b or
RK3-c on a window it never had. A gated G1Q no longer lets RK3-c pass on G2Q alone.
