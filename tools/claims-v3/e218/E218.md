## 8. What E218 cannot say (stated before the data)

- **aifoundry3 card 0 is in every model's fit** (E206, E207, E210, E211, E213 and E215 arm A ran here): E218 tests never-run points on a known card, at
  its pinned 600 MHz; no card held out, no governed clock.
- **MM1-v3.2's time form is MM1-v3's refitted** (no layout term entered): H1-H3 and H6-H8 test refits, not a new time form; a NOT DECIDED is the
  expected reading whichever is better where the gaps are small (section 1).
- **MM1-v3.2 was not accepted by its own declaration** (rule 4(c)); it is frozen as built. A PASS here does not reverse that rule; a FAIL is a blind
  failure of a model its own rules had not accepted.
- **The gemmLnc term is a two-constant fit** (14 gemmLnc points in its data, aifoundry3 and aifoundry1 card 1 at 600 MHz; fp32 random but two int8 points):
  E218's G points are fp32 random in three of its regimes (no L2-resident point qualified); a G result does not separate its two constants, and the
  term's form (duplicate shire requests) is read only through W over idle. g07 has no twin (D1).
- **The layout:** MM1-v3.2 has no layout term, so the layout head-to-head HY1 cannot decide (D3); P1 tests MM1-v3.2's claim of no within-pair difference;
  the candidates' P1 is REPORTED. 5 of the 6 ranked Y pairs are one mask pair (0xf against 0x3030) at KB-24 shapes, and the controls one mask pair at two
  shapes: P1 reads mostly one placement contrast, not a range of them. y03 is incomplete (D1).
- **Family F's scored half is one shape family** (8 of 14 points `gemmL<MB>x16x3`), chosen by a score that is equal across MB but for rounding.
- **One session, one or two runs a point** (G twice in its block, Gt, Y and F twice in passes 1 and 2); no repeat across days; the tie t01 is the only
  bridge to earlier days.
- **The transistor switches are estimated, not measured**: no card counts switches; MM1-v3.2's switch outputs sit beside every point and are never
  scored. **No epilogue**; **a small GEMM's joules are a warm, repeated GEMM's**; **the meter's absolute scale**: one fixed gain per board, never set
  beside an outside meter.
- **Correlated errors** (a family's shared miss, a day's level) raise the sign tests' false-winner rates above the simulated ones; near-duplicate points
  (F's one shape family, Y's one mask pair, G's points sharing a regime) make a family's test closer to a test of its few configurations than of its
  point count.
