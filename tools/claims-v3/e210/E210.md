## 1. The question, and the answer frozen here

**MM1, the lab's matmul model, reports a matmul's transistor switches; nothing on a card counts switches** (MM1-v1 README section 7). A card can
check the count one way only: through energy when only the data changes. At one GEMM shape, format and shire mask the kernel runs the same
instructions on the same lines in the same cycles (the time equality, checked per block); only the operand words differ. So **the energy
difference between an operand pattern and random data at the same shape is data switching**, and if MM1's count is right, it is the count's
difference times one constant, plus the data part of the memory lines, which the count does not hold (MM1-v1 prices it through its own term,
phi). **E210 asks: for nine fp32 operand patterns never run in a GEMM, whose counted switches span from about a ninth of random's to beyond
random's, does the measured difference in joules over idle follow MM1's switch count - and does it follow it better than a bit-flip price, MM1-v1's
own kind term and a null?** Switches are never measured: the test is of the energy the switch count predicts.

**The patterns** (section 3): `negzero` (every word -0.0: every lane valid, nothing moves), `signs` (+-1.0), `lowmant8` and `highmant8` (1.0 with
8 random low or high mantissa bits: the same line statistics and operand flips, different switches), `bhalfeq` and `badjeq` (the same values,
B's equal pairs where a lane alternates or where it does not: the order the kernel feeds), `hd4` (successive operands 4 mantissa bits apart),
`maxalte` (maximal alternation, beyond random) and `arow50` (half the multiply-adds gated; reported only). **The card:** aifoundry3 card 0 at its
pinned 600 MHz, reads only, after E207 and its rest. **The shapes:** S1 `gemmL1x1x1` fp32 (1,024 x 512 x 512, held in the L2; MM1-v1 puts
12.1% of the data-dependent energy D in the lines) and S2 `gemmL1x1x4` fp32 (1,024 x 512 x 2,048, inside the L3; 22.6% in the lines).
**The order:** random in every block (chained A B B A), six passes, three blocks a test case, two a control; 171 runs, about
73 card-minutes (plan).

## 2. The kernel and the binary

**rgm136, E136's binary, unchanged** (`tools/claims-v3/e136/rgm136/`; no new source tree, **no host change, no kernel change**): the build E206 arm
A ran on aifoundry3, gated on the kernel's `.text` `61315bf6a449c2823fbffc33834b4c0a68aa19e8436a29c1124b15c04114b763` (the ELF
`aace138caf8071cc7679134211045a89fb09b65cc9a170bfe33409fd36b4fda0` as built on aifoundry2 and aifoundry1; aifoundry3's compiler writes another
version string into the ELF). Its sources by sha256 are in `kernel-e210.json` (`desk/kernel_e210.py --check` re-hashes them: PASS).

**The host string** (every run): `--test fma --type fp32 --pattern none --values <arm>:<gen>:<files> --shires 0xffffffff --per-shire 32
--seconds 6 --budget 7.5 --seed 1` (the kit adds `--kernel`, `--stop-file` and absolute paths), E206's exactly. random is `<arm>:randn:
tools/claims-v3/kinds/tiles/fp32/randn.bin` and sparse50 `<arm>:sparse50:.../sparse50.bin` (the host draws them, seed 1: E206's strings);
zeros and ones are `<arm>:tiles:.../zeros.bin` and `ones.bin`; **a pattern is `<arm>:tiles:` followed by its files**,
`tools/claims-v3/e210/tiles/fp32/<p>/<p>_00.bin` ... `_60.bin` (negzero: its one file). **The pool rule** (host/main.cpp, fillPools and its
check): the A pool's tile t and the B pool's tile t hold file t mod F; in the pool's packed order the A tile (r, k) of A block (mb, kb) is
t = (mb KB + kb) 2,048 + 32 r + k and the B tile (k, c) of B block (kb, nb) is t = (kb NB + nb) 1,024 + 32 k + c, so with F = 61 both operands
change at every op, as random's do (its pool holds 2,048 / 8,192 independent A tiles at S1 / S2). Every pattern is a law inside a tile, so the
cycled files carry it exactly.

**Each pattern's GEMM result against a reference.** The host checks C itself in every process (64 sampled C tiles of a short last launch against
its own double reference over the whole K: exact, approx within 1e-4 of the sum of the absolute products, or wrong; a wrong is rule R0's FAIL).
At the desk, `desk/check_gemm_e210.py` ran that comparison on the host's own pool rule and sampled tiles, C accumulated in the kernel's order with
the bench's TensorFMA arithmetic: **PASS** at both shapes for every pattern (arow50 S1 approx / S2 approx; badjeq S1 approx / S2 approx; bhalfeq S1 approx / S2 approx; hd4 S1 approx / S2 approx; highmant8 S1 approx / S2 approx; lowmant8 S1 approx / S2 approx; maxalte S1 approx / S2 approx; negzero S1 exact / S2 exact; signs S1 exact / S2 exact; 16,384 words a shape and pattern, the worst 1.6e-05 of the sum of the absolute products, lowmant8 at S2, against the host's 1e-4). Every committed tile passes its law bit for bit; no word is a NaN, an infinity or a
subnormal; -0.0 appears only in negzero (by design: the gate tests the all-zero word, finding 10's E15) and +0.0 only in arow50's odd A rows.
**Can a pattern change the kernel's path or its timing?** The kernel (sparsity.c, run_gemm_large) has no data-dependent branch; the tensor
unit's zero gate changes valid slots, not cycles (E206 at S1: zeros, sparse50, real and random within 3e-5 of each other in cycles an op,
measured); no denormal enters (the bench flushes them; none exists here). Rule T checks the cycles per block on the card.

**The operations a matmul contains**, as this kernel runs them at both shapes: tensor multiply-adds (4,096 MACs an op in fp32), loads of A and B
tiles (an op asks 64 A lines and 128 B lines), one TensorStore of C per C tile row (every 32 ops at S1, 128 at S2). No epilogue.

## 8. The rules (`score_e210.py`; a case with no complete block is NO DATA, never a failure)

| rule | what | registered threshold |
|---|---|---|
| **R0** the checks | every check of C of every run (blocks, ties, the probe, the smoke) exact or approx | one verdict; a FAIL labels every other verdict |
| **Q** the quorum | a block (R, P, P, R) is complete when its four runs ran, each with a counted launch, every check good, its host record the case's (arm, fp32, 1,024 minions, `0xffffffff`) and every counted sample at 590-610 MHz | a test case needs 2 complete blocks, a control or the reported case 1; else NO DATA |
| **T** the time equality | per complete block, the pattern runs' mean cycles an op within **+-0.1%** of the flanking random runs' mean, and their mean t_gemm within +-0.1% | a case passes when every complete block does; **a case that fails T is reported, not scored for switches** (out of B5, B10 and H) |
| **B5** the switch route's band | per scored test case, \|dJ_R1 - dJ\| <= max(**0.05 D_S**, 2 SE), D_S the committed D at the shape (S1 29.78 W, S2 20.53 W); every band absolute, so a case under about 3 W is treated as any other | **PASS when at least 75% of the scored test cases pass** (at least 6 scored, else TOO FEW; none: NO DATA) |
| **B10** (reported) | the same at 10% of D | REPORTED |
| **H1, H2, H3** the head to head | per scored test case, e = dJ - forecast for R1 and for R4 (H1), R3 (H2), R3L (H3); decided when \|\|e_R1\| - \|e_k\|\| >= 2 SE, else a tie (dropped) | the **exact two-sided sign test at 0.05** over the decided cases: R1 BETTER, Rk BETTER or UNDECIDED (n decided needs: 10 of 12, 11 of 13; under 6 it cannot decide) |

## 9. What E210 cannot say (stated before the data)

- **Switches are never measured.** The test is of the energy the switch count predicts, through one constant per shape: a pass says the count is
  proportional to the data energy over these patterns, a fail that it is not; neither says how many fins switched.
- **The counted class is the vector unit's alone** (MM1-v1's table: the multiply-add unit at gate level, the rest of the VPU by the same ratio, its
  clock nets). The memory lines' data part (S1 12.1%, S2 22.6% of D by MM1-v1), the regulators' loss and the in-run leakage rise are in the
  board's energy and outside the count; the line part is priced by MM1-v1's phi term (untested on the card's real line order), the rest absorbed by
  the constant in proportion.
- **The bench is not the silicon:** core-et's RTL (no full-custom register file), zero delay with one glitch ratio, Verilator 5.042; GEMM states
  sampled at 4 depths (S1) and 4 (S2); the card's TenB path not modelled.
- **ones on aifoundry3 is a transfer** from card 1; ones@S1 and ones@S2 run in E210 as controls and say how good the transfer was (reported).
- **Ungated patterns only are scored:** gating (arow50, sparse50, zeros) needs a clocked-bit price that committed data cannot check; R1g is
  reported. The clock part of the count cancels in every scored difference, so **E210 tests the signal-net part of MM1's count, not its clock
  part.**
- **One card, one clock, fp32, two shapes**, one session; no interval between sessions (E146's replicate of E136 is the lab's only spread between
  sessions on a GEMM: 0.2-0.4% in seconds, +-0.6% in W over idle).
- **The meter's absolute scale**: one fixed gain per board, never set beside an outside meter; the routes' constants absorb it, and fractions of D
  are unaffected.
- **The patterns are designed, not real data**; a real tensor's switching is E206's real kind (one point at S1, a calibration input here).
