# E122: E108's sign-location designs on a second card, with the accumulators read back (Q134, aifoundry3 card 0, 600 MHz)

*Pre-registered Sun 1 Nov 2026 (week 44), on the Intel, before the claim of aifoundry3 and before any E122 launch, in Q134
(lane `a3-e122-signs`). It bears on [the kinds page's section 11](https://spacesheep.dev/@yaroslavvb/horace-kinds-of-numbers#11-what-is-still-open),
**item 5, "Where a random sign's ~5 W goes"**, which [E108](../../../docs/reports/data/2026-11-01-e108-sign-located/README.md)
narrowed on one card (aifoundry1 card 1): the like for like on zero products ALIKE (R3), and in fp32 most of the rest going
with the 256 accumulators holding different values (R12 MOST 0.970 ± 0.013, R7 MOST 1.133 ± 0.090). Its own README's
may-not-say list includes "anything about other cards". The frozen numbers are in [`predictions-e122.json`](predictions-e122.json),
written by [`make_predictions_e122.py`](make_predictions_e122.py) from the committed files it names (every number read from
its file, never retyped; each file checked by sha256), and committed with this file. Any change after this commit goes in
the Amendments section at the end, dated, before the data it touches. Card: **aifoundry3 card 0 only** (no `V3_DEVICE`;
lib.sh names the card `aifoundry3` and takes `/run/lock/etsoc-shire0.lock`), 600 MHz latched by its boot-time TDP of 0,
~522 mV (E78: 521.7 mV), TDP 0 / threshold 65 °C as read, never set. E122 writes no knob: no governor move, no TDP,
threshold or clock set, no reset, no PCIe, driver, firmware or PMIC work, no `--reset-ms`.*

## The question

Do E108's designed tiles, runner and rules give E108's verdicts, and its watts by E82's card scale, on a second card? Above
all: on aifoundry3, does a held tile whose accumulators differ as a walking tile's do still carry most of the walking tile's
rest (R7, R12), are fp32's and fp16's zero-product shares still alike in one session (R3), and do the accumulators still read
back as designed (R1)? E122 adds no tile and changes no line of E108's. Where E108 compared against a card-1 number, E122
compares against this card's (below).

### X: the cross-card rules

For 26 of E108's registered quantities Q: **d = Q(E122) − s · Q(E108)** and **h = √(h122² + (s · h108)²)**, the two sessions'
95% half-widths in quadrature, where s is E82's per-type card scale for a W contrast (`docs/reports/data/2026-10-31-e82-kinds-aifoundry1/scored.json`
› `cross_card.primary.x3.per_type_median`: **fp32 1.030017, fp16 1.033232**) and 1 for a ratio or a difference of ratios.
**PASS if |d| + h ≤ w; FAIL if |d| − h > w; else INCONCLUSIVE; UNRESOLVED below 2 passes.** **w = 0.75 W** for the 17 W
contrasts (the steps, the held parts, `Bz_het`, `Bz_rest`, `het_mx`, `rest`, the R8 legs, the alternation contrasts, A on
B held and walking, `A_vs_B_priced`), as E121 used; **w = 0.15** for the seven shares and share differences (`fp32.share_z`,
`fp16.share_z`, `fp32.share_abs`, `formats_z`, `design_fp32`, `fp32.share_het`, `fp32.share_het_z`): half the 0.30 width
of R7's ABOUT HALF band, and 1.5 times R3's ALIKE band; **w = 0.30** for `fp32.alt_ratio` and **0.60** for
`alt_walk_ratio` (their E108 intervals are ±0.19 and ±0.48). **The aggregate: PASS if no X rule FAILs and at least 13 of
26 PASS; FAIL if 3 or more FAIL; else INCONCLUSIVE.** The table of every X rule (E108's value, the scaled prediction, w,
and its PASS/FAIL/INCONCLUSIVE probability at n = 2, 3, 4 passes, simulated at E108's per-pass sd) is
`predictions-e122.json` › `cross_card.rules`. If every quantity replicates exactly, the aggregate PASSes in essentially every
simulated session at 3 or 4 passes and in 12% at 2 (`aggregate_power_true_delta0`); the rules most likely to read
INCONCLUSIVE are `fp32.alt_walk` and `alt_walk_ratio` (E108's ±0.62 W and ±0.48), A on B held (±0.51 W),
`fp32.alt_held`, `fp32.share_het` and `fp32.alt_ratio`. A real difference smaller than about w reads INCONCLUSIVE, by design.

**Reported, never a verdict:** every quantity and tile with its rail figure and its ratio to E108's; this session's card
scale (D of `fp32.randn`, `fp16.randn`, `int8.full`, `anchor_pi` over E108's); every M4 miss; how many of E108's 13 verdicts
come out the same; the dips removed per pass; every block's two `ettelem config` reads and the session-start and session-end
reads. No pass is added after any reduction is read; no pass is chosen after the fact; the arms are not a fallback.

**What the rules cannot say.** Two cards of one design, with E108's one fixed set of designed tiles (rank-one accumulator
patterns beside random ±1 operands, not a real tensor). The location is a statement about watts on the board, paired within
passes; which nets carry it is the bench's model, not a measurement. E108's post hoc readings (R12 priced with the
alternation counts, 0.68–1.05; the fJ per differing bit) are not rules here and are not re-derived as verdicts.

## The session, the smoke gate and the read-back

Right after the claim edit, one detached script, `session_e122.sh`: `et-who` (the card must be free); the session-start
`ettelem config` (read only, under the card lock, `timeout 10`); **the smoke**, `block_e108.sh 12201 --smoke` unchanged
(E108's eight 2 s runs: `anchor_pi`, `fp32.c_mx_f`, `fp32.b_B_w`, `fp32.s_walk`, `fp32.zeros`, `fp32.randn`, `fp16.z_s`,
`fp16.zeros`, at the current reading, no preheat). **The gate PASSes if the block ended ok, all eight items ran, every run
is ok on 1,024 minions, cycles per op are within 0.5% of 546, and both of its config reads say TDP 0 W, threshold 65 °C,
600 MHz.** If it fails, no read-back and no pass runs, and the claim is released. Then **the read-back**,
`block_readback_e122.sh 12202` (96 launches, each under `timeout 10`, under the card lock; about 40 s here, by E116's 22
reads in 8.4 s); a failed read is recorded and the session goes on to the passes, which do not depend on it. Then the passes,
then `et-who` and the session-end `ettelem config`.

## Card budget

**At most 40 card-minutes from the claim edit to its release edit**, smoke, read-back and every heating gate included. The
session's budget is **in seconds, anchored on the claim edit's time** (`E122_T0`): a pass starts only if now + projected ≤
T0 + 2,400 − 240 s and ≤ 23:15 PST, where projected is the longest measured pass so far, 450 s until the first one ends
(E108's passes took 393–439 s on card 1; E121's 42-launch passes on this card 378–404 s, so a 47-launch pass is about
7–7.5 min). A pass in flight is never cut. With the smoke and the read-back (about 2 min), four passes fit; at 8 minutes a
pass, three. The 240 s reserve covers the session-end read and the release edit. The claim is a clause in the day's one
`#community-lab` post (`for 1 h`: 40 minutes plus a margin, rounded up to the half hour), added and later dropped or marked
released by editing that post (gyroscope `_request/request-aifoundry.md` §4). No new card session after 22:30 PST; every run
ends by 23:30 PST.

## Self-test

[`selftest_e122.py`](selftest_e122.py) builds reductions from E108's own per-pass D on card 1 times E82's scale (plus 0.03 W
noise) and synthetic read-backs from `e108.json`'s registered words, and scores five worlds: **replicate** (13 of 13 of
E108's verdicts, R1 PASS, X PASS with no FAIL), **no_location** (`c_mx_f` read as `s_abs`, `b_B_z` as `z_s`: R7 and R12
LITTLE, X-fp32.share_het and X-fp32.share_het_z FAIL, X aggregate FAIL), **two** passes (scored), **one** pass (every power
rule NOT SCORED, R1 still scored), and three read-back faults (one tile's words off: R1 FAIL; one minion disagreeing:
UNREAD; no reads: UNREAD). [`selftest_e122.txt`](selftest_e122.txt): 17 of 17 checks, run on the Intel before this commit.
The reduction path is E108's `reduce_e108.py`, unchanged, which `selftest_e108.py` tested on E78's generator. Nothing it
prints is data.

## How to run it (for this lane)

On the Intel, after this commit is on origin/main, copy `tools/claims-v3/signloc/` and `tools/claims-v3/e122/` to
aifoundry3's `~/aifoundry/tools/claims-v3/` (E78's card files are already there, byte-identical to their freeze:
`run_e122.sh setup` checks). On aifoundry3, from `~/aifoundry`:

```bash
bash tools/claims-v3/e122/run_e122.sh setup              # no device access: hashes, tiles, dry runs, a stub session
# -> add the clause to the day's #community-lab post: "using aifoundry3 for 1 h, until ~HH:MM PST [doc](<…>)"
bash tools/claims-v3/e122/run_e122.sh go <claim epoch>   # detached: smoke + gate, read-back, passes, session-end read
bash tools/claims-v3/e122/run_e122.sh wait               # bounded (540 s) waits until "session done"
```

Then copy `build/claims-v3/aifoundry3/signloc/p122*`, `signloc-smoke/p12201`, `signloc-readback/p12202`,
`build/e122-session.log` and `build/e122-logs/` to the Intel, drop or mark the clause released, and run
`PY=python3 nice -n 10 bash tools/claims-v3/e122/collect_e122.sh 'p122[1-9][0-9]' <copy>`; copy
`build/e122-out/2026-11-01-e122-signs-replicate/` to `docs/reports/data/` and write its README (results first; registered
against happened; departures dated).
