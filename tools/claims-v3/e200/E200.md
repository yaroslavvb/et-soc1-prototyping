**C1, THE CLOCK RULE (E194's form, for E197's device processes), in place of E197's 600 MHz gate and its kit's 600 MHz stop.**
- **A train process counts only if** every telemetry sample from its first launch's start + 1.5 s to its last launch's end reads
  **800 MHz** (within 1 MHz, E197's own tolerance), **and** its last launch ran at **780-810 MHz by its own count** (its `cycles_max`
  over its `wall_s`). Otherwise the process is **NOT COUNTED** (it gives no value, under E197's reducer's own reason "clock"), and it
  is **never re-run**. A sample with no clock field is not judged, as in E197's gate. The 1.5 s allow the governor's climb (at most
  1.325 s in E190 arm A's 84 processes); the own count's band is E194's: E192's 3,255 committed launches of this host family on
  aifoundry3 read 0.99652-0.99951 of their clock by that count, 797.2-799.6 MHz at 800, while a launch at 700 MHz reads about 699
  (`desk/clock-window-e200.txt` 4).
- **The rate's clock.** E197's reducer computes R at the session's median clock; for E200 that median must read 800 MHz within 1 MHz,
  else every process of that session is set aside by the clock (a card whose governor never stepped up gives no E200 datum, where
  E197's reducer would have scored it at 600 MHz: `desk/synth-e200.txt` case Z1).
- E197.md 8's row "every sample of a train process within 1 MHz of the session's clock (600 MHz)" is read as this rule; E197's kit's
  live stop "a minion clock other than 600 MHz ... on three samples in a row" is replaced by C3.

**C2, nothing is read before a device process's first launch's start + 2.0 s** (THE CLOCK RULE's 1.5 s for the climb, and half a
second more for the board meter to follow the clock's step: E197's reducer treats a low reading as held for a whole meter pass, two
or three samples, E197.md 7).
- **A train process is read on its quads 2 and 3** (launches 5-12), **never on quad 1** (launches 1-4). Under W1, launch 4 starts

## 15. What this cannot say (stated before the data)

- **Two cards and two clocks at once.** E200 against E197 changes the card (aifoundry2 against aifoundry3), the clock (800 against 600
  MHz, with their voltages) and the die; the ratio of a price says how far it moved, not which of the three moved it. v2(800) and E190's
  reading are set beside as references, not as a decomposition.
- **The climb costs precision.** C2 reads two quads a process where E197 reads three: each train's standard error about 1.22 times
  E197's for the same noise, so a price E197 resolves can read NOT RESOLVED or BOUNDED here with no change in the card.
- **T15 is at risk of the cap** (section 10): if the climbs run long, T15, `field_bit3` and `lin_field` are not run.
- **The core rail's figures carry the climb.** E197's rail share divides the rail over a burst's last 1.5 s by the fraction a first-order
  filter reaches after a step at the burst's first launch; under the governor the clock's step comes up to 1.5 s later, so the rail is
  still rising there by an amount that depends on each burst's own climb, and the per-block shares scatter. On the synthetic session (an
  arbitrary rail share of 0.60) the eight per-block shares read 0.25-0.82 and their median 0.71 (`desk/synth-e200.txt`, G1's line and
  section 18 item 6). The rail share, the rail pJ and the electrons through the rail are REPORTED with that caveat; the board prices,
  which decide every verdict, are not touched.
- **The die drifts within a block** (section 9: about 55-62 C by the card's sustained line): leakage moves with it, slowly; the quads'
  A B B A form cancels a linear drift, as in E197, but the absolute level of every anchor carries the block's temperature.
- **The governor's own steps.** A process whose clock leaves 800 MHz after its climb is set aside, not corrected; if the die passes 65 C
  the sitting ends early (C3). E200 has no data at 700 MHz and reads none.
- **A launch's own count rests on this host family's overhead** (E192: 0.99652-0.99951 of the clock); a different overhead on this box's
  build would show in the smoke's own counts, which the kit reads before any train (C3).
- **aifoundry2 is the lab's card for the governor's behaviour, its meter one fixed gain**; each price comes from one card and one or two
  sittings. E197.md 14's limits hold here too (the board-only trains, T3 and T4 pricing the whole result path, stored-value leakage
  riding on T9 and the `d64` kinds, T7 and T14's front-end caveat, the read-port arms' reach, the L1 data miss count's entry and exit).
- **The sealed forecast:** E200's lab files say nothing of it; the model session scores it.
