## What it does to predicting power and heat

M7 says what aifoundry2's governor does with a kind of number in a 6 s burst, from the tile's bytes alone: **the clock it settles at**
(E136's relation P(f) = B(f) + c·s(f), s(f) = (V_f/0.517 V)²·f/600, c = c_zeros + D/1.01865 with D M7's power over the type's zeros,
and E83's dither about an effective cap W* = 65.58 W ± 2.75 W), **the board power at that clock**, and **the die's rise** (0.140 °C per
board W over a 21.5 W idle). Its error so far is in-sample to the cap: end to end over E80's and E83's 56 kinds bursts the cap was fitted
on, 8.5 MHz and 1.86 W rms; where Q138(m)'s verification split the bursts by the clock they ran at, 13.9 MHz and 2.35 W on the 21
governed, 0.61 MHz and 1.50 W on the 35 at 800 MHz; heat 0.68 °C leave-one-kind-out (`m7.json` › `aifoundry2`; `posthoc-m7.json` ›
`aifoundry2_split`). **E143 measures the out-of-sample error on 36 kinds no fit saw**, by the group M7 puts each in before the run: the
19 tiles it predicts **governed** (below 795 MHz), where the clock term is tested, and the 17 it predicts **pinned** at 795–800 MHz,
where only "stays at 800 MHz" and the power at 800 MHz are. Split the same way, the reference rows give **7.6 MHz and 1.94 W rms
(governed, 17 rows) and 8.9 MHz and 1.83 W (pinned, 39 rows, the four bursts predicted at 800 MHz that ran at 761–779 MHz among them)**.
The before/after the owner's goal needs is that reference against E143's blind rms, per group, for the clock, the board power and the
rise.

## The question

1. **Does M7 predict the governed burst clock on new kinds as well as its reference rows say?** (F1), and do the tiles it puts at
   800 MHz stay there? (F2). Do its frozen ±21.3 MHz bands hold? (F3)
2. **Does it predict the board power at that clock?** Governed (W1), pinned (W2), its ±3.37 W band (W3).
3. **Does the burst heat law predict the die's rise?** (H1), its ±1.41 °C band (H3).
4. Has the card drifted since E80 and E83? (R0, a diagnostic)

## What runs

**The harness is E80's, the one M7's aifoundry2 term was fitted on.** M7's burst clock, board power and rise were fitted on E80's and
E83's kinds bursts, and Q138(x)'s file says its aifoundry2 predictions are for "a 6 s burst of the kinds harness, E80's harness". So the
runner is E80's chain as E146 ran it on this card an hour before (E64's `run_hpl.py`, E80's `run_kspeed.py`, E101's `run_e101s.py`,
E105's admission rule `run_e105.py`, E119's `run_e119.py` with E111's idle-login rule), imported unchanged through
[`run_e143.py`](run_e143.py), which adds one thing: pool 5's tiles as run values, each checked against `blind5.json` (2,048 B; sha256 of
the whole, of A and of B) and the three frozen inputs checked by sha256 before the card lock is taken. E140's contribution is the blind
scoring's form (§ Decision rules: the rms against the bootstrap of the model's own out-of-sample errors, and the binomial coverage
line) and its tile check; E140's own harness (E78's 4.5 s runs at a pinned 600 MHz) is not the shape these predictions are for. The
programs are E80's binaries, checked by sha256 before the session: `build/sparsity_t2/host/sparsity_host` `7adadf4f…6ac0d2`,
`build/sparsity_t2/kernel/sparsity.elf` `990807f4…fd6ea24`, `build/ettelem-hpl/ettelem` `77a46171…accfeb5` (E80's `s1/session.json`).

**A burst** is E80's BURST block: one process of `sparsity_host --test fma --type <t> --values file:<tile>` on 1,024 minions for 6 s
(budget 7.5 s), started from a die reading ≤ 55 °C (waits up to 300 s), a 4 s tail, the sampler every 100 ms **without `--reset-ms`**.
**Read as E80 and E136 read it** (`reduce_hpl.run_metrics` and `reduce_kspeed.burst_extra`, imported unchanged by
[`reduce_e143.py`](reduce_e143.py)): `burst_mhz`, the mean sampled clock from 1.5 s after the first launch to the end of the work;
`burst_w`, the mean board W over the same samples; `die_start` and `die_max`, the first and the largest die reading inside the work;
the rise = `die_max − die_start`. The kernel's own clock (its cycles over its wall time from 1.5 s on) is reported beside the sampled one.

**The session** (queue [`queue_e143.py`](queue_e143.py), detached on aifoundry2, E146's queue with E143's names): the box's state and
`ettelem config` (start); the smoke (the idle card for 30 s, the rest reading; one 2 s launch of each type's zeros anchor, each with its
check of C and the kernel's cycles per op within 10% of 546 / 546 / 318); **the two stop-path tests on live samples** (below); then block
BURST: **3 passes, each the 36 pool tiles and E80's three replicate tiles** (`fp32/randn`, `fp16/randn`, `int8/full` of
`tools/claims-v3/kinds/tiles/`), each pass in its own seeded shuffled order (order_seed 143; run_hpl's `load_protocol` shuffles a pass
at a time, so a pass is whole before the next starts), 117 bursts; `ettelem config` (end). The session's cap, 38 card-minutes, cuts pass
3's tail if the gates run long (`run_e119.py` starts a run only if its planned time fits).

**The stops** (E136's registered stops, under the keys `run_hpl.py`'s watcher reads, as E146 wrote them): one die reading (the minion
shires' mean or hottest sensor) at or above **80 °C**, or the board above **93 W on three samples in a row**, ends the session at once
(exit 4). The watcher reads each live sampler line as it is written. **The admission rule** (`run_e105.py`): a burst runs only if its
frozen estimate of the first second at 800 MHz, B(800) + s(800)·(c_zeros + D/1.01865) with M7's D, is at most **86 W** (stricter than
E101's 88 W); after any run that read 93 W, nothing at or above its estimate runs. The pool's highest estimate is 84.3 W
(`fp32.wideexp48_dense`), E80's fp32 randn 83.1 W (the highest it read); the highest first-second reading of any kinds burst on this card
so far is 83.9 W (E83, `fp32/randn_small`), and its governor brings every such burst to its cap within about a second. Nothing here
drives the card toward a PMIC over-power or over-temperature reset beyond what E80, E83 and E146 ran.

**The stop-path tests** (E146's; "registered stops must read live samples ... test the stop path in the smoke"): after the smoke and
before the session, the same runner, watcher and sampler with one stop lowered, so that it must fire on live samples:
**STOPTEST-BOARD** (`board_cap_w` 30 W on three samples, one 6 s burst of the pool's `fp32.anchor_randn`): the queue passes it only if
the session ended with exit 4 for "board", the trip came while the launch ran and within 1.5 s of the third live line over 30 W, and the
launch ended within 3 s of the trip. **STOPTEST-DIE** (`abs_stop_c` 30 °C): only if it ended with exit 4 for "die" within 1.5 s of the
first live line, before any launch. Either failing halts the queue with no session (`QUEUE-HALT`).

## Decision rules

Over counted runs (every launch ran and passed its check of C; `burst_mhz`, `burst_w`, `die_start`, `die_max` exist). A tile's measured
value is its mean over its counted passes; a tile with fewer than 2 is left out and named. **Fewer than 2 counted passes in all: every
rule NOT SCORED.** A tile's group is frozen in `predictions-e143.json` (governed: M7's `burst_mhz` < 795 MHz; 19 tiles, 14 of them
priority 1; pinned: 17, all 11 int8 tiles among them). "Bootstrap" = the 2.5–97.5% range of the rms of n errors drawn with replacement
from the reference errors, n the tiles scored (20,000 draws, seeded; `e143_common.boot_rms_range`): **PASS** at or below the 97.5%
point, **FAIL** above; below the 2.5% point is reported as "better than the reference says". "Coverage" = **PASS** if the number of tiles
inside M7's frozen 95% band is at least the 2.5% point of Binomial(n, 0.95) (E140-P3's line; 31 of 36).

| rule | quantity | set (at least) | reference | verdict |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| **E143-F1** | burst clock | governed (8) | M7's end-to-end clock errors on the 17 kinds bursts it predicted governed (7.59 MHz rms) | bootstrap |
| **E143-F2** | burst clock | pinned (8) | the 39 it predicted pinned (8.87 MHz rms) | bootstrap |
| **E143-F3** | burst clock | all 36 (10) | M7's frozen band (±21.3 MHz, 800 the top) | coverage |
| **E143-W1** | board W in the burst | governed (8) | the 17 governed rows' board errors (1.94 W rms) | bootstrap |
| **E143-W2** | board W in the burst | pinned (8) | the 39 pinned rows' board errors (1.83 W rms) | bootstrap |
| **E143-W3** | board W in the burst | all 36 (10) | M7's frozen band (±3.37 W) | coverage |
| **E143-H1** | die rise (die_max − die_start) | all 36 (10) | the burst heat law's 112 leave-one-kind-out residuals on E80's and E83's kinds bursts (0.6845 °C rms, recomputed exactly as `fit_m7.py` computes them) | bootstrap |
| **E143-H3** | die rise | all 36 (10) | M7's frozen band (±1.41 °C) | coverage |
| **E143-R0** | E80's three replicate tiles | 3 | each within 25 MHz (E80-R4's bar) and 3.37 W of E80's and E83's mean cell (fp32 randn 713.3 MHz, 68.99 W; fp16 randn 727.7 MHz, 66.59 W; int8 full 800 MHz, 49.80 W) | PASS / FAIL, a diagnostic: it qualifies the verdicts above, never changes them |

**Reported, never a verdict** (`results.json`): every tile's passes; M6's and M5's rms on every set beside M7's, and the paired
difference M7 − M6 and M7 − M5 with a bootstrap interval over tiles; the burst D (a tile's `burst_w` less its type's zeros anchor's
`burst_w` in the same pass) against each model's `burst_D_w` and band; the heat law on the measured board W (the rise less 0.140 °C/W ×
(measured `burst_w` − 21.5 W)), which separates the heat law from the power error; the sets split by the clock measured instead;
priority-1 tiles alone; per format; each pass scored alone; the kernel's own clock; the session's peaks.

**Why these lines, before the data.** They are E115's and E140's, so the three blind tests read alike: the reference errors are the only
honest estimate M7 has of its error on this card, and the interval is what they imply for that many new tiles. They are M7's end to end
from its nested D through the frozen cap, as its frozen bands were sized; the cap was fitted on those bursts, so the reference flatters
the model (Q138(m)'s C1: with the cap left out per kind, 8.64 MHz and 1.88 W against 8.50 and 1.86), and a blind FAIL is the more
informative outcome. The split is by the group M7 predicts, because that is all that is known before the run; Q138(m)'s verification
split by the clock measured, which leaves the bursts the model wrongly put at 800 MHz out of the pinned set, and its 13.9 MHz is quoted
as such. The heat reference is per run, and a tile here is a mean of up to three passes, so H1's line is, if anything, lenient.

## Precision, before the data

At the full plan (19 governed, 17 pinned, 36 in all) the bootstrap lines are: **F1 4.66–10.23 MHz**, **F2 0.36–15.47 MHz**, **W1
1.41–2.43 W**, **W2 1.25–2.44 W**, **H1 0.52–0.84 °C**; coverage 31 of 36 (16 of 19 governed, 14 of 17 pinned, reported). F2's line is
wide because four of its 39 reference rows are bursts M7 put at 800 MHz that ran at 761–779 MHz: one such tile in 17 would still pass
it. **How well one pass repeats** (E80's and E83's two passes of each cell, `burst_mhz_runs`): on the 17 cells M7 predicts governed the
two passes differ by 3.6–32.6 MHz (median 8.8), a single pass's sd about 9.9 MHz, so a three-pass mean carries about 5.7 MHz of pass
noise and the reference's two-pass cells about 7 MHz; the reference's 7.6 MHz rms is near that floor. On the 39 cells it predicts
pinned, 33 ran both passes at 800 MHz and four were **bistable**, one pass at 800 MHz and the other at 721–755 MHz (`E80:fp32.nn_like`,
`E80:int8.neg_ones`, `E83:int8.sorted` and one more): a pinned tile can go governed in one pass of three, and F2 is the rule that sees
it. E80 and E83 keep no per-pass board power in their cells, so the board's pass noise is not stated here; E143 reports its own.

## What the rules cannot say

- **The pinned tiles test little of the clock term**: at 800 MHz the clock cannot move up, and a pinned tile's board power tests D and
  the power at 800 MHz. F1 and W1 are the clock-and-voltage term's test.
- **The reference is in-sample to the cap** (above). The models differ on this card only through D, by at most about 1 W in the
  burst, so M7 against M6 and M5 is reported, not ruled.
- **The burst heat law's window is a 6 s burst's**, die max less die start in whole °C readings; it is not the 4.5 s window of E141 and
  E142, and it does not test the long-hold model (E144).
- One card, one firmware build (BL2 0.22.0), one evening; the die starts are gated at ≤ 55 °C, not fixed.

## Card budget and time

At most **50 card-minutes** (the brief): the smoke and the two stop tests about 3 min, the session's cap 38 min (117 bursts at about 14 s
each are about 27 min; E146 ran 104 runs in 25.1 min on this card). The day's one `#community-lab` post gets this card's clause, `using
aifoundry2 for 1 h, until ~HH:MM PST`, before the first launch, and loses it at the release. Q138's gates: no new card session after
23:00 PST, every run ends by 23:50 PST; the queue's slot ends before 23:50 and its hard stop (the stop file 120 s before the slot's end,
SIGTERM at 30 s) holds it there. If the Tailscale approval lapses (about 22:00 PST), the detached queue keeps running and the data is
collected when ssh answers again (section 9's protocol). An idle login of another user does not block (the owner's ruling of 1 Nov
2026, E111's rule in the runner); any other user's activity, device process or lock ends the session at once.
