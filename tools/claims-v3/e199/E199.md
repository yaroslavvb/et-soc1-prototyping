## 1. The question

**Do E192's per-arm prices, measured on aifoundry3 card 0, hold on aifoundry1 card 1 - and within what?** X01 prices one flipped bit and
one carry position on the scalar integer path of every minion: 31 arm-against-control contrasts in pJ an instruction, and 24 ladder
quantities built from them (the f2 count ladder's slope, the field prices, the `and` fields, the two carry ladders' slopes, C1, C2, C3 and
their checks), each on two meters (the 12 V input and the core rail). E192 measured them on aifoundry3 card 0. E199 measures them again on
card 1 with nothing changed but the card, and scores card 1's values against aifoundry3's by two registered rules: **T1**, each price within
the two values' combined 95% interval; **T2**, each price within its combined interval after one card-to-card scale inside a stated
allowance, the family's fitted scale itself inside that allowance (section 6). A PASS says E192's prices are card-portable at E192's
resolution (T1), or up to a scale the lab has already seen between these two cards (T2); a FAIL says card 1 differs beyond that.

### 6.2 Decided, or NOT DECIDED

A price is **decided** when card 1's reducer output has it with **n >= 4** measured passes and an SE, and card 1's **SE is no larger than 3
times aifoundry3's** for the same price. Otherwise it is **NOT DECIDED**, with its reason recorded: no card-1 value (the arm or its control
dropped in every pass, a stop before it ran); fewer than 4 measured passes, or no SE; or card 1's SE above 3 times aifoundry3's (card 1 could
not test the price at E192's resolution: a noisy session never passes by its noise).

*Changed before any datum (verify-e199 F3; Mon 16 Nov 2026, lane fix-e199):* the first form decided a price at **n >= 2**. With card 1
drawn from E192's own prices and no card difference at all, E199-T1 then read FAIL in 54% of draws at 2 measured passes and 16% at 3
(verify-e199's simulation, REPORTED), so a session cut short by a stop or the cap could FAIL by chance. At 4 passes the same simulation,
re-run on this scorer, reads 8.5% for E199-T1, and 1.5% at 8 (6.8, REPORTED). A session cut short before its fourth measured pass now
decides no price: NO DATA, never a FAIL. The scorer reads the 4 from `forecast-e199.json` > `rules` > `n_min`; T1 and T2 keep the lab's
z = 2.

### 6.3 T1: within the combined 95% interval

For each decided price, with m3 +- se3 aifoundry3's and m1 +- se1 card 1's: **holds when |m1 - m3| <= 2 sqrt(se1^2 + se3^2)**, the lab's 95%
interval (mean +- 2 SE, E192.md section 6) of the difference of two independent means.

### 6.4 T2: within a stated allowance for a card-to-card scale

- **The allowance: s in [0.943145, 1.03707]**, the smallest and largest of twelve named card-1-over-aifoundry3 ratios and 1, each read at
  its committed sha256 by `forecast_e199.py` and frozen in `forecast-e199.json` > `allowance`:
  - **(1) the blind day's three scalar-integer programs, measured on both cards** with the same hosts, arguments and counts: J over idle a
    launch (the mean of four passes), E191's second session on card 1 (`7156ac15`) over E189 on aifoundry3 (`fa9098c4`): SHA-256 0.964493,
    sort sorted 1.03707, sort shuffled 0.952967;
  - **(2) the energy catalogue's per-card means** (the three-card check, 25-26 Sep 2026) for the rows of E192's own ops, card 1 over
    aifoundry3: add 1.00331 (random) and 1.02087 (zeros), xor 0.982623 and 0.985238, and 0.973817 and 1.03007, nop 0.982653 and
    0.943145 (both harts), add with one hart 1.02000 (random).
  - *Why these and not the idle laws or E190's arms:* the idle laws price the card at rest, which every contrast subtracts (they set the
    predicted peak instead, section 7); E190's arm A was aifoundry2 at 800 MHz and its arm B card 1 at 600 MHz, a pair that does not
    include aifoundry3. The two sources above are the lab's direct card-1-against-aifoundry3 readings of scalar integer switching energy at
    600 MHz. Copies and sparse parity (1.106-1.144) are left out: they are dominated by DRAM, tensor and on-chip memory traffic, which no X01
    arm makes.
- **The family's one scale:** s* is the scale in the allowance under which the most of the family's decided prices hold (found exactly: the
  count is read at the allowance's ends, at 1, at the clipped s_hat below, at every solution of every price's boundary equation inside the allowance and
  at the midpoints between them; ties go to the value nearest the clipped s_hat, then nearest 1). Because 1 is in the allowance, **T2 never
  holds fewer prices than T1.** (`forecast-e199.json` > `rules` > `T2_words` says the same since its re-make before any
  datum: verify-e199 F1.)
- **Per price, T2 holds when |m1 - s* m3| <= 2 sqrt(se1^2 + (s* se3)^2).**
- **THE SCALE:** the weighted least-squares s_hat = sum(w m1 m3) / sum(w m3^2), w = 1 / (se1^2 + se3^2), over the family's decided prices;
  its SE is 1 / sqrt(sum(w m3^2)), times sqrt(chi2 / (D - 1)) when that is above 1 (chi2 = sum of w (m1 - s_hat m3)^2: the scatter about one
  scale widens it). **The scale holds when s_hat +- 2 SE meets the allowance.** s_hat and its SE are reported for every family.

### 6.6 The verdicts

For a family of N registered prices, **K = ceil(0.85 N)** and **F = N - K + 1** (31: 27 and 5; 24: 21 and 4); D prices decided, H holding, M =
D - H missing:

| rule | NO DATA | PASS | FAIL | NOT DECIDED |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| T1 | D = 0 | H >= K | M >= F | otherwise |
| T2 | D = 0 | H >= K at s* and the scale holds | M >= F at s*, or D >= K and the scale misses the allowance | otherwise |

A PASS and a FAIL cannot both occur (K + F = N + 1). **The headline is family A's two verdicts, E199-T1 and E199-T2**; the other six are
registered and reported beside them, and none overrides another. A FAIL is reported as FAIL.
