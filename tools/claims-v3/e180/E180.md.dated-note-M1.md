## In short

- **The model's reading.** october-forecast-model says that on one-number vectors fadd.ps switches no data line in steady state, for any
  value: the only modelled differences are the bubbles between instructions (2 x the popcount of the sum per bubble) and the held
  state's leakage, and they nearly cancel. On aifoundry3, 5s cost **+9.09 fJ** more than 4s per instruction (8 lanes): +27.38 fJ in the
  bubbles and -18.29 fJ in the leakage. Above the fadd.ps zeros, 4s cost 65.03 fJ and 5s 74.12 fJ (5s/4s 1.1398).

  **+0.0042 W**, beside E180's registered P of **+0.1649 W**: P is 39.6x the model. The model's 4s and 5s above zeros are +0.0298 and
  +0.0340 W, beside P's +0.330 and +0.495 W.

  words it misses a value-dependent static term.
- **How it is read.** After E180's data, against E180-H's interval in E180.md section 5's SIZE words, and beside the 4s and 5s kinds' D.

## 3. What differs between 4s and 5s: the model's words and its ledger

The model's statement, verbatim:

> **fadd.ps**: with one-number vectors no data line switches in steady state, for any value. The only modelled differences are the bubbles between instructions (2 x popcount of the sum per bubble: 8.0 has 2 one-bits, 10.0 has 3) and the leakage of the held state. They nearly cancel: 5s cost about 9 fJ more per instruction, about 1e-4 of the instruction's modelled energy.

The model's ledger of one fadd.ps instruction on one-number vectors, per card, gives these 4s and 5s figures and 5s/4s ratios. For 4s
and 5s no card data exist; the ledger closes fadd.ps against the lab's constant 1.0 kind.
