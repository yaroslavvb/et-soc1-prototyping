## 0. The answer this registration expects, in one table

A **one-number vector add** is the energy catalogue's own fadd loop (`workloads/enercat`, mode `fadd.ps`) with all eight source
registers holding one number v in every lane: every instruction computes v + v in eight lanes, on both harts of all 1,024 minions.
No operand bit ever changes, and every result is the same. What switches, in the open RTL, is the write-back: txfma's result is
AND-ed with its valid bit, so at every cycle without a result the write-back bus and its three bypass copies fall to zero, and rise
again at the next result. Each 1-bit of the result costs a fall and a rise there. **So a number costs power in proportion to the
1-bits of v + v, not to its size.**

| per instruction (8 lanes), aifoundry3, 600 MHz | 4 + 4 = 8.0 | 5 + 5 = 10.0 | 5s minus 4s |
|---|---:|---:|---:|
| result word, its 1-bits | 0x41000000, 2 | 0x41200000, 3 | +1 |
| (i) bits flipped, the whole-minion bench (named nets) | 49.99 | 74.98 | +25.00 |
| of which: txfma's result / RF write data / three bypass registers | 10.0 / 10.0 / 30.0 | 15.0 / 15.0 / 45.0 | |
| per lane | 6.25 | 9.37 | +3.12 |
| P (primary): watts above the fadd.ps zeros, the catalogue's random-row price | +0.330 +- 0.128 | +0.495 +- 0.192 | **+0.165 W** |
| arms: C (the const row's price) / E (E60's measured result-path price) / F / A | | | +0.220 / +0.069 / +0.035 / +0.019 W |
| chance the card reads it POSITIVE (6 passes), if P / C / E is right | | | 0.88 / 0.98 / 0.26 |

**The ladder is the strongest test.** Over 21 one-number fadd.ps kinds whose results hold 0 to 30 one-bits, P forecasts **+0.165 W
per result bit**: a matrix of 0.5s (0.5 + 0.5 = 1.0 = 0x3F800000, seven 1-bits) costs +1.15 W over zeros and a matrix of 8s
(16.0, three 1-bits) +0.49 W, so **the smaller number costs more**; the number whose sum is the largest float, 0x7F7FFFFF (thirty
1-bits), +4.95 W. Six passes read that slope to +-0.003 W a bit, enough to tell every arm's price apart (section 5). The integer add
`fadd.pi` on the same numbers is forecast **flat** (its write-back holds its value between results), and the accumulating add
c += v (section 1 (b)) is forecast **+2.96 W** above the plain add of 4s. The full tables are `forecast-e180.txt`.

## 1. The computations, in the lab's energy catalogue

Every computation is a configuration of the energy catalogue's host (`workloads/enercat/host/main.cpp`), run as the catalogue runs
an instruction row (`workloads/enercat/run_catalogue.py`: `--pattern <op> --operands <fill> --harts 2`, 3 s bursts of 0.4 s
launches, `--window 240000000` cycles at 600 MHz, between idle brackets; `tools/claims-v3/catfull` ran the whole catalogue this way
on four cards on 25-26 Sep):

- **(a) fadd.ps on one number** (catalogue mode 160, `gen_ops.py`'s generated block, unchanged): `RUN(8 x "fadd.ps f(8+i), f(i),
  f((i+1)%8)")`, i = 0..7, with `--operands file:<image>`, the host's own option (`main.cpp` lines 5 and 243-254): exactly 256 B, the
  image of f0..f7, given to every hart. A one-number image is one 32-bit word 64 times (`make_e180_kinds.py`, `images/`). So every
  instruction reads (v, v) and writes v + v into a sink nothing reads.
- **(b) the accumulating add, c += v** (new; [`gen_acc_e180.py`](gen_acc_e180.py), 12 kernel lines and 2 table rows,
  [`acc-modes-e180.inc`](acc-modes-e180.inc)): modes 261 `fadd.ps.acc` and 262 `fadd.pi.acc`, appended after `gen_ops.py`'s last mode
  in its own form, so every existing mode keeps its id and its code text. The block is `RUN(8 x "fadd.ps f(8+i), f(8+i), f(i)")`
  after `fxor.pi f(8+i), f0, f0` sets the eight sums to zero at each launch's start: eight read-write chains a hart, each adding v once
  per block, so a sum walks 0, v, 2v, ... through the launch, as E178's accumulator walks. Nothing committed is edited: the generator
  copies `workloads/enercat` into a build tree (`build/enercat-e180-src`) and appends there; the kit builds that tree on the box
  (`build/enercat-e180`), and E180 runs every configuration on that one build.
- **(c) fadd.pi**, the integer add (catalogue mode 172), on integer words and on two of (a)'s images: its result path differs (the
  short pipe), so it splits the write-back's return to zero from anything the operands' or results' bits might cost otherwise.
- **Anchors in every pass:** the catalogue's own fills under their own names, `zeros`, `const` (1.0f in every word, so `const` is the
  one-number kind v = 1) and `random` (floats in [0.5, 2), seed 1, every hart its own 256 B), under fadd.ps and under fadd.pi: the six
  rows the catalogue measured on 26 Sep; and `zeros` under each accumulating mode (its own instruction bits, the sum stays 0).

## 3. Forecast (i): what switches, from an RTL run of the catfull loop on the whole minion (`bench/`)

**The bench** ([`bench/README.md`](bench/README.md)) runs the catalogue's inner loop on core-et's **whole `minion_top`**
(`b38a1a3`, github.com/openhwfoundation/core-et), not on the vector unit alone: the front end with both thread buffers and the
scheduler that alternates the harts, the integer pipe with its CSR replay for `csrr hpmcounter3` (the kernel's `cycles()`, four
reads before every loop-back branch), the dcache and `vpu_top` whole. So the loop's gaps between results are the RTL's own. The
program ([`bench/asm_e180.py`](bench/asm_e180.py)) is RUN()'s loop: 64 vector ops, `++iters`, `.p2align 4`, four `csrr`, `bltu`.
What remains testbench code: the neighbourhood's instruction cache (a hit four cycles after the request, the frontend's F1 to F5),
its PMU read path (two flops, as `neigh_pmu.v`), and idle ties; the prologue sets the end state of `load_vsrc` with register-only
instructions. Verilator 5.052 on the mini with `fma_toggle_v2`'s `-fno-dfg` and its register accounting, unchanged; the binary
re-builds to the same sha256 (`6e8dbab0...`).

**The check against the silicon's own issue rate** (`bench/validation-e180.jsonl`, 58 cases; the catalogue's `ops_per_cycle_per_hart`,
26 Sep, three cards): with hart 1 started a few cycles after hart 0, both harts issue **0.3808** vector ops a cycle each (the
catalogue's fadd.ps, fadd.pi and add: **0.3730**, +2.1%); one hart alone 0.6208 (the catalogue's hand-written `spin`: 0.6214; its
generated `add` and `fmadd.ps`: 0.581). No parameter was fitted to get these.

**The loop's gaps.** A gap is a cycle with no result at the write-back.
- With the harts a few cycles apart (7 to 137 tried), **every gap is a single cycle**: 0.3124 gaps a result (layout A), a gap
  roughly every third result, because each hart's loop end (its four replayed `csrr` and the branch) leaves the other hart to issue
  alone. That is the primary state.
- Hart 1 started 7, 23 or 101 cycles late settles in a second offset state with a few five-cycle gaps among the single ones:
  0.2811 gaps a result (-10%). Started on one cycle, the harts run in lockstep, and both reach the loop end together: one 47-cycle
  gap an iteration, 0.0078 a result, forty times fewer. Lockstep is not stable in the bench (any asymmetry between the harts' prologues breaks it), and a
  kernel's two harts never start on one cycle; it is an alternative state, reported.
- Over the loop's position against the 32 B fetch blocks (layouts A, B, o12, o16r, o28) and icache contention (a grant every 8
  cycles) the primary state's gaps run 0.250-0.328 a result: the write-back term x0.80 to x1.05 of layout A. That is in P's
  uncertainty.

- **fadd.ps:** in a one-number kind nothing moves but the write-back's return to zero, and it moves by the result's 1-bits: the
  named count is 24.995 per 1-bit of v + v for every one of the 21 one-number fadd.ps kinds (a 1-bit on each of 5 nets, twice, at 0.3124 gaps
  a result, in 8 lanes). The RF's read data holds between reads (the open RTL's register file is a behavioural macro with registered
  reads that keep their value when not enabled), so **the operand's 1-bits cost nothing** in this RTL.
- **fadd.pi:** its result goes through the short pipe's enabled flops, which hold between results: no named class moves for any one
  number. All nets show one more thing: the short pipe's barrel shifter is not operand-isolated, and shifts the operand by each
  instruction's rs2 field (1..7, 0 around the block), so its internal nets toggle with the operand's bit pattern (all-nets count 23
  to 653 an instruction; fadd.ps's write-back bus 103 per result bit).
- **The accumulating modes:** the sums change every block, so the write data and the bypass registers switch at every result, the RF
  words and its read data switch with the walk, and the gaps add the sums' 1-bits. Counted at three points of a launch (the sums

- **(ii) all nets:** Verilator's toggle coverage over `vpu_top` (every bit and direction, aliases counted as `fma_toggle_v2`'s `nets`

## 5. The rules (statistic, threshold, verdicts and power; section 6 gives the noise model)

A kind's per-pass D is its kept bursts' over-idle power in that pass (the catalogue's reduction, `analyze_catalogue.bursts_of` and
its leakage correction, unchanged), the mean of its runs where it runs more than once; every rule reads D differences inside a pass.
**Fewer than 2 kept passes: every rule NOT SCORED.** The interval is m +- t(0.975, n - 1) sd / sqrt(n) over kept passes. SIZE: PASS if
the forecast is inside; **FAIL HIGH if the forecast is above the interval (the model prices more than the card reads), FAIL LOW if
below** (E178's words, as its dated note A2 settles them). Power from `power_e180.py` (20,000 draws a case, 4,000 for L, O and R), FULL
plan, aifoundry3's noise.

kinds on their operands' 1-bits.

**What each outcome means.**
- **E180-H POSITIVE with SIZE PASS:** a matrix of 5s costs more to add than a matrix of 4s, by what the result's one extra 1-bit and
  the write-back's return to zero say. With E180-L's slope inside P and E180-O NULL, **the owner's sentence is measured for the add**:
  different numbers, a different number of transistor flips, therefore different power, and a model that tracks it.
- **E180-L** reads the price of one result bit at the write-back directly: inside P, the random row's all-in price holds for this
  path; inside C, the one-number price is higher than random data's (read with E180-O: an operand term would also put L inside C);
  inside E, the write-back costs per bit what E60 measured for the short pipe's result path; inside F or A, a uniform price per RTL
  toggle is enough. Outside all: none of them.
- **E180-O POSITIVE** (with H and L positive): the operand's held 1-bits cost power too, which the open RTL's behavioural register file
  cannot show and the silicon's full-custom one may (a precharged read); then L's slope is not the write-back's alone.
- **E180-I NULL with E180-X POSITIVE:** the integer add's held write-back costs nothing by the number, the float add's returning one
  does: the mechanism is the return to zero, not the numbers' bits as such. E180-I POSITIVE: the integer path pays too (the shifter's
  unisolated nets, or something the RTL lacks).
- **E180-A POSITIVE:** a running sum costs more than a fixed result, by the walk's flips; its SIZE says which price.

## 6. The noise model and the power (`noise_e180.py`, `power_e180.py`)

From the catalogue's committed repeat runs only (`catalogue.json`, 26 Sep 2026: three shuffled passes on each of three cards,
`noise/noise-e180.json`). On aifoundry3: one burst's sd about its configuration's mean, sigma1 = 0.196 W (vector-unit rows 0.243 W);
**the sd over passes of a within-pass difference of two vector-unit configurations, sigma_d = 0.1787 W** (median of 7,021 pairs).
The simulation draws each run as its kind's truth + a pass term (cancels in every within-pass difference) + independent noise
sigma_d / sqrt 2 = 0.1264 W, also at x0.8 and x1.6; a kind-level model error of 0.03 W for the shape rules. Truths: P, C, E, F, A and
no difference. On card 1, sigma_d is 0.1993 W.

## 10. What I expect, frozen so it can be wrong

5s read above 4s by about 0.17 W (E180-H POSITIVE, SIZE PASS), and the ladder is linear in the result's 1-bits with a slope inside
P's, not C's, E's, F's or A's, though I hold C as the likelier alternative (the const row reads high). The operand's bits cost nothing
(E180-O NULL). 0.5s cost more than 8s. The integer add is flat (E180-I NULL) and the float add of 5s sits about 0.5 W above the
integer add of 5s. The accumulating add of 4s costs about 3 W more than the plain add of 4s, and 5s and 4s accumulate at the same
power. The const anchor ties the catalogue's +0.2 W.

## 11. What the rules cannot say

The bench is the Erbium-branch open RTL, a later revision of the same lineage, without the silicon's full-custom register file (E178
A1-8's caveat): its read ports hold their data between reads, which the silicon's may not. E180-O tests the consequence, not the
circuit. The price of a flip is calibrated on the catalogue's rows, not derived from capacitance; the card's slope measures it. The
gap structure is the bench's; the silicon's issue rate is 2% lower, which, in the bench's single-cycle-gap state, means about 9% more
gaps (0.3405 a result at the measured rate). Glitches, wires, the clock tree and the icache and PMU outside the minion are not
modelled. One card, one session: nothing here separates a card's level from a gain.
