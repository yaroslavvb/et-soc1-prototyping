## 2. CX-E's verdict is printed beside its false rate at equal distribution (F3)

**The registered power figure.** E220.md section 8 registers that the cross-card energy test (CX-E: W over idle given the measured op time, at
the X twins measured at 3 W or more on both cards) reads LARGER HERE at equal error with a simulated rate of **0.095 on arm A and 0.331 on
arm B**. "Equal error" there means this card's |error| drawn as |N(0, sigma_a3)|, with sigma_a3 the rms of the twins' frozen aifoundry3 errors.

**Why that rate is high: the aifoundry3 errors are heavy-tailed.** At the same rms they have far more small values than a normal error, so a
normal draw lands above most of them:

| test | twins | rms | median abs error | median abs error of a normal error at that rms |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| arm A (CX-E) | 41 | 7.24% | 3.68% | 4.88% |
| arm B (CX-E) | 31 | 7.00% | 3.18% | 4.73% |

A LARGER HERE under that null is therefore as much a test of shape as of size.

**At equal distribution** this card's |error| is drawn with replacement from the same twins' own aifoundry3 errors. Under that null, the false
LARGER HERE rate is much lower. Figures from this note's script, 20,000 simulated days a test, the registered test's ties, minimum and sign test:

| test | false LARGER HERE, equal distribution | false LARGER HERE, normal at equal rms | registered (normal, k = 1) |
|---|---|---|---|
| A:CX-E | **0.0032** | 0.1084 | 0.095 |
| B:CX-E | **0.0019** | 0.3379 | 0.331 |
| CXP-E (pooled, 72 cases) | **0.0033** | **0.4986** | none registered |
| A:CX-T (beside) | 0.0039 | 0.0088 | 0.009 |
| B:CX-T (beside) | 0.0039 | 0.0021 | 0.004 |
| CXP-T (beside, 94 cases) | 0.0052 | 0.0089 | none registered |

verify-e220's own re-simulation gave 0.003 and 0.001 at equal distribution, and 0.118 and 0.336 normal. The pooled CXP-E's normal-null rate
