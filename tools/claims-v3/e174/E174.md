## The primary, exactly

For each scored tile, **`predict_pool8_a2.burst(pred, anchors, die_starts)`** on the frozen `predictions-pool8-aifoundry1c0.json` (the frozen code,
imported after its sha256 is checked), with the session's zeros-anchor reads (every counted burst of each format's `anchor7_zeros`) and each tile's
own counted die starts. Its **M10** block is `e170_common.pred_card0` on M10's D600 (burst D = κ0 × the relation's D at the burst's clock and die
starts; board = the session's zeros level for the format at the tile's die starts, in full, + that D; clock the relation's), with the die rise
replaced by **M10's term c2 on that predicted board**, 0.2415 °C per W over 21.5 W, and its own band ± 0.593 °C (A2-1, reported, no rule reads it).
Its **M9** block is the same chain on M9's D600, its rise by M9's burst heat law (0.1400 °C/W). If no zeros-anchor burst was counted, there is no
level and no prediction: every rule NOT SCORED. **Measured:** board = the tile's `burst_w` mean over its counted passes; burst D = the mean over
passes of `burst_w` less the same pass's zeros anchor of its format; clock `burst_mhz`; rise `die_max − die_start`. The zeros anchors are inputs,
never scored; only they reach the anchors input (the scorer refuses otherwise).

## What the rules cannot say

- **One card, one session, one clock (800 MHz).** The admitted kinds are the cooler ones: 21 of N1's 31, int8 above all; no random-like float.
- **κ0 is carried unchanged** (rule E): E170-X0 read the switching scale 3% higher (0.977) and die-start referred 0.931; a C0-W miss is the card
  term's and D's together.
- **c2's reference is its own in-sample fit's leave-one-tile-out residuals on E170**: C0-H1 tests whether a second session lands inside that
  spread, not whether c2 is the true coefficient.
- **The die start is the firmware's whole-degree reading**; no inlet thermometer. **"The fix" is unknown**: E174 never approaches the 75 W alarm,
  so it says nothing about the fix beyond "nothing tripped at these powers".
