the bootstrap's own coverage under its null, not a power.** C0-W's line is the 97.5% point of the rms over draws from those same 42 errors, so
97.5% of such draws pass by construction (`predictions-e174.json` › `forecast_information_only`: `W_at_or_under_line` 0.9747, seed 1741). Read it
as: C0-W PASSes in 97.5% of reference-like sessions, by construction. The informative forecast is C0-B's 88.5% (and E173's A2-Bp 88.6%).
