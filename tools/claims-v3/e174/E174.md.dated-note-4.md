  box session never reads. [`run_e174.py`](run_e174.py) reads on the box only its `BOX_FROZEN` (8 files, all synced) and Q139(k)'s three run-time
  files (synced). Its own docstring says the desk-only files "are never synced there".

  present (E170's kit files `harness-e170.json` and `score_e170.py`, from an earlier sync). That left 18 absent, exactly D1's 18 (§§ 2-3).
- **Why no one saw it before the day.** Every desk test of the kit ran in a full clone, where all 32 files are present. Q142(kg)'s verifier,
  Q142(v2)'s verifier and the readiness critic of Q142(v2)/(r2)/(h) all missed it. That critic read READY at `f49b7ddd`.
