## Why not E164's 600 MHz design

The round's brief asked for E164's design ported to card 0, and for the governed variant in E165's form if card 0 does not hold 600 MHz under load
without a set. **It does not.** At its own static TDP of 65 W, card 0 idles at 600 MHz and every kernel steps it to the 800 MHz cap:

- E84 (31 Oct): "600 → 700 → 800 MHz in 0.5–1.4 s" at every process start; zeros, ones and int8 random held 800 MHz;
- E118 (1 Nov): only the static TDP set to 0 held 600 MHz (11,182 of 11,182 samples); after TDP 65 was written back, one burst stepped 600 → 700 →
  800 MHz, 700 MHz 0.88 s in and 800 MHz 0.20 s later (its G3);
- E114 (1 Nov): four of five kinds at the 800 MHz cap, random fp16 at the 65 °C trip.

This round sets no governor, TDP, threshold or clock, so a 600 MHz session cannot run on card 0. **E170 is E165's governed-burst design on card
0**, and at the powers it admits the governor holds every burst at its 800 MHz cap: the test moves from 600 MHz to 800 MHz, through M9's frozen
aifoundry2 relation, which already carries D from 600 MHz to the governed clock (Q140(l), `m9_a2.json`). The 25 Sep record of card 0 idling at
300 MHz under firmware 1.4.1 is stale (E84 read release 1.4.0 / BL2 0.22.0 and a 600 MHz idle; `14-card-behaviour.md` was corrected).

## What it does to predicting power and heat

M9 predicts a tile's switching power D from its bytes at 600 MHz on aifoundry3's footing; its aifoundry2 part carries D to a governed 6 s burst
(P(f) = B(f) + c·s(f), c = c_zeros + g·D, g 0.98487, s(f) at n = 2) with the session's zeros level and the die at the burst's start. **No card term
exists for card 0**: M9 holds terms for aifoundry3 (1), card 1 (0.9616) and aifoundry2 (its part), and M8's card-scale predictor from cheap reads
missed every held-out card by 1.5-8.5% (`m8.json` › `card_scale_predictor.loco`). **E170 sets card 0's term blind** (§ The card term): its level
from the session's own zeros anchors, and its switching scale κ0 = 0.9463 from committed reads of card 0 and aifoundry2 at one operating point.
**Before** (nothing of it on card 0): the blind record at 600 MHz is 0.24-0.32 W rms per card for a new kind; aifoundry2's governed bursts, end to
end with the models' blind D, E160 0.80 W and E143 0.87 W on the pinned board (Q140(l)'s `loso.json`); card 0 has no blind power score at all.
**After**: the error of predicting a new kind's power on a card the models never saw, from the bytes plus a zeros burst per format and a constant
read off committed ladders; whether that constant holds (X0); M9 against M6 there (M1); and the burst heat law on a third card (H1).

## The card term

Card 0's term has two parts, both fixed by this registration before any card-0 burst of E170 exists:

1. **Its level: the session's own zeros anchors, in full.** Per format, the mean over **every counted burst** of that format's `anchor7_zeros` at
   795 MHz or more of `burst_w − kT·(die_start − T_ref)` (`kT` 0.3954 W/°C and `T_ref` 54.38 °C, `m9_a2.json`'s), referred to a tile's own
   counted die starts by the same `kT`; a format with no such burst takes the mean of the others'. These are the reads E165 hands
   `predict_m9_a2.burst()` (`session_delta`'s form), entering here **in full** (weight 1), not at β 0.648: β is aifoundry2's session-to-session
   weight; on another card the anchor is that card's own zeros level, about 6 W under aifoundry2's (E84's p800 29.46-29.81 W against M9's part's
   35.29-36.66 W).
2. **Its switching scale κ0 = 0.9463**, against M9's aifoundry2 relation: the median of the five committed ratios of card 0 to aifoundry2 at one
   operating point (600 MHz, 0.518 V; E118 against E72, the same items and harness): the `lq` full ladder 106.74 / 113.22 fJ = 0.9428, the `lb` full
   ladder 120.22 / 125.45 = 0.9583, and the ones, pi and randn anchors over each card's zeros anchor, 7.73 / 8.10 = 0.9543, 7.76 / 8.20 = 0.9463 and
   22.59 / 23.99 = 0.9416 W (E118's README, "Three cards at 600 MHz now").

## The primary, exactly

For each scored tile (format t, its counted die starts T_i): **`b = predict_m9_a2.burst(D, t, anchors, die_starts=T_i, D_band_hw)`**, the frozen
code imported after its sha256 and `m9_a2.json`'s are checked (`e165_common.m9a2`), with D = `M9.D600_a3_footing_w` and D_band_hw = half the width
of `M9.D600_a3_95` (both frozen in `predictions-blind7-aifoundry2.json`), and `anchors` the session's zeros-anchor reads as above. Then:

- **predicted burst D** = κ0 × `b.burst_D_w` (the frozen relation's D at the burst's clock: the board less the format's zeros board at the same
  clock and die starts);
- **predicted board** = the card's zeros level for t at T_i (§ The card term, 1) + the predicted burst D;
- **predicted clock** = `b.burst_mhz`; **predicted die rise** = the frozen burst heat law (`m9_a2.json` › `burst_heat`: 0.1400 °C per W over 21.5 W)
  on the predicted board;
- **bands**: the frozen pinned half-widths of `m9_a2.json` › `bands` (board ±1.264 W, clock ±15.35 MHz), the die rise ±1.406 °C.

**Measured**: board = the tile's `burst_w` mean over its counted passes; burst D = the mean over passes of `burst_w` less the same pass's zeros
anchor of its format (`score_e165.cells_of`'s `D_w`); clock `burst_mhz`; die rise `die_max − die_start`. **The zeros anchors are not scored**:
they are the card term's input. **M8, M7 and M6 are carried through the same chain** (each model's frozen D600 into the same `burst()` call, κ0 and
level), so a comparison between models compares only their D. E165's form unchanged (β 0.648, κ 1) is reported beside, never a verdict.

## Decision rules

A run is **complete** if every launch ran and was ok and `burst_mhz`, `burst_w`, `die_start`, `die_max` exist; **counted** if complete **and every
SPARSITY line of it has a C record that reads PASS** (bit-exact; Q139(k)'s rule). A tile's measured value is its mean over its counted passes; a
tile with fewer than 2 counted passes is left out of every rule and named. **Fewer than 2 counted passes of tier A in all: every rule NOT SCORED.**
"Bootstrap" = the 2.5–97.5% range of the rms of n errors drawn with replacement from the reference errors (20,000 draws, seeded 170;
`e165_common.boot_rms_range`'s form): **PASS** at or below the 97.5% point, **FAIL** above. "Coverage" = **PASS** if the number inside the band is at
least the 2.5% point of Binomial(n, 0.95). "Paired" = the 95% interval of rms(a) − rms(b) over the tiles, paired, drawn with replacement (20,000
draws, seeded 1701; `e165_common.paired_rms_diff`'s form). A rule over fewer than 8 tiles is NOT SCORED (M1 and M2: fewer than 10).

|---|---|---|---|---|

## Safety

**The admission** (section 30: stops at least as strict as E164's; section 17: nothing that risks a power cycle; section 16: no deliberate tripping
of card 0's over-power alarm). A tile runs only if its **card-0 estimate** is at most **60 W**: E165's admission value for the tile (the largest of
its three frozen 95% upper peaks on aifoundry2), less the gap between M9's part's predicted 800 MHz zeros level and card 0's measured one (E84's
p800: 29.59 fp32, 29.46 fp16, 29.81 int8 W), less 5% of the tile's predicted switching (card 0 switches at 0.88-0.96 of aifoundry2 in every
committed pair, so 0.95 over-states it). **19 tiles are left out by name** ([`predictions-e170.json`](predictions-e170.json) › `left_out`):
`fp32.dropout30_both` (60.43 W), `fp32.exp_b_alt_nib`, `fp32.mant2_spreadexp`, `fp16.real_qwen_l3_softmax_v_h1`, `fp16.sorted_k`,
`fp16.real_s360_l3_w8a16_gate`, `fp16.real_s360_l3_qk`, `fp16.uniform_pm1`, `fp16.anchor7_randn_a`, `fp16.anchor7_randn_b`,
`fp32.real_smol_l3_w8a16_q_outlier`, `fp16.laplace_both`, `fp16.randn_x8`, `fp32.real_qwen_l3_softmax_v_sink`, `fp32.real_s360_l1_qk`,
`fp32.anchor7_randn_a`, `fp32.anchor7_randn_b`, `fp32.laplace_both`, `fp32.uniform_pm1` (78.21 W). They are in no run; the runner refuses them.
The estimate is checked against card 0's own measurements: it puts fp32 random at 78.2 W where E84 measured 72-75 W (single readings to 74.6 W),
and int8 random at 47.6 W where E84 read 42.2 W and E114 43.6 W, so it errs high by 3-5 W. **The highest that runs is 59.80 W
(`fp32.exp_only_pos12`)**, which should read about 55-57 W: 9-11 W under the board stop and 18-20 W under the alarm. E105's lock: after any run
whose highest board sample reached **62 W**, nothing at or above that run's estimate runs. Every admitted tile is predicted at the cap by M9's part
(`group` pinned): the governor's power arm never acts at these powers, and in a 6 s burst from 55 °C the die rises about 4-5 °C.

**The stops** (`run_hpl.py`'s watcher, the keys it reads, at least as strict as E164's 66 W / 72 °C): one die reading (the shires' mean or the
hottest-sensor field) at or above **72 °C**, or **one** board sample above **65.9 W**, touches the stop file (the launch ends at its next ~0.5 s
launch boundary) and ends the session (exit 4). **The alarm check** (the kit's, after every run): any sampler line of the run under **590 MHz** (the

## Card rules and budget

**aifoundry1 card 0 only**: `V3_DEVICE=0` / `ET_DEVICES=0`, card 0's lock; **after E164 has ended**, never two sessions of ours on aifoundry1 (the
runners count device openers and the kernel module's use count across both cards). No governor, TDP, threshold or clock set; no power cycle and

ramped smoke, about 2.5 min. The session's cap is **40 card-minutes** (`run_e119.py` starts a run only if its planned time fits); the slot is **45
minutes** from the queue's start, and the queue refuses a deadline that leaves less. **The registered subset for a short window**: passes 1, 2, 4
and 5 (the first two of each half, 90 bursts, about 18 min; `--cap 25`, a 30-minute slot); every rule is scored on what is counted. The round's
window (section 30): it starts after E164 ends (about T+80) and by 18:15 PST, and ends by 19:00 PST. The day's one post carries aifoundry1's clause
for both cards (section 30).
