**The decision: E170 runs as registered.** No tile leaves, and the order is unchanged. Two reasons:

1. **The margins hold without a cut.** Every implied peak is at least 5.1 W under the one-sample board stop and at least 14.2 W under the alarm. The

2. **A cut would select on M9's own error.** On aifoundry2, E165's M9 part read these two tiles' boards **+1.80 W and +2.52 W** (measured above
   predicted: E165's `cells.<label>.burst_w` less `score.reported.tiles.<name>.primary.burst_board_w`). Over E170's 39 scored tiles, E165's errors

   card's outcome would move the blind primary toward PASS.

**The card lane reads this section before box step 5.** If either tile's run reaches 62 W, E105's lock stops every later run at or above its
estimate (55.73 W or 57.35 W): most of half B's later passes. E170-S then reads FAIL. Both are registered outcomes, not faults.
