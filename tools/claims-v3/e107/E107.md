# E107: where a large GEMM falls, by reuse and by the L3 (Q133)

Sun 1 Nov 2026 (week 44), Q133, lane a3-e107-gemm. Registered before the claim and the first launch. It answers [section 11 of the kinds page](https://spacesheep.dev/@yaroslavvb/horace-kinds-of-numbers#11-what-is-still-open), **item 1, "Where a large GEMM falls"**, as it reads at `1fe8377a18af`: *"A sweep of block repeats would test whether reuse is the variable at all; 8 blocks, whose 24 MB of operands overflow the L2 and fit the L3, would separate the walk's limit, which is not measured. Then the vendor's own binary, which nobody here has timed."* It builds on [E101](../../../docs/reports/data/2026-11-01-e101-real-matmul/README.md) and [E105](../../../docs/reports/data/2026-11-01-e105-gemm-layout/README.md) and their tools, unchanged.

## The question

E105 found three conditions in one GEMM pattern at a given clock: a repeated block whose tiles fit the L2, where the clock sets the pace (T = 527/f + 0.02 us); the same block with tiles that alias in the L2, where 0.5-0.8 us of each op ignores the clock; and a walk over 16 fresh blocks past the L2 and the 32 MB L3, where about 2-2.4 us does. Its verifier (C1, C4) left two things open: the repeated block and the walk differ in how often a block is reused AND in what serves the misses (the L3 or DRAM), and no arm separates them. So:

1. **Is reuse the variable?** Walk the same 16 blocks, but pass over each block R times before moving on, R = 1, 2, 4, 8, 32. The footprint, the addresses, the ops, the C traffic per op and the DRAM traffic per first pass stay the same; only the number of passes that find the block's tiles in the L2 changes. If a block's first pass costs what a cold-walk pass costs and the others what a repeated block's pass costs, T(R) = T(c1) + (T(c16) - T(c1)) / R.
2. **What limits the walk?** Walk 8 blocks with C shared: 24 MB of A + B and one 2 MB C, 26 MB against the 32 MB L3, so every L2 miss after the first pass is an L3 hit, while every pass still overflows each shire's 512 KB L2 by 8x. If the cold walk's excess is DRAM's, most of it goes; if it is the L2-miss path's (the L3 and the mesh), it stays. Two controls: 4 blocks with C shared (14 MB, resident with room), and 8 blocks with C per block (40 MB with C, the walk E105's design would give at 8 blocks). And 16 blocks with C shared (50 MB) asks whether C's 32 MB of stores are part of the cold walk's limit.

## How T is read

T, us per op of one minion, is 1,024 over `reduce_e78.py`'s rate (the launch records inside its power window, [t0+1 s, t0+3 s)), per item, the mean over passes; an arm's T in a format is the mean of its kinds'. The kind never changed the count at a given clock in E101 or E105 (within 0.03% resident, 0.41% from DRAM), and R2 tests it again. At one clock T cannot be split into a part that follows the clock and a part that does not (E105's C2), so **the speed half, what a clock gap buys in each arm, needs a governed card** (aifoundry2, which is E111's today): it is left for later. A labelled bound is printed: if no more than the FMA's 529 cycles of an fp32 op follow the clock (true of every fp32 arm E105 fitted), x1.333 of clock buys at most T600 / (T600 - 0.2204 us).

- `c4s` and `c8s` price an L3-served miss as E101's packed `gemm1` pays it (its 5 MB block sits in the L3, and the desk L2 model has 144 of each op's 192 lines per shire missing): 3.58 ns per missing line in fp32 and fp16, 6.42 in int8, times the 128 a cold pass by column misses. On this reading DRAM sets about three quarters of the cold walk's excess.
- `c8`: 40 MB cycling through a 32 MB L3 under LRU keeps nothing, so it runs as `c16`.
- `c16s`: if the DRAM side's cost follows its bytes, C's 32 MB of the 80 MB a pass moves take 0.4 of the excess with them.

## Rules, which can fail

The full text and bars are in `predictions-e107.json` `rules`; `score_e107.py` implements them; `selftest_e107.py` shows each one failing or flipping on made-up rows (`selftest.txt`, all ok).

- **R1, the ties.** T(`c1`) within 2% and T(`c16`) within 3% of E105's aifoundry3 values, in each format. A FAIL means the rebuilt arms are not E105's, and every other verdict is labelled.
- **R2, the kind.** In every arm and format each kind's T within 1% of zeros'.
- **R3, reuse.** Per format, fit T(R) = Tinf + D / R over R = 1, 2, 4, 8, 32. REUSE IS THE VARIABLE at |Tinf - T(`c1`)| <= 5% of T(`c1`) with every point within 3% of the fit; NOT THE VARIABLE if T(`c16r32`) - T(`c1`) is at least half of T(`c16`) - T(`c1`); else PARTIAL. fp32 is primary. Frozen: REUSE in all three. Printed beside it: each R's first pass, R T(R) - (R - 1) T(`c1`).
- **R4, the walk's limit.** f8 = (T(`c8s`) - T(`c1`)) / (T(`c16`) - T(`c1`)). DRAM SETS MOST OF IT at f8 <= 0.50, NOT DRAM at f8 >= 0.85, SHARED between. fp32 primary. Frozen: 0.270 (fp16 0.270, int8 0.394), DRAM.
- **R4b, residency.** |T(`c4s`) - T(`c8s`)| <= 5% of T(`c8s`); if not, the 26 MB walk did not behave as resident, and R4 is read on `c4s`, labelled.
- **R4c, the DRAM check.** The DDR rail's die voltage droops with DRAM power (`docs/findings/05-claims.md`, 0.87 mV per DRAM watt): the desk check of E105's and E101's passes (`desk/rails_e105.txt`) reads 1.9-3.5 mV in the cold walk, 0.5-1.0 mV in the L3-fed packed `gemm1`, about 0 resident. PASS when droop(`c8s`) - droop(`c1`) <= half of droop(`c16`) - droop(`c1`), on fp32 zeros and on fp32 random (`rails_e107.py`: [t0+2 s, t0+4 s) against [t0-1.5 s, t0-0.1 s)).
- **R5, C in the L3.** f8c = (T(`c8`) - T(`c1`)) / (T(`c16`) - T(`c1`)) against f8: C ALLOCATES (its 16 MB push the walk out of the L3) at f8c >= f8 + 0.30, C DOES NOT DISPLACE at |f8c - f8| <= 0.10, else PARTIAL. Frozen f8c 1.0.
- **R6, C's traffic.** f16s = (T(`c16s`) - T(`c1`)) / (T(`c16`) - T(`c1`)): PART OF THE LIMIT at <= 0.85, NOT THE LIMIT at >= 0.95, else PARTIAL. Frozen 0.6.
- **R7, power.** S over idle on the eight new arms, zeros and random in every format (48 cells), against the frozen S: PASS at rms <= 2.0 W.
- **R8, energy order.** Random fp32's energy per op rises along the ladder: `c1` < `c16r32` < `c16r8` < `c16r4` < `c16r2` < `c16`.

Reduction: `reduce_e107.py` (`reduce_e78.py` with E107's items, as `reduce_e101.py`), `rails_e107.py`, then `score_e107.py` into `scored.json` and `results.md`.

## What this does not do

- **It does not run a large GEMM**, and it does not time **the vendor's own binary** (`gp-sdk/device/sdk/libautogen/matmul_f32_1024_512_512`: about 58 us a launch against about 2 ms of launch overhead and no cycle counter). Whether an instrumented build can be made from public sources is investigated at the desk only, in the data README; nothing of it runs on a card.
- **The speed half** (what a clock gap buys in each arm) needs a governed card; it is left for later.
- **sys_emu** could not check the new paths: it aborts any `SP_GEMM` launch that stores a C tile twice, E105's own binary included (`sim-check.txt`). The smoke checks every new arm's C on the card.
- One card, one session, one kernel; no inlet temperature.
