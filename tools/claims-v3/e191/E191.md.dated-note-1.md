## 1. Card 1's selection, checkable before the first launch (the verifier's finding 1, medium)

**The hazard** (a desk read of E189's kit as made, cited by content). E191's kit is to be made from E189's (E191.md 16). E189's kit is
right for aifoundry3's one card. It is wrong for card 1 in four places:
- `run_e189.py` removes `V3_DEVICE` and `ET_DEVICES` from the environment of every process it starts (its lines 94-95). Those processes
  include the guard's `ettelem config` and `ettelem sample`, and both hosts.
- `e189_common.py` sets `LOCK = "/run/lock/etsoc-shire0.lock"` (its line 30).
- The runbook's config read, k6's `s09_a3_reads_k6.sh` (committed), runs `unset V3_DEVICE ET_DEVICES` (its line 15) and takes
  `etsoc-shire0.lock` (its line 20).
- The runner's holder check (`foreign()`) counts only another login's lines, on either card.

On aifoundry1, `ET_DEVICES=<n>` makes a program open only card n. A program without it opens every card (`14-card-behaviour.md`; `lib.sh`'s
comments). So a port that changed only the lock and the TDP would reach card 0 with the sampler and every load, under card 1's lock.

**Why E191's config gate cannot see it.** The gate (E191.md 2) compares the TDP (65 W), the threshold (65 C) and the clock (600 MHz).
Card 0 reads the same values. From part 1 of the checks:

| | card 0 (E84, E114, E170, E174) | card 1 (27 session folders, 25 Sep to 8 Nov 2026) |
|---|---|---|
| config dicts, files | 418 in 41 | 933 in 473 |
| TDP, threshold, clock | 65 W, 65 C, 600 MHz in every one | 65 W, 65 C, 600 MHz in every one |
| `minion_mv` | **519** in every one | **499** in 924; **500** in 9 (E128, E129 and E131, 2 Nov 2026; E131's session-start read among them) |
| `power_state_name` | low_power in every one | managed_power 869, low_power 64 |
| the read's stderr, where it was kept | `PCIe target: /dev/et0_mgmt` (E84, E114); every `Physical device ID` line reads 0 | `PCIe target: /dev/et1_mgmt` (E142, E145, E157, E164, E172, E184 arm B and two September folders); every `Physical device ID` line reads 1 |

**What E191's kit must add** (registered here, before any datum; added to E191.md 16's list):
1. **`ET_DEVICES=1` in the environment of every process that may open a device.** That means the guard's `ettelem config` (start and end),
   its `ettelem sample`, and both hosts at every launch, the smoke's included. E189's two removals are dropped. A config read made outside
   the runner sets `ET_DEVICES=1` where k6's read unsets it. E164's `session_e164.sh` (its line 38) shows the form.
2. **Card 1's lock**, `/run/lock/etsoc-shire1.lock`, held for the whole session (E191.md 2). Never `etsoc-shire0.lock`.
3. **THE IDENTITY GATE, before the first launch.** Both reads below must pass. If either fails, the session ends before any launch: a gate,
   no data, never a failed prediction (E191.md 12's gate row).
   - **(a) The start config read keeps its stderr,** as E164's did (`2>"$1.err"`).
     - Its stderr names `PCIe target: /dev/et1_mgmt` and `Physical device ID: 1`.
     - Its JSON reads TDP 65 W, threshold 65 C, 600 MHz, and **`minion_mv` below 510**. Card 1 read 499 or 500 in every committed read;
       card 0 read 519 in every one.
     - The verifier proposed `minion_mv` 499 exactly. This note sets the bound at 510 instead, because card 1 read 500 in 9 reads. E131's
       session-start read was one of them, so an exact 499 would have stopped that session.
     - **`power_state_name` is never compared.** Card 1 reads managed_power on some reads and low_power on others.
     - The end read keeps its stderr too, and both are reported (E191.md 2).
   - **(b) The holder read, made with the sampler running and before the smoke's first launch** (the session's first host process):
     - every et-who holder line of our own login is on a `/dev/et1_` node or `lock:etsoc-shire1.lock`;
     - at least one of them is on `/dev/et1_mgmt` (the sampler's);
     - none is on a `/dev/et0_` node or `lock:etsoc-shire0.lock`.

     It reports counts by card, never a name, as E164's `holders-at-launch.txt` did. The read also shows that no other session of ours
     holds card 0 (E191.md 2: never two sessions of ours on aifoundry1).
4. **The desk dry pass asserts** these, over every device-opening command in the runner's and the runbook's command lines:
   - each carries `ET_DEVICES=1` (or `V3_DEVICE=1`, through `lib.sh`);
   - none names `etsoc-shire0`, `/dev/et0_` or `ET_DEVICES=0`, and none unsets `ET_DEVICES`;
   - no `dev_mngt_service` or `et-powertop` call appears at all. Both are the stock build: they ignore `ET_DEVICES` and open every card's
     management node, even with `-n <N>` (`14-card-behaviour.md`). A `-n 0` to them is card 0.
5. **The doubles gain a card-0 case each.**
   - The config double: a stderr naming `/dev/et0_mgmt` and ID 0, with 519 mV.
   - The et-who double: a line of ours on `/dev/et0_mgmt`.

   The runner's desk test shows that each case stops the session before any launch.
