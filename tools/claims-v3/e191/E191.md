### 8.3 The rules, with their words and thresholds (E189's, renamed)

For each configuration c, e_c = (the mean over its passes of the forecast) / (the mean over its passes of the measured

| rule | words | threshold |
|---|---|---|

| **E191-S1T** | score 1, given the measured seconds: J with the card at rest a launch | within 5% on at least 5 of 6 |

## 9. Replication and order

Four independent passes a configuration, one host process each: 24 scored passes. The order is four rounds, each holding
every configuration once, shuffled by `random.Random(191)` and written out in `plan-e191.json` (the runner reads it and
never re-draws it):
1. copy zeros, SHA-256, sort sorted, copy random, sparse parity, sort shuffled;
2. SHA-256, copy random, sort sorted, sparse parity, sort shuffled, copy zeros;
3. sort shuffled, copy zeros, copy random, sparse parity, SHA-256, sort sorted;
4. SHA-256, sort shuffled, sort sorted, copy random, copy zeros, sparse parity.

Each pass is energy.sh's form, as E189.md 9:
- 8 s of idle;
- ONE host process, a burst of K launches;
- 10 s of idle;
- the sampler at 10 Hz throughout (`ettelem sample --every-ms 100`, no statistics reset);
- then the die gate before the next pass.

K is in section 4: E189's rule (the most launches the host's own budget rule lets start at the band's slow end, capped
at energy.sh's 6 s target at the point), from E191's bands.

**The cap is 110:** no pass starts after 108 card-minutes from the first card command, so the session ends by 110. The

## 11. Stops and commands

- **Stops** (live samples), as E189's: board power at or above **65 W**, or `minshire[0]` (the shire mean) at or above **70
  C**, ends the queue. The running process finishes (a device process is never killed), nothing further starts, then
  the closing reads and release.
- **Predicted peaks are about 47-50 W.** That is card 1's idle at 55-60 C (31.5-34.1 W) plus sparse parity's 15.80 W over
  idle. E137's highest board reading on this card was 60.68 W.
- On card 1 the outer entries of `temp_c.minshire` are latches (46 and 65 C in every sample, E145): the stop and the gate
  read the mean, `minshire[0]`.
- **The clock:** every sample must read 600 MHz. The first sample off it sets its pass aside; a second one, in any pass,
  stops the session.
- **The die gate:** a pass starts only when `minshire[0]` reads 60 C or less. It waits up to 10 minutes, else that pass
  is skipped: no data. A die below 52 C does not block: the pass is scored given its own T0 and labelled so.
- **Every device command is a READ**, as E189.md 11, classified by the kit's `dmcmd_audit` (GO) at the firmware build
  card 1 reports (release 1.4.0, BL2 0.22.0, since 31 Oct 2026). The two hosts open the ops node only. `ettelem sample`
  runs without `--reset-ms`, and `ettelem config` at the start and the end. **No statistics reset, no set, no reset, no
  power cycle, no firmware-revisions query.** The host's package counter is not read unless his go allows it (default
  no).

## 12. Fallbacks, and what each means

As E189.md 12:

| what happens | what it means |
|---|---|
| a build fails on the box | that program is SKIPPED: no data, never a failed prediction; at most two build attempts a Pacific day |
| a stop (board, die, clock) | the passes done are scored; the rest are NO DATA |
| a gate: the look finds the card or a device node held, the rest rule unmet, the smoke fails, the window closed, card 1's start config not 65 W / 65 C | no session (or no further pass): no data |
| a program's own check fails on the card | that pass is a FAILED RUN, reported; not a datum and not a failed prediction |
| a burst cut short by the host's budget | scored on the launches it ran, flagged SHORT |
| the counters arm is dropped | said so in a dated kit note before any datum |
| the cap is reached | the queue ends after the current pass |

## 14. What this cannot say (stated before the data)

The limits E189 states hold here too (E189.md 14):
- the meter's absolute scale is one fixed gain, never set beside an outside meter;
- the reducer reads whole degrees;
- the copy's write path is priced by analogy;
- the seconds of SHA-256 and the sorts rest on issue rates;
- the simulator has no cache model;
- the ledger programs' card path has never run.

Card 1 adds these:
- **The reducer's leakage law is aifoundry2's on every card** (`energy_reduce.py`: 23.257 W e^((T - 80)/36)). Its slope at
  55 C is 0.323 W/C. Card 1's own, from the ledger's law, is 0.483 W/C. So on card 1 the reducer's correction leaves
  about a third of a burst's own leakage rise in the measured J over idle. By the ledger's rise term that is about 0.16
  J of sparse parity's 8.13 J and 0.04 J of SHA-256's 2.25 J: **about +2% on every configuration** (desk arithmetic).
  On aifoundry3 the two slopes nearly agree (0.316 and 0.323 W/C at 55 C). The rule is not changed for it.
- **Two literals are aifoundry3's** (section 3). If card 1's own rows hold, the random copy reads about 9% and the sorts
  5-6% above E191's forecast for that reason alone.
- **`int8.pm1`'s D is aifoundry3's**, carried (section 3, about 0.5%).
- **Card 1's meters:**
  - its board readings dip in launch gaps (E82);
  - its minion rail sometimes reads its running maximum (E145);
  - its SRAM rail reads about 0.71 of the power its reset windows show (E184 arm B).

  The scored numbers use the SP's board average through the unchanged reducer, as E189's do. The rails are reported
  only; read the scratchpad-against-SRAM-rail cross-check with E184 arm B's caveat.
- **aifoundry1's runtime is its own build**, not aifoundry3's. The ledger host and sparse parity's host must be built and
  checked there by behaviour, by the kit. A failed build is SKIPPED (no data), never a failed prediction.
- **Card 1's card-1 prices were measured weeks ago** (the catalogue's three-card check, 25-26 Sep; E82, 31 Oct). E184 arm B
  read card 1's board steps 1.05 times September's on its loads (8 Nov). A drift of that size would show here as a
  uniform excess over idle.
