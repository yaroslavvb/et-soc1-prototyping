## The question

With the row mask 0x0000 nothing issues, yet int8's TensorFMA loop costs **N = 1.567 ± 0.209 W** more over idle than fp32's on this card (E124's Y1, registered). Q136(c) counted the open RTL's later revision (core-et, Erbium) for every candidate the critic named ([its README](../../../docs/reports/data/2026-11-02-q136c-tima-bench/README.md)): int8's loop differs from fp32's in **TenC's reads** (16 every int8 cycle, 4,096 of the 5,092 an op at a new address), **the TIMA controls** (about 107 register bits clocked a working cycle), and **int8's A path** (64-bit A reads into TenTMP, TenA and TenTMP writes). fp32's own no-issue level bounds TenB and the scratchpad's reads; TenC's read port and the TIMA controls stay live, and the bench cannot price either at a price the cards agree on (result 5b). Its section 6 proposed the arm registered here: **one A row**.

## What the RTL says one A row does (Q136(c)'s counts, `counts.json` › `arms_pace`, frozen in `predictions-e129.json` › `arms_bench_per_op`)

| an op, mask 0x0000 | `m0.fp32` (16 rows) | `m0.int8` (16 rows) | `m0r1.fp32` (1 row) | `m0r1.int8` (1 row) |
|---|---:|---:|---:|---:|
| bench cycles (card: + 7.75, E109's rule) | 536.25 → **544.0** | 310.25 → **318.0** | 54.25 → **62.0** | 54.25 → **62.0** |
| TenC reads; at a new address (at the card's cycles) | 0; 0 | 5,092; 4,096 | 0; 0 | 996; **0** |
| scratchpad reads (A + B) | 288 | 224 | 48 | 40 |
| TenA writes; TenTMP writes | 256; 0 | 192; 64 | 16; 0 | 8; 8 |
| TenB writes (B's 32 half-lines × 8) | 256 | 256 | 256 | 256 |

So with one A row **both formats run at the same op rate** (about 9.91 × 10⁹ ops a second, predicted), int8 still reads TenC every cycle but **always at one address**, and its A path shrinks (A reads 192 → 8 an op). B's reads and TenB writes stay, equal in both formats, and cancel in the difference. A second, chip-wide, the TIMA controls keep 0.893 of their 16-row rate and TenC's reads 1.003 of theirs (`priced.json` › E124's contrasts, `d_counts_per_s`).

**N1 = S(`m0r1.int8.zeros`) − S(`m0r1.fp32.zeros`)**, beside N in the same pass:
- **N1 near the bench's 0.07–0.14 W** (card order): the excess goes with something int8 does for each row of A, its TenC read-address walk or its A path. The TIMA controls at the cards' issue price would be excluded too (they would keep 0.98 ± 0.49 W).
- **N1 near N:** it is work int8 does every cycle at the same op rate, neither the walk nor the op rate: TenC's reads (if they are int8's alone on silicon), the TIMA controls at the cards' issue price, or the core. The arm does not separate these.

The arm is safe on this card: the sparsity README's errata note puts TensorFMA16A32/32 with B in the L1 scratchpad at risk only with 4 A rows (type D); 1-row A with B in the scratchpad is the layer's own safe form, and int8's TensorIMA8A32 is not in the note. Nothing issues, so its power is low.

## Rules (frozen in `predictions-e129.json` › `rules`; scored by `score_e129.py`)

Every statistic is computed per kept pass, paired inside the pass, then its mean and 95% interval over passes (t at n − 1 degrees of freedom). D is over the format's zeros (interpolated in time between the pass's three zeros anchors), S over the run's idle bracket.

| rule | statistic | line | verdicts |
|---|---|---|---|
| **R0** the answer (E109) | which of G_op, G_lane, G_rest carries G | a share carries it if its 95% lower bound ≥ G/2 | PER-OP / LANES / PER-TIME / MIXED |
| **R1** per-op work (E109; critic 5) | G_op = e_op(int8) O(int8) − e_op(fp32) O(fp32) | PASS if lower ≥ G/2; FAIL if upper < G/2; **NOT-SCORED with fewer than 6 kept passes** | PASS / FAIL / INCONCLUSIVE |
| **R2** the lanes (E109) | G_lane = U(int8) − U(fp32), U = −D(m0) | LANES-CARRY if lower ≥ G/2; LANES-OPPOSE if upper < 0 | CARRY / OPPOSE / NEITHER |
| **R3** per-time (E109) | G_rest = G − G_lane − G_op | PASS if lower ≥ G/2; FAIL if upper < G/2 | PASS / FAIL / INCONCLUSIVE |
| **Y1** the no-issue gap (E124) | N = S(m0.int8.zeros) − S(m0.fp32.zeros) | PASS if lower ≥ 0.84 W; FAIL if upper < 0.84 W | PASS / FAIL / INCONCLUSIVE |
| **Y2** per-op ceiling (E124) | e_op(fp32), e_op(int8) | PASS if both upper < 769.7 pJ; FAIL if either lower ≥ 769.7 pJ | PASS / FAIL / INCONCLUSIVE |

**What a result changes.** V1 PER-ROW: int8's no-issue excess goes with its per-row work, the TenC read-address walk or its A path, and the TIMA controls at the cards' issue price and TenC's reads as such are excluded as its carriers. V1 PER-CYCLE: it is work int8 does every cycle at the same op rate; the walk and the A path are excluded, and TenC's reads, the TIMA controls and the core remain. R1 with six or more passes: FAIL bounds per-op work below half the gap (registered), PASS puts the gap on per-op work. Departures, if any, are dated amendments written before the data they touch.
