## 1. What A1 says

Amendment A1, clause 3 (`tools/claims-v3/e211/E211.md`, section Amendments), verbatim: "section 6's text corrected (the static loop counts
36 / 35 / 31; sys_emu's steps 47.742 / 41.695 / 32.762, and 40.566 for gemmLnc, are not instruction counts)".

The text it put in section 6, verbatim: "The steady-state cooperative loop, read statically from the desk builds' disassembly (verify-e211),
runs **36 instructions an op in rgm136's gemmL** (2 of them stack loads, on the path from the wait to the A and B loads), **35 in rgm211's
own copy of gemmL's walk** (1 stack load) and **31 in rgm211's gemmLn** (none); after the wait the FMA is the 6th, 6th and 9th instruction
and the A load the 28th, 27th and 24th. The kit's sys_emu runs (mask 0x1, fp32, 2x2x1) give 47.742, 41.695 and 32.762 steps an op for the
three, and 40.566 for gemmLnc on rgm136's ELF: steps are not instructions (they run +10.6, +5.6 and +0.6 over the static counts, about 5
steps for each stack load)."

The same counts or the same reading of the steps also stand in: A1 clause 1 ("rgm136's gemmL at 36 instructions an op with 2 stack loads
on its path from the wait to the loads, rgm211's gemmLn at 31 with none"); E211.md's bullet "*Added by A1:* two compiled loops" ("31
against 36, section 6"); `rules_e211.py` and `rules-e211.json` (A1's finding text); `KIT.md` (the kernel check's table heading, its

The steps are the kit's committed simulator record (`tools/claims-v3/e211/logs/simcheck-e211.txt`: shire mask 0x1, fp32, 256 ops a

**The loops A1 counted** belong to another routine: `run_gemm`, the small-GEMM routine both ELFs carry beside `run_gemm_large` (the
counted gemmL run never entered it):

| ELF | loop | source | instructions an op | stack loads | FMA / A load / B load / wait | counted executions |
|---|---|---|---:|---:|---|---:|

These are A1's numbers exactly (36 and 35, 2 and 1 stack loads, the FMA 6th, the A load 28th and 27th). Besides `addr2line`, the code shows
it: their tensor loads take the stride register x31 from other registers (`mv t6,a7` and `mv t6,s4` in rgm136, `mv t6,t1` and `mv t6,s1`
in rgm211), as `run_gemm` passes its runtime `a_stride` and `b_stride`, while every `run_gemm_large` loop loads constants (`li t6,64` and
`li t6,65`: the stride 64, with the B load's id bit), as its source passes 64. A1's 31 for gemmLn is right.

**The counted run.** Round Q198's lane `mm-timing` ran rgm136's gemmL1x1x1 (fp32 random tiles, shire mask 0x1, 32 minions, seed 1) under
the simulator on aifoundry3 and counted it with `tools/counter/count_kernel.py` in log mode. Its record is not committed (sha256
`6bd8db2f2cedf1aad197264f00404ed7ebd5ee68ee13ae2b79054987a3ed0051`); the disassembly it names (sha256 `3f481ff6...`) equals the pinned
rgm136's line for line. In it:
- every one of the 64 harts retired exactly as many instructions as its `cycles_span`, the simulator's steps (3,300 and 3,300 on each of
  the 32 harts that ran the GEMM; 57 and 57 on the others): **one instruction a step**, as the counter's docstring reads a hart that never
  waits, and as the kit's own record says ("The simulator's 'cycles' are its steps (one instruction a hart a step: not time)");
- the 32 GEMM harts' per-PC counts are identical. On each, the 46 PCs of `run_gemm_large`'s steady op ran 62 or 64 times (9 PCs 62
  times, 37 PCs 64 times: two C-tile rows of 32 ops, the first op of a row entering past the FMA and the last op's FMA after the loop), the
  store path's 6 other PCs once, the loop's range 2,932 of the 3,300 instructions; the kernel's own cycle read was 3,125 steps for its 64
  ops (48.828 an op);
- `run_gemm`'s loop (A1's 36) and gemmLnc's loop ran 0 times on every hart.

**Steps against instructions.** With the loops the arms run, the kit's step counts sit 1.5 to 1.8 steps an op above each loop's census,
for loops holding 0, 5, 8, 8 and 12 stack loads: the excess is the work outside the steady loop (each C-tile row's set-up, store and
last FMA, and the launch's) spread over 256 ops a minion, not the stack loads. "About 5 steps for each stack load" came from the loops A1
counted: the loops the arms run are 10 (rgm136) and 5 (rgm211) instructions longer than those, which hold 2 and 1 stack loads. A1's
differences do not follow from its own figures either: 47.742 - 36 = 11.742, 41.695 - 35 = 6.695 and 32.762 - 31 = 1.762, not +10.6,
+5.6 and +0.6.

## 3. The corrected statements

- Section 6 (and KIT.md's correction paragraph): the steady-state cooperative loop runs **46 instructions an op in rgm136's gemmL** (12 of
  them stack loads, 8 before the A load and 11 before the B load; 4 stack stores), **40 in rgm211's own copy of gemmL's walk** (8 stack
  loads, 1 stack store) and **31 in rgm211's gemmLn** (none); after the wait the FMA is the 12th, 10th and 9th instruction and the A load
  the 35th, 31st and 24th. gemmLnc's loop (rgm136's ELF, non-cooperative) runs 39 (8 stack loads, 3 stack stores). The kit's sys_emu
  steps (47.742, 41.695, 32.762 and 40.566 an op) are instructions, one a hart a step: each is the kernel's whole count over its ops, 1.5
  to 1.8 above its steady loop at these small shapes.
- A1 clause 1's restatement, the "*Added by A1*" bullet and the findings row: the controls compare rgm136's gemmL loop at **46**
  instructions an op (11 stack loads on its path from the wait to the B load) with rgm211's gemmLn at 31 (none): 15 instructions an op
  apart, not 5.
- The scorer's label: simulator steps are not time; they are instruction counts (the kernel's whole count over its ops, not the steady
  loop's).

## 4. How it was checked (commands; every check below RAN unless marked)

1. The ELFs: copies of the registration's desk builds whose sha256 equal `tools/claims-v3/e211/kernel-e211.json`'s `elf_sha256` (rgm136
   `aace138c...`, rgm211 `00402801...`); `riscv64-unknown-elf-objcopy -O binary -j .text <ELF>` gives the `.text` pins (`61315bf6...`,
   `82c79807...`), and gives them also for the kit's aifoundry3 builds (the bytes the card ran).
2. `riscv64-unknown-elf-objdump -d <ELF> > <name>.dis` (GNU objdump 2.45, the lab toolchain).
3. `python3 a1_census_e211.py --dis rgm136=rgm136.dis --dis rgm211=rgm211.dis --counted rgm136=<the counted record>` (this folder; its
   method is in its docstring: every inner loop holding one TensorFMA, two TensorLoads and one TensorWait; every path once round it inside
   its address range, with no branch-direction rule; the steady op is the path with no TensorStore). Its output is the appendix.
4. The source names: each kernel tree as committed (`tools/claims-v3/e136/rgm136`, `tools/claims-v3/e211/rgm211`) rebuilt with the pinned
   builds' own compiler and linker flags plus `-g` (two LTO jobs); each `-g` build's `.text` equals its pin, so
   `riscv64-unknown-elf-addr2line -f -i -e <-g ELF> <address>` names the inlined function at each loop of the pinned code.
5. A cross-check: the four steady paths equal Q214 `v3-design-b`'s census PC for PC (its lane record, not committed).
6. NOT RUN: a counted run of any rgm211 loop or of gemmLnc's (their censuses are desk: static paths, checked by the kit's step counts
   only); any card or simulator run for this note.
