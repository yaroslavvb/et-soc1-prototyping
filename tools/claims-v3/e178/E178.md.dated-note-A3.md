## A3-1. The arm: what is read, by which statistic

**Every one-number contrast the scorer reads, 52 in all, in its order:**
- the four couple rules: E178-H-int8, -fp32, -fp16 (5s - 4s) and E178-U (fp32 ones - e178_uf);
- E178-S-int8 (85 - neg_ones), with 127 - neg_ones beside it (A1-1), and A2-2's 85 - 127;
- every pair of section 4 that `forecast-e178.json` lists (the bridge, the crosses, equal flips with different values, equal popcount
  with different flips, his 4s against 5s), and each format's largest and smallest pairs, as `score_e178.reported` reads them.

**Three columns, one statistic.** Each contrast is read three times, by the scorer's own statistic for it, imported unchanged:
- the **board**, D: this is the scorer's own reading, recomputed. Its verdicts stay the scorer's (`results.md`, `scored.json`);
- the **minion rail**, Drail;
- the **remainder off the minion rail**, D - Drail: the SRAM and NoC rails' part and everything off the three metered rails. It is not
  the anatomy's "off-rail" line, which leaves out all three rails.

For the couple rules, x_p is `score_e178.couple_xs`, the mean over the pass's segments in which both runs were kept of the in-segment
difference, from the primary's `runs.csv`. For the rest it is `score_e178.pair_xs`, the difference of the two kinds' per-pass values in
the primary's `e178.json`. The interval is `score_e178.interval`, m ± t(0.975, n - 1) sd / sqrt(n), over the kept passes that give a value.
Fewer than 2 such passes: NOT READ. E178-C applies as the scorer applies it: when `scored.json` is given, a contrast with a kind E178-C
left out is not read.

**Never a verdict.** No rule, verdict, threshold or registered power figure reads the arm, and none of A1-1's four-rule reading or A2-2's
limit changes. Its readings are words of their own, so that none is mistaken for one of the scorer's:
- the interval **reads above 0** (its lower end above 0), **reads below 0**, or **spans 0**;
- each frozen forecast, P and F, is **inside**, **above** or **below** the interval.

The scorer's words (POSITIVE, NEGATIVE, NOT RESOLVED, PASS, FAIL HIGH, FAIL LOW) stay its verdicts' alone.

**The identity.** In every kept pass, board = rail + remainder for every contrast, exactly but for rounding, whenever every kept run of the
two kinds has a Drail. A kept run without one is named, the rail and the remainder are read on the runs that have one, and the contrast
is flagged (`rail_reads_the_board_runs` false).

## A3-2. Its forecast, frozen by a mechanical rule with no measured error

**The rule.** For a contrast (a, b) of type t, the rail forecast is the registered board forecast times r_t, and the remainder's is the
board forecast times (1 - r_t):
- the board forecast is `forecast-e178.json`'s frozen P of a less b on aifoundry3, and F likewise;
- r_t is the median, over every item of E171's committed reduction (`e171.json` `items`) of type t whose D mean over passes is above
  2 W, of Drail.mean / D.mean.

No E178 datum, no measured error and no fit enters. The forecast is not rescaled by the session's s or rates. For int8, s = 1 and E171's
rates spread 1.6e-4; for the floats, s moved E171's forecasts by about 0.6%.

## A3-3. Why, post hoc

*Post hoc: reads of committed data after their sessions and before any E178 datum. None is a forecast, and none decides anything.*

- **The minion rail is the only line that resolved a one-number difference** (round A's anatomy, `anatomy.json` `tensor_pairs`; this
  reader re-reads every one of its 12 pairs from Q139(u)'s footing reductions to 3.3e-12 W, check T5). int8 -1 against 1 reads:
  - on aifoundry3 (E78, 6 passes): **+0.093 ± 0.018 W on the rail**, against +0.069 ± 0.116 W at the board;
  - on card 1 (E82, 7 passes): **+0.162 ± 0.063 W**, against +0.116 ± 0.119 W.

  The remainder off the rail reads -0.025 ± 0.118 and -0.046 ± 0.108 W there, about as wide as the board. So for that pair the board's
  noise sits off the minion rail, and the rail reads the difference 6.5 times (E78) and 1.9 times (E82) more finely.
- **For the floats it is no finer.** Over the anatomy's ten float one-number pairs the rail reads ±0.09-0.20 W, against the board's
  ±0.09-0.18 W.
- **A caution from E171** (this reader, check T4; E171's own items, never E178's). Two random tiles of one format, pool 8's anchor
  pairs, read the rail coarser than the board: ±0.24 (int8), ±0.28 (fp32) and ±0.49 W (fp16) a pair at 4 passes, against ±0.09, ±0.17
  and ±0.14 W, with the remainder ±0.21-0.42 W. There the rail and the remainder move against each other, and their sum, the board, is
  the steadier line.

So the finer reading is a fact of the int8 one-number pair on two cards, not a property of the rail, and nothing is claimed for the arm's
resolution before the data: its intervals carry their own widths. *Design arithmetic, never a forecast:* at E78's per-pass sd for that
pair on the rail (0.017 W, one run each), H-int8's three-couple mean would read about ±0.016 W at four passes; at the sd of E171's random
int8 pair, about ±0.14 W. Its rail forecast is +0.156 W.
