## A4-2. Fewer than 2 kept passes with the scorer's scored.json: NOT READ, not a refusal (fault 2)

**What.** With fewer than 2 kept passes and `--scored`, A3's reader refuses with exit 7 and says the files "are not one session's", which is
false. The scorer's own scored.json for such a session has no reported pairs: `score_reduction` returns early (`score_e178.py` lines
391-395) with `NOT SCORED` and every rule NOT SCORED, so A3's cross-check finds "scored.json lists None - None here" at each of the 45
reported pairs. The card day's command always passes `--scored`, so a TWO session with one pass set aside, or a FULL one with three, would
have recorded a refusal whose reason is false.

**The fix (v2).** v2 first reads what the scorer read (`scorer_state`):
- scored.json's `primary.kept_passes`, where present, must equal the reduction's kept passes, or v2 refuses 7, saying so;
- a primary with reported pairs is cross-checked as A3's reader does, unchanged;
- a primary that reads `NOT SCORED` beside fewer than 2 kept passes is the scorer not scoring: nothing is compared, the rules' NOT SCORED
  is carried, no column may read a value, and the session reads `NOT READ: N kept passes, fewer than 2 (the scorer's own NOT SCORED: ...)`
  with exit 0;
- a primary that reads `NOT SCORED` beside 2 or more kept passes, or holds no reported pairs and no `NOT SCORED`, refuses 7, saying which.

## A4-3. A kept run without a Drail is flagged in both forms; the remainder A3-1 names (fault 3)

**What.** A3-1 says that for a kept run without a Drail "the rail and the remainder are read on the runs that have one, and the contrast is
flagged (`rail_reads_the_board_runs` false)". A3's reader does that for the four couple rules only. In pass form (section 4's pairs and
extremes, S-int8 and the beside contrasts) it reads each (pass, kind) from the reduction's means: the rail's over the runs that have a
Drail, the board's over every kept run, and the remainder as the board's mean less the rail's. The identity then holds by construction, so
the flag read true. The session-level list (`consistency.pass_kinds_with_a_run_without_Drail`) still named the run. It has not happened on
committed data (0 of 335 kept runs in E171, 0 of 304 in the kit's collector test).

**The fix (v2), keeping A3-1's words.** A contrast holding a (pass, kind) named in that list reads `rail_reads_the_board_runs` false in
either form, and names the pairs in `runs_without_Drail`. In pass form, that (pass, kind)'s remainder is the mean, over its kept runs that
have a Drail, of D - Drail, as A3-1 says; the board keeps the reduction's mean over every kept run (the scorer's reading), and the rail
the reduction's mean over the runs that have one. Every (pass, kind) with no such run is read exactly as A3's reader reads it. v2's
outputs list the contrasts flagged (`flagged`), and the md table gains a column, "every kept run has a Drail".
