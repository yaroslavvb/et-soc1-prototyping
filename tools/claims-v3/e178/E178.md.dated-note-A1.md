## A1-2. The one-number add: section 1 (b)'s "null" is withdrawn (major fault 2)

Section 1 (b) set the vector-unit add aside for two reasons. Neither holds.

**(1) "Its forecast is null in steady state ... no bit to flip by the number."** That counted only the operands and the values
stored. It missed the write-back path, which section 3 found falls to zero between results in the TensorFMA. The same RTL (core-et
`b38a1a3`, github.com/openhwfoundation/core-et, `rtl/shire/minion/vpu/`) gives the float vector ops the same path:
- `fadd.ps` and `fmadd.ps` decode to the lane's FMA unit (`vpu_decoder.v:85`, `:90`, the txfma column).
- That unit's result is AND-ed with its valid bit (`txfma_7s/txfma_top.v:1588`; the bench builds `txfma_7s`,
  `rtl-sim/fma_toggle_v2/Makefile:20`), so it is zero on every cycle without a result.
- F8's control is an enabled flop that holds across bubbles (`vpu_ctrl.v:1553-1561`; `f8_txfma_op` is its txfma bit, `:1585`). So
  the write data `f8_wdata` follows the unit's result (`vpu_bypass.v:221-222`, `:232`) into the register file's write port
  (`vpu_lane.v:521`), and three bypass registers copy that port every cycle (`vpu_rf.v:40`, `:46`). These are section 3's `wb_rz`
  and `byp`.
- The catalogue loop issues 0.36-0.37 instructions a cycle on each of the two harts (`docs/energy-manual/03-instructions.md`, lines
  17-20), so the unit's write-back has gaps between results. At each gap every 1-bit of the result falls and rises again.

So **a one-number float vector add pays a small popcount-of-the-result term**. 4 + 4 = 8.0 (`0x41000000`, two 1-bits) and 5 + 5 =
10.0 (`0x41200000`, three): a matrix of 5s adds one bit a lane, 64 more flips at each gap over the write data and its three copies
(8 lanes × 2 × 4), plus the unit's own output nets. Its size, from how many gaps the loop makes and the price of these flips, is not
derived here.

The integer add `fadd.pi` decodes to the short pipe instead (`vpu_decoder.v:190`). Its results pass through enable flops that load
only when a short-pipe result is written (`vpu_bypass.v:160-212`; `vpu_ctrl.v:1244`, `1334`, `1403`, `1463`, `1538`), so its write-back
may hold one value through the loop. Its forecast is open, not null.

**(2) "The energy manual's committed rows already measured that null."** The manual's "constant" is 1.0f in every source word
(`workloads/enercat/host/main.cpp:233`, `:283`).
- Its float rows compare a result of 2.0 (`0x40000000`, one 1-bit a lane) with zeros' 0.0. They read +0.5 pJ (`fadd.ps`, 22.9
  against 22.4) and +0.1 pJ (`fmadd.ps`), at about ±1 pJ (about ±0.45 W at the catalogue's issue rate). That fits a one-bit term and
  cannot measure a 4s-against-5s one.
- The `fadd.pi` row adds 1.0f's word as an integer (`0x7F000000`, seven 1-bits a lane) and reads equal to zeros (12.7 pJ both). That
  fits a held integer write-back but tests nothing at this resolution.

**So no E178 text says the one-number add is null:** section 1 (b) (1) and (2), and section 9's "measured precedent for the vector
arm's null", are withdrawn. Records and pages quote this section instead. The vector arm stays outside E178. Its later registration
derives the forecast from an RTL run of the catfull loop (both harts, the loop's own instruction stream and gaps), before any vector
card datum. It builds on the catalogue (`workloads/enercat`, `tools/claims-v3/catfull`), on core-et's own vector power stimulus
(`dv/tests/minion_core/c_asm_tests/power_vpu/ps`), and on et-platform's public `test-compute-kernels/src/vpu_txfma_power_virus`
(github.com/aifoundry-org/et-platform at `836a4ab`; the prior-work stock-take of 5 Nov names both). Their flips can be counted on the
bench before any card runs them.
