## What this cannot say

- One session, one card, one evening; the plateau moves 0.3–1 °C between sessions with nothing changed (item 8), which sets every band's
  floor, and there is no inlet thermometer: the rest die is the room's proxy.
- The rest die is a whole-degree reading (±0.5 °C), and in the trip regime G2 tests the governor's hold at the 65/66 boundary, not the chain.
- G1's frozen t63 rule reads the open loop's 49.6 s, which a die from rest does not reach on this card (A1.8); it is reported as frozen.
- Holds of 15 minutes reach about 98% of the open loop's plateau; a stage slower than about 10 minutes is not resolved.
