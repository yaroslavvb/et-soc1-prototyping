*Written Mon 2 Nov 2026 (week 45), Q137 (lane `a1c1-q137`), before the claim, before any launch and before any E137 data. It answers [the kinds page's section 11, item 5](https://spacesheep.dev/@yaroslavvb/horace-kinds-of-numbers#11-what-is-still-open), "Where a random sign's ~5 W goes", where [E128](../../../docs/reports/data/2026-11-02-e128-fp16-signs/README.md) left it: on E128's matched tiles, whose held and walking tiles alternate between adding and subtracting exactly as often, the share of fp16's rest that goes with the sums changing read +0.10 and +0.05 on two passes, and the share with the accumulators differing 0.90 and 0.95, so E128-R8 and E128-R9 read INCONCLUSIVE at t(0.975, 1) = 12.71 ("more passes of the same tiles are what it needs (four would make t(0.975, 3) = 3.18)"). ## The set-aside rule (written here; `predictions-e137.json` › `set_aside_rule`)

E131's rule, with E137's minimum of four:

- **S1, the primary.** E78's pass gates unchanged (each type's zeros line departs ≤ 0.25 W from its straight line and spans ≤ 0.60 W; each random anchor spans ≤ 1.0 W; the π tie drifts ≤ 0.60 W; at most 2 failed runs; card 1's config read). It carries every verdict if it keeps **at least 4 passes**.
- **S2, only if S1 keeps fewer than 4.** A pass set aside **only** by the π tie's drift is restored whole (π enters no rule but the session tie).
- **S3, only if S1 and S2 keep fewer than 4.** A pass set aside only by the fp32 or int8 anchors' gates (and the tie) is restored for fp16's items: every rule reads fp16 D, which is over fp16's own zeros line. A pass set aside by fp16's own anchors (`fp16.zeros`' departure or span, `fp16.randn`'s span) is never restored.
- **Never restored:** a pass with more than 2 failed runs, a config read other than card 1's 65 W / 65 °C, a block that did not end ok, an `.attempt-*` directory.
- **Per rule:** a quantity is formed only in kept passes where every tile it reads has a kept run (a run skipped by E78's launch rule, as E128's `fp16.signs` in 12811, leaves that pass out of that quantity only). **A rule is scored only if its quantity has at least 4 passes**, else NOT SCORED with its n.
- Restored values come from `reduce_e137.py --relax-anchors` at the same frozen slope (a pass's reduction does not depend on the others' at a fixed slope). No pass is added after any reduction is read; no pass is chosen after the fact; every reading is reported and every verdict names the reading that carries it.

## Decision rules

Intervals are t(0.975, n − 1) over per-pass values, a ratio or difference formed within each pass (E128's convention). Arms (dips kept; gates off; leakage auto, 0.1994, 0.3803, 0.545, 0 W/°C) are scored S1-only on their own passes; a verdict that differs at an arm is marked arm-sensitive.

Reported, no rule: every quantity of `predictions-e137.json` › `quantities` with M4 beside it; each item's D against M4; a **pooled arm** of E128's two passes (its committed per-pass D) and E137's, each pass one replicate, with R1–R4's bands labelled as the arm's; the session's maxima against the stops and the PMIC's 75 W alarm.

**What I expect, frozen so it can be wrong** (E128's two passes are known, so these are informed priors, not blind ones): R1 **MOST** (E128 0.902, 0.950); R2 **ALMOST NONE or INCONCLUSIVE** (E128 +0.098, +0.050: a mean near +0.07 with four passes may straddle +0.10; M4 says −0.04); R3 MOST; R4 ALIKE or INCONCLUSIVE; R5 PASS; R6 PASS; R7 PASS or INCONCLUSIVE; R8 PASS or INCONCLUSIVE; S1 PASS.

**What each outcome does to the prediction of signed fp16's power.** M4 prices a random sign by its lane C-read, result and store bits (core, bus and rest terms) and has no term for the running sum changing. R2 ALMOST NONE with R1 MOST: the sign term needs nothing more, and fp16's rest on zero products is priced by the accumulator bits alone, as fp32's. R2 SOME: the model is missing a sums-changing term of the measured size, and the README says how many watts it is at this card's clock. R7's rms is the out-of-sample error of M4's sign term in fp16 on this card, against E128's two-pass reading of the same contrasts.

## Stops

**No power cycle and nothing that risks one** (the owner's rule of 2 Nov 2026). The registered stops, each ending the pass (`run_e78`'s EXIT_HOT) and the queue: a telemetry line with **board_w ≥ 68 W**, or **any minion-shire reading ≥ 72 °C** (`run_e137.py`'s guard), or `run_e78`'s own `--max-c 72` on the first shire reading (its PMIC field ≥ 82 °C, a die reading on this runner). Margin: E128's two passes of these tiles plus the fp32 ones on this card read at most 59.79 W on the board and 61 °C as the shires' mean, no shire above 65 °C (`docs/reports/data/2026-11-02-e128-fp16-signs/desk/telemetry-max-e128.txt`); E137 keeps `fp32.randn`, the hottest kind, as an anchor; the PMIC's alarm is 75 W. Also: the STOP files (`build/e137/STOP`, `build/claims-v3/STOP`); another user's device process, a device node or this card's lock held by anyone else (D1: an idle login does not block on aifoundry1); `timeout 10` on every device process.
