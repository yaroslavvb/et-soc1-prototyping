## 2.1 A session that kept fewer than 2 passes reads NOT SCORED in the pooled read (the verifier's fault 1)

**What was wrong.** E171.md registers "Fewer than 2 kept passes: every rule NOT SCORED", and P8-KA pooled needs 4 check anchors over the two
600 MHz sessions. Note 1's check 6 counted the rows a scored file holds and required both check anchors to have one. The committed scorer
writes no rows for such a session: `score_reduction` returns every verdict NOT SCORED with the mark `"<k> kept passes, fewer than 2"`, and
`score_session` adds only P8-KC's block. So `score_p8_600_v2.py ka-pooled` refused (exit 6) a genuine scored pair that should read NOT
SCORED, and step 22 v2 stopped with 16, saying the two files were not E171's and E172's. The verifier showed it on the scorer alone (its
A2(iii): v2 exit 6, "0 checked"; the committed `ka_pooled` on the same files reads NOT SCORED, n 2) and end to end through step 22 v2 with
the real scorer and gate (its B5: E171 collected, then the pooled read 16).

**Registered now.** The pooled reading is the one `tools/claims-v3/p8-600/score_p8_600_v3.py ka-pooled <E171 scored.json> <E172
scored.json>` gives (the next commit), and no other: v2's `ka-pooled` and the committed scorer's are not the registered pooled reading.
v3 keeps note 1's checks 1-5 unchanged. Its check 6 reads:

6. each file is its own card's: either its rows are (note 1's check: every scored row's a-priori D equals its rate times that card's frozen
   per-op coefficient within 1e-9 W, and both check anchors have a row), or the file is the committed scorer's own NOT SCORED session: its
   primary carries the scorer's mark `"<k> kept passes, fewer than 2"` with k the length of its `kept_passes` and k < 2, and holds no rows,
   no rule but P8-KC, and no verdict but NOT SCORED. A file with that mark beside rows, beside two kept passes, with a count that is not its
   kept passes', beside a verdict other than NOT SCORED or beside a P8-KA block, or with no rows and no mark, is refused (exit 6).

Such a session holds no P8-KA block, so it adds no check anchor: with the other session's two, the committed `ka_pooled` (called
unchanged) reads NOT SCORED, "2 check anchors, fewer than 4"; with both sessions short, "0 check anchors". That is E171.md's NOT SCORED
case, as registered. No reading can become PASS or FAIL from one session: the pooled test needs 4 check anchors, and a session gives at most 2.
