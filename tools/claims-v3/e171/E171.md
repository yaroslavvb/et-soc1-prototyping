## How the scorer computes them (the forms POOL8-REGISTRATION leaves to the kit)

The scorer is **`tools/claims-v3/p8-600/score_p8_600.py`** (next commit), shared by E171 and E172 and run with `--card aifoundry3`. It checks the predictor's hash lists (`predict_pool8_a2.check_lists`, then `predict_pool8.check_inputs` over POOL8-SHA256SUMS's 209) and the predictions file's sha256 against the freeze's `SHA256SUMS`, then imports `predict_pool8_a2.py` and reads every line from its functions. Nothing in it is fitted or tuned.

- **The registered primary reduction**: Q139(u)'s footing, `reduce_e171.py --leak 0.20563713796102187 --no-refit --drop-dips` (E78's `reduce_e78.py` unchanged; E82 A2's dip rule once), E78's run and pass gates. Reported beside, never the verdict: the session-own footing (leakage fitted on the session, dips kept); leakage fitted with the dips removed; the frozen slope with the dips kept; leakage 0.1994, 0.3803 and 0 W/°C with the dips removed; E78's pass gates opened. Each is scored by the same code, with its own scale.
- **A tile's measured value** is its mean over the kept passes it ran in, from the reduction's per-pass values (E163's `measured`, copied): D for power, `dT_win` for the die rise. A tile with fewer than 2 is left out and named. **Fewer than 2 kept passes: every rule NOT SCORED.**
- **The scale, then the primary**, by the frozen predictor and in that order (POOL8-REGISTRATION § 3): `predict_pool8.scale(predictions, reduction)` reads only the anchors' items (M10's own float anchors that ran, `fp32.randn` and `fp16.randn`, and pool 8's four float scale anchors); then `predict_pool8.primary(predictions, reduction, scale)` gives each tile's M10 primary (s × measured rate × the per-op coefficient), its 95% band, the a-priori arm (s = 1, the measured rate), M9 at the measured rate and the 4.5 s heat at the primary D. **The scorer refuses (exit 5, nothing written) if any anchor the scale used is not one of M10's float anchors or pool 8's scale anchors, or is a check anchor or a zeros anchor** ("the check anchors never enter s").
- **P8-KC** is E163's C status, copied: for each pool-8 tile, every launch record of the tile in a kept pass's `runs.jsonl` must have a C record with the same `t_start_ms` and `t_end_ms` that `ccheck.py check` judged PASS; ROUNDING, UNMODELLED, FAIL, a missing record and a kept pass without a `runs.jsonl` are not PASS. **A tile not all PASS leaves every scored set**, and every rule is also scored over all tiles beside it (reported).
- **The bootstrap rules** (P1-P3, B2, H1-H3) take `predict_pool8.boot_range(reference, n, rule)` at the scored n; **B1** takes `predict_pool8_a2.mix_range` with F1's scored fp32 and fp16 counts; the H references are `predict_pool8_a2.heat_ref(card, t)`; the coverage lines (C1, H4) are `binom_low(n)`; a rule under its minimum (`pool8-registration.json` › `scoring.minimum_tiles`) is NOT SCORED.
- **The paired rules** (M1, M1f, M1i, A1): d = rms(a) − rms(b) over the set's scored tiles in sorted name order; 20,000 draws (`scoring.paired_draws`) from `random.Random(80000 + Σ ord(rule id))`, each drawing as many tiles as the set holds, with replacement (`sel[rng.randrange(len(sel))] for _ in sel`, E163's `compare_rule` form); the interval is the sorted values' [int(0.025 × 20,000)] and [int(0.975 × 20,000) − 1]: BETTER if its upper end < 0, WORSE if its lower end > 0, else NOT RESOLVED. For M1 a = D − M10 primary and b = D − M9 at the measured rate; for A1 b = D − M10's a-priori arm at the measured rate.
- **P8-KA**, per session: over KA's scored tiles, e_p = D − M10 primary and e_0 = D − M10 a priori; PASS if Σ e_p² < Σ e_0² and every measured D is inside M10's primary 95% band; FAIL otherwise; NOT SCORED if s = 1 (no float scale anchor measured) or no KA tile is scored. **Pooled** over E171 and E172 (minimum 4), read once both are collected: `score_p8_600.py ka-pooled <E171 scored.json> <E172 scored.json>`, the same test over the union.
- **P8-LG**: for each scored float re-run, Δ = D (this session) − D_ref (its `lg_reference` entry: the entry whose tile name equals the re-run's, else the first), grouped by that entry's session; `design_power_groups.lg_test(groups, detail=True)` gives the verdict, each group's a and g with intervals and the null; each tile's Δ with Welch's 95% interval (`pick6.welch`, this session's se and n against the reference's) and M10's primary errors on RR are reported.
- **P8-G** as § 7: B1 and B2 FAIL HIGH or FAIL LOW count as FAIL. **P8-HM** is NOT SCORED (no hold).
- **Reported beside, never a verdict:** M10's a-priori arm at the frozen stated rate (`D_stated_w`), M9 at the nominal rate, every rule per class (S, U, L, H, R) and over all tiles, the priority-2 kinds, the anchors, the measured rate against the stated one, and the scale with its anchors' ratios.
- **The scorer refuses (exit 4, no `scored.json`)** if any session of § The sessions that must carry a C log has no finished check of C. The registered reading is then NOT SCORED for every rule, and the collector's gate has already stopped (exit 14).
