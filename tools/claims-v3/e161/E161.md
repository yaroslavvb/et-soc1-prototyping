## What it does to predicting power and heat

**The term tested** is the whole path from a computation's description to its die over minutes on a governed card: the tile's bits, then
M8's board power at 800 MHz (Q139(m)), then Q138(t)'s frozen chain from board watts to the die, closed through the 0.442 W/°C leakage law,
plus aifoundry2's governor (Q138(t)'s trip rule, a die that would pass the trip is held at 65.9 °C, and M8's 65.2 W cap). On the trip side
the same path gives a **sustained clock from the bits** (the level whose board holds the die at 65.9 °C).

**The out-of-sample error before** (all from committed data, none from the bits over minutes):
- given the measured board watts, the chain reads a governed 15-minute hold to 0.37-0.76 °C rms (E144b, blind, 2 Nov; plateaus +0.72 /
  +0.12 °C);
- from the planned power (not the bits), E144b's G1 plateau +1.09 °C and G2's trip-rule plateau −0.21 °C;
- from the bits, only over a 6 s burst: 0.585 °C rms (E143, M7) and 0.68 °C nested (M8, `m8.json`);
- **from the bits over minutes: never scored.** Q139(e)'s expected 95% sizes: 3.15 °C for G1q (the 800 MHz regime), ±0.60 °C at the trip,
  and the clock to ±35 MHz from the power path, with the heat path's R able to move it 11-23 MHz lower (A1 C2).

**After:** E161 measures each against the frozen numbers below. The session figure is the out-of-sample error of heat over minutes from
the bits on this card (G1q's plateau error where the bits set the die; G2q's error where the governor sets it), and G2q's clock error is the
first out-of-sample figure for a sustained governed clock from the bits on a new kind.

  whole-degree reading (±0.5 °C) and the room's only proxy, and there is no inlet thermometer.

- G1Q's band (3.15 °C) is 2.4-3.1 times the critic's 1.0-1.3 °C (Q139(e)'s D4), so its verdict cannot separate M8 from M7; the
  informative figures are the errors themselves, E − A and the session rms.
- Holds of 13 minutes read about 98% of the closed loop's plateau on this card; a stage slower than about 10 minutes is not resolved.

## How to run it (for this lane)

At the desk (`/usr/bin/python3`, numpy 1.26.4): `make_protocols_e161.py`, the audit (`logs/dmcmd-audit.txt`), `reduce_e161.py --self-test`,
`ccheck.py domain` on the tiles. On aifoundry2, from `~/claude/et-soc1-prototyping` with these files synced in: `bash
tools/claims-v3/e161/run_e161.sh check`, `… dry` (the toy card, every step), `… config start`, `… go <smoke-not-before> <smoke-latest>`,
`… watch`, `… config end`. After the queue: `collect_e161.sh <data dir> <from s> <to s>`, `ccheck.py check` on each step's C log at the desk,
then `/usr/bin/python3 tools/claims-v3/e161/reduce_e161.py <data dir> --json <data dir>/results.json > <data dir>/reduce.txt`. Data:
`docs/reports/data/2026-11-03-e161-heat-e2e-aifoundry2/`.
