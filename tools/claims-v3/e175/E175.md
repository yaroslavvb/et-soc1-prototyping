## What it does to predicting power and heat

**Before:** aifoundry2's governed burst clock has one blind score, M9's part on pool 7 (E165: 12 governed tiles, 3.03 MHz rms, mean −1.04 MHz;
board 0.71 W rms, mean −0.43 W), and Q140(l)'s held-out end-to-end figures (36 governed cells: 4.50 MHz, 0.78 W). M10 (the model refitted with a
per-session float scale and the single high B bits counted by the convergence rule; held out 0.185 W on floats at 600 MHz) is carried to aifoundry2
by **rule E** (POOL8-REGISTRATION.md § 5, `3e97f583`) and has never been scored under the governor on a governed tile. **After:** M10 carried's
governed clock and board errors on 16 new kinds spread across the governed range (about 727-795 MHz predicted), scored against rank 2's fixed lines
(E175-A2-G, E175-A2-Bg) and against the held-out figures (E175-A2-F1, E175-A2-W1); 8 new pinned kinds in the same session (E175-A2-Bp, F2, W2), so
that **rank 2's three lines (E175-A2-R2) can read PASS for the first time on blind kinds**; M10 against M9's part on the board (E175-A2-M1); the burst
heat law on a fourth pool (E175-A2-H1). The governed clock is where power prediction meets speed prediction: under the cap the board sits at the
governor's plateau (about 67.1 W) and the kind of number sets the clock, so this is the test of "how fast does this kind of number run on this card"
from the bits alone.

## The question

1. **Does M10 carried predict the governed burst clock of new kinds within rank 2's lines?** (E175-A2-G: rms ≤ 4.0 MHz and |mean| ≤ 2.0 MHz.)
2. **Is the governed board level right on new kinds?** (E175-A2-Bg: |mean| ≤ 0.5 W.) And the pinned board? (E175-A2-Bp: |mean| ≤ 0.5 W and rms ≤
   1.0 W.) Rank 2 on M10 carried, all three (E175-A2-R2).
3. **Is it no worse than held out?** (E175-A2-F1, W1 governed; F2, W2 pinned.)
4. **Is M10 carried better than M9's part on M9's D on the same bursts?** (E175-A2-M1, paired, the board; the clock beside.)
5. **Does the burst heat law (0.1400 °C/W) hold on new kinds?** (E175-A2-H1.)
6. Is every launch's C right (E175-C), and has the card drifted (E175-R0, E80's two tiles)?
7. Reported, never a verdict: the governed clock error's slope on the predicted clock (E175-GS), each stratum's errors, M9's part on every set, the
   peaks against both models' upper peaks, k_T per burst, the zeros anchors under the frozen fallback.

## The design: how the tiles are drawn, kept or replaced (committed here, before any tile exists)

**The seed.** `SEED_COMMIT = "4576fb9078726ec4ae186f2e91170986f6e03c58"` (lab commit `4576fb90`, Q142(x): pool 8's predictions frozen, 5 Nov
02:30:56 PST, an ancestor of this commit); `SEED = int(sha256(SEED_COMMIT.encode("ascii")).hexdigest()[:16], 16)` = `0xbd33bd555026eb2e` =
13633448671095221038, pool 8's own rule applied to that commit (DESIGN.md § 1). Its value follows from a commit that exists and cannot be chosen.
Every draw is on one stream per tag, `numpy.random.default_rng([SEED, the first 8 bytes, little-endian, of sha256("e175/" + "/".join(tag))])`,
make_kinds' and make_blind8's scheme under the prefix `e175/`.

**The generator** is pool 8's committed one, unchanged: [`../kinds/blind8/make_blind8.py`](../kinds/blind8/make_blind8.py) (`153b720f`, sha256
`fee9a4f0…a76d14ea`, checked before it is imported), its twelve float families `U_FLOAT` (tdist, lognorm, mixture, uniform, expo, grid, rowscale,
sparse, ar_i, ar_k, bimodal, cauchy: DESIGN.md § 5.3) and its `u_float()`, with make_kinds' `encode`. [`make_e175_pool.py`](make_e175_pool.py)
(this commit) draws **candidate i = 0, 1, …, 63** (the cap: 64):
1. on stream `cand/i`: the format (fp32 or fp16, uniform), the family (one of the twelve, uniform), and the truncation m (fp32 0-23, fp16 0-10,
   uniform);
2. on stream `fam/i`: the family's own parameters and values, by `u_float(family, stream, K)` (fp16 clipped at ±60,000 as make_blind8 does);
3. the low m mantissa bits of every element of A and B cleared (sign and exponent untouched; m = 0 leaves the family's draw as it is), then
   `make_kinds.encode`. The truncation is the knob that spreads the kinds' switching power across the governed range: the families alone give
   random-like tiles, which govern at the admission's edge in fp16 and lie over it in fp32 (pool 8's own U tiles);
4. **refused, and the next candidate drawn**, if make_blind8's `tile_refusal` (2,048 bytes, no NaN or Inf, encode gives back the bytes) or its
   `class_check` (class U: the bytes must read as no single-bit, held or whole-line structure) refuses it, or its bytes equal any committed tile file
   under `tools/claims-v3/kinds/` (the pools blind3-blind8, the kinds' tiles, the real cuts) or an earlier candidate's;
5. labelled from its index and bytes: `<fmt>.e5c<ii>_<family>_t<m>_<6 hex of the tile's sha256>`; class `U-trunc`; priority 1; role `kind`.

**Which candidates are kept** (`make_e175_pool.py select`, this commit), reading only the frozen predictor's predictions, never a measurement or
an error. For each candidate in stream order, after its bench counts reach the convergence rule's N (term b, POOL8-REGISTRATION.md § 2) and
`predict_pool8_a2.py predict <pool> aifoundry2` has predicted it:
1. **The admission.** If the larger of M10 carried's and M9's part's 95% upper peaks (`peak_board_hi95_w`, M8's peak rule as the part computes it)
   exceeds **82 W**, the candidate is out and **the next draw replaces it** (the brief's rule; E173's 82 W marks).
2. **Governed** (the frozen file's group: M10 carried predicts the burst below 795 MHz at the fallback and T_ref): its stratum by M10 carried's
   fallback clock, **S1 below 745, S2 745-762, S3 762-779, S4 779-795 MHz**; kept if its stratum holds fewer than 4.
3. **Pinned** (795 MHz or more): kept if the pinned set holds fewer than 8.
4. The stream stops at the candidate that fills all four strata (16 governed) and the pinned set (8).
5. A candidate the bench cannot bring to its N within N = 81, or that the frozen predictor refuses, is refused and the next one drawn (named in the
   selection's record).
6. **At the cap**, if a stratum is short, its open slots go to the governed candidates kept out only because their stratum was full, in stream
   order, up to 16 in all; a pinned set short of 8 runs as it is (E175-A2-Bp is then NOT SCORED under its minimum). **Fewer than 8 governed within
   the cap: E175 is NO-GO**, and only a dated amendment, committed before the tiles it governs, may extend the stream.

The bench counts the candidates in stream order, in chunks, and the selection runs on the candidates counted so far: because the rule reads the
stream in order and stops where the sets fill, counting more candidates after that changes nothing. **Every candidate counted is committed with its
predictions**, kept or not, so the selection can be re-run from the committed files.

**The pool** (`make_e175_pool.py build`): pool 8's three zeros anchors (`fp32/fp16/int8.anchor7_zeros`, pool 7's floors, one file's bytes under three
types, copied with their blind8.json records) and the 24 kept tiles, in pool 7's JSON form (`e175.json`), with a `SHA256SUMS`; `check` re-derives
every tile from the stream and its label from its bytes.

## The stops (E165's and E173's, unchanged) and the governor untouched

- **One die reading at or above 76 °C, or the board above 85 W on three live samples in a row**, ends the session at once (exit 4), under the keys
  `run_hpl.py`'s watcher reads.
- **The run-time admission** (`run_e105.py`): a burst runs only if its frozen estimate is at most **82 W** (a pool tile: the larger of M10's and M9's
  95% upper peaks; E80's replicates: E165's estimates, 80.52 W and 53.67 W); after any run whose highest board sample reached **82 W**, nothing at or
  above that run's estimate runs (E159's lock), the same tile included.
- **The governor is untouched:** no governor, TDP, threshold or clock set; no PMIC command but the stock voltage reads; no statistics reset (no
  `--reset-ms`); never the firmware-revisions query; no power cycle and nothing that risks one. Nothing here drives the card toward a PMIC
  over-power or over-temperature reset: every tile's frozen 95% peak is at most 82 W, E165 ran this harness to 81.16 W and E160 to 79.37 W.
- An idle login of another user does not block (the owner's ruling of 1 Nov 2026); any other user's activity, device process or lock ends the
  session at once, and a holder before the start blocks it (bounded polls, at most 30 min, else skipped).

## Decision rules

A run is **complete** if every launch of it ran and was ok and `burst_mhz`, `burst_w`, `die_start`, `die_max` exist; **counted** if complete and
every SPARSITY line of it has a C record that reads PASS (Q139(k)'s rule). A tile's measured value is its mean over its counted passes; a tile with
fewer than 2 is left out of every rule and named; fewer than 2 counted passes in all: every rule NOT SCORED. **The sets:** governed = the 16 kept
governed tiles (minimum 8), pinned = the 8 kept pinned tiles (minimum 8), all scored = both (minimum 10); the zeros anchors and E80's replicates are
never scored. Every error is measured − predicted.

against both models' frozen 95% upper peaks; k_T per burst against 0.395 W/°C; the zeros anchors under the frozen fallback; every pass alone; every
rule over the complete runs (C not required) beside the counted.

## What the outcomes mean

- **E175-A2-G PASS:** from the bits alone, M10's D at 600 MHz carried through the part's relation and E83's dither about the cap predicts the clock
  of new governed kinds within rank 2's lines; the governed clock line holds blind. **FAIL:** it misses by more than rank 2 allows; GS then says
  whether the miss grows with D (the relation's gain g or s(f) between 700 and 800 MHz) or is a level (the cap's W, the zeros level: a session term
  no model of the bits predicts).
- **E175-A2-Bg PASS:** no resolved board level on governed bursts (the governor's plateau, W plus the cap's term, is right on new kinds). **FAIL:** a
  level the session's zeros anchors did not remove.
- **E175-A2-Bp PASS:** pinned bursts' board within |mean| ≤ 0.5 W and rms ≤ 1.0 W, so M10's D at 800 MHz through the part's relation holds on new
  kinds. **FAIL:** it does not.
- **E175-A2-R2 PASS:** rank 2's three lines hold for M10 carried on new kinds in one session: aifoundry2's governed clock and board, and its pinned
  board, are predicted blind within rank 2. **FAIL:** the record names the line that failed.
- **E175-A2-F1 / W1 (governed), F2 / W2 (pinned) PASS:** the clock's and the board's rms on a new pool are no worse than Q140(l)'s end-to-end
  held-out errors. **FAIL:** worse than held out.
- **E175-A2-M1 BETTER:** M10's D (its refit and term b's counts) improves the governed bursts through a part fitted on measured D, so the 600 MHz
  gains carry to another clock and card. **NOT RESOLVED:** the part's own error (about 0.8 W) hides the D difference. **WORSE:** M10's D hurts here.
- **E175-A2-H1 PASS:** the burst heat law (0.1400 °C/W) holds on new kinds within its held-out error. **FAIL:** worse.

## What the rules cannot say

- **One card, one firmware build (BL2 0.22.0, by host-boot continuity from E69's read), one session.** The score is conditional on the session's
  own zeros anchors and die starts, as the model is meant to be used.
- **The kinds are one generator's** (pool 8's twelve float families, mantissas truncated): a blind test of the governed line across switching power,
  not of every kind of number; int8 never governs on this card (its hottest kinds read about 50 W).
- **The governed board is the cap's plateau** (about 67.1 W for every governed tile at the fallback), so Bg tests the plateau's level, not D; D
  enters the governed bursts through the clock.
- **The 82 W admission rests on M8's peak rule through M9's part** (both models' peaks); aifoundry2's PMIC over-power configuration stays unread (it
  waits on the owner's word).
- **The selection reads the predictions:** the kept tiles are those the models place in the governed range, so a kind the models misplace as pinned
  or over 82 W is never in the governed set (the pinned set and the selection's record report the others).

## Card budget and time

**About 38 card-minutes:** per pass, 17 governed bursts (16 kept and E80's fp16 randn) at E165's measured 16.2 s and 12 pinned (the three zeros
anchors, 8 kept and E80's int8 full) at 13.5 s is 7.3 min; five passes, 36.5 min; the smoke and the two stop tests about 1 min. **The session's cap
is 42 card-minutes** (`run_e119.py` starts a run only if its planned time fits; it cuts pass 5's tail if the die gates run long), **the slot 48
minutes** from the queue's start, its hard stop inside it. **The registered subset for a short window:** passes 1-3 (87 bursts, about 22 min) under
`--cap 25` in a 31-minute slot; every rule is scored on what is counted.
