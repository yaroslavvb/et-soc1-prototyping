## Design

**Card, and nothing set.** aifoundry2 card 0, release 1.4.0 / BL2 0.22.0, under its own governor, TDP 65 W and threshold 65 °C as read. **No governor
move, no TDP, threshold or clock set, no reset of any kind, no statistics reset, no firmware-revisions query, no residency read, no PMIC command but the
stock telemetry's voltage-register reads** (the owner's rules of 2 November 2026). `ettelem config` (read only, the lock non-blocking, `timeout 10`) is
read at the start and end of every step, at the start and end of every session and after every run. Binaries: the box's `build/sparsity_t2` and
`build/ettelem-hpl` (E80–E111's, not rebuilt; their sha256 are recorded at the check and in every session). Tiles: Q130(s)'s, checked against
`kinds.json`.

**The runner is E111's, with one command removed** ([`run_e133.py`](run_e133.py)): E80's `run_kspeed.py` over E64's `run_hpl.py`, imported and run
unchanged, with E111's idle-login rule (`../e111/idle_logins_e111.py`, the owner's ruling of 1 November 2026). `run_hpl.py` reads
`ettelem residency 2 3 4 5 6` after every run (`hpl/run_hpl.py:622`); raw states 4–6 are no state at BL2 0.22.0 and each read logs SP errors
(Q136(w), result 5), so **here that command is never sent**: the wrapped `run_out` returns at once, starts no process, opens no device and writes one
line to `e133-not-sent.jsonl`. No reducer reads residencies. **The stops are tightened** to E126's: 80 °C on the die mean or the hottest sensor, 93 W
on three samples (E111's were 85 °C and 97 W; E111's own W2 peaked at 88.04 W in fp32 randn's first seconds at 800 MHz, die mean 68 °C, the hottest
sensor's running maximum 70 °C).

**One queue, detached on the box** ([`queue_e133.py`](queue_e133.py), E111's queue with E133's steps; [`run_e133.sh`](run_e133.sh) drives it). The steps

**The queue's rules, frozen** (E111's, with E133's times and budget):

- **The rest rule** (E98's): before every step after the smoke, no row of the box's own 5 s history with a die over 55 °C, a board over 26 W or the
  card held on two rows running, over the step's rest, and the previous step ended at least that long ago.
- **Nobody else.** The card lock free and nobody else active by the idle-login rule (a user whose every session has been idle for at least 120 minutes
  and none of whose processes started under 120 minutes ago does not block; a new login, a terminal used again, a new process, any other user's device
  process, the module's use count over the session's baseline or the card lock held by anyone does, and the runners end a session at once, exit 3).
  Otherwise the queue polls every 20 s, never taking the lock to wait. A login is never named in any file.
- **Times.** The smoke not before the claim's minute and not after 11:00 PST; **no step starts after 15:00 PST**; a hard stop at **16:00 PST**
  (stop-file at −120 s, SIGTERM at −30 s, never a device process signalled); a step that could not end by the cutoff is skipped. W3 not before 14:15
  PST, its latest start 14:45 PST.
- **The card budget: 110 card-minutes**, the smoke included. No step starts if the steps' card minutes so far plus its plan (SMOKE 1.5, W2 50, END 2.5,
  W1B 30, END2 2.5, W3 24) pass 110, and every session's cap is its protocol's, never past what the budget leaves. On E111's actual times the morning
  uses about 81.5 card-minutes; with W3 about 103.
- **Exits** (E111's): 0 next; 3 next, flagged; 1 next once, a second halts; **4 (a safety stop) runs END or END2 only, then stops**; 2 and 5 halt.
- **The W3 rule.** At about 14:05 PST the Intel reads aifoundry2's own history. W3 is claimed and run only if the free card's idle die (10-minute mean)
  is at least **1.0 °C above W2's REST idle die**, the budget holds it (used + 24 ≤ 110) and nobody else holds the card; then the day's post names the
  card again and `GO-W3` is touched. Otherwise `SKIP-W3` is touched and W3 is reported as not run.
- **Beside the steps**: `sensors -j`, `uptime` and `ettelem config` at both ends of every step; the camera phone's `/health` once a minute (E111's
  descriptive room reading: it charges); every box's 5 s history collected at the end, numbers only.

## Decision rules (frozen; `reduce_e133.py` implements them)

**Part A, each hold** — E111's rules, its code, unchanged:

- **E133-A1 = E111-A1, steady?** The OLS slope of 30 s bins of the fine W over the last 600 s, in °C/min at k = 0.442 W/°C: **PASS** if |slope| ≤ 0.02
  °C/min, else **FAIL**; without a fine W (the clock not held in 70% of the window) the die's own 30 s bins stand in, and the verdict says so.
- **E133-A2 = E111-A2**, c's range against each band: PASS inside, FAIL wholly outside, INCONCLUSIVE otherwise (aifoundry2's [0.62, 0.82]; the lab's
  September model [1.25, 1.70]; E97's aifoundry3 plateau [0.86, 0.95]; the 76 s line [0.40, 0.52]).
- **E133-A3 = E111-A3**: c(H-F32) / c(H-I8), its range from the two ranges' extremes, against [0.90, 1.11].
- **E133-R1, the replicate (new)**: c(E133) / c(E111) per kind, its range from the two ranges' extremes (E111's committed `c_lo`, `c_hi`), against
  [0.90, 1.11], A3's band: **PASS (REPLICATES)** inside, **FAIL (DIFFERS)** wholly outside, **INCONCLUSIVE** otherwise.
- **E133-R2, the room and the governor (new)**: H-F32's plateau share at 600 MHz: **AT THE FLOOR** if ≥ 0.90 (E111's `clock_share_min`), **AT THE
  TRIP** otherwise; scored against the prediction keyed on W2's REST idle die. At the trip c is the governor's operating point, still per board W.
- A hold that did not run all 190 processes is read on what it ran and marked.

**Part B, the drift**, the probe as the unit, E98's frozen numbers:

- **E133-B0, B1, B2, BV, B4 = E111-B0, B1, B2, BV, B4** on the combined probes (E98-R0's gate; E98-R1, the 76 s line against the rest die, against 0 and
  0.25; E98-R2, the hold's die against the rest die, band 1.00–1.25, floor 0.25; E98-V; E98-S4, fp16 ones at the trip).
- **E133-B3c, a cool or warm probe against the night's baseline (new; E111-B3's reading, both sides)**: each E133 probe's rest-die lead dx over the mean
  of P3, P6 and P7 (W-derived, E98's), its line lead and its hold lead, each over dx, for every E133 probe with |dx| ≥ 0.5 °C, cool or warm; the mean
  ratio with a 95% normal interval from E98's frozen per-probe scatter (hold 0.375 °C, line 0.089 °C; one probe against a mean of three). The line on

## What this cannot say

One card, one morning, one hold per kind again: two sessions per kind in all, on two days and at two times of day. **No inlet thermometer exists on any
box**, so the room is read only through the rest die and E111-A4's coarse, self-heated proxies; "a cooler room" means a lower idle die and board W.
The die is the firmware's whole-degree 34-sensor mean; the plateau's fine structure rests on the fine W and E64's k, and only while the clock is held.
If H-F32 sits at the trip, its c is the governor's operating point and A1 reads the whole-degree die. A morning probe is a cool probe: it says nothing
about 23 October's warm session itself, only about the slope through it. W3 needs a warm afternoon, which may not come.
