in Q134 (lane `a3-e123-tile-spread`; optional). It answers what [E94](../../../docs/reports/data/2026-11-01-e94-tile-spread/README.md)

unresolved as registered". The frozen numbers are in [`predictions-e123.json`](predictions-e123.json), written by

**aifoundry3 card 0 only** (no `V3_DEVICE`; lib.sh names it `aifoundry3`, takes `/run/lock/etsoc-shire0.lock`), 600 MHz,
TDP 0 W and threshold 65 °C as read (never set). E123 writes no knob: no governor move, no TDP, threshold or clock set, no
reset, no PCIe, driver, firmware or PMIC command, no `--reset-ms`.*

**The question.** The kinds table has 15 † rows, kinds whose zeros are drawn at random, so a committed tile is one draw of its
kind. E94 re-drew four of them on aifoundry1 card 1. For the other eleven the page prints a prediction: "float kinds gating
25–75% of lanes … predicted on fp16 50% zeros and 50% zero words; … the other nine † rows 0.32 W or less, predicted", and "fp16 50%
zeros at its kind's mean would read S about 14.7 W against 15.22, at the band". fp32 "ReLU × small weights" stayed UNRESOLVED
(six draws knew its mean to ±0.58 W), and aifoundry3, whose table it is, got no number for it. E123 measures, **on aifoundry3
itself** (no translation between cards): each of the twelve rows' kind's mean, whether E78's committed tile needs a correction,
and the between-draw SD the † note quotes.

## The statistics: the draw is the unit, the gate share is known

Every tile's per-pass D goes into E94's two-way fit per rule, `y(tile, pass) = tile + pass + e` (`score_e94.two_way`,
imported). N² = MS_res / n, the noise of one tile's mean.

**PRIMARY, registered here for the first time: the gate-share adjusted kind's mean.** E94 found, after its data, that moving
each draw by the gate law to its rule's mean gate share tightened a float kind's mean three- to fivefold (E94 result 7), and
that inside a rule D falls by about D(random) per unit of gate share (result 8: each fitted slope's interval held D(random)).
E123 freezes that slope: **β = E78's D of the type's random anchor on aifoundry3** (fp32 24.72, fp16 22.16, int8 7.06 W per
unit). Each draw: `y'_r = y_r + β (g_r − ḡ_rule)`, ḡ_rule = kinds.json's 400-replicate mean. The kind's mean is the mean of
y'. Its SD is pooled over the rule's class: `s'² = Σ SS' / Σ (k − 1)` (G: 15 df, LF: 9, LI: 6); N² is pooled over the class's
fits too. The committed tile's offset is `y_c − mean(y')`, ± t(Satterthwaite df) × √(s'²/k + N² + (β · se(ḡ))²). The kind's S
is the committed tile's S less the offset (S and D share the pass's zeros level); nJ per op and pJ per MAC from it. The pooling
assumes the adjusted draws' SD is common inside a class; per-rule s' is reported beside.

**SECONDARY, E94's own form**, for every rule with three or more draws: the plain mean over draws, the rule's own s, E94's R1 and
R2 (`score_e94.rule_stats`, imported), labelled secondary.

**Single-pass fallback.** If only one pass is kept, N² = σ² with σ assumed (0.15 W float, 0.12 W int8; above E94's measured
0.04–0.11 W) and 60 df; every verdict is labelled "single pass". No pass kept: NOT SCORED.

## Decision rules

All on the primary reduction (dips removed, 0.1994 W/°C); every verdict is also computed at the arms and a verdict that moves is
marked arm-sensitive. A tile missing a kept pass leaves its rule, named; the committed tile missing leaves the rule UNRESOLVED.
No pass is added after a reduction is read; none is chosen after the fact. Failures are reported as failures.

**The statement the page gets, decided now.** Per row, R2's primary verdict, measured on aifoundry3 (no translation). The † note
gets class G's per-rule τ and LF's and LI's pooled τ, with their intervals, labelled measured on aifoundry3. If R5 FAILs, class
G's verdicts are the fitted-slope estimator's. The rows E94 translated (fp16 ReLU × small weights, fp32 50% zeros) are not re-run
and keep what E94 gave them.

**Reported, never a verdict:** per rule the E94-form statistics and per-rule s', the plain draws' mean, the kind's S, nJ per op and
pJ per MAC; per draw its offset, gate share and frozen gate-law delta; R6's rms for LF and LI; each verdict at every arm; each
block's two `ettelem config` reads.

**What the rules cannot say.** One card, one clock, one session, two passes. Two or three draws per row in LF and LI: their per-row
verdicts lean on the class's pooled SD, and int8's ±0.25 W band is near the noise (most LI rows will read UNRESOLVED, by the
power table). The primary's slope is frozen from E94's post-hoc reading; R5 tests it in class G only, and in LF and LI the gate
share barely varies, so the adjustment there is small (at most about 0.15 W) and untested. The rule is synthetic: nothing here says
what a real tensor's kind is, and the speed columns are not re-drawn. int8's bus counts are provisional (kinds README).

## Self-test

[`selftest_e123.py`](selftest_e123.py) builds synthetic reductions in reduce_e78's per_pass form and scores six worlds: **law**
(draws where the frozen slope puts them, committed tiles at E94's frozen offsets: R5, R6, R7, R8, S1, R4:LF PASS; fp32.nn_like NO
CORRECTION), **off** (committed tiles 3 W off, 1 W in int8: every R2 CORRECTION, S1 and R7 FAIL), **steep** (twice the slope:
R5 FAIL, the fitted slope recovers fp32.nn_like), **wild** (draws at SD 1.5 W: R4 and R6 fail), **single** (one pass: scored and
labelled) and **none** (NOT SCORED). [`selftest_e123.txt`](selftest_e123.txt): 28 of 28 checks. The reducer wrapper was run on two
of E78's committed aifoundry3 passes (p7810, p7811): every D equal to E78's own per-pass value; no E123 number was computed.

## Card budget

**At most 35 card-minutes, from the claim edit to the release edit.** `queue_e123.sh` is anchored on the claim edit's time T0: a
pass starts only if now + projected ≤ T0 + 2,100 − 240 s and ends by 23:15 PST, projected = the longest pass so far, 760 s before
one ends (E121 ran 42 launches in 378–404 s here; 76 launches is about 700–760 s). Smoke about 1 min, two passes about 25–26
minutes, the session-end read and the release: about 30 minutes. A slow first pass refuses the second, and the single-pass
fallback scores. The claim says 1 h (35 min and a margin, rounded up to the half hour). No session starts after 22:30 PST; every
run ends by 23:30 PST.

## How to run it (for this lane)

After this commit is on origin/main, copy `tools/claims-v3/e123/` to aifoundry3's `~/aifoundry/tools/claims-v3/e123/`. On
aifoundry3, from `~/aifoundry`:

```bash
bash tools/claims-v3/e123/run_e123.sh setup             # no device access: hashes, tiles, plan, dry pass, the queue's stub test
# -> edit the day's #community-lab post (one post a day): add "using aifoundry3 for 1 h, until ~HH:MM PST [doc](...)"
bash tools/claims-v3/e123/run_e123.sh smoke             # pass 12301 and its gate
bash tools/claims-v3/e123/run_e123.sh go <claim epoch>  # the queue, detached; the budget rule decides
bash tools/claims-v3/e123/run_e123.sh wait              # bounded waits
```

Then copy `build/claims-v3/aifoundry3/kinds/p123*`, `kinds-smoke/p12301` and `build/e123-*` to the Intel and run
`PY=/usr/bin/python3 nice -n 10 bash tools/claims-v3/e123/collect_e123.sh 'p123[1-9][0-9]' <copy>`; copy
`build/e123-out/2026-11-01-e123-tile-spread-rest/` to `docs/reports/data/`, write its README (results first), and edit the day's post.
