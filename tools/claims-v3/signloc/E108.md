# E108: a random sign's ~5 W located further: the like for like on zero products, and the rest by axis (aifoundry1 card 1, 600 MHz)

*Written Sun 1 Nov 2026 (week 44), Q133 (lane `a1c1-e108-signs`), before the claim, before any launch and before any new E108 tile ran on any card. It answers [the kinds page's section 11, item 5](https://spacesheep.dev/@yaroslavvb/horace-kinds-of-numbers#11-what-is-still-open), "Where a random sign's ~5 W goes", as that list reads at `1fe8377a18af`: "on one card, a bracket by held tile, 0.19–0.45 in fp16 and about 0.35–0.56 in fp32. ## The question

1. **The like for like the item names.** E106 put fp32's zero-product held share at about 0.35 and fp16's at about 0.45, from E104's `z_s` tiles in an earlier session against E106's steps: "not resolved". E108 runs both formats' `z_s`, `c_abs`, `signs` and `ones` in one session, with E106's held tiles (`fp32.s_abs`, `fp16.z_ss`) beside them, so the formats and fp32's two designs are compared pass by pass.
2. **Where the rest goes.** E88's `s_walk` and `s_abs` share their signs at k ≥ 1 and differ only in the k = 0 slice: one sum walks, the other is absorbed (2^100). Their difference, E88's R32s (2.26 ± 0.16 W on this card), is the rest with the draws matched. What `s_walk` has that `s_abs` lacks is two things: **(i) its 256 accumulators differ from each other,** so a lane that alternates between C[i][l] and C[i][l+8] and moves from row to row reads, adds and writes back a different word every micro-op; `s_abs`'s are all n 2^100. **(ii) Its sums change** (store bits, carries, a product that is not zapped). A held tile whose accumulators differ as `s_walk`'s do, counted on the lane's C words, carries (i) without (ii): `c_mx_f`, held by absorption like `s_abs`. On zero products, which hold without the absorbed subtraction's swing of the adder's output (E104), `b_B_z` holds exactly `b_B_w`'s sums at every op's end, with its B stream: the B side's rest splits into `b_B_z` − `z_s` and `b_B_w` − `b_B_z`. E88 measured (i) for random positive operands (R32bus 3.80 W of R32's 5.04 W) but never for random signs.
3. **Three axes, each walking and held by E88's absorption,** to say what the rest depends on: **(a)** product-sign alternation against operand sign changes at a fixed product sign; **(b)** A's sign against B's; **(c)** the accumulators' exponents aligned against misaligned.

## The tiles

[`make_e108.py`](make_e108.py) writes them, with every expected accumulator from `accum.c` (the RTL's arithmetic), the manual's sentence and fl(n S): on all 25 tiles at every length the three coincide, so a read-back miss means a tile is not what it is called. New, all fp32, all built on `s_abs`'s random signs at k ≥ 1 (E88's draw, seed 8802), on the side each names:

| tile | axis | design | C after n ops | eff_sub |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| `b_A_f` | b | A = `s_abs`'s A at k ≥ 1, B = +1; k = 0 (2^50, 2^50) | n 2^100, held | follows A's sign: changes at a row boundary only |

s, t, a, b are draw 290 of a seeded search (`make_e108.py --search`: `default_rng(10800)`, 4,000 draws; among those whose `c_mx_f` makes the lane's C words toggle within 0.2% of `s_walk`'s, averaged over eight op indices across a launch, the one whose `c_sg_f` count is nearest 2,048, then the first); e_i = floor(log2 a_i), f_j = floor(log2 b_j), so the three are nested. `s_walk` is the ladder's walking end. **An exponent-aligned walking tile with random signs cannot be built exactly**: 15 random ±1 on a seed keep every sum in one binade only with a seed above 45, and then the sums pass 2^24 inside a launch (48 × 542,000 = 26 M), where ±1 rounds. So axis (c) is held only, with `s_walk` as its misaligned walking end.

Reused byte for byte (`make_e108.py` › `REUSED`): `fp32.ones`, `fp16.ones` (E78's), `signs`, `c_abs` in both formats and `fp32.s_abs` (E92's), `z_s` in both formats (E104's), `fp16.z_ss` (E106's), `fp32.s_walk` (E88's); for axis (a), E92's `fp32.h_add` and `h_sub` (held: the product sign fixed per accumulator against alternating, on the same operands) and `w_h_add`, E104's `fp32.m_h_sub` (walking, `w_h_add`'s magnitudes), and E104's `fp16.w_h_add` and `m_h_sub`. fp16 has no held pair by absorption, because its absorbed subtractions walk (E104), so its axis-(a) pair is walking only.

**What I expect, frozen so it can be wrong:** R1 PASS; R2 PASS; R3 FP16 HIGHER near +0.10, or INCONCLUSIVE (M4 says 0.036); R4 ABSORBED HIGHER; R5 PRICED ALIKE; R6 NOTHING MEASURABLE in both legs; **R7 MOST** (the bits say so: the lane's C and result words toggle about 64,000 bits an op in both `c_mx_f` and `s_walk`, and `s_walk`'s walk adds about 12,000 store bits; M4 puts the share above 1, which would mean the absorbed held tile's own swing costs about what the walk does); **R12 MOST or ABOUT HALF** (M4 0.964); R8 RISES on the mantissa step, RISES or INCONCLUSIVE on the exponent step; R9 FALLS; R10 INCONCLUSIVE or 1.55x HOLDS; R11 PASS; S1 PASS.

**The statements decided now.**
- **How R7 and R12 read together.** R7's tiles are held by absorption, R12's by zero products. An absorbed subtraction swings the adder's output between C and C − ε before rounding back (E104), and a walking sum does not. R7's numerator, `c_mx_f` − `s_abs`, has that swing in both tiles; its denominator, `s_walk` − `s_abs`, is the accumulators differing and the walk, less the swing `s_abs` has and `s_walk` lacks. So a share near or above 1 says the walk costs about what the absorbed swing costs, not that the walk is free. R12 has no swing in either held tile, and is the cleaner split of B's rest; it carries alternation counts that differ by up to 468 an op, which M4 prices.
- **R7 MOST:** "On one card, most of the rest of a random sign's step (the step less its held part, with the draws matched) is there in a held tile whose accumulators differ as the walking ones do: it goes with the lane reading and writing back different words, not with the sums changing." Item 5's "the rest is not located" goes; what stays is the part that goes with the sums changing (`s_walk` − `c_mx_f`) and that this is designed tiles on one card.
- **R7 ABOUT HALF:** "about half of the rest goes with the accumulators differing, and about half with the sums changing"; the rest is located as two parts.
- **R7 LITTLE:** "the rest goes with the sums changing, not with the accumulators differing": E88's bus swing does not carry over to random signs.
- **R12 the same word as R7:** the location holds on both held designs. **R12 a lower word than R7:** on zero products less of the rest goes with the accumulators differing, and the absorbed design's swing is part of what R7 attributes to them.
- **R3 resolved** (FP16 HIGHER, LOWER or ALIKE): the like-for-like sentence of the page takes that word and the one-session numbers; INCONCLUSIVE keeps "not resolved" with a narrower interval.
- **R9 FALLS, R10 1.55x HOLDS or GOES:** the moving-sum ratio of item 5 is resolved in that direction; INCONCLUSIVE keeps it unresolved.
- **R2 FAIL:** the step moved between sessions by more than 1 W, and every share here is read against this session's step only.
