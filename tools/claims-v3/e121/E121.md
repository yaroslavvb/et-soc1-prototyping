## The question

E93 tested the public RTL's zero gate where "the word is zero" and "the value is zero" disagree, on **aifoundry1 card 1**, and
scored 23 PASS, 1 FAIL of 24 rules: the gate compares raw 32-bit words, so `-0.0` and denormal words leave the lane on; int8's B
gate wants all eight bytes of a column pair, the pair is `(l, l+8)`, and int8's A test is the whole word. The page closed the
item **at power resolution on one card**. **E121 asks whether the same tiles, the same runner and the same rules give the same
verdicts and the same watts on a second card** (aifoundry3, the card of E78's kinds matrix), so that the closure stops resting
on one card. It adds nothing new to the tiles.

Three outcomes are possible and each is registered: the gate's rules hold on aifoundry3 (the 22 gate rules PASS); they hold
and the watts carry across by E82's card scale (the cross-card rules PASS); or one of them does not, which is reported as
failed with the value beside the line.

### X: the cross-card rules

For each of the 24 contrasts C: **d = C(E121) − s · C(E93)**, with s = E82's per-type card scale, aifoundry3 over card 1
(`docs/reports/data/2026-10-31-e82-kinds-aifoundry1/scored.json` › `cross_card.primary.x3.per_type_median`: **fp32 1.030017,
fp16 1.033232, int8 1.027062**; over 32 kinds the median is 1.0308, IQR 1.025–1.034), and **h = √(h121² + (s · h93)²)**, the two
sessions' 95% half-widths in quadrature. **PASS if |d| + h ≤ w; FAIL if |d| − h > w; else INCONCLUSIVE; UNRESOLVED below 3
passes.** **w = 0.75 W for fp32 and fp16 contrasts, 0.50 W for int8's** (R6a–c, R7). **The aggregate: PASS if no X rule FAILs and
at least 12 of 24 PASS; FAIL if 3 or more FAIL; else INCONCLUSIVE.**

*Why these tolerances:* the card scale's per-kind spread (E82: sd 1.2%, a few kinds 1–2% beyond pass noise, the widest fp16
`ones` at 4.8%) is worth up to ±0.3–0.6 W on a 12 W contrast; one card's drift between sessions at a matched slope is up to about
0.2 W (E93's tie to E82: ≤ 0.17 W; E95 against E78 at 0.1994: −0.05 W in fp32 random); the slope frames differ (0.1994 here,
0.2414 in E93) by ≤ 0.15 W on the hottest cells. Together that is about 0.4 W for the 12 W fp32 contrasts, and w is about twice
that; int8's contrasts are at most 5.5 W, so 0.5 W.

### If passes fall out

E93's rule, unchanged: **at least 3 kept passes score; a cell with fewer than 3 is UNRESOLVED and never counts as PASS; with 2 or
fewer every rule is NOT SCORED and the session is reported as exploratory.** No pass is added after any reduction is read; no pass
is chosen after the fact; the arms are not a fallback.

**Reported, never a verdict:** every tile's D, S, pJ per MAC, ΔT and its ratio to E93's D; this session's own card scale (D of
`fp32.randn`, `fp16.randn`, `int8.full` and `anchor_pi` over E93's); `score_e93.reported`'s block (−0's premium, denormal against
−0 per side, the recoder asymmetry, int8's unit costs, the mask tile, cycles per op, duty); E78-R5's checks; every gate failure;
each block's two `ettelem config` reads and the session-start and session-end reads.

**What the rules cannot say.** Power is still the only reading: no micro-op counter is read, so gated lanes are inferred, not
counted, on this card as on card 1. Two cards of one design, with E93's one fixed set of tiles. A second card can confirm R5's
failure and the +0.65 W of a half-gated int8 tile, but not explain them.

## The smoke and its gate

Right after the claim edit: `run_e121.sh smoke` runs `block_e93.sh 12101 --smoke`, E93's eight 2 s runs (`anchor_pi`,
`fp32.dnr_both`, `fp32.nz_both`, `fp32.zeros`, `fp32.randn`, `fp16.dnr_both`, `int8.b_pairs_rtl`, `int8.zeros`) at the current
reading, no preheat. Its block's opening `ettelem config` (under the lock, `timeout 10`) is the session-start read. **The gate
PASSes if the block ended ok, every run is ok on 1,024 minions, cycles per op are within 0.5% of 546 (fp32, fp16) and 318 (int8),
all eight items ran, and both config reads say TDP 0 W, threshold 65 °C, 600 MHz.** If it fails, no registered pass runs, and
the claim is released.

## Card budget

**At most 35 card-minutes from the claim edit to its release edit**, smoke and every heating gate included. `queue_e121.sh` runs
with its budget **in seconds, anchored on the claim edit's time** (`E121_T0`): a pass starts only if now + projected ≤ T0 + 2,100
− 120 s and ≤ 23:15 PST, where projected is the longest measured pass so far, 450 s until the first one ends (E93's estimate;
E78's aifoundry3 runs took about 9.8 s, so a 42-launch pass is about 7 min). A pass in flight is never cut. With 7-minute passes
four fit (the fifth would end past the limit); at 8.5 minutes three. After the queue it reads `ettelem config` once more under
the card lock (the session-end read). The claim is a clause in the day's one `#community-lab` post (`for 1 h`: 35 minutes plus
a margin, rounded up to the half hour), added and later marked released by editing that post (gyroscope
`_request/request-aifoundry.md` §4). No new card session after 22:30 PST; every run ends by 23:30 PST.

## Self-test

[`selftest_e121.py`](selftest_e121.py) builds reductions from E93's own per-pass D times E82's scale (plus 0.05 W noise) and scores
five worlds: **replicate** (gate summary PASS, R5 FAIL and R8 PASS, 24 of 24 verdicts as E93's, reading `rtl`, X PASS, tie PASS),
**value_gate** (−0 and denormal tiles gated, pairing `(2l, 2l+1)`, one A byte gating: R1, R3, R6b, R7 and R8 FAIL, gate summary
FAIL, reading `alt`, X FAIL), **scale_off** (every cell × 1.12: every gate rule but R2-fp16 PASSes, the tie FAILs, X FAILs),
**three** passes (scored) and **two** passes (NOT SCORED). [`selftest_e121.txt`](selftest_e121.txt): 23 of 23 checks. The
reduction path is E93's `reduce_e93.py`, unchanged, which `selftest_e93.py` tested on E78's generator. Nothing it prints is data.
