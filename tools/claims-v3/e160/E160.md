## What it does to predicting power and heat

M8 says what aifoundry2's governor does with a kind of number in a 6 s burst, from the tile's bytes alone: **the clock it settles at** (M7's relation
P(f) = B(f) + c·s(f), s(f) = (V_f/0.517 V)²·f/600, c = c_zeros + g·D with D M8's power over the type's zeros at 600 MHz and g = 1/1.01865, and E83's
dither about an effective cap **refitted to W0 = 65.20 W**), **the board power at that clock**, **the die's rise** (M7's burst law, 0.140 °C per board
W over a 21.5 W idle, carried unchanged), and, new in M8, **the highest board sample of the burst** (P800(c) + 1.94 W expected, + 2.95 W at 95% with c
at the top of D's band). **The error before**, all of it in sample to the cap or out of kind on the same sessions (`m8.json` › `aifoundry2`): end to end
over the 92 kinds bursts of E80, E83 and E143 **7.16 MHz and 1.86 W rms** (cap held out per kind 7.22 / 1.87); split by M8's predicted clock, as E160's
groups are, **6.68 MHz and 2.10 W on the 36 governed rows, 7.45 MHz and 1.69 W on the 56 pinned rows** (four bistable cells among them); heat 0.68 °C
leave one kind out; the peak rule 1.60 W rms against E143's highest samples, in sample. The last blind score on this card was M7's on pool 5 (E143):
governed 6.63 MHz and 1.60 W, pinned 1.01 MHz and 1.18 W, heat 0.585 °C. **E160 measures M8's out-of-sample error on pool 6's 48 tiles that run**
(the 44 new kinds less the one real tile dropped below, and the 6 anchors less the dropped fp32 random anchor), by the group M8 puts each in before the run: the 17 it predicts governed (below 795
MHz), where the clock term is tested, and the 31 it predicts at 795-800 MHz, where "stays at 800 MHz", the power at 800 MHz and the zeros' level are.
The before/after the owner's goal needs is that reference against E160's blind rms, per group, for the clock, the board power, the rise and the peak.

## Decision rules

A run is **complete** if every launch of it ran and was ok (`launch_ok == launch_n ≥ 1`) and `burst_mhz`, `burst_w`, `die_start`, `die_max` exist
(E143's "counted"); it is **counted** if it is complete **and every SPARSITY line of it (the calibration launch included) has a C record that reads PASS**
(bit-exact; ROUNDING, UNMODELLED, FAIL and a missing record are not checked: Q139(k)'s rule). A tile's measured value is its mean over its counted
passes; a tile with fewer than 2 is left out of F, W and H and named. **Fewer than 2 counted passes in all: every F, W and H rule NOT SCORED.** A tile's
group is frozen in `predictions-e160.json` (governed: M8's `burst_mhz` < 795 MHz; 17 tiles that run, 12 of them priority 1; pinned: 31, all 19 int8
tiles among them). "Bootstrap" = the 2.5–97.5% range of the rms of n errors drawn with replacement from the reference errors, n the tiles scored (20,000
draws, seeded 160; `e160_common.boot_rms_range`): **PASS** at or below the 97.5% point, **FAIL** above; below the 2.5% point is reported as "better than
the reference says". "Coverage" = **PASS** if the number of tiles inside M8's frozen 95% band is at least the 2.5% point of Binomial(n, 0.95) (E140-P3's
line; 42 of 48).

more informative outcome. The split is by the group M8 predicts, because that is all that is known before the run. PK is new: section 22's 82 W line is

beside, so a C failure that is the reference's (a first-read note) does not hide the power data.

## What the rules cannot say

- **The pinned tiles test little of the clock term**: at 800 MHz the clock cannot move up; a pinned tile's board power tests D, the zeros' level and the
  power at 800 MHz. F1 and W1 are the clock-and-voltage term's test; where the governed burst reaches the cap, the board power tests the cap (65.20 W).
- **The reference is in sample to the cap** (above). The models differ on this card mostly through D and the cap, so M8 against M7 and M6 is reported,
  not ruled.
- **The host is not E80's binary.** The device code is, and the burst's launches are the same count of the same length; the C log adds DMA in the gaps.
  E160's power is E160's harness's. R0 against E143's own cells of last night (same tiles, E80's host) reads the two together with the day's drift; it
  cannot separate them.
- **The burst heat law's window is a 6 s burst's**, die max less die start in whole °C readings; it does not test the long-hold model (E161).
- One card, one firmware build (BL2 0.22.0), one morning; the die starts are gated at ≤ 55 °C, not fixed.

## Card budget and time

At most **50 card-minutes** (the brief): the smoke and the two stop tests about 3 min, the session's cap 40 min (150 bursts at about 14 s each are about
35 min; E143 ran 117 in 27.1 min on this card). The queue's slot is 50 minutes from its start, and its hard stop (the stop file 120 s before the slot's
end, SIGTERM at 30 s) holds everything inside it. The day's one `#community-lab` post gets this card's clause, `using aifoundry2 for 1 h, until ~HH:MM
PST`, before the first launch, and loses it at the release (section 15). Q139's gates: no new card session after 10:30 PST, every run ends by 11:30 PST.
If the Tailscale approval lapses (about 10:06 PST), the detached queue keeps running and the data is collected when ssh answers again (section 9). An
idle login of another user does not block (the owner's ruling of 1 Nov 2026, E111's rule in the runner); any other user's activity, device process or
lock ends the session at once.

## How to run it (for this lane)

From the tree's root on the Intel: `python3 tools/claims-v3/e160/make_predictions_e160.py > tools/claims-v3/e160/predictions-e160.txt`,
`python3 tools/claims-v3/e160/make_protocol_e160.py > tools/claims-v3/e160/protocol.txt`, `python3 tools/claims-v3/e160/selftest_e160.py >
tools/claims-v3/e160/selftest.txt` (ALL PASS), the dry queue (`queue_e160.py --dry` with `protocol-dry.json` and the two `-dry` stop tests: run_hpl's
toy card, no device; [`logs/dry-queue.txt`](logs/dry-queue.txt), its reduction [`dry-reduce.txt`](dry-reduce.txt)). On aifoundry2, from
`~/claude/et-soc1-prototyping` with these files synced in and `build/ccheck` built by `build_host_ccheck.sh`: the binaries' sha256 and `et-who` checked,
the slot file `SLOT E160: start <epoch> ... end <epoch> ` written, then `setsid nohup python3 tools/claims-v3/e160/queue_e160.py --slots <slot file>
--runs build/e160/runs --cap 40 > build/e160/queue.out 2>&1 < /dev/null &`. Collect `build/e160/runs` to
`docs/reports/data/2026-11-03-e160-blind6-aifoundry2/aifoundry2/`, check C at the desk (`ccheck.py check <session>/c-log.jsonl --out
ccheck-<session>.json --jobs 6` per session) and reduce: `python3 tools/claims-v3/e160/reduce_e160.py <the sessions> --ccheck <the ccheck files> --out
results.json > reduce.txt`.
