after it, the session names FL3 primary and Q139(u)'s frozen slope is the arm."* Round B's `m10-law.json` (`6f9ae408`) is M10 and M9

(P1-P3's lines, C1's coverage) could be scored on it.

leave-one-kind-out errors and its interval pools. `6f9ae408`'s `m10-law.json` is a post hoc refit and **does not trigger the switch**.
Until such a freeze is committed, FL3 stays the registered arm and the frozen model's own footing stays the primary, as FL3's first clause
says.
