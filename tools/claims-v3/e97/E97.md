# E97: the rest of the heat column, and a measured steady state (Q132, aifoundry3 card 0, 600 MHz, pre-registration)

*Written Sun 1 Nov 2026 (week 44), 01:15–02:30 PDT/PST, on the Intel, before the card was claimed. Frozen numbers: [`predictions-e97.json`](predictions-e97.json), written by [`make_predictions_e97.py`](make_predictions_e97.py)
from the committed sources it names. Data will go to `docs/reports/data/2026-11-01-e97-heat-low-power-steady/`. This closes, or says how far it closes, item 8 of the kinds page's section 11: heat below about 12 W of switching.
Card: **aifoundry3 card 0 only**, 600 MHz, about 522 mV, TDP 0 and threshold 65 °C as read, never set. E97 writes no knob: no governor move, no TDP, threshold or clock set, no reset, no `--reset-ms`.*

## The question

E78 measured every kind's switching power, but the die's whole-degree reading resolves the 2 s warming only above about 12 W of switching. Below that the heat column is an extrapolation, *S × 0.118 °C/W*. E89 read the die's 34 sensors as raw 12-bit
codes (0.061 °C a code), switched seven of those 32 kinds against their zeros once a minute, and found heat following power (E89-R2 PASS ×7); it left 25 kinds extrapolated and says "no steady-state rise is measured" (E89's "Joins and traps"). So two parts:

- **Part A, the rest of the column.** Every kind and real tile below about 12 W that E89 did not measure, on E89's harness: E78's other 25 kinds and E86's three int8 GPT-2 tiles, beside the same resolved reference, fp32 random. *Does each one heat the die by the same degrees per watt as the reference, and what is its 2 s rise over idle?* If all 28 run, no E78 kind stays an extrapolation.
- **Part B, a measured steady state.** Three kinds held about 30 minutes each, from a rested die, with the idle read before and after: *how far does the die rise per watt, is the rise linear in power, how much of it arrives by 60 s, 5 minutes and 30 minutes, and how much of it is leakage feeding back?* The page's section 4 quotes a loop gain near the trip of 0.5–0.6 on the lab model's 1.47 °C/W, resistances that model says not to quote as physics. Part B measures this card's.

## Part A: E89's harness, byte for byte, on 29 blocks

**Reused unchanged (sha256 in `predictions-e97.json` › `harness.reused_unchanged_sha256`; every E97 runner and reducer refuses to run on a difference):** `run_e89.py`, `reduce_e89.py`, `pvt_sample.py`, `lib.sh`, `e78_common.py`, `e86_common.py`, `kinds.json`, `real/real.json`. `run_e97.py` and `reduce_e97.py` replace the block names on those modules in memory and call their `main()`, the way E86 and E95 reuse E78. So: a block is a 20 s lead-in of its reference, then 2 cycles of [kind 30 s][reference 30 s]; a half is back-to-back launches of `sparsity_host --test fma` on 1,024 minions, each at most 6.8 s under `timeout 10`; the sampler reads board power, the SP's statistics, the firmware's die reading, the 34 sensors (command 74, group 0, a read), on-die voltages and clocks at 10 Hz; the reduction is E89's: a shared quadratic trend plus one fundamental per lap, H = T fundamental / board fundamental. Its gates apply to each block (every sample at 600 MHz, 515–530 mV, launches rc 0, cycles per op within 0.5%, ≥ 5 samples/s). Because `run_e89.py` is unchanged, its log lines and `configs.json` say "E89"; the pass numbers and the directory say E97.

**One change of frame, marked.** E89 ran each kind against its type's zeros, and int8 zeros against idle. A kind with E78 D below 2 W against its own zeros would swing the die under 0.25 °C, and E89's int8 −1 (D 1.63 W) was already its weakest cell (with the pass as the unit its R4 read INCONCLUSIVE). **So a kind with D < 2 W runs against idle** (S 2.0–4.1 W, a swing of 0.27–0.57 °C): its over-idle cell is then measured directly instead of a sum, at the price that its r mixes in what a launch costs over idle. E89's int8 zeros showed the size of that: r = 1.11 per board W, 0.99 per minion-rail W. fp32 zeros and fp16 zeros can only run against idle, and their rises serve the float kinds' over-idle sums.

**A pass:** the card lock, a read-only `ettelem config`, the sampler, the start gate (idle until the die reads ≤ 62 °C, at most 60 s), a 15 s idle bracket, the 28 blocks shuffled with `Random("E97|aifoundry3|<pass>")`, then `f32randn` last (E89's rule), a 5 s tail, the sampler stopped with SIGTERM, a second `ettelem config`. 140.3 s a block, about 69 minutes a pass. **Passes 9710 and 9711.** The two orders are frozen in `predictions-e97.json` › `harness`. **Real2's eleven int8 tiles are not run:** the brief runs them only if the time allows, and with Part B it does not. They stay E78-style extrapolations; E95 measures their power.

## Part B: three holds

**A hold** is E89's chain with no square wave: back-to-back launches of one kind on 1,024 minions, each at most 6.8 s under `timeout 10`, for about 30 minutes, 250 launches. E89's raw passes put the kernels on 97.4% of a chain's time, the gaps about 0.3 s each; the reduction states each hold's launches, gaps and compute share from its records. **H1 int8 full** (S 9.65 W), **H2 fp32 ones** (S 10.29 W: the same power, a different kind), **H3 fp32 randn** (S 26.70 W). The sampler is E89's and runs for the whole of Part B, 10 Hz, on one file.

**The lab's 78 °C stop stays, and H3 is expected to reach it.** No launch starts at a firmware die reading ≥ 78 °C (nor at ≥ 70 W of board power, the PMIC's over-power alarm being at 75 W, E84). On the chain fitted to E89 with E71's leakage on this card, fp32 random at its natural chain reaches that reading in about 220 s, and has no steady state short of a runaway. So a hold has two phases: **natural**, the chain above; if it reaches the stop, no launch until the reading is back at 75 °C, then **reduced**: 3.5 s launches (a launch runs whole kernels: eight, 3.9 s of compute), one every 7 s, a compute share near 0.56, predicted to settle near 71 °C. Reaching the stop again ends the hold. A reading of 82 °C ends the pass at once. This keeps the brief's kind and gets two facts instead of one: how long fp32 random takes to reach the stop from idle, and a steady state at the highest power below it, approached from above. If H3 never reaches the stop, it is a steady state at full duty, E97-B10 reads FAIL, and that is a finding.

**The rests.** R0, after Part A, sets the *starting idle*: idle until the 34-sensor mean of the last 60 s is within 0.05 °C of the 60 s ending 5 minutes earlier, at least 10 and at most 30 minutes; the starting idle is the mean of its last 120 s. Every later rest ends when the last 60 s read within **0.1 °C of the starting idle**, at least 10 minutes, capped by the plan; the last rest runs 5 minutes more once it gets there. The rests are the measurement too: the times to come within 1, 0.5, 0.2 and 0.1 °C of the starting idle, and the board's settling, are reported (`rests`). Lab data says idle power settles in about 5 minutes; from the live history, the whole-degree reading is still about 0.4 °C high 15–30 minutes after a run.

**The plan, by the time left to the deadline, 07:13 PST (every session ends by 07:15 PST).** Part A comes first, so the holds are what gets cut, then Part A's second pass: `queue_e97.sh` starts a Part A pass only if its projected end (the longest so far, 71 minutes before the first ends) is before the deadline, and Part B takes what is left. `run_e97b.py` decides the rest, in this order, and records every decision:

1. R0 (≤ 30 min, and leaving H1 and 10 minutes). If less than 35 minutes are left at the start, no hold.
2. H1, 30 min (less only if less than 40 minutes are left; not under 15).
3. After H1: **≥ 135 min left:** R1, H2 for 30 min, R2 (≤ 35 min), H3, R3. **≥ 65 min left:** H2 chained on H1 for 15 min, with no rest: the same power, so the die is near its steady state and a kind's difference shows in about 10 minutes; then R1 (≤ 35 min, and leaving H3 and 10 minutes, but at least 20), H3, the last rest. **≥ 45 min left:** no H2. **Otherwise** a last rest only.
4. H3 for 30 min, less if the last rest would get under 10 minutes; not under 15.
5. The last rest until its criterion or the deadline. If it ends on the deadline, the idle after is the asymptote of an exponential fitted to its tail, and half the gap to the measured reading enters the bound.

Started by about 02:30 PST, that is Part A to about 04:55, R0 to 05:25, H1 to 05:55, H2 to 06:10, R1 to about 06:35, H3 to 07:03 and 10 minutes of last rest: all three kinds, H2 chained. **A chained H2 is a departure from the brief's "each after a rest"**, taken only when the time says so, and its arrival shares do not exist. Started 15 minutes later, H2 goes; about 30 minutes later, H3 goes too; if R0 ends before its cap, those margins grow by what it saved.

**What is computed** ([`reduce_e97b.py`](reduce_e97b.py), on the sampler's one file):

- **The baseline** is the straight line between the idle before (the last 120 s of the rest before the hold) and the idle after, the same for board power and the rails: that handles the room's drift across the hold on the card's own reading. Half the before–after difference enters an **error bound on one hold**, combined with the window's scatter (30 s bins). **It is not a replicated interval: each kind is held once.** [`room_e97.py`](room_e97.py) reports the host sensors on the three boxes and any idle card on them across each span (from `~/live/history`), never a verdict.
- **The steady rise** ΔT is the 34-sensor mean over the baseline in the hold's last 300 s; ΔP, ΔP_minion likewise. **Rise per board W** (open loop: the leakage is inside the watts) = ΔT/ΔP; per minion-rail W = ΔT/ΔP_minion; **per switching W** = ΔT / (E78's S × the window's compute share, from the host's records), with leakage's feedback in it.
- **Steady?** The slope of 30 s bins over the last 600 s (300 s for a chained hold); if it moves, an exponential's asymptote is reported beside. A hold that did not run its time, or had under 15 minutes in its reduced phase, gets no steady reading: its rules read as absent, and what it did is reported.
- **The arrival:** the rise at 2, 10, 30, 60, 120, 180, 300, 600, 900, 1,200 s after the hold starts, ±7 s (a launch period), and its shares of ΔT.
- **Leakage**, from the pass's rests pooled, on E71's rule F (nothing launched for 20 s, 600 MHz): a quadratic of idle board power on the 34-sensor mean over 0.5 °C bins, each bin one point. Its rise from the idle to the hold's T is **leakage's part of ΔP**; its slope k at that T gives the **loop gain k × ΔT/ΔP**; ΔT × (leakage's part / ΔP) is **the rise owed to leakage**; (ΔP − leakage's part) / (S × share) is **the closure** (does E78's switching power survive at the hold's temperature?). Each cooling's slopes in E71's bands are straight lines over the firmware's reading, as E71 fits them. A cooling starts 20 s after the launches stop, about 4–5 °C below its hold's T, so the hottest hold's T lies above the curve: the fit is carried that far, and the distance is reported. E97-B7's 58–62 °C band needs H3's cooling (H1's starts near 59 °C).

## Rules (frozen in `predictions-e97.json` › `rules`)

Beside it `scored.json` keeps the lap as the unit (E89's registered unit, n = 4, t = 3.182); `reducer/scored.json` is E89's own rules run by E89's reducer. A rule whose hold did not run reads as absent, and the README says why. Failures are reported as failures; no band moves after this commit. **Reported, not scored:** H and every r per block by pass; both units; the trend arms; the rails' shares, die mean, phase; the 2 s rise by cycle on three baselines: E89's straight line over the last 5 s, a straight line over [−8, −2) s, a quadratic over the last 20 s. (E89 C6 asked whether the cycle effect is the baseline. A desk look at E89's raw passes, [`desk/c6_e89.txt`](desk/c6_e89.txt): E89's schedule leaves a gap of 0.9–1.7 s before cycle 1's kind half against 0.3–0.6 s before cycle 0's, so cycle 1's baseline ends falling; on the quadratic baseline fp32 random reads 2.83 and 2.85 °C where E89's line reads 2.81 and 3.03 °C.); the 30 s swing; every kind's **2 s rise over idle**, measured against idle or derived (its rise over its type's zeros + that zeros' rise over idle: fp32 and fp16 zeros from this run, int8 zeros E89's 0.340 ± 0.006 °C, assuming 2 s responses add) beside *S × 0.118*; the derived fp32 random against E78's measured 3.16 ± 0.08 °C as a check of that sum; per-sensor patterns; the highest latch and any fault bit (E89 C9); what the rests took; the room.

**What this cannot say.** One card, one firmware build: command 74's raw codes have been seen only here. Each kind is held once, and a pass is two laps a kind, so Part A's pass-level intervals are wide. The 34-sensor mean is not a calibrated die temperature (one conversion for every sensor), and a mean hides hot spots. Over a 30-minute hold a stage slower than about 10 minutes is barely resolved: a slow creep shows as a slope, not a resistance. A chained H2 starts from H1's steady state, so it says nothing about arrival. Every launch runs on all 32 shires. The bound on a hold rests on the idle before and after being comparable. E73's and the thermal model's numbers are other cards' and, for E73, another place on the board: failing them on this card does not refute them on theirs.

## Card budget, safety, claim

- **From the claim to 07:15 PST at the latest.** The queue on this card (E99, E95, E101's power session) ends around 02:20 PST. E97 needs about 2 h 25 min for Part A (with the probe and the smoke) and at most about 2 h 30 min for Part B. If it starts late it cuts, in this order: H2, H3, H1, then Part A's second pass, as frozen above.
- **The claim** is one line, `for <N> h, until ~07:15 PST`, posted only after the previous claim reads released and `et-who` is clean, and released by an edit.
- **Nothing is set.** The management traffic is reads: `ettelem config` at both ends of every pass and of the session, and the sampler's six commands (command 74 group 0 only; the sampler refuses group 6). Every launch runs under `timeout 10`; the card lock is held (`flock -n`); the sampler stops on SIGTERM. A pass stops if another user holds the card, if the telemetry stops, or on a STOP file. Other users' idle logins do not block (E78's D1); their logins never enter a file. The hard stop is 82 °C; the clock leaving 600 MHz, or three failed launches in a row, ends the holds and leaves a last rest.
- **The order of work:** this file, the predictions, the code and the desk checks committed and pushed; then the `#community-lab` claim; then `run_e97.sh probe` (three sampler ticks: are command 74's readings still raw codes?), `smoke` (two blocks of 10 s halves; then a 65 s Part B pass with a forced stop), and `go`, detached on the box. Departures get dated amendments, written before the data they touch.
