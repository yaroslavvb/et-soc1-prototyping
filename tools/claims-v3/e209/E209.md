## 1. The question, and the answer frozen here

**Do MM1-v2's frozen forecasts of a matmul's clock cycles, seconds and joules hold on never-run cases on a card whose governor sets its
clock, and does MM1-v2 beat MM1-v1 there?** "Hold" is registered as: per time case (cycles, seconds) or per point (joules), the measured value
within a band of twice MM1-v2's own held-out error, at 90% of cases, each scored twice - **given the measured clock** and **with the clock
forecast** (section 5); "beat" as: closer to the measurement than MM1-v1 by a bootstrap interval of the mean difference in |ln| stated now, and
than F1 (cycles) and F2 (joules) by an exact sign test (section 7).

**The models.** **MM1-v2** (`tools/matmul/mm1/v2/`) is MM1-v1 refitted with E208, the model session's matmul sweep (rgm136 at 74 arms; on
this card under its governor in sessions a2c0-S1 and a2c0-S2), with the terms E208 identifies: the L2 time in cycles, the subset contention on
the DRAM stream only, the L3 retention refitted, per-card terms, a price for the constant-one tile, the rest law per card, and **this card's
clock as an input**, with a governor clock model (post hoc) for the clock forecast. **MM1-v1** (`tools/matmul/mm1/v1/`, landed `25d2e74e`)
is the same map and equations before E208, called unchanged with this card's arguments (V by the energy manual's line, G by the M7 relation);
it prices no energy for the constant-one and real tiles here (NOT FORECAST). **F1@800** is E190's arm-A route at 800 MHz (the source of E206's
F1) generalized with E206's lines; **F2@800** is ledger v3 priced as E206's F2 prices a GEMM, with the card aifoundry2 at 800 MHz; both are desk
generalizations made by the lane that built MM1-v2, and both already miss E208's aifoundry2 points (MM1-v2's README, section 5), so **the
informative head-to-head is MM1-v2 against MM1-v1**. His question - how well the timing simulation matches reality - is read beside every point:
the measured cycles over the simulator's instruction count and the counter's mean over the tensor unit's floor (section 7, readings).

**How far apart the models are** (desk, from the frozen files at 799.0 MHz; F1 and F2 at 800 MHz): MM1-v2 and MM1-v1 differ by 5.0% rms in ln in the wall cycles over the 41 time cases (13 more than 2% apart, 13 within the tie), by 6.8% in the counter's mean, and by 3.3% in J over idle over the 39 points MM1-v1 prices (13 more than 2% apart); MM1-v2 and F1@800 by 17.1% in the wall cycles (38 of 41 more than 2% apart); MM1-v2 and F2@800 by 80% in J over idle (42 points). The bands do not separate the models (section 7); the head-to-head rules do. MM1-v2's own README gives the same gaps per point (cycles MM1-v1 / MM1-v2 4.8% over 45, F1@800 / MM1-v2 17.1%; W MM1-v1 / MM1-v2 2.6% over 39, F2@800 / MM1-v2 84.9% over 42).

**The arm used: `gemmL<MB>x<NB>x<KB>`** (`SP_GEMM_LARGE`): C (MB x NB blocks of 1,024 x 512) = A (MB x KB blocks) x B (KB x NB blocks), each
block the pattern's 64 x 32 x 32 tiles; every minion owns two row tiles and one column tile of every C block and runs each C tile's whole K
(KB x 32 ops) before its one TensorStore. M is a multiple of 1,024, N of 512, K of 512 (fp32), 1,024 (fp16) or 2,048 (int8). Ops a minion a GEMM:
MB x NB x 2 x KB x 32. **Shire subsets** (`--shires <mask> --per-shire 32`): shire s computes rows 128 (s >> 2) .. +128 and columns 128 (s & 3)
.. +128 of every C block; E209's masks are `0xffff0000` and `0xffff` (16 shires), `0xff` (8), `0xf`, `0x1111` and `0xf000` (4) and `0x1`,
`0x80000000` (1). Each minion's work is the full-chip GEMM's; the host checks the participating minions' tiles of C, so every subset point is
checked.

**The data** (E101's `--values <arm>:<gen>:<files>` grammar, `--seed 1`): random (`randn`; fp32 normal(0, 1), fp16 the same rounded to half,
int8 uniform -128..127 from `int8/full.bin`), zeros and the constant one (a repeated tile: E208's kit tiles, copies of
`tools/claims-v3/kinds/tiles/`), and **real** (E206's four real tiles a format under `tools/claims-v3/kinds/real2/tiles/`). Each file by sha256

and `--stop-file`; E208's `host_argv`). The kit may place the tiles under its own root, as E208's kit does (`src/tiles/`): the bytes register,
and the scorer reads a record's `--values` by its arm, generator and files' `<format>/<name>`.

**The operations a matmul contains**, as this kernel runs them: tensor multiply-adds (4,096 MACs an op in fp32, 8,192 in fp16, 16,384 in int8),
loads of A and B tiles (an op asks 64 A lines and 128 B lines; where they come from is the line map), and one TensorStore of C per C tile row.
**No epilogue** (bias, activation, requantization).

## 8. What E209 cannot say (stated before the data)

- **It tests rgm136 on its grid only**: the `gemmL` arm, M a multiple of 1,024, N of 512, K of one block per format. MM1-v2's "from sizes and

  sit beside every point (REPORTED, reading i) and are never scored; the day tests the cycles, seconds and joules they are built on.

  the capped flag (e44) and K1's burst clock; MM1-v2's governor model and its chain are post hoc on this card's E208 runs, and 'never capped'
  already agreed on 141 of E208's 160 runs here.

- **No epilogue**; **a small GEMM's joules are a warm, repeated GEMM's** (a launch repeats the same A and B, so a footprint the L3 holds is served

   between 600 and 700 MHz) through that window has no `win` view: it ran and is not set aside, yet it carries no reading. The frozen scorer
   then leaves its point out of every rule and head-to-head without naming it, while the run's STEP flag reads "scored at its view's measured
   mean clock", which does not apply to it. **Such a point is NO DATA in every rule** (section 7's heading: never a failure), **and the kit's
   step 13 names it** for the day's note, beside every point with no kept run (it did not run, or every run was set aside) and every point
   with a time reading but no energy reading (capped or thin). Self-test day H (every run at 700 MHz in a `win` view) is a stand-in the
   reducer cannot produce; route m at 700 MHz is exercised only there. The scorer is unchanged.
3. **A label must name a point or a time case (finding 5).** The frozen scorer applies `--labels` by set membership and echoes every label it
   is given as applied, so a label that names no point (a typo such as `e1`) or no time case (a case spelled `0xFFFFFFFF` for `0xffffffff`)
   would leave its point in every rule while the output says it was applied. **Before step 13 passes labels, the kit checks** every
   `ran_before_points` entry against `points-e209.json`'s point ids and every `ran_before_cases` entry against its `time_cases` keys, and
   refuses (exit 10) on any that names nothing: the card lane corrects the dated note and the labels file, never the scorer.
4. **Wording, corrected in place (finding 10, a nit).** Section 7's footnote to T2: its (ii), 7.88%, is the E208 fold's counter-mean error
   over E208's points on all three cards, not a leave-one-experiment-out error over all points (MM1-v2 records none for the counter's mean;
   `rules-e209.json` > bands > T2 > ii_src said so already); (i), 9.1%, sets the band either way. Section 7's sentence on the watts points:
   F1's and F2's watts under 3 W do not make a watts point; only MM1-v2's W at 799.0 MHz or the measured W under 3 W does (`score_e209.py` >
   `point_figures`), so H6 is decided there in ln of J unless the card reads under 3 W. Neither changes a number or a verdict.
5. **Unchanged, and why:** the stops (section 6: this card's own lines, 85 W on 3 samples and 80 C, E208's; verify-e209's finding 1 asks the
   orchestrator to confirm them before the card day, as reg-e209 did); the weak head-to-head with MM1-v1 (finding 2: sections 7 and 8 state
   it, with its power table); everything else.
