## 0. The answer this registration expects, in one table

A **one-number kind** (a, b) is a TensorFMA tile whose A matrix holds a everywhere and whose B matrix holds b everywhere. No operand bit
ever changes inside a launch. What switches is the accumulator walking 0, p, 2p, ... (p the step a k-group adds), the buses that carry it,
and buses that return to zero between uses. **A held 1-bit** is a 1 in an operand word that stays put for the whole launch; E178's flip
model prices such bits only where a bus returns to zero (int8: TenB's bypass, the B word, once a k-group; section 3). So every forecast
below says a held bit costs on B's side only. The trains ask whether A's side costs too.

## 3. How a held bit can cost, and what each form of reading sees

In a one-number kind the open RTL's flip model (E178.md section 3, read from core-et `b38a1a3`) counts, per op: the accumulator's storage,
its read and write data (all set by the product: one walk), the scratchpad's read data alternating A and B chunks (32 switches an op in
int8, 62 in the floats, each 8 x the A/B Hamming distance; set by the distance), the floats' write-back bus (the accumulator's popcount,
returning to zero between k-passes), and **int8's TenB bypass, which carries the B word and returns to zero once a k-group: 256 x
popcount(B word) flips an op.** The A word is held at the units' inputs by enable flops and does not move. So in the model a held 1-bit
costs on B's side, through TenB, and nowhere on A's. A held bit could also cost where the open RTL does not reach (the full-custom
register file and scratchpad are not in it): a read port that discharges per 1 read, or another bus that returns to zero. Those would
put a cost on A's side, or on both.

**Four splits, told apart by the design** (beta in W per held 1-bit a byte): **B-ONLY** (beta_A = 0; every forecast), **A+B** (both sides
cost), **A-ONLY** (beta_B = 0), **NEITHER**. Within a product group the walk and the A/B distance are fixed, so only beta_A h_A + beta_B
h_B moves (h the popcount a byte): T3 = 7 (beta_A + beta_B), T2 = 7 (beta_B - beta_A), T4 = beta_B - beta_A.

**Two forms of reading, on one card in one session:**
- **The kinds passes** (E178's form, unchanged): each kind one process of nine 0.49 s launches, E78's heater and approach (launch at 58 C
  after 59 C, 2 s of quiet), its D the board over the format's zeros in a 2 s window, leak-corrected on E178's registered footing. A pair is
  read as adjacent couples, three a pass. It reads every kind's level against zeros, at a couple-level noise of 0.125 W a pass.
- **The trains** (new for E182, a host-only change): one process alternates the pair's two kinds launch by launch, 13 launches after the
  calibration launch, launch 0 a settle, launches 1-12 three quads, each quad S F F S (or F S S F), processes back to back with the
  orientation alternating. Both kinds share one thermal state; a linear drift cancels inside each quad, a curvature between the two
  orientations. No leak correction is applied (none is needed between two kinds at one temperature).

**The host-only change** (the kit's; nothing committed is edited): the C-logging host's copy (`tools/claims-v3/ccheck/patch_host_ccheck.py`,
applied unchanged) gains a second tile file and a launch schedule: `--values file:<F's tile> --values-b file:<S's tile> --abba SFFS|FSSF
--launches 13`. Both tile sets are copied to the card once; each launch points the kernel's A and B arguments at one set; the kernel, its
class, the launch, its timing, the C log's records and every device call are the C-logging host's. Each launch's record names its kind; the
C log carries both tile sets and each launch's C with its tiles' fnv, so every train launch's C is checked against its own tiles. The kit
verifies the change at the desk (against the check of C's mock runtime) and compiles it on the box as its first step; if that compile or
the train smoke fails, the session runs the kinds passes only (section 7) and every train rule reads NOT SCORED.

## 5. The rules (each with its statistic, threshold, verdicts and power; section 6 gives the noise)

### 5.1 The train's statistic ([`e182_stats.py`](e182_stats.py), which the kit's scorer imports unchanged)

- **A launch's reading:** the mean of the block's 10 Hz `board_w` samples in [the launch's start + 170 ms, its end] (its SPARSITY record's
  times), after E82 amendment A2's dip rule over the block's samples (`board_w[i] < min(board_w[i-2], board_w[i+2]) - 4.0 W` drops sample
  i); fewer than 2 kept samples: no reading. Raw board watts: no leak correction, no zeros subtracted.
- **A quad** (launches 4q+1 to 4q+4, q = 0, 1, 2; launch 0 is the settle): with all four readings, sigma (L2 + L3 - L1 - L4) / 2, sigma = +1
  in an SFFS process and -1 in an FSSF one, so every quad reads D(F) - D(S).
- **A process's value:** the mean of its complete quads. **A unit:** processes 2j and 2j + 1 of the plan (one of each orientation), the mean
  of their two values when both exist. **The train's estimate:** the mean over units, with the 95% interval t(0.975, n - 1) sd / sqrt(n).
  Fewer than 8 units: NOT SCORED. A process during which a stop fired, or with a launch whose record says not ok, gives no value.
- **beta_A = (T3 - T2) / 14 and beta_B = (T3 + T2) / 14**, each with the Welch-Satterthwaite interval of the two independent trains.

### 5.4 Reported beside, never a verdict

  leak-0 arm, `scored.json` > `arms` > `leak0`).
- **The couples' order term** (the card day critic's covariate): over each pair's couples, the mean of (the first run's D minus the second's)
  less the orientation-signed pair difference, on the registered footing and on leak 0.
- **The raw footing arm** (leak 0) of every kinds-pass rule and level; every kind's D against P, P + ACC and E; the session's s and rates.
- **In each train:** the carry-over reading (the mean of L2 - L3 in SFFS quads, where L2 follows an S launch and L3 an F launch, against the
  same in FSSF); the first and second halves' estimates; each orientation's mean (the curvature); the train's anchors' levels; the
  minion rail's level over the train (its filter of about 1 s smears a 0.49 s alternation, so it reads no quad).
- **Card 1's frozen forecasts** (`forecast-e182.json`), not read: E182 is kitted for aifoundry3 only.

**What each outcome means.** E182-SPLIT B-ONLY with E182-B's SIZE PASS against E: a held bit costs only where the model says it does (TenB,
B's side) and at E178's measured price; the flip model's account of a one-number kind holds and M10's int8 price is too high by E178's
factor. B-ONLY with SIZE FAIL HIGH against both: B's side costs less than either price. A+B: A's held bits cost too, by a path the open RTL's
model does not hold (the full-custom arrays, a read port, another return-to-zero bus); the next model needs a per-held-bit term on A. A-ONLY or
NEITHER: TenB's priced flips are not what the card pays for, and the int8 order E178 read comes from somewhere else. E182-F POSITIVE or
NEGATIVE: in the floats, held bits cost on one side more than the other (with A on the recoded side), where every forecast says 0. E182-HT
REPEATS: the train reads E178's pair as E178 read it; with E182-TR HOLDS or BETTER, one-number pairs and single-bit prices can move to trains.

## 6. The noise model and the power ([`noise/noise_e182.py`](noise/noise_e182.py), [`power_e182.py`](power_e182.py))

- **The trains' noise, from E178's committed telemetry** (`noise/noise-e182.json`, post hoc, the registration's input): each kept run's
  launches 1-4 and 5-8 read as quads under section 5.1's rule. int8 one-number kinds: 134 quads, **sd 0.03485 W** (robust 0.0346), mean
  -0.0042; float one-number kinds: 304 quads, **sd 0.03804 W**, mean +0.0087 (a curvature inside a process: launches 2 and 3 read above 1 and
  4, which the orientation pairs cancel); a run's two quads correlate -0.10 (int8) and -0.05 (floats): close to independent. Launch 0 reads
  0.21 W under the later launches in the float runs (0.02 W in int8): the settle launch is not read. The launch window held 3 samples (3-4).
  Timing: a launch 0.490 s, 3 ms between launches, 193 ms from a process's spawn to its first launch, 47 ms after its last.
- **The sizing rule** (`plan_e182.py`): a train's quads n_q = ceil((1.96 sigma_q / 0.005 W)^2), its processes the next even number at or
  above n_q / 3: int8 187 quads, **64 processes**; fp32 223 quads, **76 processes**. The forecast half-width is then 0.0049 W (BASE) and
  0.0099 W (DOUBLED, Q150's allowance of a factor 2 for switching transients). A process takes about 6.92 s (the spawn, the calibration
  launch, 13 launches, the exit and 0.25 s of the runner's checks).
- **The simulation** (`power_e182.py`, seeds random.Random(18200 + case), 4,000 draws a case): quads drawn N(x + o kappa, sigma_q^2) with
  o = +1 in SFFS and -1 in FSSF and kappa = +0.009 W (the float curvature); each quad lost with chance 0.01; the plan's process counts and
  section 5.1's statistics exactly. The truths: each forecast (P, P + ACC, E), E178's measured value, zero; for the split, B-ONLY at P's and at
  E's price, A+B equal a bit (E's total halved on each side), A-ONLY, NEITHER, and a mixed case (beta_A 0.002 with beta_B at E).
- **The kinds passes' noise** (E178.md section 6, carried as registered, never E178's lucky 0.020 W pass sd): one read of a kind in a pass
  0.125 / sqrt 2 W, a kind read three times in a pass (the pairs' kinds) 0.125 / sqrt 6, a session term 0.041 W a kind; 2,000 draws a case
  at three and four passes.

**The design's figures** (`power-e182.txt` has every case): a train's median 95% half-width is 0.0051 W at BASE and 0.0102 W at DOUBLED
(int8 and fp32 alike, by the sizing rule); beta_A's 0.00051 and 0.00102 W a bit a byte, about a tenth and a fifth of E178's B-side price
(0.00547) and a thirtieth and a fifteenth of M10's (0.01570). The kinds passes' quartet fit reaches 0.014 W a bit at three passes: it can check the trains' split only
for a split as large as M10's price, which is why the split is the trains' rule and the quartet fit a reported reading.

## 11. What the rules cannot say

- A train reads a difference between two kinds, never a level: the levels come from the kinds passes at E178's resolution (0.1 W at four
  passes). beta_B joins TenB's priced flips and any other B-side held-bit cost: no one-number design can split them, since both are linear
  in B's popcount. beta_A and beta_B are per bit a byte averaged over where the bits sit; which bit positions cost is not asked.
- beta_A and beta_B from T2 and T3 take a held bit to cost the same at the products -1 and +1 (each contrast holds its own walk
  fixed); the quartets' fit reads the same split inside each of the products 15, 85 and 127, but only at the kinds passes' resolution.
- A first-order carry-over from one launch to the next attenuates an A B B A contrast by (1 - c); the window's 170 ms start makes c small,
  and the trains report it (section 5.4), but no rule corrects for it.
- One card, one session: nothing here separates a card's level from a gain, and card 1 is not run.
- The float swap tests held bits on the recoded side against the other at one pair of words; a null is not evidence of equality at every
  word.
