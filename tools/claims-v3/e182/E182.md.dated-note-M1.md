- **card1, the assumed held-operand band** (a measured residual turned into an assumed per-bit term, not in the central value): B-only beta_B up to +0.02052 W per held 1-bit a byte; or A+B, up to +0.01026 W on each side.

**What the model gives: neither side, at E182's resolution.** Both central betas sit inside E182's NULL band (+-0.002 W per held 1-bit a byte). The only per-bit cost in the model's structure is B's operand delivery: about +0.17 to +0.21 fJ per B bit-a-byte per slot-op, +0.0013 to +0.0017 W per B bit-a-byte at aifoundry3 (modelled). A's set bits move no data role: the A bus is held, and the accumulator walk and C reads are identical for ones and neg_ones. B's storage buffer costs 0 fJ whatever B holds. The negative central beta_A is not a price. It is T3's fall in the clock's held-state term (-1.167 fJ) and in state-weighted leakage (-1.430 fJ) between neg_ones and ones. Neither is a per-bit term: the clock term falls with data activity, and the leakage term is not monotone in popcount. The direct one-bit swap T4 shows 0.017 fJ of clock and leakage asymmetry, so the model has no A-specific term. The quartet swaps' opposite signs (+0.0126 and -0.0056 W) come from the surrogate pricing's clock term, not from the data: their data parts are +0.0037 and +0.0092 W, both on B's side. With the model's assumed held-operand band switched on, the split becomes B-ONLY at up to +0.0111 W per B bit-a-byte, or A+B at up to +0.0056 W on each side; the band does not choose between them.

**Caveats: the model's own known misses**

- Coverage: 14 of 24 kinds are on the frozen grids. The other 10 (8 int8 with bytes 0x0f, 0xf1, 0xab, 0x81; the fp32 pair 1.0 / 0x3fffffff, so train T5) read not covered. Nothing was interpolated.
- Pricing: 8 covered kinds are simulated directly (int8 ones, neg_ones, 4s, 5s, (4,5), (5,4); fp32 4s, 5s). 6 are surrogate-priced from the per-net coefficients of direct kinds sharing A's or B's value (int8 (1,-1), (-1,1), (1,85), (85,1), (1,127), (127,1)). The model's own check of the surrogate finds clock-term errors up to 0.94 fJ per slot-op (0.0074 W), the size of the quartet swaps' clock parts.
- The value-dependent static term is missed. The constant-operand vector-add step is measured at +0.40 to +0.49 pJ per instruction on three cards, about 6 to 7x the model. Post hoc, one form fits +6.55 mW [+5.17, +7.94] per held bit. For int8 the central figures carry no such term.
- The held-operand band is assumed. It comes from one measured neg_ones - ones residual per card (aifoundry3 9.91 fJ, card 1 18.26 fJ per slot-op), turned into a per-set-bit term: 0.177 fJ (A+B) or 0.354 fJ (B only) per set bit per slot-op at aifoundry3; 0.326 or 0.652 on card 1. T3's band top (+0.0684 W) equals that earlier measurement by construction, so for T3 only the central -0.0096 W is the model's own figure.
- Clock: the clock is about 94% of every int8 kind's level above zeros (179.7 of 191.4 fJ per slot-op for int8 ones), so it sets the levels, not the differences. Model over measured for the int8 ones level is 0.970 (aifoundry3) and 0.911 (card 1); for neg_ones 0.923 and 0.830 (non-blind). The float lane clock is probably a few per cent high on aifoundry3.
- In T1 (5s - 4s), the data part is +7.09 fJ per slot-op (+0.056 W). The clock's held-state term takes back 2.28 fJ (-0.018 W), leaving +0.039 W. Whether that give-back is real is not checkable on public data.
- Card 1: its rail excess over aifoundry3 (1.08 to 1.10x on floats, 1.08x on int8, 1.04 to 1.06x at the board, after V^2) has no cause placed and is not in the model. Card 1 watts here use E182's registered aifoundry3 rate (assumed; E182 does not run card 1). The freeze used each card's own measured rate: int8 1.9245e9 (aifoundry3) and 1.9239e9 (card 1) per second, fp32 about 1.1201e9.
- Rate: E182's registered rates are 0.21% (int8) and 0.26% (fp32) below the rates used at the freeze. W here = the frozen W x that ratio; the energies are unchanged.
- Glitch factors are assumed: x1.18 on the int8 core data and x1.043 for float one-number kinds.
- Operand delivery outside the lane is sized, not simulated: 2.2% of the board energy per op and 4.6% of D for random fp32. The unit wires on the shared operand bus are assumed [...].
- Launch points: int8 accumulator state at 1 launch point, fp32 at 9. Lanes 1 to 7 are simulated at launch point 1 only. Position in a launch moves a single-bit price by up to about 7x in the float unit.
- Post hoc, for E178 only (no E182 data read): the model's T1, +0.039 W, is 0.74x E178's measured +0.053 W [+0.022, +0.085] and inside its interval. M10's +0.186 W is 3.5x the measured value. Both count the same accumulator walk. The gap is the price per flip: 7.98 fJ at the board against the model's 1.55 fJ at the die (about 1.85 fJ board-equivalent).
