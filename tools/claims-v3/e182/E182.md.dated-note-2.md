## 2. The departure, stated before any datum

**From now on, section 7's "if the box's compile of the host-only change fails" reads:** two builds of the train host on the day (the
Pacific calendar, counted by the box itself in `build/e182-build-attempts.txt`) that did not read TRAIN-HOST-BUILT. A build that failed, ran
past its own 500 s bound, or whose job was lost counts. **A job that read TRAIN-HOST-INPUTS-DIFFER built nothing and does not count.** For
it, note 1's input rule holds: step 5 v2 once more, then step 6; if the inputs differ again, the fallback (KIT.md.dated-note-1.md section 2).
A good build is never rebuilt the same day, because step 6 v4 refuses that start. KIT.md step 6's "If the build fails" and its open item 2
read the same way. The smoke's half of the trigger ("or the smoke gate fails a train item") is unchanged.

**This is a departure from the letter of section 7 and of KIT.md box step 6.** The letter sends the session to the fallback after the first
failed compile; the reading allows one re-run. It is not a departure from section 7's intent, which is that the trains run only on a train
host built from the registered inputs and checked.

**Why it carries no bias:**
- It is decided before any E182 datum, and no datum can inform it. A build opens no device and measures nothing.
- A re-run builds the same pinned inputs (`harness-e182-note1.json` and `harness-e182.json`), checked before and inside the build.
- The host that runs is checked as the first would have been: HARNESS OK 73 against the registered patched source and its build record,
  then the smoke's train gate, then the desk's check of C.

**What it changes:** a build that fails once for a passing reason (a job lost on the box, a full disk, a short memory or file-system
fault) no longer ends the trains for the day. A cut connection never did: the build is a detached job, and the count is the box's. The
card-minutes do not change: the build opens no device, and card time counts from the session's T0 (KIT.md box step 9). The wall time grows
by one more build, about as long as the first.

**What it does not change:** every forecast, every rule but this trigger's reading, every threshold, statistic, power figure, kind, tile and
plan; FULL's and PASSES' caps (100 and 40 card-minutes); the stops.

**Kit note 2's words that nothing of the rules changed** are read with this one exception: its header ("No rule, forecast, threshold,
statistic, registered power figure, plan, cap or stop changes"), its section 5's "It adds no rule" (of the brief), and the brief's own header
(CARD-LANE-BRIEF.md: "no rule, forecast, threshold, statistic, power figure, plan, cap or stop changes").
