# E197 registration desk check: the build on aifoundry2 and the request's own checks (reg-e197, Q185; desk checks of code)

Built from a copy of proposal/src (byte for byte the relay folder's src/, SHA256SUMS-e197-registration) in a folder of the lane's
own, exactly as src/BUILD.md writes it: cmake -S <copy> -B <build> -DCMAKE_PREFIX_PATH=/opt/et -Wno-dev; cmake --build <build> -j4;
through the round's CPU slot at nice 10, under a 4 GB address-space cap. Nothing here opened a device: check_elf.py reads the
disassembly, make_images.py is pure Python, and the host ran only --list (in the no-device sandbox, desk/host-list-e197.*).
