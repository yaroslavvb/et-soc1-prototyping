## A1-2. A stop during the smoke's two train processes (sections 6 and 12; verify-e188 fault 4)

**The two texts.** Section 6: the session runs the fallback (PASSES) "if the box's build of the train host fails (after its one re-run), or
the smoke's train gate fails". Section 12, first item: "A stop ends the queue at once ... nothing is retried that day"; second item: "a failed
smoke train gate, sends the session to the fallback". A registered stop (board at or above 65 W, or `temp_c.minshire[0]` at or above 70 C,
on a live sample) that fires during the smoke's two train processes (block 18801's train part, before the queue starts) fails the smoke's
train gate, so the two items meet there. The kit as committed (`726a6b64`) reads it as the second item: the smoke block counts its kinds part
whole (`block_e188.sh --smoke`); the smoke's kinds gate fails on a STOP file in the smoke's own directory or a board maximum at or above 65 W
over the whole smoke's telemetry (`smoke_gate_e188.py --part kinds`), so a board stop in the train part ends the session with no block run;
a die stop there fails the train gate only (`--part train`), and `session_e188.sh` then runs the fallback queue.

**The reading, registered here before any datum:** a stop during the smoke's train processes is a failed smoke train gate (section 12's
second item, section 6's fallback). **No train runs that day, and none is retried.** The session runs the registered fallback queue
(PASSES, about 28 card-minutes, cap 40), whose every launch is still guarded by the live stops (before every launch of a kinds run, every
0.1 s while one waits, and in the in-run watcher); a stop in that queue ends it at once (section 12's first item), and nothing is retried.
Every train rule reads NOT SCORED and E188-LV is the reading, as section 6 says of the fallback. The records name the stop that failed the
gate (the smoke's train part's STOP line). A stop is never a failed prediction (section 12).

A later kit note may instead end the session on any stop in the smoke (the kinds gate reading the train part's STOP and the smoke's die
maximum as well); it would come with its own dated amendment, committed before the data it governs. Until then this reading governs.
