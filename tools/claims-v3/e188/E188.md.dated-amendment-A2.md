## A2-1. A stop during the smoke's two train processes: what the kit does, and what governs

**What the kit does** (a desk read of committed code, `726a6b64`):

- `block_e188.sh --smoke` runs the smoke's thirteen kinds items, then the first two processes of the train the kit names for the
  smoke (`run_e188t.py --processes 2`) into the smoke's `train/` directory, under one sampler for the whole block. It writes the
  train part's exit status to `train.rc` and returns 0 whatever it was ("the gate reads the train items; the kinds smoke passed").
- The train runner, and its in-run watcher, write their STOP line into the smoke's `train/STOP`, not the smoke's own `STOP`.
- `smoke_gate_e188.py --part kinds` fails if the smoke's own `STOP` exists, or if the board's maximum over the smoke's telemetry,
  the train part's samples among them, is at or above the registered 65 W stop (its lines 119–133).
- `session_e188.sh` (its lines 102–118) runs no block when the kinds gate fails ("smoke gate failed ...: no block runs"); when only
  the train gate fails (`--part train`, which reads `train.rc` among its checks), it runs `E188_PLAN=PASSES`.

So, during the smoke's train processes:

- **a board stop** (`board_w` at or above 65 W on a live sample, read by the train runner before a process or by its watcher every
  0.1 s) leaves that sample in the smoke's telemetry, so the kinds gate fails: **the session ends there, with no block run and no
  fallback;**
- **a die stop** (`temp_c.minshire[0]` at or above 70 C), or any other end of the train part, fails the train gate only while the
  board's maximum stays under 65 W: the session runs **PASSES**, as A1-2 registers.

**What governs (registered here, before any datum): the kit as it is.** A1-2's reading stands for every stop in the smoke's train
processes except a board stop. **A board stop there ends the session:** no train and no block of the fallback runs that day, nothing
is retried, every train rule reads NOT SCORED and **E188-LV is not read** (no E188 rule is scored that day); the records name the stop
(the smoke's `train/STOP` line) and the smoke gate's FAIL line. A die stop there fails the smoke's train gate only: no train runs that
day, the session runs PASSES under the live stops, and E188-LV is the reading, as A1-2 says. This errs on the safe side: after a board
stop no further launch is made that day. A stop is never a failed prediction (section 12). A later kit note may still change either
branch, with its own dated amendment, committed before the data it governs.
