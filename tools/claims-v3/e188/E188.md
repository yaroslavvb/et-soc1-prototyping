**The rule** (applied before this registration, on the private record; stated here before any E188 datum):
1. **The cost:** whole trains of 8.0 card-minutes each, by the list's own costing; seven of them inside 60 card-minutes.
2. **The criterion:** the list's own joint criterion, which takes the set of trains that keeps the weakest pair of the list's candidate
   explanations furthest apart over the set. The explanations and their figures are in the NDA-tier file named below by its sha256 only;
   no value of the criterion enters this repository.
3. **The tie-break:** many sets tie at that criterion's optimum. The tie is broken by the same criterion recomputed with two nuisance terms
   left free in every explanation (a common price per held mantissa one; and a scale on the share of each item that its product, not its
   held bits, sets); the set kept is the one whose weakest pair stays furthest apart with the first left free, and it is also the furthest
   with both, at the tighter of the two priors tried for the second.
4. **The alternatives not taken:** two other sets, each best under a different way of breaking the tie, were considered and not taken

## 5. The rules (each with its statistic, threshold and verdicts; section 7 gives the power)

### 5.1 The train's statistic

E182's, unchanged (`e182_stats.py`, through `e186_stats.py`): a launch's reading is the mean of its 10 Hz `board_w` samples from its start
plus 170 ms to its end after E82 A2's dip rule; a quad is sigma (L2 + L3 - L1 - L4) / 2 so every quad reads D(F) - D(S); a process's value
the mean of its complete quads; a unit two processes of opposite orientation; **the train's estimate the mean over units with its
t(0.975, n - 1) interval** (half-width about 0.0051 W at 38 units, registered); fewer than 8 units, NOT SCORED. A process during which a stop
fired, or with a launch whose record says not ok, gives no value.

### 5.2 The train rules (primary), one per train

For each of the seven trains (E188-B05, E188-PAIR, E188-B19, E188-AALL, E188-B10, E188-B22, E188-B04), with `e186_stats.py`'s words:

### 5.5 The fallback's levels (only if the trains do not run)

**E188-LV:** over the fallback's fifteen kinds (section 3), each kind's measured D over fp32 zeros (the mean over kept passes on E182's footing,
E178's reduction), against M12's level and M10's at the session's s and rate. Statistic: rms(D - M12) - rms(D - M10) with a 95% bootstrap

otherwise. Fewer than 2 kept passes: NOT SCORED. In FULL its arithmetic over the kinds pass's eight kinds is reported only.

**What each outcome means.** E188-GB1 POSITIVE with SIZE PASS and REPEAT AGREES, and E188-BPOS FLAT: one held B mantissa bit costs what a
held bit costs among eight or sixteen, at every place tried: a per-bit price on B's side that follows neither its place nor how many bits
hold beside it. BPOS VARIES: the price follows the bit's place in the word, and the five trains say which places cost more. GB1 REPEAT

per-bit price over all 23 bits is the one E186 read over 16; DIFFERS: it changes with how many bits hold, as E186-S23R's DIFFERS LOW
suggested for the swap. E188-M M12: M12's one price a side carries to one-bit words and to A's 23 bits; NEITHER: the trains' own SIZE words
say which part fails. T5's two halves, read as levels in one session by the kinds pass, and A's half as a train, say how T5's +0.236 W
splits between B's 23 bits and A's.

## 11. What the rules cannot say

- A train reads a difference between two kinds, never a level; the kinds passes read levels only to about +-0.13 W.
- Every train holds its words still through a launch: nothing here prices bits that move.
- Five places of B's 23, one adjacent pair and one spaced pair, and A's 23 bits together: no A bit alone, no other pair.
- M10's lines carry P10's differences; E188-GB1, BPOS and GA23 read what is left after them, so a wrong P10 difference moves them.
- T5 itself is not a train here: its split between A's half and B's comes from E188's AALL and E182's T5 across sessions, or from the kinds
  passes' two levels at their coarse resolution, and both are reported.
- One card, one session: nothing here separates a card's level from a gain, and no other card is run.
- A first-order carry-over from one launch to the next attenuates an A B B A contrast by (1 - c); the trains report it, and no rule corrects
  for it.

## 12. What a stop or a failed build means

- **A stop** ends the queue at once. A process during which it fired gives no value; a train left with fewer than 8 units, and every rule that
  needs it, reads NOT SCORED; nothing is retried that day. **A stop is never a failed prediction:** a rule with no data reads NOT SCORED, and
  only a scored rule's words say anything about a forecast.
- **A failed build** of the train host (after its one re-run), or a failed smoke train gate, sends the session to the fallback (section 6):
  every train rule reads NOT SCORED and E188-LV is the reading. If the fallback cannot run either, E188 has no data that day: it is not run,
  never failed.
- **No window, no approval, a holder on the card at the look, a login link, or E183 still unfinished:** E188 is not run that day.
