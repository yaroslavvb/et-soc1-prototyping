# E125: E113's A-side ladder on a second card (Q134, aifoundry1 card 1, 600 MHz)

*Pre-registered Sun 1 Nov 2026 (week 44, times PST) on the Intel, before the claim in `#community-lab` and before any card has run
this list, in Q134's run. Any change after this commit goes in Amendments at the end, dated, before the data it touches. Card:
**aifoundry1 card 1 only** (`V3_DEVICE=1`; firmware 1.2.0, whose governor has held 600 MHz in every sample since 25 Sep; about
499 mV; E124 read TDP 65 W, threshold 65 °C there at 19:21 and 19:46 PST). E125 writes no knob: no governor move, no TDP,
threshold or clock set, no reset, no `--reset-ms`, no PMIC command; `ettelem config` is read, read-only, under card 1's lock with
`timeout 10`, at the session's start and end. As in every run with the stock sampler, those reads and the sampler read PMIC voltage
registers (reads, no write; an open question for the owner, Q133's records).*

**What it answers.** [E113](../../../docs/reports/data/2026-11-01-e113-a-side-saturation/README.md) answered the A side's form for
the quiet partner **on one card** (aifoundry3 card 0): the layout matters on A (P1), a curve in A's own summed single-bit prices is
rejected (P2), an A mask costs about two thirds of the same mask on B (P3, P4), ρ(8) ≈ 1.35 per designed bit (P5). On the kinds
page's [§ 11, "What is still open"](https://spacesheep.dev/@yaroslavvb/horace-kinds-of-numbers#11-what-is-still-open) that is
**item 15** ("Smaller, and carried"; Q133 narrowed it with E113's A side and left "another card" open) and **item 6**'s clause on the
A-side ladders. E125 runs E113's list on a second card, so each of those answers either holds on two cards or is named as one
card's. It also asks one of E113's own open questions inside one session (N1): why its low layout's A/B ratio read near 1.0 at
k = 2 and 4 and near 0.63 from k = 8 up, when the B rungs came from E70, five days earlier.

## Design

E113's list unchanged plus two B rungs, one pass, so everything shares each pass's offset (26 runs after two unrecorded burn-in runs
of `anchor_pi`; [`items_e125.sh`](items_e125.sh)):

```
anchor_pi, aq_h0, alq_h1, alq_h2, alq_h3, alq_h4, alq_h6, alq_h8, alq_h12, alq_h16, alq_h23,
als_h2, als_h3, als_h4, als_h6, als_h8, als_h12, als_h16, lq_h0, lq_h2, lq_h4, lq_h8, ls_h8, nrd_a, nrd_b
```

- **The operands are E113's, byte for byte.** `setup_e125.sh` writes `build/hl-gen6-e113` on the box with E113's own
  `make_e113.py --check` and compares every file against the sha256 list E113's pass 1131 recorded
  ([`e113-p1131-gen.sha256`](e113-p1131-gen.sha256), 177 files: manifest, tiles, sources). On the Intel the same generator wrote 177
  of 177 identical files (~20:00 PST), and on aifoundry1 `setup_e125.sh` did too (20:11:49 PST: "identical lists: yes"), with card 1
  free ("No process holds an ET-SoC-1 device node or card lock") and the `V3_DRY=1` dry run of the list on card 1 ending "ok".
- **`lq_h2`, `lq_h4` are new to this list** (E70's B rungs at k = 2 and 4, in make_hl.py's set). They add the B side at the masks
  where E113's A/B ratio was odd, run in the same passes as `alq_h2`, `alq_h4`, `alq_h8` and `lq_h8`. E113's scorer reads them as B
  rungs over `lq_h0` and uses them in nothing it scores.
- **Runner and protocol: E113's, unchanged**, through `hl/block.sh` with `V3_DEVICE=1` (`HL_GEN=build/hl-gen6-e113`,
  `HL_EXPN=e125`): card 1's lock `/run/lock/etsoc-shire1.lock` held for each pass, every device process under `timeout 10` with
  `--stop-file`; launch at a 58 °C minion-shire reading after a 59 °C preheat (E102 launched E78's way at 58 °C after 59 °C on this
  card); 4 s runs on all 1,024 minions; `anchor_pi` first and last, the rest shuffled with the pass number and the card's name as
  seed; a pass stops at 78 °C, on telemetry older than 3 s, or when another user holds card 1, its nodes or its lock (`hl/block.sh`'s
  aifoundry1 test; another user's idle login does not block, E60's departure D1). The harness files on the box (`lib.sh`,
  `hl/block.sh`, `run_hl.py`, `reduce_hl.py`, `make_hl.py`) match the Intel's sha256 (probe 19:56 PST).

**N1: the low layout's A/B ratio at small k, inside one session.** Per pass, r_k = ΔW(`alq_h<k>`) / ΔW(`lq_h<k>`) (each over its own
zero) at k = 2, 4, 8, and **N1 = (r2 + r4)/2 − r8**, the mean over kept passes with the between-pass SE. Two readings of E113's
pattern: (i) it is the card's A side: E113's A rungs (same frame) over E70's B rungs give r2 1.019, r4 1.054, r8 0.621, **N1 0.415**;
(ii) one fraction at every k, E113's ~1.0 at k = 2 and 4 coming from comparing two sessions: **N1 ≈ 0**. **SMALL-K EXCESS if
N1 ≥ 0.20 at ≥ 3 SE; NO SMALL-K EXCESS if its 95% upper bound is below 0.20; else INCONCLUSIVE; NOT SCORED below 3 kept passes.**
No prediction between the two is made: E113's within-session r8 (0.615) and E70's B rungs make (i) the face-value reading, and
nothing measured within one session yet says which holds. Power: reading (i) gives SMALL-K EXCESS in 0.99 / 1.00 of synthetic
sessions at n = 4 / 5 with 0.15 W of per-pass scatter on the two new rungs; reading (ii) reads NO SMALL-K EXCESS (`selftest.txt`).

**The self-test** ([`selftest.txt`](selftest.txt)): a replicate truth reads Z REPLICATES, X-A PASS (16 of 16), N1 SMALL-K EXCESS; a
one-fraction truth reads N1 NO SMALL-K EXCESS; a card whose A side is 25% dearer reads X-A FAIL and X-c FAIL. SELFTEST PASS.

## Protocol, budget and time

- **Setup** (`setup_e125.sh`, files only, before the claim): the operand set and its byte check, card 1's holders, the host's load, a
  `V3_DRY=1` dry run of the list on card 1.
- **Session** (`session_e125.sh`, detached on the box right after the claim, `E125_T0` = its launch): et-who; the opening
  `ettelem config`; the host-load sampler (E70's D-b, read only); the smoke, pass 12500 (`anchor_pi`, `als_h8`, `alq_h8`, no preheat)
  and **E113's smoke gate unchanged** (block ok, every launch ok on 1,024 minions at 546 ± 0.5% cycles per op); then
  `queue_e125.sh`, passes 12501–12508; the closing `ettelem config`.
- **Budget: at most 35 card-minutes.** The queue starts no pass that would end after T0 + 32 min (the longest pass so far is the
  estimate, 330 s at first: E113's longest, 235 s, scaled to 26 runs, plus margin); a pass in flight is never cut. Expected: four or
  five passes. Minimum for a verdict: three kept passes. `build/e125-STOP` stops the queue before its next pass.
- **Time window:** the session starts by 22:30 PST and ends by 23:30 PST, or it is not started (Q134's gates). Card 1 is the only
  card this lane touches; no other of our sessions runs on aifoundry1 meanwhile (E124 released card 1 at 19:47 PST).
- **The day's post** (Q134's rule, one post a day, rewritten): a clause `using aifoundry1 card 1 for 1 h, until ~HH:MM PST` is added to
  the day's post with `edit_replace.mjs` before the session, and dropped (or marked released, if it is the last card held) after it.

## What E125 does not answer

The busy partner (every A rung has B = 1.0); other A base words; fp16 and int8 (`hl/run_hl.py` is fp32 only); the gated lane (E78's
C2 case); the RTL bench's counts of E113's new tiles; a third card; 800 MHz.
