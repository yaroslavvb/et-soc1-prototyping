## 5. THE TRANSPORT RULES (registered; the scorer is `score_e198.py`, self-tested before any card)

For each of the 36 prices: v3 ± se3 is the forecast's (E193 on aifoundry3); v1 ± se1 and its pass count n are card 1's, from the **same registered
reducer** run unchanged on card 1's committed sitting of the same name (the arm's `board_w` / `board_se` / `n`, the train's
`contrast_board_w` / `board_se` / `n`).

- **Decided:** card 1's arm or train is in its sitting's output with **4 or more** measured passes (half the registered 8) and an SE.
  Otherwise the price is **NOT DECIDED**, with its reason: the sitting not run, the arm or train absent (every run of it dropped by the
  reducer's gates, or never reached), fewer than 4 measured passes, or an SE missing. **The passes are read from the reducer's `n`,
  which counts measured passes for every price but the anchor `spin2`, whose `n` counts its runs (5 a pass in sitting A, 2 in B): spin2
  is decided at an `n` of 20 or more in A and 8 or more in B, four passes' worth of its runs** (a run dropped by a reducer gate leaves it
  short of that: NOT DECIDED, the safe side). *Changed before the land and before any datum (Mon 16 Nov 2026, Q191 `fix-e198`):
  the scorer first compared spin2's `n` of runs with 4, deciding it after one measured pass in A or two in B (verify-e198's finding 1).*
- **The combined 95 % interval:** h = 1.96 x sqrt(se1² + se3²), the registered reducer's own form for its nulls
  (`reduce_x04.py` lines 386-388).
- **E198-T1, the prices as measured:** a decided price holds if |v1 - v3| <= h.
- **E198-T2, the prices up to a card-to-card scale:** a decided price holds if v1 lies in
  [min(s_lo v3, s_hi v3) - h, max(s_lo v3, s_hi v3) + h], with **s_lo = 0.952 and s_hi = 1.144** (section 6; the min and max make the band
  right for the three prices below zero).
- **Each rule's verdict, over the 36 registered prices:**
  - **FAIL** if 8 or more decided prices miss (then fewer than 80 % of the 36 can hold, whatever the undecided ones would show);
  - else **PASS** if at least 18 are decided and **at least 80 % of the decided prices hold**;
  - **FAIL** if at least 18 are decided and fewer than 80 % hold;
  - else **NOT DECIDED**.
- **Per sitting, REPORTED** (never a verdict of the day): the same share, decided from half the sitting's prices (A 11 of 22, B 7 of
  14), FAIL from the least number of misses that leaves under 80 % able to hold (A 5, B 3).
- **The day's answer is the pair (E198-T1, E198-T2)**, read as section 1 says. Neither rule is the other's arm. Sitting B not run leaves
  its 14 prices NOT DECIDED and the rules decided on sitting A's 22 (at least 18 of them decided).
- **What the 80 % bar was set against** (desk arithmetic on aifoundry3's own data, before this commit; `selftest-mc-e198.json`): E193's
  sittings scored against themselves by halves hold T1 on 35 of 36 prices (passes 1-4 against 5-8; and odd against even passes); with
  card 1 drawn as aifoundry3's prices plus each price's own noise (2,000 draws), T1 PASSes in 1.000 of draws (median 34 of 36 holding),
