## The hottest frozen peak under G0 is 67.33 °C, not 67.24 °C

§ Stops says "the hottest frozen in-launch peak is 67.24 °C (34-sensor mean, K_OL at a 53.0 °C room)". K_S is hotter. In
`predictions-q140e.json` › `E166.room_rules` (and `E166.by_room`), K_S peaks at **67.33 °C at a 51.5 °C room** (the plan period, 4.37 s)
and at 67.28 °C at 53.0 °C (the warm-room period, 4.97 s). K_OL peaks at 67.24 °C at 53.0 °C. **Every frozen in-launch peak at a room of

the 67.5 °C K_OL room-rule line. Nothing here drives the card toward a PMIC over-power or over-temperature reset.

SL reads arm A's error slope over [t0 + 300 s, b10), about **300 s**. C10's null (`corrections.json` › `C10`) is each committed hold's
slope from 300 s to the hold's end, and the holds are not all the same length:

A least-squares slope over a longer window is less noisy, so E144's two 480 s slopes pull the pool's spread down. The stated 5.0% false-
positive rate is the registered pool's rate. Over pools matched more closely to SL's window it is higher (a normal zero-mean null, the
pool's rms about zero, as C10 and `slope_rule_e166.py` compute it):

| null pool | n | σ (°C per 10 min) | false positives at SEEN ≥ 0.430 |
|---|---|---|---|
| all seven (**registered**, σ_wide) | 7 | 0.2614 | **5.0%** |
| E154's five (E144's two left out) | 5 | 0.3075 | **8.1%** |
| E154's four 300 s windows (K_S, K_Q, K_L, K_O) | 4 | 0.340 | **10.3%** |

The two fp32 large rises (K_S, K_O) are in the last two pools too: they may carry the slow stage itself, as E166.md says of the wide pool.
**The thresholds (0.430 and 0.147) stay as registered**, and SL's reading is reported with its registered rate, 5.0%. A reader should
take the rate as about 5-10%, depending on the pool.

## X1 goes beyond RA1.4's explicit permission (a disclosure)

E166.md says RA1.4 allows "an exclusion for an incomplete K_O window". **X1 extends that exclusion, as this lane's choice, to every reading**
(K_S, K_Q, K_L, K_O's window, K_OL's long window) **and to holds that never ran** (for example one skipped at the deadline). It was
registered before any data. The consequence for RK3-a: R4 read literally counts a missing reading as "not inside"; X1 counts it as
excluded. So I = 2, F = 1, X = 1 reads **NOT SCORED** under X1, where R4 read literally would read FAIL. With no exclusion X1 is R4 exactly,
and it never turns a decided outcome into another. The critic asked generically for "an incomplete-window exclusion"; the wording in
E166.md presented X1 as what RA1.4 allows, and that sentence went further than RA1.4.

## G0's readability, in words

§ G0 says the room "cannot be read (fewer than half the samples)". The runner reads the room with E154's reader, unchanged: `run_e166.py`'s
`recent()` needs `0.5 × 120 s × min_rate_hz` = 0.5 × 120 × 5 = **300 lines**, a quarter of the 10 Hz sampler's 1,200 in R0's last 120 s.
X1's coverage condition counts **5 lines a second, 600 lines** in the same anchor window (`score_e166.py`'s `MIN_RATE_HZ`). So a room read
from 300-599 lines passes G0, and X1 then excludes K_S's reading, whose anchor is the same window. That is what the rules as registered and
implemented do; only the words "half the samples" were wrong.
