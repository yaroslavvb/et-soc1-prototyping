## Added here, before any E166 data

### G0, the start gate: R0's room at or under 53.0 °C

- **The rule.** At R0's end the runner reads the room: the 34-sensor mean over R0's last 120 s of live samples (E154's `room` mark). **The
  holds run only if it reads 53.0 °C or less.** Above 53.0 °C, or if it cannot be read (fewer than half the samples), **no hold starts**:
  the pass ends at once (exit 7), the session's closing reads run, and E166 reads **NOT STARTED (the start gate)**: no rule is scored and
  nothing reads as failed.
- **Why.** Above 53.0 °C K_S is a rest (E154's room rule), and RK3-a counts a hold that did not run as not inside, so it could read at most
  3 of 4; above about 53.25 °C K_OL's room rule shortens the long hold, and above about 53.4 °C K_O's window is incomplete (Q140(e)'s table,
  RA1.4). The gate keeps RK3-a and the long hold scorable. With the gate, K_S's period is always set by the room rule (4.37 or 4.97 s) and
  K_OL always runs 25 minutes.
- **A later try.** A session stopped by the gate may be followed by another after the card has rested longer, under this same
  registration, with a fresh R0. Every attempt is reported. **Exactly one measured pass is scored: the first whose R0 passes the gate.** No
  pass is started again after any hold of it has run.

### X1, the incomplete-window rule

A replicate reading (K_S, K_Q, K_L, K_O's window, K_OL's long window) is **complete** when its hold ran and:
- its window spans at least its plan less 30 s: b − t0 ≥ 570 s for K_S, K_Q and K_L; b10 − t0 ≥ 570 s for K_O's window; b − t0 ≥ 1470 s for
  K_OL's long window; and
- the telemetry covers its anchor window and its plateau window with at least half the sampler's 10 Hz lines (5 lines a second, the
  runner's `min_rate_hz`).

An incomplete reading is treated by **why** it is incomplete:
- **Ended by a heat line** (the hold's die line at 72 °C or its board line at 65 W fired, the launch gate held launches out at 70 °C, or the
  session line at 74 °C ended the pass): **it is not excluded.** Every planned in-launch peak is at or under 67.5 °C, 4.5 °C under the hold
  line, so a heat line is itself evidence against the prediction. The reading counts as **not inside** for RK3-a and as **outside** for
  RK3-c (when it is K_S, K_O's window or K_OL's long window), and its band verdict is FAIL.
- **Ended by anything else** (the deadline, a STOP file, the sampler, the clock guard, three failed launches, another user on the card, an
  operator's stop): **it is excluded**, reads **NOT SCORED (incomplete window: <why>)**, and its readings are reported as post hoc.

How the rules read with exclusions (I = readings inside, F = readings scored and not inside, X = excluded, of RK3-a's four):
- **RK3-a:** PASS if I ≥ 3; FAIL if F ≥ 2; otherwise **NOT SCORED (incomplete windows)**. With no exclusion this is the registered rule
  exactly; an exclusion never turns a decided outcome into another.
- **RK3-c:** FAIL if any scored reading with a measured rise of 10 °C or more is outside ±1.5% (or ended by a heat line); PASS if every one
  of the three expected large rises (K_S, K_O's window, K_OL's long window) is complete and inside, and so is every other complete reading
  of 10 °C or more; otherwise NOT SCORED.
- **G_A** needs K_O's window and K_OL's long window complete; **SL** (below) needs K_O's window complete. Otherwise NOT SCORED.
- **The session figure** is over the complete readings, with their number stated.

### SL, the 300-600 s slope decision, beside G_A (from C10's null)

**G_A stays as registered (R6).** Beside it, RA1.5's reported slope becomes a decision with a stated false-positive rate.
- **The statistic s:** arm A's error, its least-squares slope over [t0 + 300 s, b10) of K_OL, in °C per 10 min (exactly
  `corrections_q140e.hold_stats`'s `slope_from_300s_C_per_10min` on K_O's window, the number C10 read on every committed aifoundry3 hold and
  on the frozen scenarios).
- **The null** (`corrections.json` › `C10`, `a4fe976d`, out of sample for the chain): the same slope on E144's two holds and E154's five.
  A normal null with zero mean and the pool's rms about zero, as C10 computed its rates:
  - σ_wide = **0.2614 °C per 10 min**, all seven (E154's two fp32 large rises included: the conservative null);
  - σ_narrow = 0.0900, the five without them (C10's other pool);
  - σ_E97 = 0.1873, E97's two 30-minute holds, in sample for the chain (their G_A at the registered geometry / 1.5), reported only.
- **The decision:**

  | reading | s (°C per 10 min) | what it means |
  |---|---|---|
  | **SLOPE SEEN** | s ≥ **0.430** = 1.6449 σ_wide | a **5.0%** false-positive rate under the wide null (1.1% under E97's, about 0 under the narrow one) |
  | **SLOPE NOT SEEN** | s ≤ **0.147** = 0.577 − 1.6449 σ_wide | 0.577 is the smallest of the three frozen extra-stage scenarios' slopes on K_O's window (0.577 / 0.684 / 0.692 at τ = 600 / 1200 / 2400 s): a slow stage of that size reads NOT SEEN at most 5.0% of the time |
  | INCONCLUSIVE | between | |

- **Its power, under σ_wide** (printed by [`slope_rule_e166.py`](slope_rule_e166.py) into [`slope_rule_e166.json`](slope_rule_e166.json)):
  an extra stage at τ = 600 / 1200 / 2400 s reads SEEN 71 / 83 / 84% of the time; the gain scenario (D6, slope 0.101) 10%, and NOT SEEN 57%;
  the chain as frozen reads NOT SEEN 71%, INCONCLUSIVE 24%, SEEN 5%.
- **On the committed holds, post hoc:** E154's K_S (+0.513) would read SEEN, its K_O (+0.418) INCONCLUSIVE, and the other five NOT SEEN.
- **The confound stays:** with no inlet thermometer, a room that warms during K_OL reads as a slow stage. SEEN means "a slow stage or a
  room drift"; NOT SEEN excludes a stage of the scenarios' size at 5%.
- The thresholds are computed from the committed `corrections.json` by `slope_rule_e166.py`, which refuses any other copy (sha256
  `de5bba20…`). Nothing was tuned: the form (one-sided 5%, the wide pool, the smallest frozen stage) was chosen before the numbers were
  printed, and E166 has no data.
