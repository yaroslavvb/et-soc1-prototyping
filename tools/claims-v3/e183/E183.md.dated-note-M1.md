## M1-4. What is read, and how (registered here, before any datum; [`arm_m1_read_e183.py`](arm_m1_read_e183.py) is the arm's reader)

- **Reported, never a rule.** The arms are read after E183's data are collected and scored. No E183 rule, threshold, verdict or registered
  forecast depends on them (E183.md section 8). Their readings use words of their own, never the scorer's verdict words.
- **The measured side is E183's own:** step 11's collection writes `reduced-e183.json` (by `reduce_e183_v2.py`) and `scored-e183.json` (by
  `score_e183.py`) into the data directory. The reader takes the reduction, forms each train's estimate and 95% interval with
  `e183_stats.train_estimate` exactly as the scorer does, and b, c, E183-X's statistic and E183-J's two differences with `e183_stats`; E183-MECH's
  word is `e183_stats.mech_of` on E183-B's and E183-C's NULL words. Given the scorer's file too, it refuses (exit 3) unless every train, b, c and
  the MECH word equal the scorer's.
- **The model's side** is [`m1-october-forecast-model-e183.json`](m1-october-forecast-model-e183.json), refused (exit 2) unless its sha256 is the
  one the reader pins: for each version, each train's central and band at aifoundry3; its b, c, E183-X and E183-J figures made by the same
  formulas over the same trains the measured side used (b from T1 alone over 7 when T3 is not scored, as `e183_stats` takes it); its word.
- **The words:** the model's central lies **inside, above or below** the measured 95% interval; its sign is **the estimate's or the other** (no
  sign to compare when either is 0); the measured estimate lies **inside, above or below the model's band**; the model's word is **E183-MECH's
  word, or a different one**. A train E183 reads NOT SCORED, or one that did not run (the SHORT plan has no T5 or T6), is not read, and neither
  is any quantity made from it.
- **Not read:** card 1's figures; the kinds' levels.
- **To run it**, after step 11's collection and before the data commit, from the lab's top:
  `/usr/bin/python3 -B tools/claims-v3/e183/arm_m1_read_e183.py --reduced <data dir>/reduced-e183.json --scored <data dir>/scored-e183.json --out <data dir>/arm-m1-e183`
  (it writes `arm-m1-e183.json` and `arm-m1-e183.txt`). A refusal does not block the data commit; the data README records it.
- **What E183 can tell apart** (arithmetic on the frozen figures and E183.md section 6's half-widths, made here): v3 and v4 stand apart by
  T1 0.0405, T2 0.0414, T3 0.0444, T4 0.0405, T5 0.0010, T6 0.0428 W on the six trains, and by 0.0061 W a bit on b and 0.0059 on c. A price train's planned half-width is 0.0098 W at BASE
  (0.0196 DOUBLED), T5's 0.0050 (0.0100), and b's and c's 0.0010 W a bit (0.0020), E183.md section 6's. So E183's trains separate the two versions on T1, T2, T3,
  T4 and T6, and on b and c, at either noise, and not on his pair (T5).
- **The words each world gives** (arithmetic, with E183.md section 5.2's band of +-0.004 W a bit): were v3's trains the card's, b's interval at
