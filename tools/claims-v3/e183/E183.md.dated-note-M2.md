- **The words, for each version and each train scored:**

  2. the measured estimate lies **inside** the version's own band, as listed, when band low <= estimate <= band high, else **above** or **below**;
  3. the sign: **the estimate's or the other** (no sign to compare when either is 0).
- **The errors:** the version's centre less the estimate, in W, for each train read; over the trains read, the count, the count inside
