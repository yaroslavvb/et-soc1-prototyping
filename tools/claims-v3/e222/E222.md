## 8. What E222 cannot say (stated before the data)

- **How MM1-v3.3 does on never-run points.** E222 picks MM1-v3.3's terms; their composition has not met a day chosen without it: MM1-v3.3's own blind day follows on never-run points (SEL). Every candidate's family here was drawn where that candidate differs most from MM1-v3.2 (L3b by |Delta|, Y3 by posimb's separation, G3 by AF4's smallest predicted excess), which favours a decision, not a representative error.
- **The candidates were designed on their leads.** Each form was chosen after looking at its lead (their land's disclosures); E222's points are new shapes and masks on a known card (every candidate was fitted with aifoundry3 card 0's committed data among the rest), not a held-out card.
- **posimb's and L3S's head-to-heads are weak by construction** (reading 5); a NOT DECIDED leaves them out of MM1-v3.3 without saying they are wrong. **posimb's physical reading is not established** (its verifier); Y3 tests the term's numbers at new masks, not its mechanism.
- **G3 is one regime** (reading 2): AF4's zero-re-fetch regime at NB 5-6; no L2-resident gemmLnc point qualified; T's G2 (its L3-edge clause) sits at one NB (6). **AF4 is energy only**: its time is MM1-v3.2's.
- **The no-harm rules can fail only where a term acts** (reading 4); the composition's interaction is checked by construction, not by its own rule.
- **The cross-card rule waits for E220** and reads two cards' errors on the same configurations; the twins' errors here and there are both drawn on the day, so a LARGER says the errors differ, not why. Family R is four points.
- **One session, one or two runs a point** (pass 2 for L3b, Y3, T-L3, T-Y2 and R-Y; the G block once and G3 twice); no repeat across days but R and the tie.
- **The transistor switches are estimated, not measured**: no card counts switches; MM1's switch outputs are never scored. **No epilogue**; **a small GEMM's joules are a warm, repeated GEMM's**; **the meter's absolute scale**: one fixed gain per board, never set beside an outside meter. **One kernel** (rgm136), random fp32 data only.
- **Correlated errors** (a family's shared miss, the card's level) raise the sign tests' false-winner rates above the simulated ones; near-duplicate points (T-G2's one NB, G3's one regime, Y3's one mask kind) make a family's test closer to a test of its few configurations than of its point count.
