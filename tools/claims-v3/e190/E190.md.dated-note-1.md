2026) by its date only. It does not cite the lab's own public A100 measurement: **E135** (`docs/reports/data/2026-11-02-e135-a100-kinds/`,

its seconds and its joules a GEMM frozen before any datum. When E190 has data, the comparison with E135 is drawn from E135's committed
files and E190's, each labelled by its kernel (rgm136's vendor-pattern GEMM; cuBLAS), and no chip-level contrast is drawn from a gap's
size alone (C1's rule). The A100 report's own figures stay off every public file: this note quotes none of them, and none of E135's

## 5. The process time, with the calibration launch (the verifier's nit 8; no change)

E190.md 2 and `desk/timing_e136.py` estimate an fp32 process with `--seconds 6 --budget 7.5` at about 7.9 s at the slow end, "about 2 s
inside the timeout". The estimate leaves out the host's calibration launch, one GEMM: about 8.33 s at the slow end, and the worst case is
bounded by the budget at about 9.0 s, **about 1 s inside `timeout 10`, not 2 s**. Arm A's plan comes to about 33.5 card-minutes, not 33.2,
inside the 35-minute rule and the 40-minute cap (the verifier's desk arithmetic, from the registration's own numbers). **No change**: the
card lane reads P1 fp32's first process time (its `proc-00` record's
`t1_ms - t0_ms`) on the day and reports it; a process cut by `timeout 10` is flagged and loses its check (E190.md 7), never a device
held past 10 s.
