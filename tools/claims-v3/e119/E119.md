# E119: what a clock gap buys in each of E107's arms, under aifoundry2's own governor (Q134)

Sun 1 Nov 2026 (week 44), about 19:45 PST. Registered before the claim and the first launch. It answers part of the kinds page's
[section 11, item 1, "Where a large GEMM falls"](https://spacesheep.dev/@yaroslavvb/horace-kinds-of-numbers#11-what-is-still-open), as it reads at
version `90e02fa93e9c`: *"Still open: … what a clock gap buys in each of E107's arms, on a governed card"*. It is the speed half that
[E107](../../../docs/reports/data/2026-11-01-e107-gemm-placement/README.md) left: E107 ran its arms at aifoundry3's pinned 600 MHz, so it measured
energy and time per op there but no clock gap. The frame is [E105](../../../docs/reports/data/2026-11-01-e105-gemm-layout/README.md) (the two ends on
aifoundry2: ×1.333 of clock buys ×1.324 in a repeated block whose tiles fit, ×1.064 in the cold 16-block walk) and
[E101](../../../docs/reports/data/2026-11-01-e101-real-matmul/README.md) (its registered 97 W stop fired on a hot start).

## The question

E107 found that reuse is the variable: passing over each of 16 fresh blocks R times divides the walk's extra time per op by R, at 600 MHz. Does the
same law hold at 800 MHz, and so what does a third more clock buy at each rung of the ladder, and in the 8-block walk whose operands sit in the L3? If
the cold walk's extra time is a wait outside the minion clock (E105: M about 1.97 µs), the clock should buy less the less a block is reused, from
×1.32 at the warm end to ×1.06 at the cold one. For the walk the L3 serves, nothing says yet whether the L3's service follows the minion clock.

voltage-register reads (two per `ettelem config`, eleven per sample, reads with no write, in every run since September) stay as they are: whether "no

## The power limit

At 800 MHz a cool start draws its power before the governor answers. **Each cell's estimate is from its own kind's S on a pinned card**, E105's
corrected rule (E105's C5): c = c_zeros + (S − S(resident zeros)) ÷ 1.01865 from E107's `e107.json`, and p800 = B(800) + s(800) × c, E101's relation.
For a walk this over-reads, because its rate gains less than the clock. E105's run_e105.py decides, unchanged: **a cell runs only if its estimate is at or
below 88 W; after any run whose board reading reached 93 W, no cell with an estimate at or above that run's goes on**; 97 W on three samples and 85 °C
end the session (run_hpl.py). The highest planned estimate is 84.1 W (`c16r8` fp32 random). **Not planned:** random fp32 and fp16 on `c1` (92.5 and
88.6 W; E105 measured both cells on this card) and on `c16r32` (91.7 W; and 86.5 W in fp16, the one under the limit but closest to it, with E105's fp16
random reading 2.6 W over its estimate at its peak), and `c1`'s int8 full (E105's cell).

## Frozen predictions

`predictions-e119.json`, printed in `predictions-e119.txt`, from committed inputs only (`make_predictions_e119.py`). **M1:** each end's T(f) = C/f + M,
M from E105's aifoundry2 fit of the same layout (fp16's and int8's cold end take fp32's M, the wait outside the clock) and C set so that T(600) is E107's
own, measured with this binary; the ladder follows E107's law at each clock, T(R, f) = k T_c1(f) + (T_c16(f) − k T_c1(f)) ÷ R; `c8s` keeps its extra time
over `c1` at 600 MHz as a wait outside the clock. **M2** (for `c8s` only): that extra time follows the clock. X = T(600) ÷ T(800):

| X | `c1` | `c16r32` | `c16r8` | `c16r4` | `c16r2` | `c16` | `c8s` M1 | `c8s` M2 |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| fp32 | 1.324 | 1.297 | 1.236 | 1.182 | 1.121 | 1.064 | 1.182 | 1.328 |
| fp16 | 1.325 | 1.297 | 1.236 | 1.182 | 1.121 | 1.064 | 1.182 | 1.328 |
| int8 | 1.320 | 1.274 | 1.194 | 1.142 | 1.096 | 1.063 | 1.089 | 1.329 |

Zeros ÷ random, sustained rate (clock), frozen with E101's governor relation on the walk-scaled switching term: fp32 `c16r8` ×1.234 (×1.331), `c16r4`
×1.167 (×1.305), `c16r2` ×1.073 (×1.203), `c8s` ×1.168 (×1.308), `c16` ×1.017 (×1.086). In bursts: ×1.088, ×1.036, ×1.001, ×1.038, ×1.000. int8's
random draws too little to be held back much: sustained ×1.055 on `c16r8`, ×1.017 on `c16r4`, ×1.000 on `c8s`. The relation's clocks ran 25-69 MHz low
in E105, so the clocks are labelled predictions, scored by one rule (R5) and an rms.

## Rules, which can fail

The full text and bars are in `predictions-e119.json`; `selftest_e119.py` shows each failing on made-up rows (`selftest.txt`).

- **R0, the ties:** zeros T at 800 MHz of `c1` within 2% of E105's aifoundry2 `gemmc1` in fp32 and fp16, 3% in int8, and `c16` fp32 within 3% of
  E105's `gemmc16`; every check of C exact or approx. A FAIL labels every other verdict.
- **R1, reuse at 800 MHz:** per format, E107's R3 at 800 MHz: T800(R) = Tinf + D ÷ R over the five rungs, every rung within 3% of the fit and Tinf
  within 5% of T800(`c1`): REUSE IS THE VARIABLE AT 800 MHz; NOT THE VARIABLE if `c16r32` keeps half the cold excess; else PARTIAL.
- **R2, what ×1.333 of clock buys:** X per arm and format from this session's launches alone (every process's first launch runs at the idle card's
  600 MHz: T600 over launches at 590-610 MHz on the kernel's own clock, T800 at or above 790 MHz, kinds pooled; the fit T = C/f + M if too few). PASS per
  arm within 0.03 of M1; and the fp32 order `c1` > `c16r32` > `c16r8` > `c16r4` > `c16r2` > `c16`.
- **R3, the walk the L3 serves:** `c8s`'s X as a share of the way from M1 to M2: OUTSIDE THE CLOCK at ≤ 0.25, FOLLOWS THE CLOCK at ≥ 0.75, else PARTIAL
  with the share. fp32 primary; int8 and fp16 reported the same way.
- **R4, the kind:** random's T at 800 MHz within 1% of zeros' on every arm.
- **R5, the governed gap, sustained:** zeros ÷ random sustained rate within 0.05 of the frozen gap, per cell with both runs; and in fp32 `c16r8` >
  `c16r4` > `c16r2`.
- **R6, the estimate over-reads a walk:** every admitted random cell's measured burst power at 800 MHz at or below its estimate + 1 W.

The reduction is `reduce_e119.py` (E105's per-run code, copied with E119's labels; E105's fit; E107's `fit_inv`).

## What this does not do

No energy per op: a governed card has no fixed clock, so E107's pinned-card S stays the energy. No vendor binary. No large GEMM: these are E107's
arms. One card, one session, one run of each cell (two of two fp32 zeros bursts); the unit of replication is the run, and no run is a replicate of
another within it. `c4s`, `c8` and `c16s` do not run.
