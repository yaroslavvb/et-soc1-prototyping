## The two questions

**(a) Does E91 replicate E86 when it scores?** E91 (registration `eb72ce5d`, amendment A1 `7f007f53`) ran Q130(n)'s nine
real GPT-2 small tiles on card 1 on 31 Oct and scored nothing: E78's zeros-line gate set its first pass aside, three kept
passes were below its registered minimum of four, and A1's spare pass was refused by **1 s** because the lane's helper
floored A1's 6.75-minute budget to 6 whole minutes (E91 README, § Departures). **(a) runs E91's registration again,
unchanged, on new passes**, with the budget kept in exact seconds and a fifth pass registered as a spare.

**(b) Do E95's thirty real tiles cost on a second card what they cost on aifoundry3?** E95 (registration `89bd34cb`, data
`ab23b1aa`) measured Q132(r)'s thirty real2 tiles (ReLU tiles with exact zeros, more GPT-2 layers, BERT, ViT-B/16, an fp16 and
a bf16 forward pass, int8 calibrations, T5's bf16-grid weights, ViT's −0.0 layer) and three of E86's tiles on aifoundry3
card 0: of 13 frozen claims 9 PASS, 1 FAIL, 2 INCONCLUSIVE, 1 UNDER-RESOLVED. **(b) runs E95's pass, unchanged, on card 1**,
scores E95's frozen claims C1–C12 on the card-1 view of Q132(r)'s frozen file (every watt figure divided by E82's card
scale), and adds three cross-card rules against E95's committed reductions.

Both halves run in one session on one card, so the six E78 anchors and E86's three tiles that both passes carry give a
same-session tie between them.

cell is a draw; on a conv layer the draw matters, real2 README departure 6). The view leaves out the card-scale spread. Q130(n)'s
and Q132(r)'s predictors are post-hoc fits, not mechanisms, and int8's bus counts are provisional. Removing dips is this card's
registered choice; the dips-kept arm shows what it moves. (a) and (b) share a session: a session effect moves both, and the tie
to E82 is the reading for it. Four passes, or three, are small samples (t = 3.18 at four, 4.30 at three). A D says what a tile
costs, not which stage spends it. E112 is one card at one clock: speed under a governor is E96's.

## Card budget

**At most 60 card-minutes, from the claim post to its release edit (3,600 s)**, the smoke, the heating gates and the config
reads included. `queue_e112.sh` runs the registered list `a:11210 a:11211 a:11212 a:11213 b:11250 b:11251 b:11252 a:11214
b:11253` and starts an entry only if **now + projected ≤ claim + 3,600 − 120 s** (the reserve covers the session-end read and the
release edit) and now + projected ≤ 18:45 PST, where projected is the longest pass of that kind measured so far in this queue,
else its first estimate (a 390 s, E91's; b 560 s, E95's longest pass on aifoundry3, 537 s, plus a margin). Exact integer seconds, no
rounding. An entry that does not fit is skipped and the next one tried, so the list's order is the priority: (a)'s four base
passes, then (b)'s three, then (a)'s spare (E91's minimum is four kept passes, and on card 1 the first pass of two of the eight experiments
since E88 was set aside, against one of their 38 later passes), then (b)'s. A pass in flight is never cut. Expected: (a) about 365 s a pass with
its 15 s gap (E91: 331–361 s), (b) about 490 s (E95: 507–537 s on aifoundry3, where E86's pass took 361–404 s against E91's 331–361
s on card 1), so the base list ends near claim + 3,000 s, and the spare runs only if (b)'s passes take under about 500 s. No
session starts after 18:00 PST; every run ends by 19:00. The claim is one line, posted for 1.5 h (60 min plus a margin, rounded
up to the half hour): `<ai> **Claude says:** using aifoundry1 card 1 for 1.5 h, until ~HH:MM PST [doc](<https://spacesheep.dev/@yaroslavvb/horace-kinds-of-numbers>)`,
edited to append ` — released` at the release.
