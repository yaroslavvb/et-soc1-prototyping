# E126: what a clock gap buys in E107's last three arms, and E119's sustained pairs again, under aifoundry2's own governor (Q136)

Mon 2 Nov 2026 (week 45), written from 07:00 PST. Registered before the claim and the first launch. It answers part of the kinds page's
[section 11, item 1, "Where a large GEMM falls"](https://spacesheep.dev/@yaroslavvb/horace-kinds-of-numbers#11-what-is-still-open), as it reads at
version `fbb682f9393b`: *"Still open: a large GEMM itself, which needs its own reuse per L2-sized block and its footprint, C included; the clock gap in
E107's other three arms (c4s, c8 and c16s; by E107's times c4s would likely follow the L3-served walk and the other two are DRAM walks, an expectation,
not measured); and the vendor's own binary, which nobody here has timed."* Q134's final critic ranked it first and asked for exactly this run: E119's
harness, unchanged, on `c4s`, `c8` and `c16s`, plus a second session of E119's four sustained random/zeros pairs as the replicate E119 lacked (one session,
one run a cell). It builds on [E119](../../../docs/reports/data/2026-11-01-e119-gemm-speed/README.md) (the harness, the binary, the card, the two ends'
fits this design freezes from) and [E107](../../../docs/reports/data/2026-11-01-e107-gemm-placement/README.md) (the arms, their T at 600 MHz, their
shares of the cold excess and their power).

## The question

E119 found what ×1.333 of clock buys in seven of E107's ten arms on this card: ×1.32 down to ×1.065 by reuse, and ×1.13 in the 8-block walk the L3
serves, the same in every format, because that walk's wait does not follow the minion clock. Three arms were not run. Does `c4s` (4 blocks, C shared, 14
MB with C) buy what `c8s` bought, as its equal T at 600 MHz says it should? In the two walks past the L3, `c8` (8 blocks, C per block, 40 MB) and `c16s`
(16 blocks, C shared, 50 MB), is the part of the cold walk they do not pay (5% and 14% of its excess) a wait outside the clock or minion cycles? And do
E119's governed clocks and sustained gaps come back in a second session?

but the stock telemetry's voltage-register reads, which the owner's rule of 2 November 2026 allows. Every command the run sends to the card is listed
and classified below; none writes.

## The power limit and the margin

Each cell's admission estimate is E119's rule on its own kind's S on E107's pinned card, p800 = B(800) + s(800) × c, c = c_zeros + (S − S(resident
zeros)) ÷ 1.01865 (E105's C5), through E119's own code. **E119's admission limit (88 W at the estimate) and lock (93 W) are unchanged. The session's own
stops are tightened, as Q136's rule asks ("inside the registered stops with margin")**: one die reading, mean or hottest sensor, at or above **80 °C**
(E119: 85 °C), or the board above **93 W on three samples** in a row (E119: 97 W), ends the session. The critic's guard is met: E119's estimate bounds a
burst's median, not a sustained run's single readings (7 of 14 random sustained runs read 0.33-2.45 W over it near the trip), so the stop now sits at
E119's lock. **The highest planned estimate is E119's own `c16r8` fp32 random, 84.1 W; the new arms' highest is `c4s` fp32 random, 76.0 W.** E119's
highest single reading, 85.71 W in that cell's sustained run, and its die's 69 °C (the SP's running maximum; 67 °C the highest instantaneous) sit 7 W
and 11 °C inside these stops. No cell is planned that E119 did not run on this card or that reads more than E119's arms: the new arms' random draws
less than `c8s`'s (E107: 23.09, 15.61 and 15.46 W against 24.42 W at 600 MHz). On the over-power path: Q134(a) found no over-power interrupt on aifoundry2
since 26 Oct through single readings of 101.15 W (an alarm on the slow average is untested; the governor holds that average near the 65 W TDP); nothing here drives the card towards a
PMIC over-power or over-temperature reset, and nothing raises the TDP or the threshold.

| `c4s`, E119's M1 / M2 | 1.182 / 1.328 | 1.182 / 1.328 | 1.089 / 1.329 | for R1's share only |
| `c8`, S / W | 1.068 / 1.058 | 1.067 / 1.057 | 1.076 / 1.056 | the share model / the cold walk's wait; fp32 and fp16 0.010 apart: UNRESOLVED |
| `c16s`, S / W | 1.077 / 1.043 | 1.075 / 1.042 | 1.077 / 1.048 | resolved in every format |
| `c16` (E119, the DRAM walk's anchor) | 1.065 | 1.064 | 1.072 | |

S, THE SHARE: the arm is E119's cold walk scaled by E107's share at every clock, T(f) = T_c1(f) + φ (T_c16(f) − T_c1(f)), with E119's fitted ends on
this card. W, THE COLD WALK'S WAIT: the arm keeps the cold walk's whole wait outside the clock (M = 1.957 µs fp32) and only its clock-following part
shrinks. Both give the same T(600). **Expected:** S wherever the two differ by 0.015 or more (what an arm does not pay of the cold walk is DRAM service, a
wait). **The replicate's predictions are E119's measurements** (fp32, sustained): random 663.7 / 636.7 / 603.6 / 695.4 MHz and zeros 788.7 / 795.4 /
795.9 / 794.4 MHz on `c8s` / `c16r4` / `c16r8` / `c16r2`; gaps ×1.079 / 1.148 / 1.229 / 1.062; X 1.126 / 1.185 / 1.239 / 1.123.

## Rules, which can fail

The full text and bars are in `predictions-e126.json`; `selftest_e126.py` shows each failing on made-up rows and the preflight refusing (`selftest.txt`).
X is E119's `clock_x`, imported unchanged (launch means at 590-610 and ≥ 790 MHz, at least 2 and 5 launches, else the fit T = C/f + M).

- **R0, the ties:** every check of C exact or approx; each new arm's T at 600 MHz within 1.5% of E107's; each replicate arm's fp32 zeros T at ≥ 790 MHz
  within 1% of E119's. A FAIL labels every other verdict.
- **R1, `c4s`:** LIKE THE L3 WALK if |X − X(`c8s`, E119)| ≤ 0.02, per format (fp32 primary), else NOT LIKE IT; and E119's R3 share on M1→M2: OUTSIDE
  THE CLOCK at ≤ 0.25, FOLLOWS at ≥ 0.75, else PARTIAL. Frozen: LIKE THE L3 WALK, OUTSIDE THE CLOCK, all three formats.
- **R2, `c8` and `c16s`:** (a) A DRAM WALK if |X − X(`c16`, E119)| ≤ 0.03; (b) h = (X − X_W) ÷ (X_S − X_W) where the models differ by ≥ 0.015: THE SHARE
  at h ≥ 0.75, THE COLD WALK'S WAIT at h ≤ 0.25, else BETWEEN; UNRESOLVED otherwise. Frozen: A DRAM WALK everywhere; THE SHARE wherever resolved.
- **R3, the replicate's clocks:** per arm, random's sustained clock within 30 MHz of E119's and zeros' within 15 MHz: REPLICATES.
- **R4, the replicate's gaps:** per arm, zeros ÷ random sustained rate within 0.05 of E119's; the order `c16r8` > `c16r4` > `c16r2` again; and `c8s`'s
  gap at or below 1.118 (E119's R5 failure on the L3-served walk repeats).
- **R5, the replicate's X:** per arm, fp32, within 0.01 of E119's.
- **R6, the new arms sustained (fp32):** `c4s` random within 30 MHz of E119's `c8s` (663.7 MHz) and its gap within 0.05 of ×1.079; `c8` and `c16s`
  random at or above 770 MHz and the gap at or below 1.03.
- **R7, the kind:** every arm's random T at ≥ 790 MHz within 1% of its zeros' (E119's R4).
- **R8, the estimate:** every admitted random cell's burst power at 800 MHz (`p800_w`) at or below its estimate + 1 W (E119's R6).

The reduction is `reduce_e126.py` (E119's per-run loop, copied with E126's labels; E119's `clock_x`). The safety record (highest board and die
readings, with their runs) is written beside the rules and decides nothing.

## What this does not do

No energy per op (a governed card has no fixed clock); no large GEMM, no vendor binary. One card; the replicate is a second session of E119's on a later
day, the unit of replication; within this session each cell runs once (two fp32 zeros bursts on `c4s` and `c16s` in the tail). No inlet temperature:
the replicate's clocks at the trip carry whatever the room did between the two sessions.
