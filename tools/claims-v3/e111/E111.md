## Design

**Card, and nothing set.** aifoundry2 card 0, release 1.4.0 / BL2 0.22.0 (E69's reading of 27 Oct), under its own governor, TDP 65 W, threshold
65 °C as read. **No governor move, no TDP, threshold or clock set, no reset of any kind, no PMIC command other than the camera runner's
statistics reset.** `ettelem config` (read only, the lock non-blocking, `timeout 10`) is read at the start and end of the session and of every step
and after every run. Binaries: the box's `build/sparsity_t2` and `build/ettelem-hpl`, E80's, E87's and E98's. Tiles: Q130(s)'s, checked against
`kinds.json`.

**One card session, four steps, in a detached queue** ([`queue_e111.py`](queue_e111.py); [`run_e111.sh`](run_e111.sh) on the box). Planned
card time 116.2 min of the 120 allowed:

*Why the holds ride on the probes.* 120 card-minutes do not hold two 30 min holds from rest, their rests and two full probes. E98's probe ends in a
5.5 min hold of int8 neg_ones, so extending it gives the int8 steady hold and E98's hold reading in one, and tells how far E98's 5.5 min holds were
from steady. So **neither hold starts from rest**: H-I8 starts from E98's 57 °C gate after the nine runs, and H-F32 from the end of E98's hold. Arrival
shares from rest (E97-B6) are not measured. Each hold is 190 processes of 8 s, about 8.37 s apart in E98 (326.2 s for 39), so **26.5 min**; with the
nine runs before them the card has been loaded about 40 and 45 min when each plateau is read.

*Why int8 neg_ones.* It is E98's hold kind, so the extension answers the drift's question too, and it stays under the 65 °C trip at 800 MHz: on
aifoundry2's near-steady coefficient and E98's in-hold W per °C, its plateau is predicted at about 61–62 °C at a 47 °C rest die. int8 random
(E97's H1 kind) sits at the trip on this card, so its hold would be the governor's, not a constant clock's.

*Why fp32 randn reaches a plateau here.* On aifoundry2 the governor's thermal test holds fp32 randn at the 600 MHz floor (E98's 76 s runs: 600–605
MHz, 54 W, 66 °C), so its switching power is fixed and the die settles where the cooling puts it: predicted about 73 °C at 57 W at a 47 °C rest die
(the 9 Oct heavy run: 75.0 °C at 58.7 W after 600 s, idle 50.8 °C). The stops are E98's: 85 °C on the die mean or the hottest sensor (the lab's
85–90 °C stop for long full-load runs, AGENT.md §4), 97 W on three samples.

**The queue** runs detached and needs no ssh. Its rules, frozen:

- **The rest rule** (E98's): before W1, W2 and END, no row of the box's own 5 s history with a die over 55 °C, a board over 26 W or the card held on
  two rows running, over the step's rest (15, 20, 15 min), and the previous step ended at least that long ago.
- **Nobody else.** The card lock free and **nobody else logged in** (`who`), or it polls every 20 s; the runners end a session at once (exit 3) if
  another user logs in, as E98's P2 was ended. *At 11:11 PST, while this was written, another lab user had a login on aifoundry2 (since 10:12 PST).
  The queue waits for it; a step that cannot start in time is skipped and reported.* (Amended by A1, below: a login idle for 120 minutes or more
  does not block.)
- **Times.** The smoke not before 14:05 PST; W1's rest reading not before **14:30 PST** (the warm afternoon: on Q132(g)'s nine-day hour means the
  rest die stands at 47.9 °C at 14:00 PDT and 49.5 °C at 16:00 PDT, so at 15:30 PDT, 14:30 PST by the sun, within about 0.5 °C of its daily
  plateau), W1 not after 16:30 PST, **no step after 18:00 PST**, a hard stop at **18:55 PST** (stop-file at −120 s, SIGTERM at −30 s, never a device
  process signalled). A step that cannot end by the stop is skipped. On plan: SMOKE about 14:05, W1 14:30–15:15, W2 15:35–16:25, END 16:40,
  CAM 16:43–17:05.
- **The card budget**: no step starts if the steps' card minutes so far plus its plan pass 120 minus the smoke's. CAM is the step it drops first.
- **Exits.** 0 next; 3 next, flagged; 1 next once, a second halts; **4 (a safety stop) runs END only, then stops**; 2 and 5 halt.
- **CAM** also needs the camera's `/health` to read ok and streaming. Its sampler resets the SP's statistics once a second (`--reset-ms 1000`), as
  E63, E73 and E85's runner always has: **allowed by the owner's ruling of 31 October 2026** (AGENT.md §5); W1, W2 and END sample without it, as E98
  did. After CAM the queue runs the registered camera reduction (E85's and E73's chain: `reduce_hm.py`, `fpa_trace.py`, `lockin_hlf.py`,
  `dips_hlc.py`) at nice 10.
- **Beside the steps**: `sensors -j`, `uptime` and `ettelem config` at both ends of every step; the camera phone's `/health` once a minute
  (`batteryTempC`, a descriptive room reading only: the phone charges); every box's 5 s history collected at the end, numbers only.

## The readings

**Part A, the steady state** (`reduce_e111.py` part A). For each hold, over its work samples:

- **The plateau** is the last 300 s; **the previous window** the 300 s before it. Die (the firmware's whole-degree 34-sensor mean), board W,
  clock, the share at the hold's clock, and the **fine W**: the mean over samples at the hold's clock with each idle-reset dip and the 3 s after it
  left out (E98's rule), existing with at least 70% of the window left. At a fixed clock and fixed data, board W moves only with temperature, so the
  fine W is a thermometer with 0.01 W steps (E98).
- **The idle** before and after: the last 60 s of the REST block (10 Hz) before the hold's step and of the next step (W2's REST for H-I8, END's for
  H-F32), die and W.
- **c = (plateau die − idle die) / (plateau W − idle W)**, per board W, open loop: the leakage's climb is in the watts, as in E97's "per board W",
  the lab model's 1.47 and the die line's 0.4573. Its **range** runs over every combination of {idle before, idle after, their mean} with {the
  plateau, the previous window}: a range, not an interval.
- **Beside, labelled:** an exponential's asymptote fitted to the fine W after the hold's first 120 s (τ on a grid, 60–5000 s), the die it implies at
  k = 0.442 W/°C, and c there; the die's own slope.
- **The inlet proxies** (E111-A4, beside, never a verdict). In three 300 s windows (ending at the idle-before REST's end; the plateau; ending at the
  idle-after REST's end), rows with load under 2: aifoundry1's and aifoundry3's NIC and NVMe, every free card's idle die (not held, W under its idle
  bar; either card held blanks aifoundry1's), aifoundry2's own NIC and NVMe in the idle windows only. Each proxy's move from its idle mean to the
  plateau, in °C of aifoundry2 rest die by E98's nine-day relations (a1 NIC 0.92, a1 NVMe 0.91, a1 c0 die 0.89, a1 c1 die 1.10, a3 NIC 0.903), and
  c with the rise corrected by it. The phone's battery reading in the same windows. The proxies are coarse and self-heated by their own hosts; other
  lanes may hold aifoundry1's and aifoundry3's cards, so holes are expected.

**Part B, the drift** (`reduce_e111.py` part B). E98's own reading of a probe, `reduce_e98.read_probe`, imported unchanged, on E98's committed probes
(P1, P2, P3, P6, P7, with E98's history) and on W1 and W2 (with E111's), W1's hold read on its first 39 processes as E98 reads a hold. E98's
scoring is copied into `score98()` verbatim and **checked against E98's committed `results.json` on E98's probes alone before anything is scored**
(it reproduces R0–S4 and every arm key for key; the scorer refuses if not).

**Part C, the camera** (`reduce_e111.py` part C), from `lockin_hlf.py`'s `lockin.json` for block E111-F05: the ROI's and the board's fundamental
with their lap jackknife, H(f₀) at 0.005 Hz and the odd harmonics 0.015, 0.025, 0.035, 0.045 Hz out of the same fit (E73's route). The lag is the
phase of H, which the camera's drifting gain cannot touch.

## Decision rules (frozen; `reduce_e111.py` implements them)

**Part A, each hold.**

- **E111-A1, steady?** The OLS slope of 30 s bins of the fine W over the last 600 s, in °C/min at k = 0.442 W/°C (E64's, frozen): **PASS** if
  |slope| ≤ 0.02 °C/min (E97-B1's bar), else **FAIL**. Without a fine W (the clock not held at the hold's clock in 70% of the window) the die's own
  30 s bins stand in, and the verdict says so.
- **E111-A2, the coefficient**, c's range against each band: **PASS** if it lies inside, **FAIL** if it lies wholly outside, **INCONCLUSIVE**
  otherwise. Bands: aifoundry2's near-steady readings [0.62, 0.82]; the lab's September model [1.25, 1.70]; E97's aifoundry3 plateau [0.86, 0.95];
  the 76 s line [0.40, 0.52].
- **E111-A3, linear in power**: c(H-F32) / c(H-I8), its range from the two ranges' extremes, against [0.90, 1.11]: PASS inside, FAIL wholly
  outside, INCONCLUSIVE otherwise.
- A hold that did not run all 190 processes is read on what it ran and marked; a hold the governor did not leave at its clock (share under 0.90 in the
  plateau) is marked "clock not held", and its c is the governor's operating point, not a constant clock's.

**Part B, the drift**, the probe as the unit, E98's frozen numbers:

- **E111-B0, the gate**: E98-R0's gate on the combined probes (at least 4 counted, the rest-die range at least 0.75 °C); failing it, every slope rule
  reads UNDER-RESOLVED.
- **E111-B1** = E98-R1 on the combined probes: the 76 s line against the rest die, against 0 and 0.25 (E98's partition: SUPPORTS, INCONCLUSIVE,
  BELOW_TARGET, POSITIVE_BELOW_TARGET, ABOVE_TARGET, OPPOSITE).
- **E111-B2** = E98-R2 on the combined probes: the hold's die against the rest die, against the band 1.00–1.25 and the floor 0.25. **E111-BV** =
  E98-V of B1 and B2.
- **E111-B3, does P1's lead repeat?** Each warm probe against the mean of P3, P6 and P7: its rest-die lead dx (W-derived, E98's), its hold-die lead
  and its line lead, each over dx; a warm probe has dx ≥ 0.5 °C. Over the warm probes, the mean ratio with a 95% normal interval from E98's frozen
  per-probe scatter (hold 0.375 °C, line 0.089 °C; one probe against a mean of three). The hold on E98-R2's partition, the line on E98-R1's; whether
  P1's own 2.25 and 0.52 lie inside is printed beside. **No warm probe: UNDER-RESOLVED.**
- **E111-B4** = E98-S4 on the combined probes: fp16 ones at the trip, MHz and W against the rest die.

**Part C, the camera**, block E111-F05, five analysed laps:

- **E111-C1, detected**: the ROI's fundamental ≥ 3 SE and the board's ≥ 5 SE. The sign-flip p is printed with its floor, 2 / 2⁵ = 0.0625, and is
  not a bar.
- **E111-C2, the lag at 0.005 Hz**, H(f₀)'s phase with a 95% t interval on 4 df: **SLOW** if it lies wholly above 40° (a longer pole or a
  distributed path; P1D refuted there); **P1D** if wholly below 40° and it overlaps [22.2, 34.3]; **BETWEEN** if wholly below 40° and above 34.3°;
  **BELOW** if wholly under 22.2°; **INCONCLUSIVE** if it holds 40°.
- **E111-C3, the session against E73**: the 0.025 Hz lag (the fifth harmonic) against E73-F25's 75.60 ± 7.82°: INSIDE within 3 SE of the
  difference, else OUTSIDE.
- **E111-C4, the hold**: at least 95% of in-launch samples at ≥ 790 MHz and the die mean under 65 °C (E73's R6).

**What would refute.** Part A's prediction is refuted by a coefficient wholly outside [0.62, 0.82], or a FAIL of A3. The room reading of the drift is
refuted, as E98 registered, by B2 wholly under 1.00 or over 1.25, or B1 holding 0 and excluding 0.25. P1D at 0.005 Hz is refuted by C2 = SLOW; the slow
and diffusive forms by C2 = P1D.

## What this cannot say

One card, one afternoon, one hold per kind: the holds are replicates of E97's design on another card, not of each other. The die is a whole-degree
reading here (command 74's raw codes have never been sent to this card, and E111 does not send them), so the plateau's fine structure rests on the fine
W and E64's k. Neither hold starts from rest, so how much arrives when is not measured. The inlet proxies are coarse, self-heated, and absent where
another lane holds a card. A room that does not warm makes the warm probes cool ones: B3 then reads UNDER-RESOLVED, and B1, B2 still gain two probes.
The camera reads the back of the board, not the die, through E73's ROI, and five laps give a jackknife on 4 df. Nothing here tests the 23 October
session itself, unless the afternoon reaches its 49.3 °C idle die, which is reported if it does.

## Amendments

- **A1 (Sun 1 Nov 2026, 13:5x PST; written before the `#community-lab` claim and before any launch, while no E111 data existed).
  On aifoundry2, a user who is only logged in, and whose every session has been idle for at least 120 minutes, does not block.**
  *What happened.* Since 10:12 PST another lab user has had one login on aifoundry2 (pts/8), idle since about 10:29: 2 h 33 min at
  13:02, 3 h 23 min at 13:52. No device node, card lock or device process was theirs: `et-who` read "No process holds an ET-SoC-1
  device node or card lock" at every look from 10:42 to 13:52. aifoundry2's runners end a session, and this queue waits, while anyone
  else is logged in (lib.sh's own test, which E60 kept for aifoundry2 when it relaxed it for aifoundry3 and aifoundry1). The coordinator
  was told at 13:05 and had not answered by 13:50. Waiting longer would have cost the warm afternoon, and by 16:15 everything.
  *The rule now* ([`idle_logins_e111.py`](idle_logins_e111.py)): the runners' one `who` call (`run_hpl.py`'s and `run_hlc.py`'s
  other-user check, before every launch process) and the queue's login count are answered from `who -u`: a user blocks unless every
