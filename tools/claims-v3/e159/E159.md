# E159: a large GEMM's sustained power and governed clock, G3's blind test, under aifoundry2's own governor (Q139)

Tue 3 Nov 2026 (week 45), about 05:30 PST. Registered before the claim and the first launch. It answers **rank 3 of Q138's final critic**, *"A large
GEMM's sustained power and governed clock on aifoundry2 (items 1 and 6) ... Register G3 (P0's power, closed through the frozen chain and the trip rule)
for 2-3 new GEMM shapes held 80-120 s in fp32 random, int8 full and zeros, with the predictions frozen first. Rules: sustained clock within 15 MHz, board
within 2 W, window die within 1 °C. Score the chain on the measured trace beside it (registered this time) and E136's law as the old arm"*, and the
kinds page's [section 11](https://spacesheep.dev/@yaroslavvb/horace-kinds-of-numbers#11-what-is-still-open), **item 1** ("Where a large GEMM falls")
and **item 6** (the power model). Q139 continues the owner's ask of 2 November 2026, about 18:40 PST: *"all cards are available, feel free to settle any
unanswered questions regarding horace's power model"*, with Q137's goal, *"to be able to predict the power requirement and heat generation of various
kinds of numeric computations as accurately as possible"*.

**Why.** E146's fresh shape `F6` held fp32 random at 745 MHz and 56.15 W where G2 (E136's sustained law on PS's power) said 800 MHz and 52.5 W, and its
window die read 3.6-4.6 °C over E136's law (R5). Q138's critic found that Q138(t)'s heat chain, given the measured watts, reads that die to about 1 °C,
so the miss was the power and clock path. Q139(q) wrote **G3** ([its README](../../../docs/reports/data/2026-11-03-q139q-gemm-g3/README.md)): E136's
burst law P0 as the power at each clock, an effective leakage constant of 0.1 W/°C above the run's start die, E119's dither at the burst cap, and the
governor's hold at 65.5 °C, closed through Q138(t)'s frozen chain. Post hoc on 56 sustained GEMM-pattern runs (14 of them large GEMMs), leave one session
out, G3 reads the clock to 10.8 MHz, the board to 1.86 W and the window die to 0.72 °C rms, where E136's law reads 37.7 MHz, 3.71 W and 1.39 °C; but
`F6` out of sample is 36 MHz high, and G3's two constants were chosen on those runs. **E159 is G3's blind test**: three shapes nobody has run, on a card
session after the freeze.

**The freeze precedes this registration.** Q139(q)'s design and predictions were committed at `91def7c4` (04:16:13 PST) and its corrections after
verification at `ea38c8c3` (05:08:35 PST; no frozen file changed); both are on `origin/main`. `score_e159.py check` reads **CHECK OK: all 28 files the
E159 scorer reads equal their bytes at 91def7c4** (run at about 05:20 and again at 05:31 PST). Nothing of E159 exists. This registration changes no shape, kind, order, hold,

## Rules, which can fail

Q139(q)'s rules (`predictions-e159.json` `rules`), applied by its frozen scorer unchanged; measured − predicted per hold:

- **The rules:** the sustained clock within **15 MHz**, the board within **2 W**, the window die within **1 °C**. Per quantity, **G3 PASSES** when every
  scored hold is inside, else **FAILS**, with the count. The rms of every arm per quantity; **G3 beats E136's law** on a quantity when its rms is lower.
- **The arms, all registered:** G3 (the primary); G3 at Q138(t)'s constants; **Q138(t)'s chain on the measured trace** (the heat path alone, given the
  watts; die only); **E136's law, the old arm** (footing G2 on the same P0 levels: the sustained cap 60.827 W, a held cell at 62.06 W; its die law with the
  hold's measured start); G3 given the hold's own first second (a diagnostic, no verdict).
- **The attribution:** a G3 clock or board miss on a hold whose first-second power p800 is more than 4 W from P0(800) (E146 R3's band) is P0's level; a
  die miss where the chain on the measured trace is inside 1 °C is the power path, not heat (and with the chain outside 1 °C, the heat path).
- **Which holds are scored:** *"A hold cut by a stop or not run is not scored; the registered subset is the first six of the order."* The verdicts are
  given over **every scored hold (the primary)** and over **the registered subset alone**; if the cap or a stop leaves the subset short, its verdict is
  labelled incomplete.
- **R0, the ties:** every check of C in every launch (smoke, stop tests, holds) is ok; a failure labels every verdict.

**The reduction** is [`reduce_e159.py`](reduce_e159.py): (1) `score_e159.check()`, else exit 2 and nothing scored; (2) a hold is COMPLETE when all 12 of
its processes have a work launch record with rc 0, every kernel record of it is ok (the check of C), and no safety trip falls inside it; otherwise it is
CUT, listed and not scored; (3) each session directory is scored on its own by `e159_g3.score`, unchanged, on a temporary copy of the session with the
same telemetry and launch records and the cut holds left out, and the rows are pooled (a session the queue resumed after another user's login starts its
chain from its own first sample); (4) the attribution, R0 and the safety record (peaks per run, trips, holds not admitted, peak locks, the stop tests'
records, the TDP and threshold read after every run, card time). `selftest_e159.py` ([`selftest.txt`](selftest.txt): ALL PASS) checks the protocol against
the frozen design, the runner's stop limits, the queue's stop-path check on made-up records, the reduction on E146's committed session (F6's holds
relabelled as H1's cells: both scored, 745.1 MHz / 56.15 W measured; a hold missing a process cut), and the attribution rule. A toy-card dry run of the
whole queue (smoke, both stop tests, the session's ten runs) through `run_e159.py` and `reduce_e159.py` ([`logs/dry-queue.txt`](logs/dry-queue.txt),
[`dry-reduce.txt`](dry-reduce.txt)) fired both stops on live samples and scored all nine holds.

## What this changes for the prediction (Q137-Q139)

The term tested is **a governed large GEMM's sustained power and clock path** on aifoundry2: whether a sustained GEMM's clock, board power and die follow
from its burst power at each clock (P0), the chip's heat chain and the governor's trip, with no sustained cap fitted to earlier runs. The README reports
**the blind out-of-sample error per quantity before (E136's law, the old arm) and after (G3)** on E159's holds, the chain on the measured trace (heat given
the watts) beside them, and, per hold, whether a miss is P0's level, the power path or heat. If G3 holds, a sustained GEMM's clock and die can be
predicted from bits-only burst levels and the frozen heat path; if it fails on clock but the chain passes, the governor's held-state clock mapping (or P0's
per-shape level) is the next term, as Q139(q)'s C1 says.

## What this does not do

No new binary, no vendor binary, no sys_emu run. One card and one session: no interval between sessions; each hold runs once, so the unit of replication is
the hold, and three shapes × three kinds is a small blind pool. No room thermometer (the anchor is the measured rest die). The die is the firmware's
whole-degree reading; G3 predicts window means only, not the firmware's 1 s steps. P0's line law is new at H2 only (H3's levels are E136's `g2k`'s; H1's
sit beside `D8`'s and `F6`'s): E159 is blind for G3's two constants on all three shapes.
