About this page

A collector on aifoundry2 reads the three machines every 10 minutes, read-only and at low priority: /proc, the card driver's counters in sysfs, et-who, the lab's health check (et-lab-health, rev 3, sent over ssh), et-lab-manifest, nodewatch's logs, claudes status and the spacesheep session list. It bakes the result into this page and republishes it when something changed, and at least every 60 minutes; an open tab reloads itself on each new version. It never opens a card's device node; a live card sample is off unless someone switches it on, and then waits for a free card.

The page is private because it names the lab's users. For other people it shows only a login name, session and process counts, idle time and card holds; never commands, files, addresses or where anyone connects from.

How to update it

  • On aifoundry2, in the checkout: tools/lab/dashboard/update.sh now collects, rebuilds and republishes at once; open tabs update within seconds. update.sh now --card-sample also takes a card telemetry sample, behind every rule of the card etiquette.
  • From another machine: ssh aifoundry2 claude/et-soc1-prototyping/tools/lab/dashboard/update.sh now.
  • Or ask Claude in the aifoundry2 session: "refresh the lab dashboard", "why is aifoundry1 amber?".
  • A machine that shows "approval needed": run ssh aifoundry1 true (or aifoundry3) on aifoundry2, approve the address it prints, then update.sh now.
  • Silence a known alert: update.sh ack <alert id> [days] [note] (the id is in each alert's detail); update.sh status shows the last runs and deploys.
